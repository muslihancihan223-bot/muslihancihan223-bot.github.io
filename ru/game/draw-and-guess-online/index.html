Как Играть

1. Join a Room: Enter a public room or create a private one to play with friends. Minimum 3 players are required to start. 2. Take Turns Drawing: When it’s your turn, you will receive a word to draw. Use the drawing tools to illustrate it clearly. 3. Guess the Word: While another player is drawing, type your guesses in the chat. The faster you guess correctly, the more points you earn! 4. Score Points: Points are awarded for correct guesses and for how many players successfully guess your drawing. 5. Rounds & Winner: The game continues for several rounds. At the end, the player with the highest score wins. 6. Use Hints (Optional): Some versions have hints or drawing aids to make guessing easier.
