Как Играть

Desktop Controls: Left Click: Interact with UI buttons, purchase upgrades, and drag & drop Runes in the Forge. Space: Unleash Hero Ultimate Skill. 1 / 2: Cast Secondary Elemental Skills. A: Toggle Auto-Cast Ultimate. R: Cycle Target Priority (Closest, Boss First, Lowest HP, Strongest). T / Tab: Toggle Game Speed Boost (x1.0, x1.5, x2.0, x5.0). J / K / L: Quick-switch Upgrade Tabs (J = Power, K = Survival, L = Control). Esc / P: Open Settings / Pause Game. Mobile & Tablet Controls: Tap: Select upgrades, cast skills, and navigate menus. Touch & Drag: Drag and merge Runes in the Forge tab.
