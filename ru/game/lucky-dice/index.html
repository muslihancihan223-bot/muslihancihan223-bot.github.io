Lucky Dice — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Аркады, 3D». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Aim, pull back, and launch dice across the board like a slingshot. When two matching dice touch, they merge into the next number and fly off to find their own match, triggering chain-reaction cascades. Each level gives you a limited number of throws and a target score to beat — plan your shots carefully to set off the biggest possible cascade and clear the board. Mobile Controls: Drag Back: Pull the die back to aim, like a slingshot. Release: Let go to launch the die in the aimed direction.
