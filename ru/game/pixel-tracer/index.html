Играть в Pixel Conquest

Pixel Conquest — бесплатная игра в жанре «.io, Экшен, Стратегии». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Goal: The goal is to conquer the most territory and be the last player remaining on the grid. Controls: Desktop: Use the Arrow Keys to direct your character. How to conquering Territory: Move out from your colored territory to draw a trail. Return to any part of your own territory to complete a loop. The area you've enclosed will be claimed and converted to your color. Elimination: You are eliminated if you run into the outer walls or an opponent's established territory. City Building & Scoring: As your territory grows, buildings like houses and skyscrapers will automatically be built, generating points for you over time. Conquering enemy territory, destroying their buildings, and eliminating opponents will also boost your score. Power-ups: Occasionally, power-ups will appear on the map. Move over them to collect special abilities for a limited time. Speed: Move twice as fast. Shield: Survive one fatal collision. Ghost: Pass through enemy trails without being eliminated. Bomb: Instantly claim a large circular area around you.
