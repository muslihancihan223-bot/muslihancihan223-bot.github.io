Играть в Mega Block Survivors

Mega Block Survivors — бесплатная игра в жанре «Экшен, 3D, Платформеры». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Survive as long as you can and defeat the final boss of the forest. Your weapons attack automatically. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump (double jump), Q or right mouse button for the hero ability, E to interact. Mobile: left stick to move, drag on the right side to look, a jump button and an action button that uses the hero ability and turns into a hand near chests and other objects. Collect blue cubes for XP and coins to open chests.
