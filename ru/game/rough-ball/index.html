Как Играть

Don’t Fall Down! Step into the world of Rough Ball, an addictive and challenging physics-based platformer. Control a cheeky smiley ball through dangerous rocky caves. Master slippery slopes and tricky jumps to win each level in this fun “do not fall” adventure!
