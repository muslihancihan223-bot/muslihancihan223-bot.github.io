IT LURKS — бесплатная игра в жанре «Хоррор, 3D, Выживание». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Each night runs from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. As the nights progress, the creatures become stronger and the game becomes more difficult. Check your windows regularly. Use your flashlight to see what is outside. Turn off the lights when something is nearby. Check under your bed for anything hiding there. Stay alert until 7:00 AM. Survive all 8 nights to complete the game. Controls: Desktop: SpaceBar: To Under the bed/Return to Bed Shift: Checks on window Z: Flashlight X: Turn off lights ---------------- Mouse Click Support: Interact with visual buttons and objects. Mobile: Visual Buttons: Tap to interact and play.
