Играть в Vet Tycoon: Pet Hospital

Vet Tycoon: Pet Hospital — бесплатная игра в жанре «Аркады, Симуляторы, 3D». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Drag your finger to walk (arrow keys or WASD on a computer). Pick up a pet from the waiting area and carry it to a free bed. Look at the icon above the pet, take that medicine from the cabinet — soap, a shot or a pill — and bring it to the bed. The pet gets well and leaves coins: walk to the coin pile to collect them. Step on a price circle to buy beds, wards and nurses. Nurses get tired, so bring them coffee. Earn more, open new wards and the Water Clinic!
