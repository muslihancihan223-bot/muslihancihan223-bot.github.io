Как Играть

Goal: reach 2,000 residents before the 10-minute timer ends. You start with a small village: extend it with roads, paint housing zones along them, then add shops and factories for jobs, and parks, schools, police and clinics to keep residents happy. Faster wins earn more stars. Mouse: drag to move the camera, right-click drag to rotate and tilt, scroll to zoom. WASD or arrow keys also move the camera. With a tool selected, drag to draw roads, paint zones or dig rivers; click a building to upgrade or demolish it. Touch: one finger to move or use the selected tool, two fingers to zoom, rotate and tilt.
