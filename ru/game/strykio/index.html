Как Играть

Score more goals than your opponent before time runs out. Choose a quick AI match, Career, Cups, Club Career, Training, Arcade Arena or a local two-player duel. Tied competitive matches use extra time or a Golden Goal where the mode allows it. Against AI: Left/Right or A/D to move; Up or W to jump; Down or S to fall faster; Space or J to shoot; F to lob; K for a low shot; E to prepare a charged Ultimate. Double-tap shoot for a hard shot. Move backwards and lob for a self-pass. Ball contact is required. Local Player 1: A/D move, W jump, S shoot, Q lob, X low shot, E Ultimate. Player 2: Left/Right move, Up jump, Down shoot, Right Shift lob, Right Ctrl low shot, Enter Ultimate. P or Escape pauses. Local two-player uses one keyboard. On mobile, turn your phone to landscape and use the on-screen movement, jump, shoot, lob, low-shot and Ultimate buttons. The ? button explains the controls. Choose to allow saving to restore progress later, or play without saving for the current session. The shop uses virtual coins. Optional rewarded videos can grant 120 coins when available; rewards require the SDK confirmation and cancelled videos give no reward. Separate interstitial ads may appear when entering the game and between completed matches, subject to platform availability and frequency rules.
