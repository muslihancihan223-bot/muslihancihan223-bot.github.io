Как Играть

Take control of your chef and prepare customer orders. Serve dishes quickly to maintain satisfaction levels. Complete level goals to unlock new recipes and improvements. Desktop Controls: ↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward. ↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward. ← (Left Arrow): Move left. → (Right Arrow): Move right. On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
