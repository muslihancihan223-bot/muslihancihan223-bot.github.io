+1 Tongue Escape — бесплатная игра в жанре «Аркады, На ловкость, 3D». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Train in the lobby to increase your tongue reach, then enter the obstacle course. Aim your tongue at a surface to grab it and pull yourself across gaps. Avoid hazards, collect trophies and reach the end of each stage. Desktop: WASD to move, right mouse button to look, E or left mouse button to use the tongue, Space to jump. Mobile: use the on-screen joystick and action buttons. Play in landscape orientation.
