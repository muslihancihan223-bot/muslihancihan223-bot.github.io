Как Играть

"Geometry: Black Wave" can be tricky, but with these detailed instructions, you'll be able to master it in no time: 1. Start the game and select a level. The difficulty of the level depends on the emoji drawn on the button. 3. To move up, tap and hold the screen. The longer you press the screen, the higher the wave will rise, but until it hits the wall. 4. To move downward, release the screen. This will cause the wave to start moving downward until it reaches the ground. 7. The wave can overcome various obstacles such as spikes, blocks and other obstacles. But be careful: if the wave touches any obstacle, you will lose. 8. Ride the wave to the finish line to overcome the level and get a high score.
