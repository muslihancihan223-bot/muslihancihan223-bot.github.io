Как Играть

JuicyJong — A Mind-Engaging Color Puzzle! Join an exhilarating adventure in this creative reinterpretation of the classic game of Mahjong. Discover the world of JuicyJong, where each move demands cleverness and focus! How to Play: Swipe a tile to move it in any direction. - Move tiles around the board, collecting three or more identical symbols in a row. Use special bonuses to help yourself through particularly tough levels. - Click on the tile once to see what tiles are below. - Click and hold the pointer over the tile for 1 second to see where it will land. - If you match more than 3 tiles, you get additional bonuses. JuicyJong is more than just a puzzle; it's a test of your observational skills and strategic thinking!
