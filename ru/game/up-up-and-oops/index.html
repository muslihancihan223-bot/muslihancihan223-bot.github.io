Как Играть

Climb the floating path by choosing the correct action for the next tile. If the path continues straight ahead, jump forward. If it turns, press the direction in which the path continues. Make your choice before the gauge runs out. In Classic mode, clear every step to unlock the next level. Each level becomes longer and more difficult, and your next run starts after the latest level you cleared. You can earn rewards for reaching new steps even if you fail, and you may watch an advertisement to double the reward earned from that run. In Endless mode, climb as high as possible and aim for a new high score. Classic and Endless give you three lives. After losing all three, you may use one available revive. In Online Race, compete against 2–6 players for two minutes. Reach a higher step than the other racers to finish ahead of them. Falling returns you to your latest checkpoint, placed every 20 steps. Desktop Controls: W or ↑: Move upward. S or ↓: Move downward. A or ←: Move left. D or →: Move right. Spacebar: Jump forward. Mobile Controls: UP, DOWN, LEFT, and RIGHT Buttons: Move in the selected screen direction. JUMP Button: Jump forward in the direction your character is facing.
