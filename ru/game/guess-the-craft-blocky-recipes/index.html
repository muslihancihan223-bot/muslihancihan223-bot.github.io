Играть в Guess the Craft: Blocky Recipes

Guess the Craft: Blocky Recipes — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Логические». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

ГоловоломкиЛогические

Как Играть

Goal: craft the secret item from the ingredients in your inventory. Controls: Mouse or finger - drag an ingredient from the inventory into a crafting table slot Tap a slot - put the ingredient back Clear button - empty the whole crafting table at once If a craft attempt fails, the ingredient backgrounds light up to help you: Green - the ingredient is correct and in the right place Blue - the ingredient is in the recipe, but sits somewhere else Black or grey - the ingredient is not in the recipe at all Not every item in the inventory is useful, some never appear in any recipe, so you will need some wit. Recipe Quiz has no crafting table: the recipe is already laid out, just tap the item it produces. The tutorial is always available in the settings, the gear button in the corner of the screen.
