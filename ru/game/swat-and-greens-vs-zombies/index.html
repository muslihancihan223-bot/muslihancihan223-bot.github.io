Как Играть

"SWAT & GREENS vs Zombies" is an engaging tower defense strategy game where you can upgrade and unlock new members of the SWAT squad as well as plants. Together, they will stand against waves of advancing zombies. Upgrade SWAT members and plants to create a stronger squad. - Each member of the special forces squad comes equipped with unique weaponry, strategically position them on the battlefield. - Every plant has a cooldown period; use their powers wisely in combat. - Strive to minimize losses in battle to maximize the level completion reward. Battle Controls: Drag SWAT members and plants onto the field by holding down the left mouse button/touchscreen.
