Как Играть

Drag a familiar back from the slingshot, aim and release to launch. Defeat all guards to win. On mobile, tap during flight to activate your familiar’s ability. On desktop, click or press Space. Each shot has one ability use. Drag the background to rotate the camera. Use the + and − buttons to zoom. Switch between Overview and Flight to choose your camera mode. Use fewer shots to earn more stars. Stars unlock levels and are never spent. Coins buy crew costumes. If you lose, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one extra shot, once per attempt.
