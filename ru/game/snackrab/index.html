Играть в Snackrab

Snackrab — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Животные, Слияние». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Tap a machine to make a snack. Drag a snack onto an identical one to merge them, or tap one and then the other. Drag a snack to the guest whose bubble shows it. Watch the ring around each guest: when it runs out the guest leaves. Tap the basket to spend coins on the stall and the beach. Drag anything you do not need onto the crab; a full crab dances and calms every guest. The slots in the tray keep a snack for later. The round arrow undoes your last move; the book shows what merges into what. Controls - Phone / tablet: tap and drag with one finger. - Desktop: click and drag with the mouse. Esc or P pauses and closes panels; in menus and the shop the arrow keys or Tab move the marker and Enter or Space choose. - Pause (top right) holds sound on/off, a how-to-play picture and restart.
