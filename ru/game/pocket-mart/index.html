Играть в Pocket Mart

Pocket Mart — бесплатная игра в жанре «Аркады, Приключения, Симуляторы». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Goal: stock your shelves, serve customers, collect the cash and grow your shop until every room and all ten products are unlocked. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the screen on a phone. Stand in a marked circle to work automatically: collect goods in the warehouse, stock shelves, run the register and pick up cash. Tap the button that appears at pads and desks to buy shelves, rooms, upgrades and staff. Tap Order to phone in more stock, or walk to your car, press Drive, and visit the Farm market and Mega Mall for cheaper boxes, then park in the delivery bay to unload. While driving, use W/S or drag up and down for gas and brake, A/D or drag sideways to steer, hold Space or the DRIFT button to slide, and press C or View to change camera. Esc or the pause button opens the menu.
