Как Играть

🕹️ HOW TO PLAY? • Each turn, choose one of four lanes to play your card • Create card stacks that add up to exactly 21 to score points • Go over 21 and the lane busts • 3 busts = Game Over • Build Combos and Streak bonuses to maximize your score • Finish the deck before time runs out to earn a time bonus!
