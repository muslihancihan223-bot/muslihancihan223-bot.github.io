Играть в Slimes: Evolution

Slimes: Evolution — бесплатная игра в жанре «Слияние, Idle». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

СлияниеIdle

Как Играть

Tap the box to spawn slimes (tapping speeds it up). Drag a slime onto an identical one to merge them into the next form. Higher forms earn more coins per second. Spend coins on upgrades, complete quests for gems and open the evolution map to see your progress. Reach the last form of a world to unlock the next one (+20% income forever). Rebirth for essence to earn faster. Controls: mouse or touch - tap and drag.
