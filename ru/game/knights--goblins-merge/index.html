Играть в Knights & Goblins: Merge

Knights & Goblins: Merge — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Аркады, Стратегии». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Tap a unit card at the bottom to buy a knight. Drag a knight onto an identical one to merge them into a stronger knight (up to level 10). Arrange the army on the grid: melee units in front, archers and healers behind. Press Fight! and the battle plays out on its own; use Fireball, Heal and Battle Cry during the fight. Win to earn gold and crowns, upgrade your castle and enlarge the grid. Desktop: drag with the mouse, keys 1-5 buy units, Space or Enter starts the fight, S changes speed, P pauses.
