Играть в Smash and Steal an Egg!

Smash and Steal an Egg! — бесплатная игра в жанре «Аркады, Приключения, Симуляторы». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Break eggs with your pickaxe, carry the hatched animal back to your base before the guard catches you, and place it in your pen to earn money. Spend money on stronger pickaxes, trails and pen upgrades, and train on the treadmill to run fast enough for the next zone. On PC: WASD - move LMB - swing RMB - look around Space - jump E - interact (steal, open, upgrade) F - inventory 1-7 - choose the pickaxe, bat or animals On smartphone: Joystick - move Swipe - look around Tap - swing the pickaxe Bottom right - jump and action buttons
