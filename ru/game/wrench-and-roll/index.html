Wrench and Roll — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, 3D, Машины». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

- 👆 Tap a crate that is connected to the open road. - 📦 The selected crate rolls down to your workbench. - 🔧 Collect 3 matching crates to merge and repair part of the car. - ⚠️ Your workbench has only 6 slots, so choose crates carefully. - 🔒 Clear nearby crates to unlock blocked paths and padlocked plates. - ❓ Reach mystery crates to reveal what is hidden inside. - 💣 Use bomb crates to destroy nearby steel plates. - 🧲 Use Magnet, Rewind, and Hammer boosters when you get stuck. - ⭐ Complete levels, repair cars, and earn up to 3 stars. Match the crates, fix the cars, and bring the garage back to life! 🔧🚙✨
