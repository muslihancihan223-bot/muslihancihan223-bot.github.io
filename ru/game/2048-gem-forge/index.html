Как Играть

Swipe on the board or use the arrow keys / WASD to slide all tiles. Two tiles of the same material merge into the next, more valuable one. Reach 2048 to forge a diamond, then keep going. The game ends when no moves are left. Undo (Z) takes back a move, the hammer smashes any tile. Your run is saved after every move.
