Играть в Sunny Cove

Sunny Cove — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Для девочек, Для детей». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Start by merging matching parts to assemble your first juice station. Tap a generator marked with a lightning bolt to make an item. Drag two identical items together to merge them into the next level, or tap one and then its matching partner. Collect the items shown on an order and press Serve order to earn coins. Spend coins on restoration tasks, follow conversations and visit new locations on the map. Use storage to free space and open more board cells as you progress. Play with a mouse or touch controls; progress saves automatically.
