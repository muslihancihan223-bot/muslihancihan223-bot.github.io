Играть в Coloring Book : Color By Number

Coloring Book : Color By Number — бесплатная игра в жанре «развлечения». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Use your mouse to complete each picture by coloring the numbered squares. - Left click: Choose an artwork, select a numbered color, and click squares with the matching number to fill them. - Hold left click and drag: Start on a matching square and drag across other matching squares to color continuously. - Move the picture: Hold the left mouse button on an empty or already colored area, then drag. - Scroll wheel up: Zoom in for a closer view. - Scroll wheel down: Zoom out to see more of the picture. Zoom follows your mouse pointer.
