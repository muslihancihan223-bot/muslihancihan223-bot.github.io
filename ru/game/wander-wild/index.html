Как Играть

⚔️ Fight Use cards to attack enemies, defend yourself, and apply special effects. Each turn, choose an action that brings you closer to victory. 🃏 Use Cards Combine attack, defense, and ability cards. Use Poison, Bleed, Burn, Stun, Weaken, and other effects to gain the advantage. ⚡ Charge Your Super Build up energy during battle and use your Super Attack when the time is right. Some cards and Relics allow you to charge faster or make your Super more powerful. 💎 Collect Relics Earn rewards after each victory and unlock new Relics. Equip up to 4 Relics at once and create your own combinations. 🏕️ Upgrade Your Camp Use the resources you earn to develop your camp. Upgrades improve your hero’s stats and help you defeat tougher enemies. 🗺️ Venture Further Each chapter consists of 15 battles. Defeat enemies, upgrade your hero, and venture deeper into the world of Wander Wild.
