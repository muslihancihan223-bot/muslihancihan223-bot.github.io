Block Match 3D — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Для детей, 3D». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

– Tap any block with an open face to pick it up — buried blocks can't be taken yet. – If the block's icon matches one of the goal chips at the top, it flies straight to that goal. – Any other block goes to the storage bar at the bottom. If the bar overflows, you lose — so don't hoard! – Complete a goal chip and the next category slides in; stored blocks of that category fly to it automatically. – Clear all the categories before the timer runs out to win the level. – Stuck? Use boosters: Hint finds a matching block, Magic Wand collects one instantly, Shuffle rearranges icons, and Undo returns your last block.
