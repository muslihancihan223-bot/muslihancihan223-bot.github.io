Играть в Alderun

Alderun — бесплатная игра в жанре «Экшен, Стратегии, Мультиплеер». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Start with the guided practice, or choose New Game, select a map and mode, and pick your hero. Read the map briefing: your goal may be to defend a castle, capture objectives or defeat the enemy team. Defeat enemies to earn experience and gold, learn skills, buy equipment and support your allies. Desktop: left-click to select a hero or unit; drag to select a group. Right-click the battlefield to move, attack or interact. Use the on-screen ability buttons or their displayed keyboard shortcuts. Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom. Hold Space to follow your hero. Press Esc to cancel an action, close a window or open the game menu. Touch: tap to select units and issue orders. Tap an ability, then its target when needed. Drag the battlefield to move the camera and pinch to zoom. Use the on-screen menus for skills, equipment and options. For online play, choose Multiplayer and join or create a battle.
