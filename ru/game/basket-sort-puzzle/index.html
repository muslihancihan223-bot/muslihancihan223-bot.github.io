Играть в Basket Sort Puzzle

Basket Sort Puzzle — бесплатная игра в жанре «Головоломки, Три в ряд, Логика». Без скачивания и регистрации: играйте сразу в браузере на телефоне, планшете или компьютере.

Как Играть

Tap or click a box to open it and spill its marbles onto the conveyor. Marbles drop into the basket of the same color; fill a basket with three marbles and it rolls off to the delivery truck. Empty every box to complete the level, but don't let the conveyor fill up with colors that no basket can take. Boxes in the back stay closed until you clear the way, so plan a few moves ahead. Stuck? Use the GET BOX power-up to bring matching baskets to the front. Controls: mouse click on desktop, tap on touch screens.
