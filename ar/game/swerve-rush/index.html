طريقة اللعب

Steer with A/D or the arrow keys, W / Up = gas, S / Down = brake. On phones the car accelerates by itself: steer with your left thumb (or LEFT/RIGHT buttons or tilt in Settings). Pass traffic as closely as you can: every near miss scores points, fills your nitro and grows the combo. Press Shift or Space (NITRO button on phones) for a speed boost that doubles all points. A crash ends the run; spend your cash on upgrades and new cars, then go for a new high score.
