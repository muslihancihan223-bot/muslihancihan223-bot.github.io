طريقة اللعب

The controls are simple and intuitive: Movement: Keys W, A, S, D Jump: Space bar (use it together with trampolines for super jumps!) The goal of the game is to explore the map, find and collect all the hidden capybaras. Look around! Capybaras can hide in the most unexpected places. Use trampolines and power-ups to reach the secret corners.
