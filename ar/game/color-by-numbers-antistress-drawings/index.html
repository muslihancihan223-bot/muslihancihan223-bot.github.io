العب Color by Numbers. Anti-stress Drawings.

Color by Numbers. Anti-stress Drawings. لعبة مسلية مجانية. بدون تحميل أو تسجيل — العب فورًا في متصفح الهاتف أو الجهاز اللوحي أو الكمبيوتر.

طريقة اللعب

Choose a picture Open the gallery and select a picture. Beginners will enjoy simple drawings with large fragments, experienced ones — complex compositions. The collection includes pixel art, flowers, animals, mandalas, and abstractions. Follow the numbers Each fragment is marked with a number. The palette shows which color to fill the area with. Tap on a fragment — it will be filled with the right color. Everything is intuitive. Relax and create The drawing process helps you immerse yourself in creativity and escape from worries. This is meditation and art therapy: empty outlines turn into a bright picture. Save and share When the drawing is ready, save it to the gallery or share with friends. You've created a real masterpiece! Tip: Start with large fragments, gradually moving to small details. This helps you stay on track and enjoy every stage of coloring.
