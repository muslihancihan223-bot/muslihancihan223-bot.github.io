طريقة اللعب

😊 Let's get to know the game: 🎮 Game Modes: Single-player mode - practice your controls by completing obstacle courses. Multiplayer mode - compete or team up with other players to progress through the game together. ✏️ Player Tools: Pencil - draw new elements on the level. Eraser - erase previously drawn elements. 🎮 Controls on a Computer: Move your character - press W/A/S/D. Jump - press Space. Draw or erase lines - move the mouse with the left button held down. 📱 Controls on a Smartphone: Move your character - use the virtual joystick. Jump - press the virtual button. Draw or erase lines - swipe your finger across the screen. Tap "Play" and win the game! 🎉
