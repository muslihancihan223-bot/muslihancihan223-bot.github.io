طريقة اللعب

Your goal is to identify the flag, find the country on the map, place your pin, and rack up the highest score possible! Controls: • PC: Click and drag to pan the map, use the mouse wheel to zoom, and left-click to drop your flag pin. • Mobile: Swipe to pan the map, pinch to zoom, and tap to drop your flag pin. Once you place your pin, click the "Submit" button to confirm your guess. If your pin lands perfectly inside the correct country, you earn a maximum of 10 points. If you miss, you are awarded partial points based on your distance from the target (5 points within 500km, 3 points within 1000km, and 1 point within 2000km). Complete all rounds to get your final score and share your results with your friends!
