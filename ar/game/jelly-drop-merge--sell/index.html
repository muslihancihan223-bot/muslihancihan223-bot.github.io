طريقة اللعب

🎮 How to Play Jellies fall into the jar New jellies appear from the top and drop into the container. Merge identical jellies When identical jellies touch, they merge into a more advanced and valuable version. Fill the jar Keep an eye on the fill level — the more jellies inside, the closer you are to selling. Sell the contents When the jar is nearly full or completely filled, sell the jellies to turn them into money. Upgrade your progress Improve key parameters: — jelly level — sale income — jar capacity — progress speed Earn even more Each upgrade boosts your profits and unlocks new jelly tiers.
