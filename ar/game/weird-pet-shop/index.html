العب Weird Pet Shop

Weird Pet Shop لعبة محاكاة, إدارة, مريحة مجانية. بدون تحميل أو تسجيل — العب فورًا في متصفح الهاتف أو الجهاز اللوحي أو الكمبيوتر.

طريقة اللعب

Tap "Start shift" and open the delivery box. Compare the pet with the Guide before the timer runs out. If everything matches, tap "Looks normal". If something is wrong, tap "Anomaly!" and then tap the odd detail on the pet. Care for healthy pets: rub them with your finger or mouse, because good care raises the price. Then answer the buyer in the chat: accept the offer, stay at your price or ask for more. If you push too hard, the buyer leaves. At night, tap the cage where something moves before it is too late. After the shift, check your results and spend coins on upgrades. Controls: mouse on desktop, touch on mobile.
