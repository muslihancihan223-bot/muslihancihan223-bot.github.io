طريقة اللعب

Drag or swipe to rotate the sculpture; zoom with the mouse wheel or a pinch, and twist with two fingers on touch screens. Tap or click a piece to slide it out along its arrow. If another block is in the way, the piece is pinned and you lose one attempt, so turn the sculpture and look for an open path. Remove every piece to clear the level. Stuck? The magnifier highlights a free piece, the brass wedge frees a pinned one, and a repair kit gives +2 attempts. On rush orders, finish before the timer for a gold, silver or bronze bonus.
