طريقة اللعب

How to Play Coffee Merge House The objective of Coffee Merge House is to prepare delicious products, complete customer orders, earn coins, and grow generating and merging items on the board. Click a generator to create a new product. Each generated item occupies an empty space on the board, so make sure you manage your available slots carefully. If the board becomes crowded, look for matching products that can be combined. Discover more valuable drinks, snacks, and café treats. The further you progress through a merge chain, the more advanced products you will be able to prepare. Each customer displays the product they would like to order and the number of coins they will pay for it. Once you have created the requested item, drag it from the board directly onto the customer. The order will be completed, you Keep generating, merging, and serving customers to develop your Coffee Merge House into a thriving café! Controls - Left-click or tap a generator to create an item. - Drag one matching item onto another to merge them. - Drag a requested product onto a customer to complete their order. - Click the upgrade button to purchase available improvements.
