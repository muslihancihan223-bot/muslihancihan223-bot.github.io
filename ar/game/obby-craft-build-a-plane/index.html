طريقة اللعب

How to play "Obby Craft: Build a Plane" 1. Assemble your airplane from parts You need to approach the plane (there is a crafting area next to it) Select a part in the inventory at the bottom Click to install next to the plane If you have run out of parts, you can buy them in the store. 2. Upgrade your slingshot A slingshot awaits you before takeoff. The more powerful the leveling of the slingshot, the higher and faster you will go flying. Spend coins on improvements. 3. Fly and collect coins Launch the plane. Control it in the air. There are coins scattered on the map — collect them right during the flight. This is the main in-game currency. 4. Go to the parts store and roulette Buy new blocks in the parts store. There are 2 free skins — take them at once. Spin the roulette wheel and you can win rare items or a lot of coins. 5. Go through the obby-tests An obstacle course (Obbi) awaits you. Dodge the obstacles!
