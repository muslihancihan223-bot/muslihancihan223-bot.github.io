طريقة اللعب

- Move: WASD or arrow keys. Jump: Space. - Rotate/tilt the camera: Q/R and T/G, or drag on the game view. Zoom: mouse wheel. - Press E or Enter for the nearby action. In Break An Egg, hold it to mine an egg. - Press 1 to equip or put away the pickaxe in Break An Egg; press 2 to toggle an equipped item. - In Steal An Egg, press F to swing the bat when allowed. Press X to toggle slow movement. - Click a mode’s PLAY button to start. Click menus, shop buttons and upgrade prompts to make selections. ### Touch controls - Drag the on-screen joystick to move. Drag the game view to rotate the camera; pinch to zoom. - Tap Jump or the contextual action button; hold the action button to mine in Break An Egg. - Tap the bat button in Steal An Egg and the hotbar slots to toggle tools or items. Tap on-screen menus and upgrade prompts.
