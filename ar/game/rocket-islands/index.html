طريقة اللعب

Desktop: Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Drag to rotate the camera and scroll to zoom. Press E to pick up a nearby rocket or merge matching rockets. Press Q to put a rocket down, Space to jump, and B to activate your base shield. While carrying a ready rocket, press F, then click an enemy island to launch. Escape cancels targeting or opens the pause menu. Mobile: Drag on the left side to move and on the right side to rotate the camera. Use the on-screen action buttons to pick up, merge, drop, jump, and launch. Tap an enemy island when choosing a target. Merge two rockets of the same tier, spend your income on upgrades, and use shields to block incoming attacks. Progress saves automatically. Optional rewarded ads provide temporary bonuses.
