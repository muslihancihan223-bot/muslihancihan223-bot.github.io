طريقة اللعب

Your goal is to pick the correct sequence of emojis that represent the phrase, saying, or movie title shown above the puzzle. Think of it as translating text or ideas into emoji form in the right order. Each level presents a prompt (text-based or illustrated) and a jumble of emojis. You select emojis in the precise order needed to match the intended phrase. If you're correct on the first try, you earn all the stars for that level. Otherwise, you’ll get additional attempts with guidance. Stuck on a puzzle? The game offers helpful hints that can nudge you in the right direction without giving the full answer away.
