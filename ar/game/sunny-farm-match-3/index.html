العب Sunny Farm: Match 3

Sunny Farm: Match 3 لعبة ألغاز, أركيد, مريحة مجانية. بدون تحميل أو تسجيل — العب فورًا في متصفح الهاتف أو الجهاز اللوحي أو الكمبيوتر.

ألغازأركيدمريحةمطابقة

طريقة اللعب

Mouse or touch: swap two neighbouring fruits to line up three or more of the same kind (drag, or tap one fruit and then its neighbour). The level goal is shown at the top: collect fruits, reach a score, clear the board, dig up turf (match right on it), bring baskets down (match below a basket), catch fireflies (clear the tile under a firefly), push the pinwheel to the weathervane on the Sky Islands (a match next to the pinwheel moves it along the arrows) or, in Golden Sun Valley, untie festive ribbons (match next to the bows at both ends) and bake cakes (match next to a cake: sponge, cream, berries, candles). Special pieces: four in a row — rake (clears a row or column); 2x2 square — beehive; L or T shape — barrel; five in a row — magic watering can (removes every fruit of one kind). Swap two special pieces and both go off. Fog clears when you match on it or next to it. A sleepy cloud wakes up when you match next to it — then its rain hits obstacles or brings goal fruits. Moves left at the end turn into points — finish fast to earn three stars.
