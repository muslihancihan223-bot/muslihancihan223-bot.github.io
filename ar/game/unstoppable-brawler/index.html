طريقة اللعب

PC Controls (Keyboard & Mouse): • [W, A, S, D] or [Arrow Keys]: Move your fighter around the arena. • [Left Mouse Button (LMB)] or [B]: Punch attack (Spam for rapid combo). • [Right Mouse Button (Hold RMB)] or [N]: Shield Guard (Reduces incoming damage by 80%). • [Spacebar] or [Left Shift]: Instant Dash Dodge to evade enemy strikes and traps. Mobile / Touch Controls: • Virtual Joystick: Drag to move your fighter. • Punch Button: Tap to punch enemies. • Guard Button: Hold to block enemy attacks. • Dash Button: Tap to quickly dash and reposition. Pro Tips for Victory: 1. Never stop moving: Keep circling around groups of enemies to avoid getting surrounded. 2. Collect Potions Immediately: - Red Potions: Instantly restore your Health points. - Blue Potions: Instantly refill your Dash Energy meter. 3. Guard Wisely: When heavy enemies charge their attacks, hold Guard to withstand the blow, then counter-attack! 4. Choose Upgrades Strategically: If you're low on health, grab the Full Heal or Health Boost. If you prefer high mobility, prioritize Movement Speed and Energy Regen! 5. Use Reroll (Ad): Don't like the 3 upgrade cards? Tap "Reroll Cards" to get a brand-new selection!
