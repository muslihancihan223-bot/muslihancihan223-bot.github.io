طريقة اللعب

Tap an animal — it will run straight in the direction it's facing. If the path is clear, it escapes to the pen. If another animal is in the way — they collide and stop. Your goal is to find the right order to free them all. 🐑 On PC: click on an animal with the mouse. On mobile: tap the animal with your finger. Boosters: 1. 🗑️ Remove — take 2 animals off the field to clear the way. 2. 🔀 Shuffle — randomly rotate 5 animals to face new directions. 3. 🔄 Flip — pick 1 animal and turn it the way you need. Watch out for special sheep! 💣 Bomb sheep explodes after 3 collisions. ⏱️ Timer sheep explodes if you don't free it in time.
