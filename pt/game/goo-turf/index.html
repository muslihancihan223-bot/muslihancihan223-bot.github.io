Como Jogar

Lead your slime team in single-player 4v4 battles against bots. Cover the arena with your team's goo! The team with the most painted territory when time runs out wins. Win battles to unlock new stages. Desktop controls: WASD — Move Mouse — Aim Left mouse button — Shoot Shift — Dive into your team's goo to move faster and refill ink Space — Jump Esc — Pause Mobile controls: Use the on-screen joystick and action buttons. Play in landscape mode.
