Como Jogar

Desktop Controls: ← / → (Arrow Keys) or A / D: Move backward and forward. Spacebar: Jump. Hold Spacebar while in the air: Perform a frontflip. Mobile Controls: On-Screen Joystick: Move backward and forward by dragging the joystick. Jump Button: Tap to jump. Hold the Jump Button while in the air: Perform a frontflip.
