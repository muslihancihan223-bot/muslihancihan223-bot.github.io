Como Jogar

🎯 Goal: clear every cube from the picture. 👆 Move: tap the top colored tile in the tray below, or click it with a mouse. It takes one of five slots and releases ants. Each ant carries away one cube of its own color that it can reach from outside the shape. The slot opens up when all ants have left the tile. 🧩 Strategy: pick exposed colors first. Cubes inside become reachable after the outer layer is gone. If all five slots hold tiles with nothing to collect, you lose the level. ✨ New challenges include hidden tiles, keys, ice and paint. Choose the order carefully and help the ants carry away the last cube!
