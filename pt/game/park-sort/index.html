Jogar Park Sort

Park Sort é um jogo grátis de Quebra-cabeça, Carros, Combinar. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

Como Jogar

1. Tap a vehicle to drive it in the direction of its roof arrow. 2. If the path is clear, it leaves the lot and heads to the open garage of the same color. 3. Each garage fills in order: big vehicles first, then cars, then bikes. Check the empty spaces in the garage. 4. Vehicles that can't park yet wait in the parking slots and drive in automatically when their garage opens. 5. Don't let the waiting slots fill up! When all slots are full, the level ends. 6. Watch the NEXT list to see which garages come next, and plan your moves. 7. Stuck? Use boosters: Undo takes back a move, Hint shows a good move, +Slot adds a waiting slot, and Scan locks vehicles that can't park right now.
