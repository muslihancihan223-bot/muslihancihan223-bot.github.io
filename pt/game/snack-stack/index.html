Jogar Snack Stack

Snack Stack é um jogo grátis de Arcade, Simulação, 3D. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

ArcadeSimulação3DMagnataMouseGerenciamento

Como Jogar

Move with the arrow keys or WASD, or click/tap and drag anywhere on the screen to use the virtual joystick. Customers order at their tables. Walk to the matching kitchen counter to cook the food, then bring it to the table. Collect the cash at the register and the tips left on tables, and clean dirty tables so new customers can sit. Stand on a floor area to spend money on it: new tables, new dishes, staff and upgrades. Follow the goals, expand your restaurant and build the busiest snack bar in town!
