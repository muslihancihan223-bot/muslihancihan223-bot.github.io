Jogar PopCats: Merge the cats!

PopCats: Merge the cats! é um jogo grátis de Engraçados, Mesclar, Cats. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

EngraçadosMesclarCats

Como Jogar

- Connect identical seals into larger seals by clicking on the screen with your finger or mouse. - To get to know a cat and find out its history, click on its image in the bottom panel. - Make sure that the cats do not reach the top line, otherwise the game will end! - You can change the next cat for watching ads. - Use yarn to remove the interfering cat, and remember - the main thing here is not to miss! - Collect points to take a leading position in the player rankings. - Win by connecting the two biggest cats!
