Como Jogar

Hit the blocks at the bottom of your shaft: every hit adds strength, and every broken block gives coins. Clear a layer, then go deeper. On PC use WASD or arrows to move, Space to jump, left mouse button to hit the block under the cursor (hold to keep hitting), right mouse button or Q and E to turn the camera, wheel to zoom, Shift to lock the camera behind you. On phone use the joystick on the left, the hit button on the right with jump above it, swipe to turn the camera, pinch to zoom, and tap a block to hit it. The lift takes you between town and the bottom of your shaft.
