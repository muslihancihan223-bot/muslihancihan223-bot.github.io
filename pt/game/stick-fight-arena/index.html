Como Jogar

Win two rounds to win the fight: knock out the opponent or have more health when the timer runs out. Keyboard: A/D move, W jump, hold S to block, J attack (press again at the right moment to continue the combo), S+J uppercut, K special move, Back+K second special, Back+K up close throws the opponent, Space to swap fighters in 2 vs 2, P pauses the game. While being comboed, press K for a breaker. Stamina drains on attacks and blocks; at zero you get tired. On phones use the on-screen pad and buttons. Two players on one keyboard: P1 WASD + F/G, P2 IJKL + ; and '.
