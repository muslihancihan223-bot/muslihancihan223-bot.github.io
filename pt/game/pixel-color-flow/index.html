Jogar Pixel Color Flow

Pixel Color Flow é um jogo grátis de Combinar, Color. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

CombinarColor

Como Jogar

Check the pixel board and spot which colors need clearing first. Tap a pig to send it onto the conveyor. The pig will shoot/drop balls automatically onto matching color cubes only. Watch the ammo number above the pig—each shot uses ammo. When ammo becomes 0, the pig leaves the run. If it still has ammo, it can move into one of 5 waiting slots. Tap again to send pigs from the waiting slots back onto the conveyor. Manage the conveyor capacity (if it’s full, you must wait). Keep repeating until you clear all cubes and complete the level.
