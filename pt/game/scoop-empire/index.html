Como Jogar

Goal: build the biggest ice cream empire and unlock all 5 worlds. Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag on the screen (mobile). 1. Pick up ice cream from a machine and drop it on the counter. 2. Stand at the register to serve customers, then collect your cash. 3. Stand on a price tile to unlock machines, tables, rooms and staff. 4. Clean dirty tables and throw trash or extra items in the bin. 5. Walk into floating power-ups for timed boosts, and visit the Trader by the door and the Supplier by the drive-thru for special deals. 6. Unlock "Next Location" to travel to a new world. Mouse wheel / pinch to zoom. Press the trophy button to see the leaderboard.
