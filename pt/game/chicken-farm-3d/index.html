Como Jogar

Build your first coop, buy a hen and keep the trough filled. Collect eggs and sell them at the market, then spend your coins on more chickens, buildings and upgrades. Complete missions and deliver egg orders to grow your reputation. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to look around, Shift to run, Space to jump and E to interact. Press Esc to pause and M to mute. Mobile: Use the on-screen joystick to move, drag to look around and tap the action buttons to interact.
