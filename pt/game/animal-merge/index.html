Como Jogar

Objective: Merge animal bubbles to create upgraded animals and ultimately achieve the final objective by collecting fully developed animals. Gameplay Instructions: Launching the Animal Bubbles: Tap on an animal bubble to select it. Drag the bubble backward to aim. Release to launch the bubble in the opposite direction. Merging Animal Bubbles: When two identical animal bubbles collide, they merge to create an upgraded version. Continue merging bubbles of the same type to progress. Achieving the Objective: Merge two basic-level animal bubbles to form an intermediate-level animal. Merge intermediate-level animal bubbles to create the final full-fledged animal. Once the final animal is created, it is automatically collected as part of the level objective. Winning the Level: The level is completed when you have successfully created and collected the required number of final animals.
