Como Jogar

Goal: peel potatoes, earn coins, upgrade your tools and find the key to the vault. PC: WASD - Movement Mouse - Look around LMB or E - Grab a potato / Interact (drop, peel, sell, buy) Hold LMB on the skin - Peel (or hold Space) Space - Jump, Shift - Run, C - Crouch 1-4 - Choose a tool TAB - Open the upgrade book Esc or P - Pause Smartphone: Use the on-screen controls Joystick - Movement Swipe on the right - Look around Buttons - Grab, jump and switch tools
