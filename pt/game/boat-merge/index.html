Como Jogar

Drag two identical boats together to merge them into a higher-level boat. Place boats on available routes to earn coins automatically, then use those coins to purchase more boats and upgrades. Continue merging, expanding your fleet, completing tasks, and unlocking increasingly powerful boats. The main objective is to discover the highest-level boats and maximize your earnings.
