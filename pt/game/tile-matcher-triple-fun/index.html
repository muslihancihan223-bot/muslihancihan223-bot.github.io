Como Jogar

Clear the Tropical Tile Board! Dive into Tile Matcher – Triple Fun, a relaxing triple tile-matching puzzle inspired by Mahjong Solitaire. Tap to find and match three identical fruit-themed tiles—like peaches, blueberries, and bananas—to clear the vibrant, stacked board and conquer increasingly challenging levels against a serene beach backdrop with a surfer and palm islands. **Desktop Controls:** Mouse: Click to select and match tiles. Hint Button: Click the lightbulb icon for a helpful suggestion. Shuffle Button: Click to rearrange tiles on the board. **Mobile Controls:** Tap: Select tiles to match three identical ones and clear them. Hint Button: Tap the lightbulb icon for a suggestion. Shuffle Button: Tap to reshuffle tiles.
