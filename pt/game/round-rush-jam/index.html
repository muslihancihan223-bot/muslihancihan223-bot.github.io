Round Rush Jam é um jogo grátis de Combinar. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

Combinar

Como Jogar

Tap or left-click a colored piece to send it along the direction shown by its white arrows. A piece leaves the board only when its path is clear, so move blocking pieces in the right order and use boosters when a puzzle gets tricky. Remove every colored piece before the move counter reaches zero to win the level. Controls: left mouse button on desktop; tap on mobile and tablet. No keyboard controls are required.
