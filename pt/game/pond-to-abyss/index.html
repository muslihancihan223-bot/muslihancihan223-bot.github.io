Jogar Pond To Abyss

Pond To Abyss é um jogo grátis de Simulação, Relaxantes, Fishing. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

Como Jogar

Tap or click the water to cast your line (or press Space). Wait for a bite – when the "!" pops up above the bobber, tap quickly! Reeling: hold your finger, the mouse button or Space to lift the green zone and release to let it sink. Keep the fish inside the green zone to fill the catch bar. If the bar runs out, the fish gets away. Open the Bag to sell your catch for coins. In the Shop, buy upgrades (Luck, Quick Bite, Strong Line, Big Bag, Haggler), better rods, bait and helpers. Unlock new places on the Map – the deeper you go, the rarer and more valuable the fish. Catch all 39 species for the Fishdex, and after reaching the Abyss start a new season to make every fish sell for more forever.
