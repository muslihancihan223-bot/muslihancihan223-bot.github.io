Como Jogar

1. Choose a location — the higher the level, the more valuable the containers 2. Inspect 3 containers and select one for auction 3. Peek inside through the gap or watch an ad for a full preview 4. Negotiate with bots — raise your bid or pass 5. Win the auction, open the container and discover what's inside! 6. Sell items for profit, restore worn-out pieces
