Como Jogar

A or D or Left or Right arrow to move the character. W or Up arrow to jump. Hold F to throw the sword to hang and swing. Press G to attack or hold G to throw the sword forward. When it is hanging: Use the A or D keys, or the Left or Right arrow keys to swing. W or Up arrow to go up. Use S or the Down arrow to go down.
