Como Jogar

Train on the range until your Power is above the stage's recommended Power, then go through the gate and clear every zombie in the stage. Step on RETURN x2 in the safe zone to take double wins, buy stronger guns at the stands and rebirth for lasting multipliers. Turn on Autofire when you are tired of clicking. Desktop: WASD to move, left mouse button to shoot, right mouse button + mouse to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom (all the way in for first person), V to switch the view, R to reload, E to buy or equip at a stand, Space to jump. Phone: the left stick moves, dragging on the right turns the camera, the buttons shoot, jump, reload and switch the view.
