Como Jogar

Desktop (keyboard and mouse) Movement - W / A / S / D move. - Space dash in the movement direction, or towards the aim when standing still. Aim and attack - Mouse move aims the weapon at the cursor. - Left mouse button attacks with the current weapon. Hold to keep attacking at its fire rate. - Right mouse button is the alternate attack: throws throwable melee weapons (Baseball Bat, Meteor Maul) and pokes with Sword, Tempest Glaive. Nothing with a gun. - F throws a grenade, or detonates the one already in flight. Weapons and pickups - 1 / 2 select the melee or ranged slot. - Mouse wheel cycles through occupied slots. - Click a floor weapon to pick it up when in range. Orbs are collected by walking over them. Spells, once the skill is learned - Q Repel, E Shield, R Haste, C Fire Breath. Menus and information - U stat upgrades, I skill upgrades. Each opens only while points are unspent. - Tab held shows the scoreboard. - P pauses in solo games. Mobile (touch screen, landscape) Movement - Touch anywhere on the right half of the screen and drag to move. The stick appears under your thumb. - The jump button in the bottom-left corner dashes in the stick's direction, or towards the aim when standing still. Aim and attack - Aiming and attacking are automatic. The weapon locks onto the nearest visible enemy: guns fire at it, melee weapons swing once it is in reach. With no enemy around, the weapon faces where you move. - Tap a barrel or a steam pipe to attack it instead. Tap empty ground to cancel. - The Throw / Poke button next to the jump button is the alternate attack. It appears only with a weapon that has one: Throw for the Baseball Bat and Meteor Maul, Poke for the Sword and Tempest Glaive. - The grenade button next to the jump button throws a grenade, or detonates the one already in flight. Weapons and pickups - Tap a weapon slot at the bottom of the screen to switch to it. - A Pick up button appears next to the jump button when a floor weapon is in range. Orbs are collected by walking over them. Spells, once the skill is learned - Each learned spell adds a button above the jump button: Repel, Shield, Haste, Fire Breath. Menus and information - The two + buttons right of the weapon slots open stat upgrades and skill upgrades. They appear only while points are unspent. - MENU and pause are in the top-right corner. - Turning the device upright pauses the game until it is back in landscape.
