Como Jogar

GOAL — Be the first to empty your hand, or the last player left alive. Penalties make you draw cards; whoever reaches 25 cards is eliminated, so you can also win by outlasting everyone. BASIC RULE — Play a card that matches the colour or the number of the card on top of the pile. If you have nothing playable, draw. CONTROLS Desktop: click a card to play it, click the deck to draw. When a card asks you to choose a colour or a rival, the choice appears on screen. Mobile: with a full hand the cards overlap, so the first tap lifts a card and the second one plays it. You can also swipe a card upwards to play it straight away. Runs: tap the first card of the run — it carries a magenta badge showing how long the run can get — then tap the following cards one by one; the game highlights what fits. Swipe up or press PLAY RUN to drop them all at once. ACTION BUTTONS (bottom row, they only appear when you can use them) LAST CARD! — call it when you are down to one card. If a rival presses CAUGHT! before you do, you draw. COLD BLOOD — freezes the penalty coming at you. One per match, and it doubles the next penalty you deal. DOUBLE! — raises the XP at stake. Rivals can fold before it lands on them. The lifebuoy icon in the top right turns the beginner hints on and off.
