Jogar Fruits into Baskets

Fruits into Baskets é um jogo grátis de Comida, Combinar, Easy. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

ComidaCombinarEasy

Como Jogar

🍊 Tap a fruit on the screen and it will fall. 🍊 If a fruit is stuck and can't fall — remove the ones blocking it from below first. 🍊 Collect 3 fruits in a basket and a new basket will open. 🍊 If there's no matching basket for a fruit — no worries, it will wait on a temporary plate. Clear all the fruits from the field — level complete. You're the best!
