Como Jogar

Control a tornado and absorb everything around you to grow bigger and stronger! Start with small objects such as trash, trees, and road signs, then move on to cars and even entire buildings. On PC, use WASD to move the tornado. Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse to rotate the camera and look around the environment. Use the mouse wheel to zoom the camera in and out. On mobile devices, control the tornado using the on-screen virtual joystick.
