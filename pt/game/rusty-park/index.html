Jogar Rusty Park

Rusty Park é um jogo grátis de Arcade, Simulação, 3D. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

ArcadeSimulação3DMagnataGerenciamentoIdle

Como Jogar

Drag anywhere with the mouse or your finger to walk (arrow keys / WASD also work). Walk onto a glowing pad to pay for what it builds, stand on a work spot to sell tickets or run a ride, walk over cash to pick it up. Hit junk to smash it and carry scrap to the crusher for metal. Restore every ride and hire its staff, then open the next park. Buttons on screen open upgrades, goals and the shop.
