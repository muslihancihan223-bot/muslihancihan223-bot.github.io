Como Jogar

Click or tap the strength platform to gain power and grow your character. Walk through doors into rooms, defeat the enemies inside and grab the victory plate to finish the run and return to the lobby. Spend coins on upgrades and new gear, buy treadmills and characters in the market, and rebirth to keep part of your progress. Controls: move with WASD or the on-screen joystick, click or tap to lift and attack, drag the mouse or a finger to look around.
