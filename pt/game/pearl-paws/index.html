Jogar Pearl Paws

Pearl Paws é um jogo grátis de Arcade, Simulação, 3D. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

Como Jogar

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag with the mouse. On phones, drag anywhere on the screen. Walk onto a dashed pad to build or buy. Pick up goods from the crates and carry them to the shelves. Customers pay at the register: collect the cash piles. Spend money on new shelves, stalls and upgrades, hire helpers and catch thieves by running into them. Ride the waves at the surf school.
