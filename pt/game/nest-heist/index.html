Como Jogar

Goal: raise your creatures, complete each world’s grand zoo order, discover species and gain enough fame to rebirth and unlock the next stage. 1. Walk to the conveyor in the middle of the island and buy an egg. 2. Carry it into your nest. It is placed automatically and hatches after its timer. 3. Collect food from a patch and walk back to your granary to deliver it. Pet your creatures to help them grow; their preferred food makes them happy. 4. Sell creatures to the buyers who visit your farm and use the coins to build more floors. Adults are worth more. 5. Steal from other nests and defend your own. Your companion cannot be sold or stolen. Private rooms contain only the friends who connect; they are not filled with bots. Desktop - WASD or arrow keys: move - Mouse drag or Q / E: turn the camera - Space: jump - F or the action button: perform the action shown on screen - R: pet · G: bonk · X: quick sale - C: switch camera · B: bestiary · T: board Mobile (landscape) - Left thumb: joystick to move - Drag the screen: turn the camera - Use the contextual buttons on the right for actions, jumping, petting and bonking. Your farm saves automatically. Invite friends from the game’s Invite menu and send them that room link or code. A room closes after the last player leaves and the reconnection window ends.
