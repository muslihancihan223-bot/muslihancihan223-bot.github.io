Como Jogar

Goal: catch as many dinosaurs as you can in a run and bring them to your pen, where they earn coins for upgrades and new biomes. Keyboard and mouse: Space or left click to jump, press again in the air to throw the lasso at the dino in the circle. Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse to turn and aim, or use A / D. At the ranch: WASD to walk, drag to turn the camera, E to use a station, Q to get off or on your mount, P to pause. Touch: the stick appears under your left thumb, drag on the right half to turn and aim, the arrow button jumps and throws the lasso in the air, the menu button opens the stations.
