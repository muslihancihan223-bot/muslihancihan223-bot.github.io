Como Jogar

Goal: punch through every wall, beat the boss at the end of each zone and escape all the way to space to get the Golden Donut back. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, mouse to look (click to lock the mouse). With a trackpad or when the mouse can't be locked: click to punch, drag or two-finger swipe to look, left/right arrows or Q/E to turn. Hold LEFT CLICK to punch, hold RIGHT CLICK or SPACE to vacuum rubble, walk up to the green machine to cash in, keys 1-8 to buy upgrades, Esc closes panels. Mobile: left thumb walks, right thumb looks, hold the red fist button to punch and the green button to vacuum, tap the cards at the bottom to upgrade. Tip: glowing blocks are weak spots for critical hits.
