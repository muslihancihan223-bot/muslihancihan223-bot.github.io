Como Jogar

🎯 Bring the lovers together and clear every level. 🏹 Rainbow Archer: drag to aim and set power, release to shoot. 💘 Hit two characters with arrows and they run to each other. 🌬️ Arrows bounce, break glass, cut ropes and ride fan winds. ⭐ Fewer arrows means more stars (up to 3). ✏️ Draw to Bed: draw a path from each character to a matching bed, then lift your finger. ⚠️ A path that misses its bed is rejected; characters that fall lose the level. 🍼 Collect common, rare and unique babies; spend coins on new bows and outfits.
