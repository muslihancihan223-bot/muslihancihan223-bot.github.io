Como Jogar

Goal: climb as high as you can. Reach the flag at the top of each level to unlock the next one. There are 30 levels; finish them all to complete the tower. If you fall, you lose height, so land every jump. How to play - Move across platforms and avoid traps: spinning blades, crushers, pistons, vanishing and moving platforms, conveyors and wind. - Use springs and rings to launch yourself up. Timing is everything. - You can jump twice in the air (double jump) to fix a short jump. - Collect coins and diamonds: buy skins in the shop, or skip a level that is too hard. - Set your best height and compete in the online leaderboard. - Start in the Training Hall to try every obstacle safely. Controls (desktop) - WASD / Arrow keys: move - Mouse: look around - Space: jump (press again in the air for a double jump) - Shift: run - Pause button (top left): pause and settings Controls (mobile) - Left side of the screen: floating joystick (push further to run) - Right side of the screen: swipe to rotate the camera - JUMP button: jump (tap twice for a double jump) - RUN button: auto-run on/off
