Jogar Clean the Moon

Clean the Moon é um jogo grátis de Arcade, Aventura, Simulação. Sem download nem cadastro: jogue na hora no navegador do celular, tablet ou computador.

Como Jogar

Move with WASD or the on-screen stick, drag to look around, jump with Space or the jump button (jumps are high on the Moon). The vacuum works by itself: walk over dirt to clean it and fill your tank. Step on the green pad to sell the dirt for coins, step on the orange pad to buy upgrades. Bring the sector to 100% clean, then step on the launch pad to fly to the next one. Press E or the Shine button to release a cleaning shockwave.
