Como Jogar

Slap Attack: Click/touch and drag across the ring to pull back your arm, then release to deliver a slap. Dodge Defense: When the opponent attacks, swipe in the direction of the yellow arrow (Left, Right, or Up) before the timer runs out to evade the hit. Super Slap: When your Rage meter reaches 100%, drag in circles around the ring to charge the multiplier up to 5x MAX, then release to strip enemy armor and deal massive damage! Upgrades & Skins: Tap the lobby cards to increase your Power, Health, and Income, and visit the Skins Wardrobe to customize your fighter.
