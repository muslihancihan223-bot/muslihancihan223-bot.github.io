कैसे खेलें

Recommendations: - At a higher speed, there is more destruction of cars. - Try different ways to demolish and smash the car on the same level for your enjoyment. This game is not for those who want to practice car parking (parking cars), because on frenzied descents from ski jumps, cars can reach huge speeds and stop being controlled. A regular traffic racer will not cope with this, you need the experience of a bemidji drive lover to withstand the difficulties of a frenzied crash test. The game's maps include a variety of crash test scenarios, drift, free riding - maps with ski jumps, dotted with pits that can fit a truck, huge jumps over the abyss with spikes, narrow tunnels that you need to get into, rushing down the mountain at high speed, barrels of fuel that can explode and destroy your car, hills and slides on which you can leave not only one suspension of the car, but all 4 wheels at once during the race!
