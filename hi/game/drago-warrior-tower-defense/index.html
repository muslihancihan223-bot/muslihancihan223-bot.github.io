कैसे खेलें

Your main goal is to protect the kingdom by placing towers, summoning heroes, and using skills to stop enemy waves (including giant dragons) from reaching your base—survive all waves for victory, earn upgrades, and achieve high scores with efficient strategies! PC Controls: Mouse: Click to place towers, select heroes/skills, tap enemies for targeting, and use fast-forward button. Mobile Controls: Touchscreen: Tap hero portraits/skill icons to activate spells, tap enemies for tower focus, tap fast-forward to speed up combat.
