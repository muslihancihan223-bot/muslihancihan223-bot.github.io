Catch a Fish एक मुफ़्त एडवेंचर, सिमुलेशन, 3D गेम है। बिना डाउनलोड और साइन-अप के — फ़ोन, टैबलेट या कंप्यूटर के ब्राउज़र में तुरंत खेलें।

कैसे खेलें

Catch fish, sell them, upgrade, and explore deeper seas. Watch your air: if it runs out you pass out and lose part of your catch. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to punch, throw the harpoon or sweep the net, Space to jump or swim up, C to swim down, 1 / 2 or the mouse wheel to switch tools, B for the bag, P for settings. Mobile: left stick to move, drag to look, the action button to catch, and the on-screen buttons to swim up and down.
