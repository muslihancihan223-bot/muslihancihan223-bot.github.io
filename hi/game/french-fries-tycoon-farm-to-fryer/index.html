कैसे खेलें

Move with WASD or the on-screen stick, jump with Space or the arrow button. Walk to a garden bed to plant and harvest potatoes, carry them to the sink and the cutting board, then to the fryer, and hand the fries to the customer at the counter. Step on a purchase pad or tap the Buy button to unlock upgrades. At night, catch the runaway potato for a bonus. Q/E or right mouse drag turns the camera, the mouse wheel zooms.
