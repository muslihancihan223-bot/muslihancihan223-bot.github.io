कैसे खेलें

Choose a car and level. Drive to the marked parking bay and keep the whole car inside it until the countdown finishes. Avoid collisions and finish before the timer expires. Desktop: W/Up accelerates; S/Down brakes, then reverses; A/D or Left/Right steers; Space is the handbrake; R toggles reverse gear; C changes camera; mouse drag looks around; H toggles the parking helper; Esc pauses; M mutes. You can also drag the driving joystick in the lower-center area with the mouse. Mobile: use the on-screen steering controls and pedals. Drag an empty area with a free finger to look around.
