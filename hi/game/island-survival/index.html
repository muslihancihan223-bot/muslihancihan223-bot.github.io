Island Survival एक मुफ़्त सर्वाइवल, मैचिंग गेम है। बिना डाउनलोड और साइन-अप के — फ़ोन, टैबलेट या कंप्यूटर के ब्राउज़र में तुरंत खेलें।

सर्वाइवलमैचिंग

कैसे खेलें

🎯 Goal — complete 6 stages, build a raft, and escape the deserted island. 🎮 Core mechanic — an 8×8 board with 6 types of tiles: stone, wood, food, water, leaves, fire. Match 3+ tiles in a row to collect the corresponding resource into your inventory. Combos of 4 give a bonus resource. Combos of 5 give a "wild" tile (replaces any material). 🔨 Crafting — open the menu to see recipes. To craft an item, you need the required amount of resources. All crafted items (ropes, planks, ingots, nails, tools, raft parts) are permanent and carry over between stages. 🔄 Resource reset — raw resources (stone, wood, food, water, leaves, fire, ore) reset at the end of each stage.
