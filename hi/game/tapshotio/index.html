HOW TO PLAY Press Play in the lobby to enter a match. Keep moving, use cover, and line up your shots. Switch to your revolver for follow-up shots or your knife for close-range attacks. Earn kills before the round ends to climb the leaderboard. DESKTOP CONTROLS WASD / Arrow Keys — Move Mouse — Look and aim Left Click — Fire or stab Right Click — Hold to aim or zoom Q / E — Alternative aim controls during combat Space — Jump R — Reload 1 — Equip sniper rifle 2 — Equip revolver 3 — Equip knife E — Interact with nearby cases or the weapon shop in the lobby Esc — Open the pause menu for loadout, settings, or Back to Lobby MOBILE CONTROLS Left joystick — Move Drag on the right side of the screen — Look and aim Shoot button, with sniper or revolver — Hold to aim; release to fire Shoot button, with knife — Tap to stab; hold to keep attacking Jump button — Jump Reload button — Reload Primary / Secondary / Knife buttons — Switch weapons Pause button — Open the pause menu Lobby interaction prompts — Tap to buy cases or browse the weapon shop Use Change Controls in Settings to adjust the mobile button layout.
