Main Objective & How to Win Welcome to the ultimate alchemy experience! Your main objective is to combine basic elements to discover hundreds of unique creations, ranging from simple nature and weather to advanced technology, civilizations, and magic. You start with just four basic elements: Water, Fire, Earth, and Wind. By experimenting and merging them together (for example, Water + Fire = Steam), you will expand your library of items. While the main game is an endless journey of discovery, you can "win" and prove your mastery by: Discovering every element: Unlock the entire catalog of elements. Completing Quests: Finish daily tasks, standard quests, and global achievements to earn Coins (💸). Beating Time Mode: Race against the clock to craft a specific target element as fast as possible. Conquering Quiz Mode: Test your knowledge by correctly guessing which two elements create a specific target. Tip: Stuck on what to make next? Use your hard-earned Coins to buy a Smart Hint (💡) that will tell you exactly which two items to combine! Primary Controls The game is fully optimized for both PC and mobile devices. Left Click & Hold / Touch & Drag: Drag elements from your inventory onto the board, or drag two elements together on the board to attempt a fusion. Left Click / Tap (on inventory item): Instantly spawn that element onto the center of the board. Left Click / Tap: Interact with UI buttons to clear the board, open the inventory, sort items, buy hints, or claim daily rewards. Escape (Esc) Key: Quickly close any open menus, pop-ups, or the inventory sidebar.
