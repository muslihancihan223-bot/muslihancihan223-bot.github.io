कैसे खेलें

Your main objective is to be the last car standing in the arena by destroying your opponents. You can fire rockets at your enemies or ram into them at high speed to push them off the map. As you eliminate enemies, you earn coins which can be used to buy much stronger battle cars from the garage menu. Controls (PC) Steer / Move: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys Jump: W or Up Arrow Shoot (Rocket): Spacebar Dash / Fly: Double tap the jump key quickly (W, W) Controls (Mobile) Steer / Move: Use the virtual joystick (◉) on the screen to steer your car. Jump: Single tap (1x Tap) on the screen. Shoot (Rocket): Tap the fire (🔥) icon on the screen. Dash / Fly: Double tap (2x Tap) quickly on the screen.
