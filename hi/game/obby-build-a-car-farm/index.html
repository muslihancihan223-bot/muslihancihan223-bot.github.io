कैसे खेलें

Your goal is to grow your car farm and discover all 30 car tiers. Step on floor buttons to buy and merge cars. Collect the parts they produce, take them to the service center deposit pad, then collect your in-game currency. Buy more cars and upgrade parts sales speed. PC: WASD or arrow keys — move. Space — jump; left Shift — run. Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse — rotate the camera. Mouse wheel — zoom in or out. Tab — toggle cursor lock; while locked, move the mouse to rotate the camera. Left mouse button — select interface buttons. Phone/tablet: Left joystick — move. Swipe the look area — rotate the camera. Jump button — jump; zoom buttons — zoom in or out. Tap interface buttons to use them.
