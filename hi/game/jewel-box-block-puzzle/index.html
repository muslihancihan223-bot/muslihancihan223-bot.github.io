पज़लआरामदायकलॉजिक

कैसे खेलें

Drag one of the three pieces at the bottom onto the board. A completely filled row or column disappears and gives points; clear several lines at once or in a row for a combo. New pieces appear once all three are placed, and the game ends when no piece fits. You can also tap a piece, then tap a cell. In Adventure, reach the level goals within the move limit: collect gems, clear blocks of a color or reach a score. Ice breaks in two hits, vines spread if you leave them alone for two moves. On desktop: mouse, or keys 1-3 to pick a piece, arrows to move it, Enter or Space to place, Esc to pause.
