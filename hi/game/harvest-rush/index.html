कैसे खेलें

Drive your combine through the fields to harvest crops. When your hopper is full, drive to the selling zone to sell the harvest for coins. Spend coins to upgrade speed, capacity and harvest width, unlock new harvesters and expand your farm. Harvest every field to complete the level. Controls: WASD or Arrow keys to drive. On mobile, use the on-screen buttons to steer, accelerate and reverse.
