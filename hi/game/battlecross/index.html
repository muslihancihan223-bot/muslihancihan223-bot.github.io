एक्शनएडवेंचररणनीति

कैसे खेलें

1. **Fight.** Walk up to an enemy and your hero attacks on their own. Use your skills and drink a health potion. 2. **Clear the zone.** Beat every enemy group and the leader at the end to win. You keep the experience and loot even if you fall. 3. **Grow.** Each level gives 3 attribute points. Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Endurance, Skill and Charisma unlock different skills and gear, so spend them toward the hero you want. 4. **Visit towns.** Buy gear, enlarge your potion belt at the healer, and learn skills from trainers. You have 8 active and 4 passive slots, and you may fill them from any of the 16 classes. 5. **Choose.** At the end of some zones you make a decision that cannot be undone. It changes the towns, the shops, the trainers and the ending.
