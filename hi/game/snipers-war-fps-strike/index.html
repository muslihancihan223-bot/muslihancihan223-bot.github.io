कैसे खेलें

Take out the enemy team before they get you. Use the scope for long shots and aim for the head. PC: • W A S D – move • Mouse – aim • Left mouse button – shoot • Right mouse button – scope in / out • Space – jump • R – reload • 1 / 2 / 3 or mouse wheel – switch weapon • F – inspect weapon • Tab (hold) – scoreboard • P or Esc – pause Phone and tablet (hold your device sideways): • Touch the left side – joystick to move • Drag on the right side – look around and aim • FIRE – shoot (Auto shot also fires when your sight is on an enemy) • SCOPE – scope in / out • JUMP, reload and weapon-switch buttons on the right • PAUSE and the scoreboard button at the top left
