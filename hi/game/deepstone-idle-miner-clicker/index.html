कैसे खेलें

Mine blocks to collect resources, upgrade your pickaxe and unlock new ores and biomes. Hire helpers to automate mining, earn achievements and increase your income. There is no final victory screen—keep improving your mine and aim for a higher score on the leaderboard. Desktop controls: Left mouse button: click the block to mine. Space: mine while the mining area is focused. Shop buttons: buy pickaxes, upgrades and helpers. Biome button: travel between unlocked biomes. Menu: adjust language, sound and hold-to-mine settings. Mobile controls: tap the block to mine and tap buttons to buy upgrades or open menus. Play in landscape orientation.
