कैसे खेलें

Goal: keep your colony alive and growing. Build shelter, grow and cook food, craft gear, and set work priorities so your colonists handle daily tasks on their own. Watch their needs and mood to avoid mental breaks. Controls (mouse): - Left click: select a colonist, building or item - Double click: select all similar objects on screen - Drag with left or right button: move the camera - Mouse wheel: zoom - Right click: open the orders menu for selected colonists, or cancel the current tool - Esc: cancel / close window Controls (touch): - Tap: select - Double tap: select all similar objects on screen - Drag with one finger: move the camera - Pinch: zoom
