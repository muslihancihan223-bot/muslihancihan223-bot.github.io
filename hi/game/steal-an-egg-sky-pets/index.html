कैसे खेलें

Grab eggs at the altar and carry them home to hatch pets. Step on the gold pad to collect coins and on the red pad to lock your gate. Steal pets from open nests and run home before the owner catches you; touch a thief to take your pet back. Press the two-friends button to play online: quick match, a private room or join by code. Desktop: WASD or arrows to run, Space to jump, E to grab or steal, drag the mouse to look, wheel to zoom. Mobile: left thumb to run, swipe on the right to look, tap the hand button to grab.
