कैसे खेलें

Drag from a house to the shop of the same colour to build a road; cars enter buildings only through the driveway. Deliver the target number of trips to win. If cars pile up at a house for too long, the city jams and you lose. Use lights, roundabouts, overpasses, avenues and one-way streets to fix jams; tap a car with "!" to let it go first. Keys 1-7 pick tools, Space pauses, H shows the heat map, Ctrl+Z undoes.
