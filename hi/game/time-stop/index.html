कैसे खेलें

Goal: Survive as long as you can. Clear every wave in the arena, pick a perk, then go through the exit to the next arena. Kill enemies to earn points, build combos, and climb the leaderboard. Main idea: Time stop is your main weapon. While time is stopped, bullets freeze in the air. Grab them and throw them back, or take free shots at enemies that cannot react. Time stop lasts a few seconds and recharges, so use it wisely. Controls (PC): WASD — move Mouse — look around Left Mouse Button — shoot / throw F or Right Mouse Button — stop time E — pick up weapons and bullets Q — throw the held item Controls (mobile): use the on-screen joystick to move, drag the right side of the screen to look and use the on-screen buttons to shoot, stop time and grab. Tips: Riot-shield enemies block bullets from the front, so flank them. Kamikazes must be killed from a distance. Spend your points in the Upgrades shop for permanent bonuses.
