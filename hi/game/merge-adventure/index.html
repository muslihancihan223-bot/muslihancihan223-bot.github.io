कैसे खेलें

Objective Merge matching items, complete customer orders, and use the rewards to renovate every area of the neighborhood. Keep upgrading buildings and unlocking new merge chains to progress through the story. Controls - Mouse: Click an item or button. Click and drag an item onto an identical item to merge them. - Touchscreen: Tap an item or button. Touch and drag an item onto an identical item to merge them. - Tap or click producer items to create new objects. Select a completed order to deliver its requested items. There is no single fail state. You win each renovation stage by fulfilling orders, collecting the required resources, and completing its building upgrades.
