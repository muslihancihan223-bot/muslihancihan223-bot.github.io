कैसे खेलें

Press BONK! and hit when the marker reaches the end of the swing bar. In the air, keep the nose near the horizon to keep your speed. When you stop, claim your coins, upgrade and fly further. Tips: - Wait for the marker to slow down at the end of the bar for a Perfect Bonk. - Save nitro for when you slow down and use it in short bursts. - Fly into air streams for a big speed boost. Keyboard and mouse: - Bonk: click BONK! (or press Space), then click again (or press Space) at the end of the bar - Steer the nose: hold W / S or Up / Down - Nitro: hold Space or Shift - Pause: press P Touch: - Bonk: tap BONK!, then tap the screen at the end of the bar - Steer the nose: drag up or down (or hold the arrow buttons) - Nitro: hold the nitro button
