कैसे खेलें

Walk through the portal at your base and pick a raid. Find the altar and take the egg, then hold out until the green evacuation portal opens and carry the egg through it. Eggs go into nests and hatch into pets that earn twice as much. Step on the cash pads to collect income, then visit the counters for weapons, upgrades, pets, the codex and the shop. PC: WASD to move, mouse to look and shoot, Space to jump, R to reload, E to interact, number keys to switch weapons, Tab to free or capture the cursor, P to pause. Mobile: left stick to move, drag to look, on-screen buttons to fire, jump and reload.
