कैसे खेलें

Aim with the mouse or your finger and tap or click to shoot the next numbered block onto the 5x7 board. A merge needs a vertical match to start; once it fires, the cascade takes every connected equal tile including sideways ones, and the result lands on the tile you just shot. Each merge sends a blocker tile to every other player, on a cooldown that grows through the match. If your board tops out you can watch a rewarded ad to clear a column, otherwise you are eliminated. Every 60 seconds the lowest scorer is knocked out. Reach 2048 first to win.
