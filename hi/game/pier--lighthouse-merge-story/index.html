पज़लमर्जआरामदायक

कैसे खेलें

Drag two identical items together to merge them into a better one. Tap generators (the fryer, the tool crate, the taffy puller and more) to make new items; each tap costs one energy, and energy refills over time. Fill the characters' orders to earn starfish, then spend starfish on restoration tasks with the hammer button: every repair brings the pier back and uncovers the next clue. Finish a day's tasks to open its chest, finish a place to travel on to the next one. Works with mouse and touch.
