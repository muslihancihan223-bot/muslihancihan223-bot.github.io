कैसे खेलें

Stop the Snake Before It's Too Late! Take control of a powerful drill and defend the surface from massive segmented snakes crawling up from underground. In this intense arcade defense game, you'll time your strikes to shatter the snake's body piece by piece. Swing your drill, aim carefully, and destroy every segment before the snake's head reaches the top! Desktop Controls: Tap to launch drill attack. Spacebar to launch drill attack. Mobile Controls: Tap to launch drill attack.
