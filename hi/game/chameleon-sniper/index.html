कैसे खेलें

Somewhere on the island, enemies are hiding, painted to match the surface they're stuck to. Turn the island, scan every wall, roof and rock, zoom in with your scope and take them all down. Any hit is enough. If you run out of bullets or time, the level is lost. Miss once at most to earn 3 stars. Tips: - Look for shapes, not colors: a head or an arm sticking out of a flat wall, a bump on a roof or a rock. Turn the island to check the far side too. - Headshots and leftover bullets pay extra coins, so take your time and aim for the head. - Can't find the last one? Recolor paints every enemy a bright new color, Night Vision turns everything else green, Beams shows where they all are, and Mark puts a big red crosshair on one. - Not enough bullets to clear the island? Upgrade Bullets, and upgrade Size to make enemies easier to spot and hit. Controls: Mouse and keyboard: - Look around: move the mouse - Turn the island: A / D (or arrow keys) - Aim and shoot: right mouse button (or Space) to zoom, move the mouse to aim, left click to shoot Touch: - Look around: drag on the screen - Turn the island: hold the arrows - Aim and shoot: hold AIM, slide to aim, let go to shoot
