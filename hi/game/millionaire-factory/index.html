कैसे खेलें

Metal Production 🔩: At the heart of the game lies a massive Metal (Gear) button. Each tap 👆 earns you Metal, which serves as your primary source of income. Metals → Money 💵: You can convert collected Metals into Money 💸 to keep your economy running. For example: ➡️ 1 Metal = 0.1 Money ➡️ 10 Metals = 1 Money Income and Robot Program Upgrades 🤖🏭: With the money you earn 💵, you can: Purchase upgrades that increase the amount of Metal gained per tap 💪. Invest in automation systems such as the Basic Auto Robot 3️🔨, Regional Robot Program ⚒️, and National Automatic Robot Fleet. These systems generate passive income every 5 seconds. Factories 🏭: At the start of the game, you begin with a single factory, which you can upgrade 🔧. You can also purchase up to three additional factories 🏭, each of which can be further developed. Every factory has a progress bar 📊 representing its Metal production. Once the bar fills, the factory automatically sells the produced Metal, adds the profit 💵 to your balance, and the cycle restarts. Fast Investments⚡: 📊 In the investment section, carefully observe the **candlestick movements** left by the dynamic system. ⏱️ Within about **15 seconds**, predict whether the price will go **up or down.** 💰 Then, open a **trade** in the direction you believe it will move, using your chosen amount. 🎯 If your prediction is correct, you’ll earn **double** the amount you invested! 💎🚀 Achievements 🏅: There are 6 unique achievements 🌟 to unlock throughout the game, based on the total Metals collected 🔩, upgrades purchased 🔝, and wealth accumulated 💰.
