How to Play

Goal: complete each mission's objectives (shown at the top left) by building a working village: roads, homes, food and production chains, then soldiers to hold off bandits. Mouse: left click to select and build. Right click to send the selected soldiers (ground: move, bandits: attack, watchtower: man it). Hold and drag to move the map, or WASD / arrow keys. Mouse wheel: zoom. Esc: cancel or menu. Space: pause. 1-3: game speed. R: road tool. X: demolish. Touch: tap to select and build, drag to move the map, pinch to zoom. Tap a company to select it, double tap the ground or a target to send it. New to the game? Start the Campaign: the first missions teach you step by step.

About the Game

Every sword has a story. In Crown & Crafts nothing appears out of thin air: ore is dug at the mine, carried to the smelter, forged by the smith and handed to a recruit. Grain becomes flour, flour becomes bread, and bread only feeds your people once a porter walks it to their door. You do not control each villager: you design the system they live in. Build a living medieval economy with over 30 buildings and 28 goods, plan roads, porters, carts and depots, then defend what you built from bandits and rival lords. A 4-chapter campaign with 25 story missions, optional goals and three stars of mastery, plus side missions, challenges and Conquest against up to three rival lords. Chapter 1 is free; later chapters unlock with one-time packs. Earn Renown and Crown Seals to unlock edicts, blueprints and banners. Plays in English and Vietnamese.
