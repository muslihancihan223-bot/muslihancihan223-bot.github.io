How to Play

Tap or click a free screw to unscrew it. It flies into the capsule of its color; if there is none, it waits in the tray at the bottom. Don’t let the tray overflow, or you lose a heart. A plate with no screws left falls off; open every latch to unlock the door. Special screws each have their own rule, and a hint shows it the first time you meet one. In boss fights, pull down anywhere on the screen and release to shoot: hit the glowing weak spots and shoot down flying gears. Mouse or touch, no keyboard needed.

About the Game

Screws: Escape the Clocktower is a steampunk screw puzzle with boss fights. Unscrew nuts and bolts, sort them by color into capsules, and drop every plate to open the door. Watch out for bomb, frozen, electric, chained, paired and colorless screws. At the end of each chapter, beat a boss with a slingshot. 5 chapters, 25 story doors, 55 bonus doors in Free Play, and a Boss Rush mode.
