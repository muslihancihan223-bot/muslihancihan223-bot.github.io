How to Play

Mouse: - Hold the left mouse button to cut, press, spray, scrub or peel - Hold and drag the left mouse button to move a tool over the object - Hold and drag the right mouse button to turn the object - Mouse wheel to zoom in on rugs Touch: - Tap or hold to cut, press, spray, scrub or peel - Drag with one finger to move a tool over the object - Drag with two fingers to turn the object, pinch to zoom On-screen buttons pick the tool and turn the object. The hint at the bottom of the screen always tells you what to do next.

About the Game

TINGLORA: ASMR is a calm, satisfying game with 81 levels. Every level is a different little ritual, and the levels come in a mixed order, so the next one is always a surprise. Slice crystal fruit into tiny cubes, crush glass and soda cans under a hydraulic press, pop the bubbles in crystal-clear slime, peel a whole apple in one long ribbon and carve bars of soap. You can also wash muddy sneakers and a sports car with a pressure washer, bring dusty rugs back to life and restore a rusty knife or an old film camera. Then test squishy materials in machine challenges with rollers, wires and spikes. Features: - 81 levels across 9 kinds of play: cutting, hydraulic press, slime, peeling, soap carving, rug cleaning, pressure washing, restoration and material challenges - Soft, crunchy, crackling sounds for every action - Real-time physics: things bend, squish, snap and shatter - Earn coins and unlock 9 different knives - Zen mode hides the interface so you can just watch and listen - No timers and no fail screens: play at your own pace
