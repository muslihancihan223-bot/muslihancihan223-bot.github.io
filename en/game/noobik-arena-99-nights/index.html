How to Play

On desktop: WASD or arrow keys — move. Mouse — aim. Left mouse button — shoot. Space or Shift — dash. Q — switch weapon. G — throw a grenade. In the shop, click upgrade cards to improve your hero and weapons. During the building phase, choose a turret, fence, or barracks, then click the arena to place it. Click an existing building to select it, view its stats, and open building upgrades. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and the buttons for shooting, dashing, switching weapons, and throwing grenades.

About the Game

Noobik Arena - 99 nights is a fast 3D survival arena where the hero fights waves of monsters, collects coins and experience, unlocks upgrades, and improves weapons. Between waves, you can build defenses: place turrets, fences, and barracks, select buildings with a click, and upgrade their stats. Survive as long as possible by combining weapons, grenades, dashes, perks, and defensive structures against increasingly dangerous enemies.
