How to Play

Click or tap the dirt to dig — each tap breaks a chunk. Spend dirt on upgrades at the bottom: stronger taps, auto-dig, and a bigger cart. The game auto-saves and keeps mining while you're away, so you always come back to more.

About the Game

Help the mole dig to the center of the world! An idle clicker where you tap to break dirt, buy upgrades, and watch your mole gear up with pickaxes, drills and a cart. Mining keeps going while you're away — claim your dirt when you return. How deep can you get?
