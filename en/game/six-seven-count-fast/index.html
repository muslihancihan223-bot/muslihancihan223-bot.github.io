Six Seven: Count Fast is a free Puzzle, Arcade, Skill game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Look at the pile while it is shown and decide whether there are 6 or 7 items, then answer before the timer bar runs out. On a phone tap the left half of the screen for 6 or the right half for 7. On a computer press Left Arrow, A or 6 for six and Right Arrow, D or 7 for seven, or click the buttons. Items of a different color and shape are decoys and do not count. One wrong answer ends the run. At streak 67 the final boss asks how many items you saw: pick with the buttons or keys 1 to 4. P or Esc pauses, M toggles sound.

About the Game

Six or seven? A pile of apples, stars, coins or hearts flashes for about a second, and the flashes get shorter with every right answer. Count it by eye and answer 6 or 7 before time runs out. The piles get trickier as your streak grows: mixed sizes, overlapping heaps, drifting piles and decoy items that don't count. Reach a streak of 67 to face the final boss: 67 items laid out as a giant 67. Dress up your character in the shop with 75 cool and funny items, from shades and chains to a traffic cone and a dino suit. Beat your best streak and climb the leaderboard.
