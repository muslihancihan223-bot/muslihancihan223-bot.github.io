About the Game

Catdoku Sudoku Puzzle combines colorful region puzzles with simple but challenging placement rules. Your goal is to place exactly one cat inside every colored territory while making sure no two cats share the same row or column. Cats also need their personal space. They cannot touch each other diagonally, even at the corners. Every placement affects the entire board, so careful observation and logical thinking are essential. The puzzles begin gently, allowing you to understand the rules and develop your strategy. As you progress, the boards become more challenging and require deeper planning. Eliminate impossible spaces, compare colored regions, and use row and column restrictions to discover the correct solution.
