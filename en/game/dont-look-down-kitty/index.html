How to Play

Climb as high as you can and chase down the Trashy Dragon! Jump from block to block, grab coins and flags, and don’t fall: a fall sends you back to your last flag. At the end of every chapter, beat the Dragon in the arena to rescue a friend and open the road to the next world. Clear all 9 chapters to save your buddy. Desktop controls: Mouse (move left/right) or A/D or ←/→: steer Left click, Space, W or ↑: jump (press again in the air to double jump) Jump again after a double jump: jetpack (once you’ve unlocked it) S or ↓: stand still E: Capy’s shop (at a camp) P: pause menu L: leaderboard · G: goals Mobile controls: Left side of the screen: drag to steer Right side of the screen: tap to jump, tap again in the air to double jump Tap after a double jump, or the 🚀 button: jetpack ⏸ button: pause menu, settings and sound

About the Game

The Trashy Dragon has kidnapped your puppy buddy and is flying off to eat the stars! Climb after him as a brave little cat, up a wild tower of cars, crates, cakes and clouds that runs from the city streets through Candy Skies and the Cloud Kingdom to the Moon, the Sun, a black hole and the Milky Way. Hop from block to block and double-jump the gaps. Balance on wobbly lifts and dodge sliding walls, hail, meteors and grumpy pigeons. Every chapter has a silly boss, from the Overfed Pigeon to the Udderly Furious Space Cow, and the Dragon waits at the top of each one. Rest at Capy’s camp, dress up your cat, rescue five friends along the way… and whatever you do, don’t look down! • 9 chapters, each with its own world, music, hazards and bosses • 37 cat skins plus 60+ hats, faces, trails and auras • Daily Climb: a new 3-minute tower every day, with a rival to beat • 100+ achievements, plus Expert and Cat-astrophe modes for pros • Easy one-thumb controls on phones, mouse or keyboard on computers
