How to Play

Goal: clear the rooms of each floor, collect items and defeat the floor's boss to go deeper. Beat the Great Mask to win the run, then take one of the new paths or try the endless Abyss. Keyboard (solo): WASD to move, Arrow keys to shoot, E or Left Shift for a bomb, Space for the active item, Q for a card, P to pause, Backspace to go back. Two players on one keyboard: the left player moves with WASD, holds Space to fire and lock aim, Q for a bomb, E for the active item, X for a card. The right player moves with the Arrow keys, Enter or Right Shift to fire and lock aim, Right Ctrl for a bomb, comma for the active item, semicolon for a card. Gamepad: left stick to move, right stick or ABXY to shoot, LB for a bomb, RB for the active item, RT for a card, Start to pause. Press A in the lobby to join. Touch (landscape): the left stick moves, the right stick aims and shoots. Tap the bomb, active item, card and pause buttons. In Settings you can switch between fixed and floating sticks, change their size and opacity, and use a left-handed layout.

About the Game

The Children of the Spark venture into the depths to free the light held by the Great Mask. Each run brings a shifting dungeon, secret rooms and a new mix of finds. Play solo or gather up to four players in local co-op on one screen. On a computer, share a keyboard with a second player or connect gamepads. On a phone, play solo in landscape with fixed or floating sticks. Meet at boss doors and help fallen teammates return to the fight. Discover more than 200 items, dozens of trinkets and powerful synergies. Finds change your hero's look during a run: new masks, hats and wings, growing or shrinking, and transformations when you combine matching items. Turn sparks into rays, guided shots or bombs, and mix fire, ice, poison and lightning. Explore six special room types, from the Arcade and its machines to the Dice Room and the rare Observatory. Face 52 bosses across the chapters and the Abyss; victories open new opponents and chapters. After your first victory over the Great Mask, champion bosses appear and the Mirror Gallery opens.
