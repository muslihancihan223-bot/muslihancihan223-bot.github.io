How to Play

Goal: keep your castle standing for as many nights as you can. Day — build up. Ride to a sawmill, quarry or mine and stand beside it to collect resources; sell them at the Market for gold. The timer at the top shows how long until the night attack. Spend gold. Stand on a glowing hex to unlock it, or walk up to the castle and towers and tap Upgrade. Choose your towers. Opening a tower hex lets you pick Ballista (fast), Cannon (heavy) or Frost (slows enemies). Night — defend. Enemies march from the portals to your castle. Towers fire automatically — tap RELOAD (or press Space) when they run dry, and ride in with your sword. If the walls fall, the run ends. Grow between runs. Nights survived earn Crowns — buy permanent upgrades in the crown menu, finish daily quests, open chests and claim your daily gift. Controls — Mobile: drag anywhere to move, tap to interact. Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, mouse to tap, Space to reload.

About the Game

Kingdom Master is a cozy build-and-defend game with a nightly twist. Every day you ride your royal horse around the kingdom: chop wood, mine stone and ore, dig up crystals and sell them at the market for gold. Spend that gold to unlock new hexes, raise your castle walls and place towers around your land. Then the sun goes down. Each night an invasion pours out of the portals and marches on your castle. Your towers fire, your cannons reload, and you charge into the fight yourself with sword and shield. Survive the night and a new day begins — bigger rewards, tougher enemies, and every fifth night a boss comes knocking. How far you get is up to you: every night you survive earns Crowns, a permanent currency you keep even after the castle falls. Spend them in the Kingdom Legacy to start richer, build thicker walls, hit harder and ride faster — then go back and beat your record.
