How to Play

Drag left/right to steer (the only control — they fire on their own) · steer onto people to pick them up · shoot towers to drop their riders · charge gates by shooting them, then drive through · your shooters merge as the crowd grows · hold the line after the finish · first wipe is free.

About the Game

MERGE SHOOT RUN is a one-touch runner where your whole squad fires while you steer. You drag left and right to guide a crowd of little shooters down a lane, and they shoot on their own, constantly. Break the towers ahead and the riders on top drop down and run to join you. Steer onto the people scattered along the ground and they fall in behind. Your shooters carry numbers and merge into heavier ones as the crowd grows, so a big crowd is worth far more than the sum of its people. The decision the game is built around is what your firepower is worth spending on. Gates stand in your path with a number on them, and shooting a gate makes that number climb — run through and you collect whatever you charged it to. But every bullet you put into a gate is a bullet that never reached the tower behind it, and a gate you ignore stays in the red and takes people off you instead. There is no right answer, only the one you can afford. Crossing the finish line is not the end of it. The horde comes at you, and every attacker that reaches your crowd kills a shooter and cuts your damage with them, so a run you scraped through can still fall apart at the last.
