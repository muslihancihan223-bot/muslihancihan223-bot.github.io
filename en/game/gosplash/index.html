How to Play

Cover more floor than the rivals before time runs out. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold left mouse button to spray, hold Shift to melt into your goo (faster, refills your tank), Space to jump, E or right mouse button to throw a balloon, Q or F for the Geyser when charged, P to pause. Mobile: drag on the left to move, drag on the right to aim, and use the on-screen buttons. Win a match to unlock the next one.

About the Game

Tiny jelly blobs, one giant house! In GoSplash your crew of four Gloops battles a rival crew to cover the floor in goo. Spray, throw goo balloons, melt into your own colour to race and refill, and unleash the Geyser when it is charged. Capture pods that spray for your team and survive the goo downpour in the final seconds. Fight room by room through the Playroom, Kitchen, Bathroom and Garden across 16 matches, earn stars and coins, and unlock new blasters and silly hats. When time runs out, the crew with the most floor wins!
