How to Play

Use a computer mouse to control the game. If you have a mobile device, then use a stylus or a finger. To complete the game, you need to click on different objects and find successful ways to escape from prison.

About the Game

You will play as a Stickman character. Your character ended up behind bars in a prison cell on New Year's Eve. Your task is to find three successful ways to escape from prison. On Christmas Day, you will be presented with ten different items in a package that can be used to escape. In the game you will face a lot of problems that you will need to solve in order to get a taste of freedom. Various locations with a New Year's Eve theme will cheer you up.
