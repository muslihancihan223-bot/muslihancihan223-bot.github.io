How to Play

Click Play and select an unlocked level. On desktop, use the left mouse button; on mobile, tap the screen. Click or tap a lane to slide the card from the bottom slot into it and move the outgoing card into the slot. Match the cards across an entire lane to clear the set and fill a customer's order. The multicolored spare card must be moved out of a lane before that lane can clear. Complete all customer orders before the timer runs out to win. Earn coins and stars, progress to new levels, and use unlocked boosters when you need help. Optional rewarded videos can provide extra coins or boosters. Use the on-screen settings to adjust music and sound. No keyboard shortcuts are required.

About the Game

Fruit Lane Cafe is a colorful card-sorting puzzle game with a busy shop twist. Slide cards through lanes, line up matching sets, and complete your customers' orders before time runs out. Plan around the next card, manage the spare card, and unlock helpful boosters as the challenges grow. Simple tap controls, satisfying chain reactions, and a growing level map make every order a fresh puzzle.
