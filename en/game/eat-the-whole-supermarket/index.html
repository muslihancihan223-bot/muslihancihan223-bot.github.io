How to Play

PC: WASD / Arrow keys - move Left mouse button (hold) - eat Right mouse button (drag) - rotate camera Mouse wheel - zoom Space - jump 1-4 - toggle tools, 5-7 - use items Tab - upgrades, R - events, L - missions, E - food index P / Esc - pause Mobile: joystick to move, Eat and Jump buttons

About the Game

Eat your way through an entire supermarket! Munch on snacks, giant burgers and whole shelves, then burn the calories on treadmills to earn cash. Spend it on a bigger stomach, multi-bites, longer reach and fun tools like a food magnet, shopping cart and vacuum. Trigger crazy events from Mr. Munch, such as the Bacon Army, and conquer three stores: Munch Mart, the night-time Munch Mini and the giant two-floor Munch Mall. Eat every last calorie in a store to win its grand prize, with $1,000,000 in Munch Mart!
