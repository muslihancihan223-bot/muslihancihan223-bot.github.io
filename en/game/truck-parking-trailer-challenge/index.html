Play Truck Parking: Trailer Challenge

Truck Parking: Trailer Challenge is a free Driving game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

Driving

How to Play

Follow the route to your objective. On trailer levels, reverse slowly toward the hitch to connect the trailer. Drive to the green parking bay, align the truck and trailer, stop inside the marked area and hold still until the parking countdown finishes. Complete the task before the timer runs out and avoid too many collisions. Desktop: use WASD or the arrow keys to steer, accelerate, brake and reverse, or drag the on-screen joystick. Drag outside the interface to orbit the camera. Use the on-screen camera, zoom and pause buttons. Mobile: use the steering wheel or arrow controls with the Gas and Brake / rev pedals. Drag outside the controls to look around. Change steering mode in the pause menu; tilt requires a supported device.

About the Game

Take on precision truck parking challenges with a trailer in tow. Follow the route, line up with the hitch, connect your trailer and guide it into the marked bay before time runs out. Watch your speed, avoid collisions and hold still to complete each parking task. Earn coins and stars, unlock trucks, customize their appearance and improve performance in the garage. Switch camera views to judge tight turns and reversing angles. Play with keyboard or mouse controls on desktop, or use on-screen steering and pedals on mobile.
