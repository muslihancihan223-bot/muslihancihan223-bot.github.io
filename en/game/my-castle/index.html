How to Play

Build your first rooms and level them up - this drives both resource output and squad size. Assign dwellers to work rooms: they gather wood, food and gold while you are away. Train dwellers in the training halls to raise their stats and unlock stronger gear. Equip your fighters with weapons and armor from chests and rewards. Mind the requirements - a weak dweller can't lift a heavy sword. Before a fight, place your fighters - melee up front, archers and mages behind.

About the Game

A medieval castle that grows from a single room into a real fortress. Build a sawmill, a kitchen, a warehouse and training halls, move dwellers between rooms and keep wood, food and gold flowing. Every dweller is a fighter of their own. Train their profession, hand them weapons and armor. Items have rarity and rolled stats - two identical swords are almost never identical. Battle starts with placement. You decide who holds the front line and takes the hit, and who strikes from behind. After that the squad fights on its own - the outcome comes from preparation and positioning, not from click speed. The arena pits your squad against squads of real players. There is a rating and tokens you spend on rewards. Chests with weapons and armor, global chat.
