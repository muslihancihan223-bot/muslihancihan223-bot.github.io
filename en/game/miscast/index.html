How to Play

- Pick 3 spells: tap them or press keys **1–8**, then **Enter** to duel. - In a duel, cast with a **tap** or keys **1 / 2 / 3**. - Watch the enemy's cast bar: **Dispel** to interrupt, **Rune Ward / Mirror** to defend, or out-damage them. - Re-tap the spell you're casting to **cancel** it (feint). - **P** to pause.

About the Game

Miscast is a fast 1v1 wizard duel about timing and reading your opponent. Build a loadout of 3 spells and climb an 8-mage ladder — ach rival teaches one new trick and hands it to you when you win. The whole game is watching the enemy's cast bar and deciding in the moment: cut a big cast with Dispel, brace with a Rune Ward or bounce it with a Mirror, or gamble that your damage lands first. Commit to a spell and you're locked in — and your foe sees it coming. Feint your own casts to bait their defenses, then punish. Duels last 60–90 seconds. When you've beaten the ladder, take on the Daily Challenge (same seed for everyone, climb the leaderboard) or see how deep you go in Endless survival. Features: - 8 spells, dozens of viable loadouts (repeats allowed — three Firebolts is a real build) - 8 distinct mage opponents, each a new tactic - The bluff: cancel your own cast to bait a defense - Daily Challenge with leaderboard + Endless survival mode - Quick 60–90s duels · Mouse, keyboard, and touch · English & Portuguese
