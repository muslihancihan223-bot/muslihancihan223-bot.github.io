How to Play

Your objective is to clear the board by finding all matching pairs of animal cards before the time runs out. Controls: Tap or click to flip a card. Memorize the animals and find their match to remove them from the board. Complete the level as quickly as possible to earn a 3-star rating and maximize your score! If you're stuck, you can use the Power-Ups (located at the bottom of the screen) to reveal rows, columns, or the entire board. Spin the Lucky Wheel for extra rewards and compete for the top spot on the Global Leaderboard!

About the Game

Safari Match is an exciting, jungle-themed memory puzzle game where players test their focus and speed by finding matching animal cards. Navigate through beautifully illustrated exotic wildlife cards against a ticking clock. As you progress, the grid expands, offering greater challenges and higher rewards. Unlock special power-ups like 'Reveal All' or 'Row Reveal' to help you conquer the hardest levels. Featuring vibrant artwork, dynamic animations, global leaderboards, and an immersive safari atmosphere, it's a premium brain-teasing adventure suitable for all ages!
