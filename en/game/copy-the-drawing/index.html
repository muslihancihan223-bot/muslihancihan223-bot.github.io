Play Copy the Drawing

Copy the Drawing is a free Drawing game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

Drawing

How to Play

1. Choose a category and level 2. Look at the example at the top of the screen 3. Draw the same picture on the canvas below 4. Use three brush sizes for precise lines 5. Tap "Undo" to remove the last stroke, or "Clear" to start over 6. When you're happy with the result, tap "Check" 7. Get a match score and earn a medal: bronze (50%+), silver (70%+), or gold (90%+) 8. Unlock new levels and categories!

About the Game

An exciting drawing game with hundreds of levels! Look at the example and recreate the drawing on the canvas — the more accurate, the higher your score. The game features 300 levels across 18 categories: from simple shapes to animals, transport, space, fantasy, and Japanese culture. Every day brings a new drawing of the day — build your streak and compete with yourself! Three brush sizes, undo last stroke, hints, and a medal system — all designed to make drawing fun and enjoyable. Gold, silver, or bronze — which medal will your drawing earn?
