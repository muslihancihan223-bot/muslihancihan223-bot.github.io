How to Play

Drag your finger to walk (arrow keys or WASD on a computer). Pick up a pet from the waiting area and carry it to a free bed. Look at the icon above the pet, take that medicine from the cabinet — soap, a shot or a pill — and bring it to the bed. The pet gets well and leaves coins: walk to the coin pile to collect them. Step on a price circle to buy beds, wards and nurses. Nurses get tired, so bring them coffee. Earn more, open new wards and the Water Clinic!

About the Game

Run your own animal hospital! Patients are waiting: kittens, koalas, red pandas, kangaroos and many more. Carry each pet to a bed, bring the right medicine — soap, a shot or a pill — and watch it get well. Every cured patient leaves a pile of gold coins. Spend your coins on new beds and wards, hire nurses who treat patients for you, and keep them going with coffee. Upgrade your wards and keep earning even while you are away. When your clinic is booming, open the Water Clinic, where dolphins, crabs and sea horses get treated in pools! • Easy controls: drag one finger to walk • 46 kinds of animal patients in two clinics • 3 wards and 40 beds in every clinic • Hire up to 4 nurses per ward • Golden patients pay up to 10 times more • 35 doctor outfits with bonuses — open chests to collect them • Daily gifts, a free wheel spin every day, daily and weekly tasks • 90 achievements
