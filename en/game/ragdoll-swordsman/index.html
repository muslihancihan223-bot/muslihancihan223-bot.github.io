How to Play

Goal: clear each level of enemies and beat the boss at the end of every world. PC: move with WASD or the arrow keys, jump with Space, hold the left mouse button and move the mouse to swing your weapon (F or J also attack), X grabs enemies so you can throw them into traps. Phone: move with the on-screen joystick and drag your finger to swing the blade; use the on-screen buttons to jump and grab. Earn coins to unlock new weapons and skins and to upgrade damage, health and speed.

About the Game

Ragdoll Swordsman is physics action where every hit is real. You don't control an animation, you control a living ragdoll. Hold the mouse or drag your finger and the blade follows your hand: slash, stab, parry. Mannequin enemies stagger, fall, lose arms and legs and still crawl after you. Physics decides everything, so no two fights look the same. ⚔ 30+ WEAPONS Katana, machete, nail bat, nunchaku, knight sword, bo staff, battle axe, pitchfork, spear, mace, hammer, cleaver, scythe, chainsaw, laser sword, double laser and the mega saw. Want it louder? Pistols, shotguns, a musket, a heavy machine gun and a grenade launcher. Five rarity tiers, from common to legendary. 🪤 TRAPS AND TRICKS Kick enemies onto spikes and circular saws, grab and throw them into water, lava, acid and chasms. Cut a rope and the log sweeps the crowd. Kick a lever and the crusher drops. Kick barrels, ride fans, step on pressure plates.
