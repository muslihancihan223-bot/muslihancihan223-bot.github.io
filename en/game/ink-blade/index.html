How to Play

Move: W A S D / Arrows -- Left stick -- virtual stick Slash (chain combo): Left mouse / J -- X -- Slash button Dash: Space -- A -- Dash button Guard and parry: Right mouse / L -- RB -- Guard button. Press it as the ring closes on an enemy. Pause: P or the pause icon -- Start. Mute: M or the speaker icon. Clear 4 waves and beat the boss to reach the next district. Every second wave you pick an upgrade and your health refills.

About the Game

Ink Blade is a dark isometric sword action game. Fight waves of enforcers in a rain-soaked city with one blade and a red scarf. Read every attack: a ring closes in on the enemy, and a guard timed as it closes parries the hit and staggers them. White rings can be parried, red ones must be dodged, orange ones are armored and gold ones are tricks. Fight through 8 districts, each with its own look, music and boss with parry patterns and a second phase. Pick an upgrade every two waves, refill your health, and rank up to level 160 to train skills between runs. New enemy types join as you level up. Unlock blades, sigils and outfits in the Armory, finish a daily contract, or go online: PvP up to 4v4 (deathmatch, team deathmatch, capture the flag) or co-op waves with up to 6 players, with bots filling empty seats.
