How to Play

Use the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe to steer. Leave the chocolate edge, turn and reconnect to solid chocolate to claim a shape. The first stage shows each step as you play. Watch the attack warnings and check the target percentage or special objective at the top of each stage. Press P or tap Pause to take a break.

About the Game

The edge is safe. The syrup is where the points are. Dash out as a pastry chef, carve a path and get back to solid chocolate before the monster cuts your line. Chain bold cuts, rescue stranded sweets and take on candy bosses across 336 stages in six worlds. Try the one-cut challenges, take on a computer rival in Duel, or chase a new score in Daily Breach and Weekly Cup.
