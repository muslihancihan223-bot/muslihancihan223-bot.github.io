About the Game

Egg Empire is a cozy 3D idle tycoon about building the biggest chicken farm on the island. Buy hens, collect their eggs, sell them at the shop and merge your hens into rarer and rarer breeds! How to play - Stand on the green pads to buy hens. They lay eggs that roll down the chute into the pit. - Run over the eggs to collect them into your basket. - Bring the eggs to the shop. The store sells them for you: collect your money. - Merge 3 identical hens into a new, more valuable breed. - Rebirth to multiply all your earnings and unlock new hens and incubators. Features - 25 chicken breeds to discover, plus Golden, Diamond and Emerald mutations - Lucky Blocks with surprise hens of every rarity - Incubators: hatch eggs in 15 minutes, 2 hours or 8 hours for rare, epic and legendary hens - A free Wheel of Luck spin every 5 minutes - Rebirths with huge money multipliers - A pet dog and a duck pond that grow with you and boost your farm - An obby tower with a reward at the top - Daily quests and login rewards - Your hens keep laying eggs while you're away
