How to Play

Use the numbers on the faces of the block to determine how many voxels should remain in each row. Remove all unnecessary voxels to reveal the hidden object. Desktop: drag the mouse to rotate the puzzle. Use the on-screen buttons or 1 to mark a voxel, 2 to remove it and 3 to clear a mark. Q/E change the visible layer, Z/X undo and redo, C uses a hint, and Space shows the full block again. Mobile: drag with one finger to rotate, tap voxels to interact, pinch with two fingers to zoom, and use the on-screen controls for tools, layers, hints and undo/redo. Complete the puzzle by removing every unnecessary voxel without removing the cubes that belong to the hidden object.

About the Game

Discover hidden objects inside mysterious voxel blocks! Use numerical clues, rotate each 3D puzzle, remove unnecessary cubes and reveal charming miniature creations. Solve hundreds of relaxing logic puzzles, unlock new collections and build your own gallery of voxel art.
