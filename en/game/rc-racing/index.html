How to Play

Finish ahead of five rivals. Races and tournaments pay prize money: spend it in the shop and the workshop on parts, tyres and setup. Broken parts slow you down until you repair them. W / ↑ — throttle S / ↓ — brake and reverse A / D or ← / → — steering. By default it works like a real transmitter: when the car drives toward you, left and right are reversed. Switch to Simple in Settings if you prefer. Esc — pause and back, Enter — main action, R — restart F — full screen, M — sound, mouse wheel — camera zoom Gamepads and USB RC transmitters are supported (set up in Settings).

About the Game

Race 1:8 nitro buggies from the drivers' tower, just like at a real RC track. Five rivals trained with reinforcement learning, a career from the local club to the World championship, and a workshop where you strip and rebuild your car screw by screw. Inside: • RC physics: suspension, wing, tyres and a nitro engine with needles, head temperature and glow plug. • 5 tracks: Club Dirt, Clay Stadium, Oval, Sand Quarry and Winter Park, in dry weather, rain and changing weather. • Career: tournaments, qualifying and long finals with refuelling and pit stops. • Workshop and shop: repair broken parts, tune the setup, fit new engines, pipes, tyres and liveries; mud tyres for wet days. • Quick race, time trial against ghosts, elimination; daily tasks, rewards and leaderboards. Keyboard, gamepad or a USB RC transmitter. Progress is saved.
