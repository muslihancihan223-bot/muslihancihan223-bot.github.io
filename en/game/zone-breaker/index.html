How to Play

Goal: destroy everything to fill your backpack with bricks. Sell them for coins, upgrade your gear, level up to open new areas, then reach level 55 and Rank Up for even more coins per brick. Blast structures with your rocket. The bricks go into your backpack. When the backpack is full, press SELL. Every brick becomes coins. Buy bigger rockets, bombs and backpacks in the Shop. Level up to unlock new areas. Each new area gives more bricks per block. Controls (computer): Move: WASD or arrow keys Aim: mouse Fire: left click Drop a bomb: G (or right click) Switch tool: 1 (rocket) / 2 (bomb) or the mouse wheel Jump: Space Sprint: Shift (free Sprint pass) Menus: B Shop · C Codes · P Passes · R Rank Up · J Daily wheel Pause: Esc Controls (mobile): Tap anything to blast it. Left thumb: joystick to move. Push it far to sprint. Drag the right side of the screen to turn the camera. Hold FIRE to keep firing. JUMP button to jump. Tap the slots to switch between rocket and bomb, and use the buttons on the left for the menus.

About the Game

Zone Breakers is a 3D destruction simulator set in a blocky brick world. Blast race cars, houses, castles and skyscrapers into pieces. Every brick that breaks flies into your backpack. Sell the bricks for coins and buy bigger rockets, stronger bombs and larger backpacks, then go smash even bigger things. What makes it unique: Real structural physics: break what holds a building up and the whole thing collapses. Towers topple and walls fall in chunks. One big open map with 16 areas to unlock, from the Race Track to the Volcano and Tower Battle, where you climb giant towers from the inside. 38 rockets, 24 bombs and 25 backpacks, from a starter launcher to a Black Hole and a Nuclear Pack. Free passes unlocked just by playing, a daily prize wheel, Super Lucky Blocks and Rank Ups to keep you coming back. Your progress is saved automatically.
