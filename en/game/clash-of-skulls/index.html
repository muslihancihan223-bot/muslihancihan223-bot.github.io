About the Game

Clash of Skulls is a tower defense game where you try to prevent enemies from destroying your base by deploying various types of units. Face a dangerous horde of evil creatures that intend to invade your lands. Combine and level up your units to have the upper hand and increase your damage output. Once you've taken care of the defense part, you can start planning your attacks to take over your enemy's territory. Hire skeleton warriors with blades, spears, scythes, explosives, battering rams, and much more... Go ahead and try it. Clash of Skulls rules! This side-scrolling strategy game features a lane-based tug-of-war mechanic where players deploy diverse skeleton units to destroy the opponent's fortified tower. The gameplay loop centers on managing a regenerating mana resource to summon units from a card-based hand, including melee warriors, archers, and heavy-hitting bosses like the hog rider. Each unit automatically marches right toward the enemy base, engaging in combat upon contact. Victory is achieved by depleting the enemy tower's health bar displayed at the top of the screen.
