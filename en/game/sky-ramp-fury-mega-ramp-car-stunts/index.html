How to Play

Drive to the finish flag without falling off the sky track. Use speed and NOS to clear jumps, then keep your car level in the air so you land on your wheels. Crash or fall and you restart from the last checkpoint. Finish with fewer resets to earn 3 stars.

About the Game

Sky Ramp Fury is a mega ramp car stunt racing game set high above the clouds. Launch your car off giant striped ramps, fly through loop tubes, cross narrow sky bridges and stick the landing to reach the finish flag. 50 levels pack bigger jumps, wilder gaps and tougher tracks. Fire your NOS at the right moment, balance your car in mid-air, and earn up to 3 stars on every run. Collect coins to unlock 5 stunt cars, from the Red Rocket to the Viper, and paint each one in your own colours. Checkpoints keep you racing after a crash, and daily rewards and missions give you bonus coins and gems.
