How to Play

The introductory tutorial will familiarize you with the core game mechanics, including purchasing and upgrading rooms. To buy or upgrade a room, click on the area where the room is located. In the window that opens, you will see information about the cost as well as a purchase button. If the room has already been purchased, a management window will appear with various content options depending on the room type. To upgrade the content of the selected room, choose the desired item and click the “Upgrade” button. Access to all main game mechanics is available from the main screen.

About the Game

Idle Magic Academy Tycoon is a charming management simulator that puts you in charge of a growing academy of sorcery. You design classrooms, recruit skilled Magisters, and gather powerful artifacts to shape each lesson. As headmaster, you guide students from simple theories to advanced combat spells, expanding your academy into a legendary center of magic. Manage your academy wisely, constantly improve the quality of education, and transform your institution into a legendary center of arcane mastery! Develop your Academy’s infrastructure, collect Arcanum Spheres, and research magical technologies.
