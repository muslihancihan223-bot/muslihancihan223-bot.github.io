How to Play

Tap or interact with the available beads/objects. Move matching colors toward their correct container. Sort and collect all colors correctly. Avoid blocking important paths or making the wrong move. Clear all beads from the board to complete the level. New levels introduce more colors, obstacles, and challenging layouts.

About the Game

Enjoy a satisfying color-sorting puzzle where your goal is to move, collect, and organize colorful beads into the correct containers. Plan every move carefully, clear the board, and complete increasingly challenging levels. With simple controls, relaxing visuals, and progressively harder puzzles, each level tests your timing, observation, and strategy.
