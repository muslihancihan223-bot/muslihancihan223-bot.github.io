Play Noob: Steal the Egg

Noob: Steal the Egg is a free Arcade, 3D, Obby game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

Arcade3DObbyFarm

How to Play

Desktop: use WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, hold the right mouse button to rotate the camera and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Press E near an egg to steal it, at your base to place it, or near the treadmill to train. Press I for pets and Esc for settings. Mobile: move with the on-screen joystick, drag to rotate the camera and use the action and jump buttons. Start with the chicken's egg in the green zone. Escape through the central fence opening, return to your base and place the egg. Hatch and grow pets for income, train your speed and buy upgrades to reach tougher animals.

About the Game

Sneak up to animal nests, steal an egg and race back to your base before its owner catches you. Hatch pets, grow your collection and earn coins to improve your farm. Train on the treadmill to run faster, equip companions and explore nine zones with 27 animal species. Upgrade your base, merge matching pets and compete for the best farm score. Play solo alongside three computer-controlled neighbours. Optional rewarded videos offer coins and cosmetic items.
