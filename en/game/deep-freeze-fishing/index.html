How to Play

Everything is played with one finger (or mouse), no keyboard needed. Drag to move your lure through the water. Flick upward through a fish's mouth to catch it, sweep through several at once for a COMBO that multiplies your coins. Swipe up to the surface to bank your catch. Tap a mutant fish to scare it away; never flick into its mouth or it steals your lure. Fill a zone's Fishdex, beat its Zone Challenge, then buy the next lure in the shop to unlock deeper zones.

About the Game

Deep Freeze Fishing is a flick-and-catch arcade fishing game set beneath a frozen lake. Snag fish by flicking your lure straight through their mouths, chain multi-fish combos off the ice ceiling, and fill your Fishdex across five ever-deeper zones. Complete each zone's Fishdex to unlock its Zone Challenge, schools of fish you'll need sharp aim to clear. Watch out for mutant fish: hook one by accident and it swims off with your lure. One-finger controls, quick sessions, 60 species to collect.
