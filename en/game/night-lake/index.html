How to Play

Computer: Hold Left Click on the water to aim, release to cast. Click or Space when the float really goes under, then hold to reel in. In a fight, keep the tension out of the red and hold A/D to lean the rod away from the fish. Row with W/S and A/D (or the arrow keys), or Right Click the water to row there. E opens the dock shop at the dock. H shows all the other keys. Phone: Left stick: push to row and turn. Right stick: hold to aim, let go to cast. Tap the right stick when the float goes under, then hold it to reel in. Ease off when the line turns red.

About the Game

Row out onto a dark lake with a lantern, a rod and the feeling that something below is watching. Night Lake is a calm, eerie fishing game. Cast your float or spin a lure, watch the float bob in the lantern light and strike when it really goes under. Every fish fights back: keep the line out of the red, lean the rod against the pull and bring it to the boat. Sell your catch at the dock, buy better baits and tackle, finish the night's quests and row on to new waters. Every lake has its own fish, sounds, weather and night, from pine lakes and jungle bayous to frozen lakes, misty marshes, a Halloween lake and a lighthouse cove, with new waters still being added. But the lake is not empty. The lantern stutters, the crickets fall silent and a shadow longer than your boat glides underneath. Monsters need their own bait and strong gear, and they can tow your boat across the water.
