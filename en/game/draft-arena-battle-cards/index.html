How to Play

Win matches by building the stronger army. Each round, click or tap one of three cards to add fighters to your army, then watch the armies fight on their own. Tap the bomb or lightning button, then tap the arena to use it at the right moment. Lose all hearts and the match is lost; win to move along the Victory Road and unlock new fighters. Controls: mouse or touch.

About the Game

Draft Arena: Battle Cards is a quick strategy auto battler. Every round you pick one of three cards to grow your army: robots, cowboys, chickens, TNT, tanks and more. Then your army fights the rival army on its own. Upgrade fighters to stronger ranks and drop a bomb or lightning at the right moment to turn the battle around. Win matches to walk the Victory Road, unlock 12 fighters and 3 arenas, open card packs, upgrade your fighters and beat each arena champion. Short matches, easy to learn, hard to put down.
