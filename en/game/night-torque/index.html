How to Play

Press Space, Up Arrow, Enter or the on-screen gas pedal to start the countdown. Hold and release to keep the revs in the green band until the lights turn green. Tap the same control to shift when the needle reaches the green zone. Clean shifts build nitro. Press N, X or Right Arrow to boost, or tap the nitro button. On a phone, turn to landscape and use the gas and nitro buttons.

About the Game

Night Torque is a drag racing game about precise launches, well-timed gear shifts and building a garage of your own. Race through 10 districts against 60 career rivals, collect 20 cars and tune their engines, gearboxes, tires and nitro kits. Choose paint, wheels, spoilers and decals to create your style. Learn the controls in your first race, practice your technique and take on 60 mastery challenges with fixed loan setups. Optional rewarded videos offer extra in-game coins.
