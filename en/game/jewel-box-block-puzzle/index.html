How to Play

Drag one of the three pieces at the bottom onto the board. A completely filled row or column disappears and gives points; clear several lines at once or in a row for a combo. New pieces appear once all three are placed, and the game ends when no piece fits. You can also tap a piece, then tap a cell. In Adventure, reach the level goals within the move limit: collect gems, clear blocks of a color or reach a score. Ice breaks in two hits, vines spread if you leave them alone for two moves. On desktop: mouse, or keys 1-3 to pick a piece, arrows to move it, Enter or Space to place, Esc to pause.

About the Game

A cozy block puzzle where every block is a cut gemstone. Drag pieces onto the board, fill rows and columns and watch them burst into sparkles. Play 300 adventure levels across 15 chapters (then endless levels) with gems to collect, ice to break, boulders and spreading vines, or chase high scores in Classic and the 9x9 mode where 3x3 squares clear too. Stars from levels furnish your own jewelry shop: 10 rooms, 130 items with 5 styles each, style sets, VIP guests and a weekly showpiece collection. Five boosters, a daily gift and daily tasks. No timers, progress saves automatically.
