About the Game

ECHO-9: Pressure Chamber is a short first-person survival horror game set on Kola Deep 9, a geothermal drilling station 11,000 m down in the Mariana Trench. Contact with the crew was lost 37 hours ago. The station is partly flooded and running on emergency power. Find 3 high-voltage fuses, insert them into the generator room panel, pump the water out of the dock and survive while the airlock of the Poisk-2 bathysphere seals. You are not alone. The Abyssal hides as rusty machinery and moves only when you are not looking at it, or when you blink. Features: - Don't blink: your eyes dry out, so every blink is a risk you choose to take. - Flashlight and lidar scanner with limited charge. Light reveals the creature, but holding it on its face provokes it. - Crouch, lean around corners and hold your breath to stay unheard. - 3D positional audio, best with headphones. - Easy, Normal and Nightmare difficulty, No Lidar and No Flashlight challenges, and a best time for every mode. - In-game hints teach the controls as you play. - English and Russian. Warning: contains flashing lights and loud sounds.
