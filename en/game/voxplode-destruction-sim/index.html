How to Play

Finish contracts: bring the target building down far enough, or carry the valuables out before the alarm runs out. Spend the cash on new tools and upgrades; in the sandbox every tool is free. Computer: WASD to walk, mouse to look, left click to use the tool, right click to aim or for the second action, 1-9 or the mouse wheel to pick a tool, Shift to run, Space to jump, E to grab or get into a vehicle, R to reload, Q to drop, Tab for the map, Esc to pause. Phone: left stick to walk, swipe on the right to look, on-screen buttons for the tool, jump and actions.

About the Game

Everything is made of voxels, and everything breaks. Knock a wall down with a sledgehammer, bring a building down with C4, watch glass shatter, fire spread and debris fly. Take out the supports and the storey fails, and everything above comes down. A city to demolish: suburbs, downtown, industry, harbor, construction site, farm and a mansion. Demolition jobs pay for bringing the target down; heists give you 60 seconds to escape with the loot, straight through the walls. Tools from sledgehammer and chainsaw to nuke, black hole, tornado and orbital laser, plus a tank, a bulldozer and a wrecking-ball crane.
