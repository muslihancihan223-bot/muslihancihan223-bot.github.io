Parking Shift: Car Simulator is a free Arcade, 3D, Car game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Choose a car and level. Drive to the marked parking bay and keep the whole car inside it until the countdown finishes. Avoid collisions and finish before the timer expires. Desktop: W/Up accelerates; S/Down brakes, then reverses; A/D or Left/Right steers; Space is the handbrake; R toggles reverse gear; C changes camera; mouse drag looks around; H toggles the parking helper; Esc pauses; M mutes. You can also drag the driving joystick in the lower-center area with the mouse. Mobile: use the on-screen steering controls and pedals. Drag an empty area with a free finger to look around.

About the Game

Put your driving precision to the test across 41 parking challenges. Choose a sport sedan or a rugged 4x4, navigate obstacles, and hold your car fully inside the marked bay before time runs out. Watch your three hearts: collisions cost health. Switch cameras, use the parking helper, and practice forward and reverse parking. Visit the garage to change paint, neon, wheels and body parts, and upgrade your car. Your progress, unlocked items and settings are saved. Play with keyboard and mouse on desktop or on-screen driving controls on mobile.
