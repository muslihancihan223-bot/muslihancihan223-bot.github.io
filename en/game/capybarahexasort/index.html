How to Play

Place a hex tile stack from the bottom tray onto an empty cell on the board. Stacks with matching top colors that touch each other will automatically sort and merge. Keep combining colors to clear tiles and reach the level goal before the board runs out of free cells. Complete the goal to win; if there is no room for another stack, the level is lost. Mobile: Tap or drag a stack, then release it over an empty board cell. Desktop: Click and drag with the left mouse button, then release over an empty board cell. Use the in-game buttons for boosters, pause/settings, sound, music, and haptics.

About the Game

Capybara Hexa Sort is a relaxing color-sorting puzzle game with a cheerful capybara theme. Place stacks of colorful hexagonal tiles on the board and watch matching top colors merge automatically. Plan every move, complete increasingly challenging level goals, collect rewards, and enjoy a satisfying blend of strategy and cozy visuals.
