How to Play

Click on boxes to open them and receive cats, coins, and rewards. Drag identical cats onto each other to merge them into a cat of the next level. Buy new boxes with coins and collect boxes from the cat beds. Discover all 10 cats in a world to unlock the next world. On PC, use the mouse. On mobile, use taps and drag controls.

About the Game

Cat Box Merge is a cozy casual game about cats, boxes, and merging. Open boxes, discover adorable cats, and combine identical ones to get new, rarer pets. Place cats on beds: they will help you and gradually bring new boxes for further progress. Collect cats, unlock new stages of progression, and move through different cozy worlds, each with its own mood, design, and unique characters. The further you progress, the more opportunities appear: new cats, pleasant rewards, boosts, and a constant feeling of growth. The game is perfect for players who enjoy relaxing clickers, merge mechanics, collecting, and a cute atmosphere. Play at your own pace, upgrade your collection, and unlock more and more fluffy heroes.
