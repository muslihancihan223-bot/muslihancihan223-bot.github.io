How to Play

Survive 12 minutes and defeat the boss. Desktop: WASD to move, aim with the mouse, hold the left mouse button to shoot, Space to dash (after you get the Stage Dive perk), Esc to pause. Mobile: the left stick moves, the right stick aims and fires automatically, the button dashes. Collect amber to level up and choose one of three perks. Pick up weapons that drop on the map and bonuses: freeze, bomb, double damage and medkit. Spend amber in the shop on permanent upgrades.

About the Game

A top-down pixel-art arena shooter about Sanych, a grumpy 60-year-old metalhead from the Baltic coast who fights endless hordes of pop music. Survive a 12-minute run against auto-tune drones, boy bands, pop divas and EDM DJs, level up and pick stackable perks, grab new guns from the map and take down the boss, The Algorithm. Earn amber, upgrade Sanych in the shop between runs and unlock new locations: the old fort and the dacha village, each with its own enemies.
