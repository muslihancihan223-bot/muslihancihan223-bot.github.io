How to Play

**Your goal:** Reach the Golden Gate Bridge at 15,000 meters! - **Mobile:** Swipe or drag left and right to steer Racco. - **Desktop:** Use the **Left and Right Arrow keys** or **A / D** to steer. - **Fire Boost:** Press **Spacebar** on desktop or tap the on-screen fire extinguisher button on mobile when you have a charge for an extra burst of speed. - **Collect coins**, break coin-filled crates, avoid dangerous obstacles, and hit ramps to perform spectacular tricks. - **Upgrade your equipment:** Spend coins between runs to improve your slingshot, lid, and income. Keep your speed, choose your path, and make it all the way to the finish line!

About the Game

Trash Surfer is a fast-paced 3D downhill arcade game starring Racco, a mischievous raccoon who surfs the steep streets of San Francisco on a metal trash-can lid! Dodge obstacles, hit crazy ramps, pull off awesome tricks, collect coins, and upgrade your equipment to go faster and farther. Race through five unique districts and conquer the ultimate 15,000-meter descent to the Golden Gate Bridge!
