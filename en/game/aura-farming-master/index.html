Aura Farming Master is a free Action, 3D, Fighting game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Goal: farm as much aura as you can and don't fall off the roof. PC: WASD or the arrow keys to move, Space to dash, E to pose, hold the mouse button and drag to turn the camera. Mobile: joystick on the left, drag on the right to turn the camera, Dash and Pose buttons. Stand in a glowing zone to earn aura every second. Press Pose and stop the arrow in the green window for a burst of aura. Walk into rivals to slap them out of the zone. Enter lobby portals to pick a mode.

About the Game

Farm aura on floating neon rooftops at sunset. Stand in the glowing aura zones to earn aura every second and build a streak multiplier, while bots run in and try to slap you out. Hit the Pose button at the right moment for a burst of aura and a slow-motion phonk moment. Spend aura on Farm Speed, Pose Window and Slap Power upgrades, unlock 20 gloves as your lifetime aura grows, dress up with over 250 wardrobe items and play Farm, Rush, Battle Royale and Duel modes.
