How to Play

Your main goal is to drive forward as far as possible without crashing into other vehicles, survive the traffic to rack up distance-based scores, and use careful stops to upgrade your vehicle for better performance—reach high scores by mastering timing and avoiding collisions! PC Controls: Mouse / Arrows: Swipe or hold left/right to steer and change lanes. Hold Mouse Button / Space: Accelerate forward. Release: Stop or brake. Mobile Controls: Touchscreen: Tap and hold screen to drive forward; release to stop. Swipe left/right: Steer and switch lanes.

About the Game

Navigate the roads in Transport Run, a thrilling 3D traffic avoidance arcade runner! Drive your colorful vehicle along winding highways lined with pink trees and multi-lane traffic, dodging oncoming cars and buses to reach the goal without any crashes. The core idea is careful timing and precision driving in dense traffic—unique for its relaxing yet tense endless run style, isometric cartoon visuals with vibrant environments, and easy upgrades earned through skillful play that make each run feel progressively smoother and more rewarding!
