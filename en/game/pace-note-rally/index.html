Pace Note Rally is a free Racing, 3D, Car game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Reach the finish as fast as you can and win medals. Watch the pace note cards: the number shows how sharp the corner is (6 nearly flat, 1 very tight), the colour shows the danger. Keyboard: W/S or arrows to accelerate and brake, A/D or arrows to steer, Space handbrake, E wipers, C camera, R reset to the road, Esc pause. Gamepad supported. On phones use the on-screen buttons.

About the Game

You can barely see the road: fog, night, rain and mud on the windscreen. Trust your co-driver! Read the pace notes, take each corner at the called speed and race through 24 stages in 8 regions, from snowy passes to an active volcano and Mars. 9 cars, upgrades, medals, daily stages and championships.
