How to Play

1-Tap a flask to pick up the top liquid. 2-Tap another flask to pour it. 3-You can only pour liquid onto another liquid of the same color or into an empty flask. 4-Use "Undo" or "Add Flask" power-ups if you get stuck!

About the Game

Dive into the relaxing world of Aqua Sort: Water Color Puzzle! Your goal is simple: sort the colored liquids into the flasks until each flask contains only one color. With over 1000 levels and unique modes like Color Fusion, Mystery, and Time Trial, this puzzle game will test your logic and patience. Unlock new themes and achievements as you play!
