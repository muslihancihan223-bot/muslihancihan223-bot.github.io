How to Play

🎮 How to Play Jellies fall into the jar New jellies appear from the top and drop into the container. Merge identical jellies When identical jellies touch, they merge into a more advanced and valuable version. Fill the jar Keep an eye on the fill level — the more jellies inside, the closer you are to selling. Sell the contents When the jar is nearly full or completely filled, sell the jellies to turn them into money. About the Game

Jelly Drop: Merge & Sell is a bright and addictive merge game where every jelly earns you points! Merge identical jellies, fill the jar to the brim, and sell the result to earn more and more. The higher the jelly level, the more points you get. Simple controls, satisfying physics, and a constant sense of progress make the game perfect for short sessions and relaxing gameplay. Watch the jar fill up as jellies merge into more valuable forms and your score grows with every sale. Upgrade your stats, increase capacity, and turn ordinary jellies into a true record-breaking machine!
