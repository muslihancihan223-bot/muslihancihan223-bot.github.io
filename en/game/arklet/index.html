About the Game

Welcome to Arklet - a cozy and meditative puzzle game where your talent for organizing turns into the art of creating harmony for the most adorable creatures. In this game, you become the caretaker of a unique ARKlet filled with charming "square" animals, each possessing its own personality, quirks, and little secrets. 🧩 Gameplay: Harmony in Every Block Your task is simple: distribute the arriving animals into cozy rooms. However, each ward is an individual. To complete a level perfectly, you must do more than just fill the space; you must ensure every animal feels completely safe and comfortable. 🐾 Consider Every Detail: Fears and Dislikes: A massive elephant might panic if placed in the same room as a tiny mouse. You will need to plan layouts so the giant doesn't even catch a glimpse of its small neighbor. Friendship and Socializing: Some animals, like meerkats or raccoons, love company, while bears or porcupines might prefer solitude in a far corner.
