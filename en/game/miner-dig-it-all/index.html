Miner, Dig It All is a free Simulation game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

Simulation

How to Play

• Mouse: hold the left button to drill. Hold and drag to lay a line of dynamite, a single click plants one stick. Right click fires the charges early. • Touch: tap to plant a stick, drag to lay a line, hold to drill. The charges fire on their own. • Space fires the planted charges. • Esc or Tab pauses.

About the Game

Your drill has one tank of fuel. See how deep the pit lets you get! Drag to lay a line of dynamite, fire it, then hold the drill to grind the shattered rock away. Clear the layer and it collapses under you, straight into a richer one. On the surface, sell the ore and buy a harder bit, wider blasts and a robot crew, then dive again. Ten layers down sits the lava core: crack it and the whole pit starts over, permanently richer!
