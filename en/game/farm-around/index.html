About the Game

Farm Around is a tycoon game where you build and expand your very own tractor farm deep in the countryside! Start with a single cart, collect resources, and process them at your mill to grow your farming empire. Use your earnings to unlock new carts, merge them into stronger tractors, and boost their speed and cargo capacity. Strategically expand your chain of tractors to maximize efficiency and profits as you watch your farm come to life. Your goal is clear, turn your humble village farm into a thriving production powerhouse and become the ultimate farm tycoon! Features: - Add and upgrade tractor carts - Merge tractors to create stronger units - Boost speed and carrying capacity - Enjoy relaxing, colorful visuals, and satisfying progression Are you ready to grow your tiny farm into a powerful farming empire?
