How to Play

In combat, everything is based on resource economics: food for your creatures is produced automatically. Use it to summon units to build your army and crush enemy bases — destroying enemy bases is the key to completing levels. After battles, it's time for development. With the coins you earn in battles, you can unlock new units and improve your base's stats. Additionally, collect artifact cards, which provide your army with powerful permanent bonuses that apply in every battle.

About the Game

Are you attracted to adorable animals, and do you have the talent of a great commander? The new game combines these two passions, giving you the chance to build an empire in a world of endless battles! Here, you'll lead armies of cute yet formidable animal warriors. Command your troops, employ clever tactics, and devise effective strategies to conquer various adversaries. Explore new lands, conquer islands, and discover unique biomes inhabited by fascinating animals. Summon new fighters to your army, level them up, and turn them into the most powerful and charming armada. Remember: with each island you conquer, your enemies become stronger. This exciting strategy game offers hours of deep gameplay for anyone who loves animals and wants to test their tactical skills, regardless of age or gaming experience.
