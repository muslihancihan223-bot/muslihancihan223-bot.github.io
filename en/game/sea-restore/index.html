Sea Restore is a free Arcade, Simulation, Clicker game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Tap the pump to add water. Spend coins on a stronger pump, stations and workers. Workers bring goods, collectors carry them to the store, and town buildings craft them into pricier goods. Sell goods or fill truck orders for more coins. Tap trash to clear the water and catch glowing eggs to hatch monsters. Fill the tide meter for a high tide with extra water. The sea keeps working for you while you are away.

About the Game

A meteor left a huge dry crater. Fill it with water and turn it into a living sea! Tap the pump, build stations and grow a seaside town through seven ages, from the First Drops to the Ocean Age. Hire fishers, collect goods, craft sushi, pies and gifts, and fill orders for the delivery truck. Catch the eggs that rise from the meteor and hatch 13 cute sea monsters, each with its own bonus. When the sea is full, start a new cycle and climb the leaderboard of all the water you have ever poured. Music by Yanni Ziangos a.k.a. YannZ CC BY 4.0
