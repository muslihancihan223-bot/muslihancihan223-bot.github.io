How to Play

Goal: skate the city, do tricks, win races against bots, deliver parcels and collect golden tokens to earn coins and unlock gear. Desktop: W / Up arrow push, A/D or Left/Right arrows steer, S / Down brake, Space jump (press again in the air for a flip, arrows pick the flip), Shift or E boost, M big map, P pause, drag the mouse to look around. Mobile: drag left/right to steer, tap to jump, tap again in the air to flip, boost button bottom right, map button above the minimap.

About the Game

Curb Rush is a 3D skateboarding game in a big open city. Skate freely through streets, parks, a harbour and a skatepark, land ollies, kickflips and grinds, chain tricks into combos and fill your Flow meter for a speed boost. Race 7 bots on 5 routes with boost pads, ramps and shortcuts, earn up to 3 stars per route, take delivery jobs against the clock, hunt 40+ golden tokens, beat the daily run and spend your coins on new skaters, decks, wheels, trails, stat upgrades and stronger tricks.
