Build a Winter Resort is a free Simulation, 3D, Tycoon game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Build a hill from blocks at the Build Hill stand, then ride it to earn money for every segment. Use your income to buy new blocks, expand your zone and upgrade income at the stands. PC: WASD or arrows - move, hold right mouse button - turn the camera, Space - jump and get off the hill, hold E - interact, get on and off the snowmobile and jacuzzi. Snowmobile: W/S - gas and brake, A/D - steer, Space - handbrake. Building: WASD - move the camera, E/Q - up and down, R - rotate a block. Mobile: joystick - move, swipe - camera, hand button - interact, jump button - jump and get out of the snowmobile or jacuzzi, on-screen pedals and steering in the snowmobile.

About the Game

Build your own snowy hill out of blocks, ride it on a sled and earn money for every piece of the track. Spend your income on new blocks, loops and pipes, expand your building zone and upgrade income at the stands. Summon a snowmobile and race around the resort for extra cash, or relax in a hot jacuzzi that slowly pays while you rest. Collect diamonds, unlock skins, claim daily rewards and rebirth to grow even faster. Can you build the most profitable hill on the mountain?
