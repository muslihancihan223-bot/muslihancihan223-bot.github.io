How to Play

Drop fruits into the board. When two identical fruits touch, they merge into a bigger fruit. Keep merging to unlock all fruit types. Plan your drops to avoid running out of space. Score as many points as possible before the board fills up!

About the Game

Welcome to Fruit Fusion, the ultimate fruit-merging puzzle experience! Drop and combine cherries, lemons, oranges, tomatoes, melons, coconuts, pumpkins, and watermelons to create bigger, juicier fruits. Keep merging to unlock massive combos and earn record-breaking points!
