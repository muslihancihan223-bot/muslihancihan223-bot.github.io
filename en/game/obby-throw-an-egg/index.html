Obby Throw an Egg is a free Adventure, 3D, Multiplayer game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Move with WASD and look with the mouse. Click or press R to throw and release in the white zone for a perfect throw. Lift the dumbbell (Space in the green zone) to gain Strength. Run your egg home past the boss, place it on your farm with E and hatch it. On phones use the joystick and the on-screen buttons. Booster pads near the spawn give x2 Speed, x2 Strength or a rare egg for a short video.

About the Game

Throw your egg as far as you can across ten biomes: the further it lands, the rarer the egg. Run it home before the biome boss catches you, hatch it on your farm and collect creatures that earn cash. Train Strength, unlock auras and trails, and play online with other players.
