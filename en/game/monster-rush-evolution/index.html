How to Play

Drag anywhere on the screen (or use WASD / arrow keys) to move. Eat anything whose number is smaller than yours. Tap the ability button or press Space to use your form's ability. When a mutation draft appears, tap a card (or press 1–3).

About the Game

Start as a small monster on a kingdom island and grow by eating everything smaller than you: peasants, trees, rocks, houses and squads of knights. Rival monsters roam the same island — catch the smaller ones and keep away from the bigger ones. When time runs out, the largest monster on the island wins. Each time you grow, the action slows down and you choose one of three mutations. Shape a Predator, a Glutton or a Titan: 18 mutations in three branches, plus a golden apex mutation for every branch you commit to. Two rounds rarely play the same way. Collect DNA to evolve into new forms — GROKK, SKARRA, FROSTFANG and IGNIS — each with its own ability: slam, dash, roar or fire breath. Explore the kingdom map: 4 regions with 10 islands each. Every island has its own goal and three stars to earn. Stars open new regions, codex entries and star chests, and round modifiers like night, meteors or a gold rush keep every visit fresh.
