How to Play

Mobile: slide your finger left or right to move. Swipe up to shoot a web and climb to the next bar. PC: use A / D or the arrow keys to move. Press W, Up arrow or Space to shoot a web and climb. Reach the top before the hot chocolate catches you, then solve the puzzle to free the trapped marshmallows.

About the Game

The Chocolate Monster has locked the marshmallow family in cages - and only you can save them! Shoot sticky webs and climb up through 100 levels of candy bars: conveyors, ice, seesaws, fireballs, melting and crumbling platforms. Solve tricky puzzles to free your friends, collect strawberries, dress up your marshmallow with hats and outfits, and beat the Chocolate Monster boss in every world. A cute climbing adventure for all ages.
