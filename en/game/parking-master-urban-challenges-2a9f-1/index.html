How to Play

Your task is to park the car in a certain area without getting into an accident and doing it as quickly as possible. Follow the arrows to find a parking space. The progress is saved when the level is completed. To go to the next level, you need to pass the previous one. WASD or arrows — movement and rotation of the car. The space bar is the handbrake. E or ~ — open the game menu. M — switch the camera.

About the Game

Explore the dynamic environment of city driving in "Parking Master: Urban Challenges," an engaging 3D simulation that hones your parking skills. Maneuver through complex urban settings with lifelike vehicles, ideal for anyone looking to improve their driving abilities on mobile or desktop platforms.
