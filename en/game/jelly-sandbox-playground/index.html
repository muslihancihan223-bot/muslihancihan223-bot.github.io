How to Play

Choose a character, object, weapon, or vehicle and place it on the game scene. Drag and move objects, collide them with each other, and watch the ragdoll physics react in real time. Create any situation you want: place characters, add weapons and props, launch objects, and build your own battles or experiments. There is no required objective — you are free to play the way you like. Use the interface to select and place objects. On desktop, the game is controlled with a mouse. On mobile devices, use touch controls.

About the Game

Jelly Sandbox is a 2D ragdoll sandbox where you can create your own scenes, experiment with characters, weapons, objects, and vehicles, and enjoy realistic physics interactions. Place characters on the level, collide them with objects, launch items across the map, trigger destruction, and watch how every situation plays out. There are no strict goals or required missions — you decide what happens in the sandbox. Create calm experiments or turn the level into an arena full of chaotic battles. Every object reacts to impacts, falls, and collisions, so each experiment feels dynamic and different. Features: • 2D ragdoll physics • open sandbox gameplay • weapons, objects, and vehicles • destruction and physics-based interactions • create your own scenes and battles • simple and intuitive controls
