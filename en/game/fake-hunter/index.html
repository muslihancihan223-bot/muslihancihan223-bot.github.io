How to Play

Each customer brings one Blobby. The official model is shown on the card in the corner. Compare the figure on the counter with the official one, turn it around to check the back label, and decide before the customer runs out of patience: REAL to buy it, FAKE to reject it. Right calls earn coins and stars, mistakes cost money. Spend your coins on upgrades between days. Mouse / touch: tap REAL or FAKE (or drag the figure right / left). Tap the tools to turn the figure, use the UV lamp or the scale. Press and hold on the figure for the magnifying glass. Keyboard: Right arrow or D = REAL. Left arrow or A = FAKE. Up arrow, W or Space = turn the figure. Z (hold) = magnifying glass. U = UV lamp. P or Esc = pause.
