How to Play

Drag tiles to restore the picture. When two matching fragments touch, they automatically merge. Merged groups can be moved as one. Complete the full image to finish the level and unlock the next puzzle.

About the Game

Rearrange picture fragments, connect matching pieces and restore beautiful images. Relaxing and satisfying puzzles for all ages with endless level variety. Jigmerge Puzzles is a calm and satisfying puzzle game where you restore images that have been split into shuffled picture tiles. Each level gives you a new photo broken into fragments that you can freely move, swap and connect. Analyze details, match shapes and watch the picture gradually come to life. The game includes many themes: animals, nature, food, winter, cars, landscapes and more. Every level features a unique image, and the library of pictures grows over time. Tiles automatically “merge” when placed correctly, creating smooth and intuitive gameplay. Jigmerge Puzzles is perfect for players who enjoy relaxing experiences, visual challenges and the satisfying moment when everything falls into place. You can play endlessly — levels cycle through the theme, and new images are added regularly. A great way to unwind, improve focus and enjoy beautiful visuals.
