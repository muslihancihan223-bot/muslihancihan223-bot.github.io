Break the Egg and Run away! is a free Arcade, Adventure, Simulation game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Move with WASD, jump with Space, drag with the right mouse button to turn the camera and scroll to zoom. Click to swing your pickaxe at an egg in reach until it hatches, then hold E to pick up the pet and carry it home to your pen before the plot's guard catches you; Q drops it. Train your speed on the treadmill: a guard catches anyone slower than its plot's recommended speed. On phones: the stick on the left moves, drag on the right turns the camera, tap to swing, hold the prompt to take a pet.

About the Game

A Roblox-style egg heist simulator. Break eggs guarded by giant blocky animals, grab the pet that hatches and run it home before the guard catches you. Pets in your pen earn coins: spend them on pickaxes, speed training on the treadmill and a bigger pen, then head for farther locations with rarer eggs and stronger guards, ten lands from the Forest on. Fill the Index with 84 unique blocky pets, hatch rare mounts, break the Titanic egg and catch the luck event, when nearly every egg is a big one.
