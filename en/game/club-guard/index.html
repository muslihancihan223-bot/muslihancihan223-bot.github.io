About the Game

Club Guard is a simulation game where you take on the role of a nightclub security guard. Your task is to check visitors at the entrance, find prohibited items (weapons, alcohol and other contraband items) and decide who to allow to enter and who to deny entry. The game is characterized by unusual humor, absurd situations and freedom of action: you can search people, take away their belongings, use force or even beat them if you decide that they violate the rules. Game Features: Interactive gameplay : Check pockets, bags and clothes of visitors to find hidden items. Absurd humor : Each visitor is unique, with unusual dialogues and behaviors. Freedom of action : You can be strict, fair or even ruthless - it's all up to you. Variety of situations : From fights to strange requests, every day at the club is unpredictable. Simple graphics : Minimalistic style and intuitive controls make the game accessible to everyone.
