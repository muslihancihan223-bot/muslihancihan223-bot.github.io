How to Play

Goal: keep the model standing through the earthquake. Pick a material at the bottom, then click or tap one joint and then another to place a member between them (drag also works). Click a member again to remove it. Press TEST to run the quake. Earn up to 3 stars: no collapse, total cost at or under the star cost, and sway under the limit. Undo and Clear fix mistakes, the wheel or pinch zooms, and the hint button shows the weak spot or the full 3-star design. Works with mouse and touch.

About the Game

A physics building puzzle about earthquake-proof design. Each stage gives you a wobbly cardboard model on a shaking table, a budget and a set of materials: wood, steel, cable, concrete, dampers and base isolators. Brace the frame, press test and watch the real-time structural simulation shake it. Stand without collapse, stay under the star cost and keep the sway low to earn three stars. 92 stages over 10 chapters add aftershocks, soft storeys, long spans, towers, snow loads, wind and vertical quakes, plus a daily test with a cost ranking, challenge badges, a collectible shelf and a free lab with no budget.
