Master of Break an Eggs is a free Simulation, 3D, Animal game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Walk past the red line to the eggs and break one with your pickaxe (Auto Swing does it for you). Carry the pet that hatches back to your pen before the guardian catches you. Pets in the pen earn money every second: spend it on stronger pickaxes, pen upgrades and the treadmill, then head to harder zones for rarer pets. Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space to jump, left click or F to swing, E to interact, Q to drop, 1 and 2 to switch tools, hold the right mouse button to look around. Mobile: joystick on the left, swipe on the right to look, on-screen buttons for jump and actions.

About the Game

Break eggs with your pickaxe, grab the pet inside and run it home before the zone guardian catches you! A Roblox-style egg stealing simulator: hatch rare and secret pets, steal pets from rival bases, upgrade your pickaxe and pen, take up to four pets along for bonuses and go from the Easy zone all the way to Insane.
