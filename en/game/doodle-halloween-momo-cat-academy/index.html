How to Play

I. English: 1, Basic controls & gameplay To play, you use either your mouse (on desktop) or finger/touch input (on touchscreen) to draw symbols on the screen. Each ghost shows one or more symbols above its head; you must draw that symbol (or sequence of symbols) correctly to cast a spell and defeat that ghost. Symbols vary — simple lines, shapes like “V” or “ʌ,” maybe a lightning bolt, etc 2. What you need to do & how it plays out. Ghosts approach; as soon as you see a symbol above them, draw the shape quickly to “zap” them with a spell before they hit Momo. As levels progress, ghosts come faster and in greater numbers — the challenge is both speed and accuracy. At the end of each stage (or at least the final one), there’s a boss ghost — often requiring drawing a long chain or repeated spells to beat it. 3.
