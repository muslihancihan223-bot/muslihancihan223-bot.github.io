Play Hospital Doctor Adventures

Hospital Doctor Adventures is a free surgery, doctor, hospital, simulation game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Use the left mouse button on desktop, or touch controls on mobile. Tap highlighted objects and drag medical tools onto the marked treatment areas. Follow the on-screen hand hints, finish each phase, and use the phase arrows to move on. Complete all treatments and dress the patient to win. Choose Next Patient, then select an unlocked patient on the map. Use the speaker button to turn sound on or off. No keyboard is required.

About the Game

Step into a colorful cartoon clinic and help nine patients feel better in Hospital Doctor Adventures. This casual doctor simulation mixes hands-on treatment with playful mini-games: check symptoms, use medical tools, bandage injuries and solve each patient's case. Start with your first patient and unlock new challenges as you finish treatments.
