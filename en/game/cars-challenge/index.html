About the Game

Welcome to the game about cars, in which you can play in different modes compete with bots, perform tasks and tuning your new cars! Modes in the game: - Arena: Clash with other cars and survive in the arena! - Spinning Wheel: The obstacle wheel is spinning, your task is to get to the finish line faster than other cars and not fall into the abyss! - Hexagons: Hexagons fall down as soon as a car passes over them. Show your dexterity in steering and jumping to stay last in the arena and win. - Insane Track: On the crazy track you will have to go through a bunch of challenges and obstacles to get to the end of the crazy track faster than the other cars and win!
