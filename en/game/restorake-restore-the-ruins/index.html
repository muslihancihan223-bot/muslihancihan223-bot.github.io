How to Play

Sweep the rake over the overgrowth to clear it. Everything you break pays coins the moment it breaks. A day starts at 40 seconds, and Longer Day and the Long Summer elixir stretch it. It ends on its timer and always pays: there is nothing to lose. Slimes steal coins from the day while they live; pop them to get the coins back. Goal: restore each ruin in 15 steps, the Jungle Palace first, then Glowwood Grove, then Dragon Canyon. Press START RAKING! for a day of clearing, then spend your coins in RESTORE to rebuild the next piece, and in TOOLS to clear faster and earn more. When the next piece is paid for mid-day, RESTORE NOW ends the day and builds it. Every restoration also earns coins while you are away. Tips: break pieces quickly one after another to build a combo; a long chain sets off a frenzy that widens the rake. Grab coin trails, catch the lucky runner that dashes across, and watch for treasures hidden in the overgrowth. Tap the visitors on the grounds to pet them. When a ruin is restored, MOVE ON to the next: you keep one tool, your treasures and your elixirs, and earn Ancient Essence to buy more elixirs. The RUINS map takes you back to any ruin you have opened, and a restored ruin can be reset to restore it again. Controls Mouse: move the pointer over the overgrowth, no clicking needed. Touch: drag a finger over the overgrowth. The pause button pauses a day.

About the Game

RestoRake is a cozy incremental game about bringing three lost ruins back to life, one sweep at a time. Drag the rake over the overgrowth and everything under it breaks for coins: weeds, bushes, brambles, rocks, mushrooms, slimes and crystals. Each day starts at 40 seconds and grows longer with upgrades; it ends on its timer and always pays, so there is no way to fail. Slimes snatch coins from your day while they live, and pay them all back when you pop them. Spend your coins to restore each ruin in 15 steps you can see. Every restoration earns coins on its own, even while you are away, and many bring something new into your days. Restore a ruin to open the next: Jungle Palace: raise the pillars, mend the guardian statues and put the roof back on. Glowwood Grove: free the great tree from its dead vines and light the grove back up. Dragon Canyon: dig a buried dragon out of the sand, bone by bone, under the moon. Upgrade six tools, chain quick clears into combos and frenzies, catch the lucky runners, pop the Slime King, follow coin trails and find 72 hidden treasures that boost every coin for good.
