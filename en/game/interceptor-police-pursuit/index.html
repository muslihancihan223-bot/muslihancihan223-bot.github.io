How to Play

The car accelerates automatically. Desktop: - A / D or Left / Right arrows: Steer. - S, Down arrow, or Shift: Brake. - Space: Boost. - F: Fire pulse in Interceptor mode. - P or Esc: Pause. Mobile: - Use the on-screen steering, brake, boost, and pulse buttons. - Tap the pause button to pause.

About the Game

Take the wheel in Interceptor: Police Pursuit, a 3D police chase game with bold comic-style graphics. Chase suspects through traffic, avoid road hazards, and stop them before time runs out. Use speed and boost in Sprint mode, ram enemy vehicles in Heavy mode, or line up pulse shots in Interceptor mode. Complete cases, earn rewards, and upgrade your police cars for harder pursuits.
