How to Play

Tap or click clothes in the wardrobe to put them on, and pick a color in the palette to recolor them. Press VS to start a fashion battle: you get a theme, build your outfit step by step, and the crowd scores it against a rival stylist. Win battles to climb the ranking and earn new items and hearts. Controls: left mouse click or tap to choose clothes, colors and buttons; mouse wheel or swipe to scroll the wardrobe.

About the Game

Style Senpai: Anime Dress Up is a cute anime fashion game where you create your dream look and then prove your style in fashion battles. Choose from 350+ clothes, accessories and face options: hairstyles, tops, skirts, dresses, socks, shoes, wings, glasses, chokers, gloves and hairpins. Recolor hair and clothes with a big palette, pick a skin tone, change eyes, brows and lips, and set the scene with a dreamy background. Win fashion battles to climb the stylist ranking and unlock new items, earn hearts for the shop and themed outfit collections, complete quests and play the Game Room mini-games.
