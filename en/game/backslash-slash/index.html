About the Game

Backslash Slash is a keyboard-first blade clicker that turns simple key presses into satisfying combo chains. Instead of tapping, press /, \, P or K to strike a human-shaped training dummy using knives, daggers, machetes and more. Each key corresponds to different attack timing and combo opportunities — master the rhythm to unlock special multi-hit moves and new weapons as you level up. Key features: Keyboard controls: intuitive use of /, \, P, K for fast-paced slashing. Weapon variety: each blade (knife, dagger, machete) has unique speed and combo patterns. Combo progression: build and maintain combo chains to multiply damage and unlock advanced techniques. Level-based unlocks: reach higher levels to gain access to new combos, weapons and passive upgrades. Upgrade system: improve attack speed, combo window, crit chance and weapon power. Focused, stylized visuals emphasizing skill and progression.
