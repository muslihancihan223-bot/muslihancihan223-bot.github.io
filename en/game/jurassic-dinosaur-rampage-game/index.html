About the Game

Play as a hungry dinosaur on a wild mission! In each level, you get a task to complete eat animals, smash environment props, and devour everything in your path. But beware: a giant dinosaur roams the map, hunting for you. If it spots you, it will eat you and the level fails! Stay hidden, move smart, and finish your task to unlock the next level.
