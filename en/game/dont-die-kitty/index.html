About the Game

Don’t Die, Kitty! is a cute action roguelite about one very brave cat. A grumpy green dragon has stolen your favourite human and flown off with her in a cage. Grab your sword, tie on your red scarf and fight your way through the Kingdom to bring her home. Every run is different. Slash through rooms full of purple goblins, pigs, sheep and goats. After each fight, pick one of three powers: spinning Whirlwind slashes, Roll Bombs, flaming fireballs and more. Mix powers from different patrons into wild combos, then unleash a Big Meow shockwave that blasts whole crowds into the air.
