How to Play

Swipe on the board or use the arrow keys / WASD to slide all tiles. Two tiles of the same material merge into the next, more valuable one. Reach 2048 to forge a diamond, then keep going. The game ends when no moves are left. Undo (Z) takes back a move, the hammer smashes any tile. Your run is saved after every move.

About the Game

2048 Gem Forge is the classic 2048 puzzle where every merge turns your tile into a more precious material: oak, granite, bronze, silver, gold, jade, ruby, sapphire, diamond and beyond to magma, galaxy and star. Realistic cut-gem tiles, sparks, shockwaves, lightning and fireworks on big merges, 4 boards to collect.
