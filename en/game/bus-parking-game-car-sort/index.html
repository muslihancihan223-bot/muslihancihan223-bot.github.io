How to Play

🎯 Board every passenger before the parking slots run out. 👆 Tap a bus to drive it to a free parking slot. 🚧 Road blocked? The bus bumps back, so clear the way first. 🎨 Passengers board the bus that matches their color. 🚌 A full bus drives away and earns coins. 🔀 Stuck? Shuffle swaps bus colors, Sort reorders the queue, VIP slot teleports a bus. 😵 Slots full and no match? Restart and try a new plan. 💡 Tip: park the bus that matches the front of the queue first.

About the Game

Bus Parking Game – Car Sort is a colorful traffic puzzle. Tap a bus to send it to a parking slot, but only if the road ahead is clear: a blocked bus bumps back! Passengers wait in a color-coded line and board the bus that matches their color, so choose carefully which bus goes first. Plan every move and never let the parking slots fill up. Use Shuffle, Sort and the VIP slot when you are stuck, earn coins for every full bus and enjoy new scenery as you win. Easy to learn, hard to put down!
