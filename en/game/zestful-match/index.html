How to Play

Tap or click on any fruit tile to move it into your tray at the bottom. Match 3 identical tiles in the tray to remove them and earn points. The tray has a maximum size of 7 tiles. If it fills up without any matches, you lose! Clear all the tiles from the game board to win the level. Use Undo to revert your move, Hint to find a match, or Shuffle to rearrange the board if you are stuck. Climb the Global Leaderboard by clearing levels and achieving high scores!
