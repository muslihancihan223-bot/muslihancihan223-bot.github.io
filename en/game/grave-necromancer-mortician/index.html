How to Play

Touch the screen and guide the necromancer with the joystick that appears beneath your finger. Walk onto marked zones to construct buildings, take orders and complete tasks. For a funeral, dig a grave, bring the coffin, cover the grave and place a gravestone. Carry an urn to the crypt and seal the niche, or bring candles to the altar, perform a ritual and trap the spirit in a jar. Collect coins at the counters, souls above completed graves and crystals from rituals. Spend your resources on new structures, necromancer upgrades and skeleton helpers. Your assistants can take orders, dig graves, carry supplies and carve gravestones on their own. Raise the graveyard level to unlock new locations. There is no way to lose, so grow your business at your own pace.

About the Game

You're a young necromancer in a huge purple hat, running the night shift at a very unusual business: a cozy graveyard. Take orders from a very unusual crowd, grow your idle tycoon, and let skeletons handle the heavy lifting. How to play ⚰ Take orders from ghosts, a zombie, a widow, a vampire, a headless knight and a witch. Customers pay up front, wait patiently and eventually fall asleep in line. 🪦 Complete funerals from the first shovel of dirt to the gravestone, place urns in the crypt, and perform altar rituals. Finished jobs bring coins and souls; rituals also award a crystal. ⚒ Build one step at a time: walk onto a zone, invest resources and watch 14 structures rise from the ground in dust and sparks. Play vertically with one finger—the joystick appears wherever you touch. ☠ Hire skeletons with souls. A gravedigger, carrier, stonemason and registrar take over the routine while you manage the cemetery and collect glowing souls. Features 🦴 Skeletons work on their own: digging, carrying, carving gravestones and taking orders.
