How to Play

🎮 On a computer, use the arrow keys: up and down to move, left and right to turn. On phones and tablets, use the on-screen joystick. 🧹 Drive over dirty floors and collect litter. Your vacuum cleans automatically! 🔋 Head to the charging pad when the battery runs low. Visit the emptying station when the dustbin is full. 🔧 Drive to the upgrade station and spend your coins on speed, battery and bin capacity. 📷 Change your view with the camera button. On a phone, stop the vacuum completely before dragging your finger to look around. 🏆 Clean 100% of the floor and empty the dustbin to finish the job and claim your reward.

About the Game

🐱 Who says cleaning is boring? Especially when a cat hops onto your vacuum! Drive a robot vacuum, give your furry passenger a ride and watch dusty floors become spotless. 🏠 Explore the living room, bedroom and kitchen. Collect litter, steer around furniture and reach the dust under the sofa. Every clean trail brings you closer to 100%! 🔋 Watch your battery and dustbin: return to the charging pad and empty the collected dirt. Earn coins to upgrade your vacuum's speed, battery and bin capacity in the workshop. Unlock new vacuums and choose your favorite! 🎵 Relaxing music, conversations between a dad and his daughter, and a cozy home atmosphere make cleaning a pleasant break. Tidy up, earn a reward and start the next cleaning job with your upgrades intact. Hop in and take your fluffy passenger for a spin - a cleaner home is just a ride away!
