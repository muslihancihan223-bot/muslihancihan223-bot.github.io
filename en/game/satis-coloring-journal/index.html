How to Play

🎯 Finish each picture with the best accuracy you can. ✏️ Drag the pen from the start dot along the dotted line to the end dot. ⛔ Stop at the end dot: drawing too far leaves an extra line. 🎨 Pick the color you think fits each part of the picture. 🖌️ Rub to fill the area, then tap Finish when it is colored. ✅ Right colors raise your accuracy. Aim for 100%! ↩️ Made a mistake? Use Undo to go back a step. 🖼️ Finished pictures go to your Gallery. Change pen skins anytime.

About the Game

Satis Coloring Journal is a relaxing draw-and-color puzzle in a cute notebook. Every picture starts as dotted lines: drag your pen from dot to dot to trace the outline, and stop right at the end! Then pick the color you think is right and rub to fill each part. Choose every color correctly to reach 100% accuracy. Color foxes, corgis, ducks, tasty snacks and holiday treats, keep your finished art in the Gallery and switch between fun pen skins. Calm, cute and so satisfying!
