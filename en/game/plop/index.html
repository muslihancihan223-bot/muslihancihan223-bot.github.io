How to Play

**Welcome to Plop! Fruit Merge!** Your goal is simple: merge matching fruits, create bigger fruits, and achieve the highest score possible! **1. Drop Fruits** 🍓 Use your mouse or touch controls to position and drop fruits into the glass blender. **2. Merge Matching Fruits** 🍊 When two identical fruits touch, they merge into a larger fruit. Keep combining matching fruits to unlock all 11 fruit tiers. **3. Create Chain Reactions** 🍉 Plan your drops strategically to trigger multiple merges, create exciting combos, and earn more points. **4. Use Special Power-Ups** ⚡ Take advantage of Ice, Pepper, and Rainbow specials to help manage the fruits inside your blender. **5. Activate BLEND** 🌀 Fill your BLEND meter through gameplay and activate it to clear smaller fruits, creating more space for new combinations. **6. Complete Daily Challenges** 🏆 Take on daily objectives, finish missions, and challenge yourself to beat your previous records. **7. Avoid Overflowing** 🚨 Keep the fruit pile under control! If your blender becomes too full, your game will end. **8. Beat Your High Score** ⭐ Merge smarter, survive longer, and try to set a new personal record with every round! **Ready to play? Drop your first fruit and start merging!**
