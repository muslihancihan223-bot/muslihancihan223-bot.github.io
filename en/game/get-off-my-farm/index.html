How to Play

Use arrow keys or WASD to move your character across the farm. On mobile, swipe the screen to move. Your weapon fires automatically, so just focus on dodging and aiming.

About the Game

Protect your farm from relentless waves of mutant pests in this action-packed defense shooter! Your quiet farmland is swarmed by strange and dangerous creatures, and only your guns and quick reflexes can keep them away from your crops. Blast through incoming enemies and pick up temporary power-ups along the way — rapid-fire shots, explosive ammo, health refills, or extra energy for your electric fence. Survive enough waves and the stage ends, giving you a choice of random upgrades: unlock new weapons, call in a crop duster airstrike, slow down enemy hordes, attract power-ups with a magnet, and more. As you advance, new heroes with unique styles and abilities become available. Every run feels fresh thanks to randomized upgrades and ever-growing chaos. Can you hold the line and prove yourself as the ultimate defender of the farm?
