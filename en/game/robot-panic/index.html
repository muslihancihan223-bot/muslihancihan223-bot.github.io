How to Play

Click (or tap) a skill in the toolbar, then click a robot to give it that skill. Get the required number of robots to the exit before time runs out.

About the Game

Lead a crowd of little robots safely to the exit! They walk forward no matter what – it's up to you to assign the right skills at the right moment so they don't fall, get stuck or walk into danger. 100 hand-made levels across 5 worlds, from a gentle tutorial to brain-melting hard puzzles. Features: - 100 levels: Learn, Easy, Medium and Hard - 8 different robot skills to combine - Classic Lemmings-style puzzle gameplay - Crisp pixel art - Progress is saved automatically
