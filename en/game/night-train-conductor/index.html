Night Train Conductor is a free Management game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

Management

How to Play

Move the conductor with the joystick on mobile, or with WASD or the arrow keys on desktop. Walk up to the samovar and the bedding cupboard to pick up tea and bedding. Icons above passengers show what they need: walk past their compartment to deliver the order. Collect coins at the compartments and stand on golden pads to buy compartments, cars and helpers. At the conductor's desk you'll find upgrades and the route goals. At stations, board new passengers and get back on the train before the whistle. When the train is complete, set off on a new route and spend its stars at the depot.

About the Game

A cozy idle arcade about a night train. Serve tea in glass holders and fresh bedding, collect coins and spend them on new compartments, cars and helpers. Grow your express from a single car into a long train with a dining car, bakery, mail car, greenhouse, cinema, observatory and more than twenty other cars, each with its own mechanics. Travel twelve lines from the heartland to the Polar Express, then endless round-the-world tours. The train keeps earning even while you rest.
