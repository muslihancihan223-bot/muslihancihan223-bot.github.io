How to Play

On a computer: Left mouse button — tap the cell (open) Right mouse button — mark a bomb with a flag Drag and drop - place a popper On a mobile device: Tap the cell — open it Drag and drop — mark bomb with flag Drag and drop - place a popper Game objective: Open all safe cells and mark all bombs with flags. To win, you must find and mark every bomb on the field without detonating any of them. Numbers on opened cells show the count of bombs in adjacent cells. Use this information for logical analysis and safe level completion. Be careful — one wrong move can end the game!
