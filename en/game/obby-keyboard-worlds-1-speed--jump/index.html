How to Play

Desktop • WASD or arrow keys: move. • Space: jump. Press again or hold to use your available midair jumps. • Mouse: look around. When the cursor is visible, hold the right mouse button and drag to rotate the camera. • Mouse wheel: zoom in or out. • Tab: show or hide the cursor. • Hold E near sneakers: buy or equip them. • Use the on-screen controls to adjust your speed and camera zoom. Use the on-screen buttons and sliders to interact, adjust speed, and zoom.
