About the Game

Cat and Granny is a chaotic first-person action game where you play as a clever orange cat outsmarting the new black cat Granny brought home! Cause mischief by knocking over flower pots, scattering objects, and scratching furniture all while making Granny think the black cat is to blame. Sneak around Granny’s house, explore destructible environments, and set up perfect pranks. Knock over lamps, plants, and chandeliers when the black cat is nearby so Granny blames him instead of you. Hide in boxes if Granny gets too close, or risk a stern scolding! Outsmart your feline rival, create hilarious chaos, and reclaim your spot as Granny’s favorite. Fun, simple, and addictive Cat and Granny lets you unleash your inner troublemaker!
