About the Game

Sky Balls 3D is an exhilarating two-player casual sports game where you test your reactions and reflexes by guiding a ball on a narrow track thousands of meters in the sky, sometimes with guardrails... and sometimes not. You aim to keep the ball on track and avoid obstacles, traps, and gaps. While high in the sky, you not only have to avoid falling, but the real challenge is to do so while collecting coins and keys to unlock new ball skins and sky-boxes. Enjoy simple controls, stunning 3D graphics, and a choice of 1-player or 2-player modes across hundreds of challenging levels. How to Play Sky Balls 3D Let's say that Sky Balls 3D seems easier than it looks, and in the first few rounds, it's actually possible to coast through to the end without finding that perfect balance to avoid getting cast off the edge of the path. But once the obstacles start appearing, you will be forced to play tactically, so don't get too used to coasting. There are multiple obstacles to look out for, like no guardrails, narrowing roads, curves, and excessive incline speed, not to mention road barriers, which you need to avoid or slightly navigate around.
