How to Play

Move with WASD or the on-screen stick. Mine ore and gather materials, then spend them on buildings and upgrades. Repair and upgrade fences before waves break through. About the Game

Mine resources, grow your base, and hold the line against endless zombie waves. Hire workers, build fences, turrets and shooters, upgrade production chains from ore to ammo, and survive tougher bosses every few waves in this tower-defense strategy game.
