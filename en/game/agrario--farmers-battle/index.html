Agrario: Farmers' Battle is a free .io, Action, Multiplayer game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Steer with the mouse on a computer, or drag your finger on a phone. Leave your field and come back: everything inside the loop gets sown. Outside your field a furrow trails behind you: if someone drives over it, you're out. Drive over a rival's furrow to take their field. Space or the Dash button throws your tractor forward. P pauses the game. Sow the most in 5 minutes and the win is yours!

About the Game

Take the wheel of a tractor and claim the field! Leave your plot, plow a furrow and come back: everything you circle becomes yours. But watch out: if a rival drives over your furrow, your tractor is out. Cut other players' trails, defend your land and climb the leaderboard. Online battles with players from all over the world. Spend coins on new tractors, roof decals and boosts in the garage. Daily bonus, daily quests and achievements.
