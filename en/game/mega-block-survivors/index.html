How to Play

Survive as long as you can and defeat the final boss of the forest. Your weapons attack automatically. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump (double jump), Q or right mouse button for the hero ability, E to interact. Mobile: left stick to move, drag on the right side to look, a jump button and an action button that uses the hero ability and turns into a hand near chests and other objects. Collect blue cubes for XP and coins to open chests.

About the Game

Roguelike survivors action in a blocky 3D world: hold off endless monster hordes while your weapons attack on their own. Run, jump and dodge through Blockwood Forest, pick level-up upgrades and new weapons of random rarity, and build a different combo every run. Play as the sword-swinging Noob, Bacon with a paintball blaster, the Builder who throws bricks and sets up turrets, or Cool Guy with a lightning electric guitar. Open chests (watch out for mimics), climb short obby towers for free loot, charge power pads for boosts, and beat six mini-bosses and two final bosses. Spend Studs between runs on permanent upgrades and new heroes, climb the leaderboards and claim daily rewards. Short runs, easy to start, plays on desktop and mobile.
