How to Play

Desktop: - W / S or Up / Down arrows: gas and brake - A / D or Left / Right arrows: steer - Shift: wheelie (Shift + S: stoppie) - Space: rear brake (in a turn: powerslide; with W: burnout) - E: bunny hop - Z, X: air tricks; 1–4: tricks during a wheelie - F: get off / on the bike; Q: spawn bike - B: build mode; M: my bikes; C: phone; V: camera - Mouse drag: look around; Esc: pause Mobile: use the on-screen stick and buttons, and drag the screen to look around. A gamepad is supported too.

About the Game

Bikeburg is a 3D open-world stunt bike game. Ride a whole city, a highway and off-road hills on 18 bikes — from a BMX and pit bikes to e-bikes, dirt bikes and a 1300 cc superbike. Pull wheelies, stoppies, flips and tailwhips and chain them into trick lines that pay out in-game cash, race AI rivals, outrun the police and build your own stunt spots with kickers, rails and a halfpipe anywhere on the map. Upgrade, paint and wrap your bikes, dress up your rider and climb the leaderboards for the best trick line, the biggest earnings and the fastest highway race.
