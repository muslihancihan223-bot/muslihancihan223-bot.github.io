How to Play

🎂 Pick a party theme and bake your cake step by step. 🥚 Drag the ingredients into the bowl, then hold to mix. 🍰 Choose a mold, pour the batter and drag the cake into the oven. 🍌 Make a banana, blueberry or chocolate sauce for the frosting. 🍬 Tap candies, toppers and candles to add them; drag to move. 🗑️ Drag a topping off the cake to remove it. Tap Finish when done. 🎉 Celebrate at the party, then play the minigames. ⏱️ Minigames: reach the goal before the timer runs out!

About the Game

DIY Cake Making lets you bake a party cake from scratch! Add flour, eggs, milk, butter and sugar, stir the batter, pick a round, square, heart or star mold and bake it in the oven. Make a banana, blueberry or chocolate sauce, frost your cake and decorate it with candies, toppers and candles for Birthday, Christmas, Princess, Valentine and more. Then enjoy 4 bonus minigames: Cake Ninja, Card Flip, Sort Bridge and Delivery. Mix, bake and decorate your dream cake!
