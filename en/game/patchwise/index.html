How to Play

Fill the whole cloth with the patches shown in the tray below the board. Use each patch exactly once. Patches may be turned and flipped. • Drag across the cells to sew a patch. • Drag on from the end of a patch to make it longer. • Tap a patch to remove it. • UNDO takes back your last move. RESET clears the board. • Stuck? HINT sews one correct patch for you. Pinned patches came from a hint and stay put. Each cloth gives up to 2 stars: one for finishing it, one for finishing without hints. Collect half the stars in a world to open the next one. You don't have to finish every cloth.

About the Game

Patchwise is a calm logic puzzle about bojagi, the Korean art of sewing scraps of cloth into one wrapping cloth. Each board is an empty cloth, and the tray below it shows the patches you have to use. Drag across the cells to sew a patch, and fill the whole cloth so that every patch in the tray is used exactly once. Patches may be turned and flipped. 10 worlds, 285 hand-picked puzzles Every world adds one new rule on top of the last: • Patchwork: use exactly the patches in the tray. • Knots: a knot shows the size of the patch it sits on. • Holes: leave the holes empty. • Stitches: stitched cells must belong to the same patch. • Seams: a seam line must stay a border between two patches. • Buttons: every patch gets exactly one button. • Grain: a patch must run longer along the grain of the cloth. • Printed: printed patches cannot be turned or flipped. • Colored Buttons: same color, same patch; different colors, different patches. • Corner Knots: a corner knot counts the patches meeting at that corner.
