About the Game

The First Emberpyre A fast action roguelite where you descend through ten haunted floors to take back the kingdom’s stolen flame. Story For a thousand years, the kingdom of Aldmere burned on a single flame: the Ember. Every hearth, every forge and every cradle was lit from it. Now the Hearth-Flame has gone out, and every fire in the kingdom is dying with it. Maldrek, the Draugr Lord, has dragged the Ember down into the Deep. You are the Emberbearer, the last hope of Aldmere. Fight through restless graveyards and the realms below, defeat the wardens guarding each floor, and reach the Ember Throne to reclaim the flame before the cold takes everything. Features Fight through 10 floors, each with its own biome, enemies and boss Defeat a warden at the end of every floor, then face Maldrek himself Choose boons to build a different run each time Open treasure chests and find hidden lore pages that reveal the story of Aldmere Spend the gold you earn on permanent upgrades, so every run makes you stronger Pick your hero and fight in a fast, responsive, hold-to-attack combat style Play on desktop with keyboard and mouse, or on mobile with touch controls
