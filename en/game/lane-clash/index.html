How to Play

Defend your fortress across five lanes. Enemies run toward your wall — tap a lane shield to spawn warriors (when cooldown is ready). When units clash, their power cancels out: the weaker is destroyed, the stronger keeps going with the leftover power. If an enemy reaches the wall, your base loses HP equal to that enemy’s power. How to win Clear each wave by defeating or blocking all enemies before your base HP drops to zero. Survive the wave to earn rewards, upgrade your lanes, and advance to the next wave. About the Game

Lane Hold is a casual 3D lane-defense game. Hold your fortress across five parallel lanes: tap a lane shield to spawn warriors, clash power head-on, and stop enemies before they breach the wall. Clear short waves, upgrade lanes and the base between rounds, and use Shield, Bomb, and Freeze when the pressure rises — no joystick, no chasing camera, just tense lane battles.
