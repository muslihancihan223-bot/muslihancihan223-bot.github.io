How to Play

🖥️ Desktop (Mouse) Select & Drag: Click and hold Left Mouse Button on any of the 3 available blocks at the bottom of the screen, then drag it onto the grid. Place Block: Release the mouse button over valid highlighted grid cells to drop the block. 📱 Mobile (Touch Screen) Select & Drag: Touch and hold your finger on any block shape at the bottom, then slide your finger to position it on the grid. Place Block: Lift your finger when the block aligns over open grid cells.
