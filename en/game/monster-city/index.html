How to Play

Touch: drag on the left side to walk. PUNCH and SMASH attack. JUMP and GRAB work as before, and tapping GRAB again throws. The orange button fires the special, and the round arrows turn the camera. Combos: PUNCH PUNCH PUNCH (One-Two Hook), PUNCH PUNCH SMASH (Uppercut), SMASH SMASH (Hammer Fist). In the air, SMASH gives a Ground Pound and PUNCH a Flying Punch. Holding something, SMASH gives an Overhead Slam. Keyboard and mouse: WASD or the arrow keys walk. J or left-click punches, K or right-click smashes, Space jumps, L or F grabs, I fires the special, and Q and E turn the camera. The combos work the same way. Goal: level the city, finish the three goals for a bonus, and earn up to five stars. HOW TO PLAY on the title screen replays the tutorial.

About the Game

You are the giant monster, and the city is yours to flatten. Monster City is a 3D arcade destruction game: pick one of five monsters, pick a city, and get smashing. Punch towers until they crumble, pick up cars, trees and even boats and hurl them, and let rip with each monster's special attack: a ground slam, fire breath, an eye laser, a sonic screech or a plasma blast. Keep the destruction coming to build a combo, finish the round's three goals for a bonus, and level every block to earn up to five stars. Three cities are laid out differently every time you play, there is a new Daily Smash every day, and ten ranks to climb with extra skins for every monster. It is cartoon destruction with no gore: the townsfolk run for it, and nobody gets hurt.
