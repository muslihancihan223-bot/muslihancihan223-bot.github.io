How to Play

Goal: get rich by flipping things - buy low, sell high and save up for a penthouse. Your seller reputation opens new places in town. Desktop: WASD walk, Shift run, mouse look, E action, Space jump, Tab phone, B bag, M map, hold G weapons, P pause. Phones and tablets: the left side of the screen moves, the right side looks around, action buttons are on screen. The phone has listings, chats with buyers, the map, tasks, the garage and plates.

About the Game

Open-world reseller simulator in a sunny town by the ocean. Find things and bargains, list them on your phone, haggle with buyers in chats and meet them to close the deal. Repair broken items and sell them for many times more. Roll lucky licence plates at the plate office: hold the rare characters, re-issue the rest, then sell the plate to collectors or put it on your own car. Buy bikes and cars, flip cars from shipping containers at the port, open your own beach kiosk and save up for a room, a house and a penthouse in the tallest tower. Explore the beach, the port, a mountain road, an airfield and hidden stashes. Single player.
