How to Play

Break every glowing rune to crack the egg open, then fire a fireball through the gap to hatch your dragon. Mouse: click and drag to aim, the dragon fires automatically. Touch: touch and drag to aim. P or Esc pauses, R restarts the level. Hatch before the frost timer runs out to earn 3 stars.

About the Game

Hatchfire is a cute dragon brick breaker game where you bounce fireballs to break your baby dragon out of its egg. Aim, fire and watch your fireballs ricochet around the inside of the shell, smashing blocks, setting off bombs and collecting power gems. Break every glowing rune to crack the egg open, then fire your way out to hatch! With 200 handcrafted levels across 5 colorful worlds, Hatchfire mixes the classic brick breaker formula with relaxing puzzle strategy. It's easy to pick up and satisfying to master, whether you have two minutes or an hour. Features: - 200 levels across 5 worlds: Fire Mountain, Frost Peaks, Forest Grove, Storm Sky and Golden Dunes - Bouncing fireball gameplay: aim once and watch the chain reactions - Power gems: Multi-Ball adds a fireball, Magnet widens your catch zone, Turbo speeds things up - Bombs that blow whole sections apart, tough stone and brick, and unbreakable iron - Spinning rings and hard eggs for players who want a challenge - Earn up to 3 stars per level by hatching before the frost timer runs out - Collect 4 baby dragons in your Dragon Book: Tangerine, Blossom, Lilac and Clover - Plays on desktop and mobile, in landscape or portrait - Pro
