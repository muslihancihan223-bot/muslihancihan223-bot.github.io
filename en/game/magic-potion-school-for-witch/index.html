How to Play

Brew Magical Potions! Enroll in Magic Potion School for Witch, the enchanting puzzle adventure where you play as a young witch wielding a magic wand to cast spells as bouncing balls that strike and reduce numbered magical bubbles and shapes in a bubbling cauldron. Clear all enchantments before time runs out to brew potions successfully and conquer challenging levels in wizarding school! About the Game

Magic Potion School for Witch is an enchanting match-3 puzzle game where young witches cast spells with a wand to connect and pop numbered magical bubbles and shapes like triangles and squares in a bubbling cauldron, mastering potion brewing through increasingly challenging levels in a whimsical wizarding academy.
