How to Play

Draw one line with your finger or the mouse. When you let go, it turns solid and the bees attack. Keep your buddy safe until the timer runs out. Bees stick to the honey and add weight, and the honey dries after a few bees. Use less ink to earn more stars. If you're stuck, a hint appears after two tries, and after four you can skip the level. You can't draw inside the red ring around a hive, so stop the bees on their way to your buddy.

About the Game

Honey Line is a draw-to-protect physics puzzle. Draw one line of honey to shield your buddy from an angry swarm, then hold on for about seven seconds. The twist: bees stick to the honey and weigh it down, so your shape has to hold up under a growing swarm. Honey runs out, too: each line can only catch so many bees before it dries. 60 levels in three worlds (Meadow, Forest, Dusk) add acorns, thorns, seesaws, heavy bumblebees and fast wasps. After that come endless Remix levels and a new daily level every day. Win honey jars to dress your buddy in 6 colours and 9 hats and to change the colour of your honey line.
