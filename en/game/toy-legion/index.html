Toy Legion is a free Kids, 3D, Merge game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

- Desktop: mouse. Click a recruit button to buy a soldier; drag a soldier with the left mouse button onto its twin to merge, onto another soldier to swap, or onto an empty cell to move it. Click BATTLE! to fight, click the big round ability button when its ring is full. P pauses / resumes a battle (or use the pause button), the x1/x2 button changes the battle speed. - Mobile: tap the buttons; drag soldiers with one finger.

About the Game

Recruit little plastic soldiers, merge twins into tougher tiers on a 5x4 grid and send your toy army marching against wind-up tin robots and giant toy bosses. The wind-up robots are invading the playroom! Recruit toy soldiers with coins, drag two identical soldiers together to merge them into a stronger tier with better gear, and arrange your formation on the 5x4 deployment grid: tough fighters in front, shooters in the middle, cork cannons at the back. Press BATTLE! and watch your legion march down the play-mat lane and fight three robot squads in a row. Fire your commander ability at the right moment to turn the battle around. - 5 soldier classes (Brawler, Blaster, Guardian, Cannoneer, Medic) x 5 tiers (Recruit to Legend), each tier with its own 3D model and gear - 40 campaign levels in 4 toy worlds: Bedroom Carpet, Study Desk, Sandbox and the moonlit Attic - Crowned Champion robots and 4 giant toy bosses with telegraphed special attacks - 3 commander abilities: Paper Plane Strike, Rally Horn and Bandage Drop - Toy Box Siege: an endless wave mode with a boss every 10th wave - Permanent upgrades, a collection of 25 soldier cards, the Robot Files, 26 achievements and a 7-day dail
