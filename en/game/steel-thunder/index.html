How to Play

On PC, use the keyboard to control your tank and the mouse to aim and move the camera. Fire with the left mouse button or Space. Use the right mouse button or Shift to enter sniper mode. On mobile devices, use the virtual joystick to move and swipe across the screen to control the camera. Fire, aiming, ammunition, and boosters have separate on-screen buttons. In Team Battle, fight alongside your allies and destroy the enemy team. In Free-for-All, every tank is your enemy. Destroy as many opponents as possible and finish at the top of the leaderboard. Use cover, flank heavily armored enemies, aim for weak spots, and keep an eye on the condition of your tank.

About the Game

Jump into fast-paced tank battles inspired by World War II warfare. Choose your tank and enter quick matches. Fight alongside your team or face every opponent on your own in Free-for-All. Choose between accessible Arcade rules and a deeper combat mode featuring armor zones, ricochets, penetration, and damage to individual tank modules. Aim for weak spots, damage tracks, engines, turrets, and guns. Switch ammunition, use boosters, and take advantage of terrain and cover. Win battles, earn rewards and tank experience, unlock new vehicles, and upgrade each tank to match your playstyle.
