How to Play

Tap or click the floor to walk there. Hold and drag anywhere to steer the manager, or drag quickly to move the map. Tap the yellow bell above a guest to check them in, and the broom bubble above a room to clean it. Use the menu buttons to build, hire staff and decorate. On desktop, arrow keys or WASD also move the manager. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard.

About the Game

Cat Hotel 24/7 is a cozy isometric hotel game where you are the cat manager. Tap the yellow bell to check guests in, walk over to clean rooms when the broom bubble pops up, and get paid when guests check out. Build new rooms on empty plots, hire a receptionist and a housekeeper, open a cafe, spa, pool and more, remodel the lobby in three styles, decorate rooms with new floors and wallpaper, and meet 24 different cat guests for your album. Chat with your cat guests and help the neighbors with little story requests delivered by letter.
