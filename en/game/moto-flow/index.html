How to Play

Hold the gas (Right Arrow, or the right half of the screen) to ride the hills. In the air, Left Arrow (left half of the screen) leans back for a backflip, and holding both stretches you into a Superman. Land on both wheels for a perfect landing and a turbo. Ride as far as you can, collect coins and spend them on bikes, stages and tuning. Space or Enter rides again, M mutes.

About the Game

Moto Flow is a physics motorbike game about rhythm. Hold the gas over endless hills, lean back for backflips, stretch out into a Superman and nail the landing. Every perfect landing fires a turbo and builds your combo. Six bikes ride differently, from the MX dirt bike to the legendary ONI-X with its awakening mode and the DELI-50, a delivery moped that starts as the slowest bike and gets faster with every perfect landing. The engine sound is built from real motorcycle recordings. Race up to three other riders live, or play Survival, where the last place drops out every 25 seconds and item boxes can turn it around. Bots fill any empty seats.
