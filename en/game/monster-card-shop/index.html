How to Play

Tap the button above a shelf to restock packs. Tap the till to serve customers: swipe the card, or give the right change. Tap trash and spills to clean them. Spend coins on upgrades, new shelves, staff and shop expansions. Open packs to fill your album: every new card and every completed set boosts your income. Join auctions and tournaments when they open (watch the event button), grade duplicates in the lab, and follow the quests for rewards. Come back every day for free gifts.

About the Game

Run your own trading card shop! Name your shop, stock the shelves with booster packs and ring up every customer yourself: swipe their card or count out the change. Open packs to collect 64 adorable monsters across four worlds (Forest, Ocean, Volcano and Galaxy), from cute common critters to shiny legendaries. Keep the shop clean, hire a cashier, a stocker and a cleaner, and expand your store as it grows. Bid against collectors in live auctions, send your best cards to the grading lab for a GEM MINT 10, trade with visiting traders and battle with your strongest monsters in the Monster Cup. Your shop keeps earning even while you are away.
