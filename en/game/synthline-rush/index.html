How to Play

Drive to a track in the paddock and cross the green line to start a lap. Finish the lap as fast as you can to set a best time, then beat your own ghost to earn more Watts. Spend Watts on new tracks and faster cars in the garage and shop. Controls: W A S D or arrow keys to drive, Space to handbrake, Shift for nitro, hold R to reset the car, G to switch the guide line target, M to mute, P to pause. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

About the Game

SYNTHLINE RUSH is a neon synthwave arcade racer. Cruise around an open paddock, drive into any of the tracks and set your fastest lap, then race the ghost of your own best run. Every lap earns Watts, which unlock new tracks and let you buy and customise faster cars. Easy to pick up, hard to put down: just one more lap. Plays with keyboard on desktop and on-screen touch controls on phones.
