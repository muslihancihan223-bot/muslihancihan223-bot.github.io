How to Play

How to Play Bean Tower: Climb, Combo, Survive Climb as high as you can. Soon after you start, the tower begins to scroll up, and every 30 seconds it speeds up (HURRY UP!). Fall off the bottom of the screen and the run ends. Run before you jump: the faster you run, the higher you jump. Walls bounce you back at full speed, so use them to turn without slowing down. Combos: jump two or more floors at a time, again and again within 3 seconds. The longer the combo, the bigger the bonus. Grab coins, diamonds and power-up bubbles on the way, and spend them in the shop between runs. Controls Keyboard: Left/Right arrows or A/D to run; Space, Up or W to jump; Esc or P to pause. Touch: hold the arrow buttons at the bottom left to run (or anywhere on the left half of the screen); tap the jump button or anywhere on the right half to jump. Gamepad: d-pad or left stick to run; A or d-pad up to jump; Start to pause.

About the Game

About Bean Tower: Run, Jump and Combo Your Way to the Sky Bean Tower is an endless arcade climber. Run, jump and bounce off the walls to climb as high as you can before the scrolling tower leaves you behind. Speed is everything: the faster you run, the higher you jump, and chaining big jumps into combos earns huge points. Every 100 floors the tower adds a new twist, so each climb gets wilder the higher you go.
