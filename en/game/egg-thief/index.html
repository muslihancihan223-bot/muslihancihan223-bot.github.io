How to Play

Mobile: drag anywhere on the screen to move. A short drag sneaks quietly, a long drag runs. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, hold Shift to sneak. You can also click and drag with the mouse. Eggs are picked up and delivered automatically.

About the Game

Sneak up on a sleeping guardian, grab its egg and carry it home before it wakes up. Steal An Egg! is a 3D stealth chase game you play with one finger: push the stick a little to tiptoe in silence, push it all the way to sprint – and make noise. Common eggs lie at the edge of the nest. The rare ones sit right under the guardian's nose, and they are heavy. Every egg you bring home hatches into a pet that earns coins, and coins open the next zone: a yeti in the snow, a fire dragon on the lava, a snake in the desert, a gummy bear in candy land and an alien in space. Other thieves raid the same nests – while a guardian chases one of them, its eggs are yours. Rebirth to start a new world with guardians that patrol and watch, keep your collection and fill it up to Cosmic rarity.
