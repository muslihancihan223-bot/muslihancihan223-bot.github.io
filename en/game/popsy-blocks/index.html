How to Play

Drag shapes onto the board. Fill an entire row or column to clear it. As you climb through the campaign, special mechanics — locks, trains, safes, ice and more — add new twists to each level. Stuck on a tricky spot? Use boosters to clear space and keep going.

About the Game

Pop, drag, and clear your way through a world of candy-colored bubbles! Popsy Blocks is a cozy block puzzle with a satisfying pop-it twist — drag colorful shapes onto the board, fill rows and columns, and watch them pop away. But it's never just blocks: unlock trains racing on rails, safes hiding secret codes, soap dishes blowing bubbles, locks guarding golden keys, and ice waiting to melt. Every campaign level throws something new at you. Out of ideas? Marathon mode is always there for one more round.
