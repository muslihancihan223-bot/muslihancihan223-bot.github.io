How to Play

Tap the miner to dig down. Tap a nearby block to tunnel to it. Drag anywhere for a joystick. On PC: WASD or arrows to move, Space to dig, F for dynamite.

About the Game

Head down the mine and dig deeper and deeper! Every layer hides new ore: coal, copper, iron, silver and gold. Find treasure chests and blast through rock with dynamite. Ride the lift up to the village: sell your loot at the market, trade 100 silver for a gold bar, and get better pickaxes and backpacks at the forge. Your big goal is the Golden House. Bring gold bars and watch it grow block by block: first one floor, then a second with a roof and a diamond on top.
