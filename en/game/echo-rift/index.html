How to Play

Survive, complete each checkpoint task and defeat the island's boss to rescue Kaede. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift or Space to dash, 1-4 to pick a card, R to reroll, P to pause. Touch: drag anywhere to move, tap the round button to dash, tap a card to pick it. Your weapons attack on their own.

About the Game

Echo Rift is a pixel-art survival action game. Kaede has been pulled into the rift, and you are the Echo, a small ninja who follows her through ten islands to bring her home. Your weapons fire on their own: you move, dodge and pick a new power card at every level-up. Evolve weapons and combine them into supers. Each island is a journey of seven checkpoints with a different task at each one: charge a shrine, herd lost echoes, ride a mine cart, outrun a lava flow. Then beat a three-phase boss and lead Kaede to the door. 10 islands, 40 different tasks, 12 bosses, 10 heroes, Boss Rush and Endless modes.
