How to Play

Survive every wave with your squad. WASD to move, mouse to aim, left click to shoot, right click to aim down sights, R to reload (or refill at an ammo station), Q to kick, F to open supply boxes, X for the flashlight, scroll to switch 1st/3rd person. On phones: on-screen sticks and buttons. Spend your cash between rounds on better guns.

About the Game

Blocky co-op zombie survival shooter. Team up with survivor bots or friends online, hold out against waves of zombies and bosses across many maps, earn cash and EXP, rank up and unlock dozens of guns with attachments, armor and items. Easy to Hard map tiers, supply drops, vehicles and objectives.
