How to Play

Tap the bubbles containing words that belong to the same category. If the combination is correct, the selected bubbles will merge into one larger bubble showing the category name. Some completed categories can be used in later combinations, so watch for new connections. Complete every available combination before you run out of moves. Use boosters whenever you need a little help.

About the Game

Words and Bubbles is a relaxing association puzzle where you find words connected by a common meaning. Merge matching bubbles, reveal new categories, and use completed categories in further combinations. As you progress, the connections become more interesting and less obvious. Enjoy thousands of levels, satisfying bubble merges, achievements, and helpful boosters. Play at your own pace, sharpen your attention, and discover hidden links between all kinds of words.
