How to Play

Drive your e-rickshaw through the city and follow the map and distance indicator to reach each destination. Pick up passengers from the marked locations and safely drop them off at their destination. Avoid hitting pedestrians, vehicles, and other obstacles, as crashes can reduce your health or cause the mission to fail. Complete missions to earn money and unlock additional e-rickshaws. Controls: W / Up Arrow – Accelerate S / Down Arrow – Brake / Reverse A / Left Arrow – Steer left D / Right Arrow – Steer right Follow the mini-map and destination markers to complete each mission.

About the Game

E-Rickshaw Simulator puts you behind the wheel of an electric rickshaw as you transport passengers around a busy city. Complete a variety of pickup and drop-off missions across different locations, including homes, schools, hospitals, museums, hotels, offices, and shopping areas. Follow the route, drive carefully through traffic, and make sure your passengers reach their destination safely. Earn money by completing missions and use your rewards to unlock new e-rickshaws in the shop. Watch your health, avoid collisions, and prove that you can handle every route the city throws at you.
