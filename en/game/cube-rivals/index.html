Play Cube Rivals

Cube Rivals is a free 3d, shooting, first person shooter game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

3DShooting

How to Play

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to shoot, right click to aim, Space to jump, Shift to dash, Ctrl or C while running to slide, 1 / 2 / 3 to switch weapons, Q for the last weapon, G for your gadget, R to reload, Tab for the scoreboard, Esc to pause. Mobile: the left side of the screen is a floating joystick, drag on the right side to look, tap the big button to shoot (or enable auto-fire in the settings), plus buttons for jump, dash, crouch, aim, reload, weapon swap and gadget. Eliminate rivals to score. Health regenerates after a few seconds without damage. Aim for the head, use cover, dash to dodge and throw your gadget at groups.

About the Game

Cube Rivals is a fast arena shooter for everyone. Pick your loadout - an assault rifle, SMG, shotgun, sniper, burst rifle, LMG, rocket launcher or crossbow, a pistol, revolver, hand cannon or throwing knives, a knife, bat, katana or hammer - and a gadget like a frag grenade, landmine, med kit, barrier, smoke bomb or jump boost. Then jump into a lobby, vote for the map and fight. Movement is the heart of the game: sprint-speed running, high jumps, a dash with a short cooldown and a slide that keeps your momentum. Headshots deal extra damage, melee hits from behind are devastating, rockets launch you into the air and your health regenerates when you stop taking damage. Four modes: Team Deathmatch (4v4, first team to 50), Free For All (8 players, first to 25), 1v1 Duel and Gun Game (15 weapons, finish with the knife). Three colourful maps: Sunset Yard, Frost Lab and Neon Docks. Every match pays coins and XP - unlock weapons, hats, visors and weapon wraps, level up, grab the daily gift and climb the eliminations leaderboard. Real online matches: press Play and you are matched with other players who are online right now. Lobbies fill instantly: if no one is around, skilled bots with their own names, levels and loadouts jump in, so there is always a match ready, and real players can join a running match at any time and take a bot's place. Play with mouse and keyboard or on your phone with a floating joystick and auto-fire.
