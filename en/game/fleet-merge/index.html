How to Play

Tap a ship type to buy a squadron. It flies into your dock. Drag a ship onto an identical ship (same type, same tier) to merge it into a higher tier. Drag ships to arrange your formation. The top row is the front line. Counter triangle: Line ships beat Lancers. Lancers beat Artillery. Artillery beats Line ships. Keep Artillery in the back. Lancers hit harder from the side (×1.25) and from behind (×1.5). Press FIGHT. Battles are automatic, and you can tap to change the speed. Spend credits and cores on upgrades, deploy heroes, and come back for patrol rewards and daily missions.

About the Game

Command a fleet of starships in this merge auto-battler. Buy Line ships, Artillery and Lancers, then merge identical ships to build stronger ones. Your dock is your battle formation: every ship deploys exactly where you place it. Read the enemy fleet on the radar and put the right counter in front of each target. Then watch the battle play out on its own. Push through the Star Map and take on bosses and giant Arks. Earn three stars in every sector. Recruit heroes such as Nova, Valkyrie and Aegis, and learn their tricks in the Tactics Drill. Hold the line in Defense, hunt convoys in Cargo Raid, and challenge other commanders' fleets in Fleet Clash. Rise from Cadet to Grand Admiral and top the power ranking.
