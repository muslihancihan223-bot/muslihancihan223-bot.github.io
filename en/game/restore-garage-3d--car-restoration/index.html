How to Play

Goal: restore each car and sell it for more than you paid. Use the tool shown at the bottom of the screen: on a computer hold the left mouse button and move over the car, on a phone or tablet drag your finger over it. Hammer: tap or click on the dents. The bar at the top shows the progress of the current stage. Drag the background to turn the car and reach every side. On the paint stage, prime the bare metal first, then pick a color. When the car is finished, take the money or double it with an ad. Buy your next car in the Market and spend coins in Upgrades on faster tools and a bigger garage. Settings: gear button in the top right corner.

About the Game

Restore Garage 3D is a relaxing car restoration game in full 3D. Buy beat-up cars at the car market, bring them into your garage and turn rusty wrecks into shiny rides, then sell them for a profit. Every car goes through five satisfying stages: foam and pressure wash the dirt away, sweep a laser over the rust, tap out the dents with a hammer, prime and paint the body in the color the buyer loves, and polish it to a mirror shine. Spin the car around to find every spot. - 9 cars, from the tiny Dumpling hatchback and the Beetlebug to a ranch pickup, a retro bus and a 4x4 - 7 tools, each with its own feel and sound - Upgrade your tools to work faster and expand your garage from a shed to a dream studio to unlock rarer, more valuable cars - Match the buyer's favorite color for a bonus, or double your earnings with an ad - Calm recorded sounds and music, no timers and no fails
