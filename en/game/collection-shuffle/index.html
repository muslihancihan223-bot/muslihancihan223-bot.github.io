How to Play

1. Select a card and insert it into the top of a column. 2. The new card pushes every card in that column downward. 3. The bottom card is pushed out and returns to the position of the card you selected. 4. Use this circular movement to rearrange the board and group matching items together. 5. Complete every required collection before you run out of moves. Plan each shuffle carefully. One move changes several positions at once!
