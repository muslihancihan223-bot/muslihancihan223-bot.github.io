How to Play

You are given several colored rolls. Each roll is like a ribbon or band that you can tap to start unrolling. When you tap, the roll moves/unrolls and overlays onto the puzzle area. The goal is that the overlapping colours and their positions match the sample image you’re trying to replicate. The challenge comes from getting the direction and position of each roll correct. If you unroll a roll the wrong way, or overlay in the wrong spot, the final image will look different from the target.
