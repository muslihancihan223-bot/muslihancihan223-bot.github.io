Play Ants: Color Puzzle

Ants: Color Puzzle is a free Logic, Matching, Puzzle game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Click or tap a colored tile at the front of a queue to send out a team of ants. The ants automatically collect accessible blocks of their own color and carry them to the hole. The number on each tile shows how many blocks that team can collect. Choose colors available along the edges of the picture and keep an eye on your open team spaces. When a team finishes its job, its space becomes available again. Clear the whole picture to complete the level. Use boosters if you get stuck!

About the Game

Tiny ants, big appetites! Help a hardworking colony take colorful pictures apart and carry every little piece back to the anthill. Choose which ants to send next, then watch them march out and collect blocks of their own color. You only have a few spaces for active teams, so the order of your moves matters!
