Restaurant Day is a free Food, Management, Simulation game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Choose a dish from the menu and check which ingredients are needed. If something is missing, buy it in the shop. Then start cooking and complete mini-games: drag food, slice ingredients, mix, hold the button, tap at the right moment and follow the hints on the screen. The more accurately you complete each action, the better your result. Earn coins and stars, unlock new dishes, upgrade appliances and expand the restaurant menu.

About the Game

Become the chef of a small restaurant and prepare different dishes step by step. Buy ingredients, refill your stock, unlock new recipes and upgrade kitchen appliances. Each dish includes several stages: slice ingredients, mix food, knead dough, watch the timing and complete other simple challenges. Successful cooking brings rewards, stars and new ways to grow the menu. The game is a good fit for players who enjoy cooking, light arcade tasks and a relaxed casual experience.
