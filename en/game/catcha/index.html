How to Play

Collect as many cats as you can and complete the Album. Left click / tap to open gift boxes, buttons and menus. Drag a cat to pick it up and move it; release to drop it, or fling it to toss it. Put two cats that are ready for love (hearts above their heads) close together and they will make a gift box. Open boxes to get new cats, sell or buy them at the Auction, and level up.

About the Game

Catcha is a cozy pixel-art cat collecting game. Fill your room with cats, and when two of them fall in love they leave you a mystery gift box. Open boxes to discover cats of six rarities, from Common to Super, on collectible cards: HOLO, R-HOLO, G-HOLO, STAR and the rarest FULL ART. Buy and sell boxes and cats at the Auction, complete your Album, level up, and let your favorite cats stroll in the Garden. Over 100 cat skins, a room that changes from morning to night, and your progress is saved automatically.
