How to Play

✅ How to Play: ✅ ❗️ For Computer ➡️ Movement - WASD or arrow keys. ➡️ Jump - Spacebar. ➡️ Sprint - Shift. ➡️ Use abilities - I and U. ❗️ For Phone ➡️ Movement – move your finger on the left joystick. ➡️ Camera rotation – drag your finger on the screen. ➡️ Jump – tap the button with the person icon. ➡️ Use abilities – tap the two buttons to the right of the jump button.

About the Game

Four children have gone missing in the forest. You have 99 nights to find them before the woods claim them forever. 🔦 Life-Risking Search: Explore a vast, living forest that changes with every sunset. The children are scattered across different, most dangerous zones of the forest. You must find and lead each one to safety. ⚔️ Survival and Defense: Gather weapons, avoid traps, and seek shelter. Fend off packs of hungry wolves, rogue bears, and other dangers. Manage your basic needs: health and hunger. 🦌 The Main Antagonist — The Deer: The Deer guards the forest's secrets and considers the children its property. It cannot be killed with ordinary weapons. You can only drive it away, deceive it, or temporarily restrain it. 🌙 The Day-Night Cycle — Key to Survival: Day: A relatively safe time for exploration, gathering resources, and crafting. Night: Time of the hunt. Darkness awakens the forest's horrors. It is at night that you will encounter the Deer.
