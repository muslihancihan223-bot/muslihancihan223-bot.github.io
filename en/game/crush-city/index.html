How to Play

Drag to rotate the city, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a building to fire the selected weapon. Weapons have limited shots per city. Destroy 50/70/90% for 1-3 stars. Coins unlock new weapons and upgrade their power and shots. Keyboard: arrows/WASD rotate, Space fires, 1-9 pick a weapon.

About the Game

A 3D demolition sandbox on a floating city island. Every building is made of glass, wood, brick, concrete and steel blocks held up by real structural physics: blow out the lower floors and the whole top tips over, cracks apart and crashes into its neighbours. Rotate the city, pick your spot and fire. Unlock and upgrade nine weapons, from a cannonball and bombs to rockets, a wrecking crane, cluster bombs, a meteor, a tornado, an earthquake and a black hole. Gas stations explode in chains, cars fly, trees bend, and a cartoon crowd cheers every collapse. Endless cities from quiet suburbs to downtown towers and factories.
