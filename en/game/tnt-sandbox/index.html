About the Game

"TNT Sandbox" is an obby-style block sandbox where your main goal is to blow everything up with TNT. Earn rewards for explosions and building, buy powerful TNT, and enjoy the destruction! The game features spreading fire: ignite not only TNT, but trees too! Explore a randomly generated world with caves and bodies of water, but don't forget to blow it all up!
