How to Play

Cover more ground than the opposing team before time runs out. Splatting rivals clears a path, but painting the arena wins the match! Swim through your team's paint to refill your paint tank and recover health. Enemy paint slows you down and damages you. Painting also charges your special ability, so keep covering ground and use it at the right moment to help your team push forward. - WASD / ZQSD = Move - Space = Jump - Mouse = Aim - Left mouse button = Fire - Right mouse button = Use your secondary ability - F = Activate your special when charged - Left Shift = Swim in your team’s paint - Hold Tab = View the scoreboard - P / Esc = Open the pause menu Controller support available. Controls can be remapped in Settings > Controls > Configure keys.

About the Game

Splash Rivals is an online multiplayer paint shooter where two teams of four battle to cover the arena in their colour. Spray the ground, splash your rivals and swim through your team's paint to refill your paint tank and recover health. Choose from seven weapons, customise your character and powers, and team up with friends or create private matches for up to eight players!
