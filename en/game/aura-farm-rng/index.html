How to Play

Press Roll to receive a random aura and coins. Open Auras, choose My auras and equip a find. Spend coins on luck and roll-speed upgrades. Gather materials in the Wilds and use Crafting to make items. Auto-roll unlocks after 25 rolls. Desktop: R or Enter to roll; WASD or arrow keys to move; Space to jump; F or a short click on the world to attack the nearest enemy in range. Drag the mouse or use Q/E to turn the camera. Scroll to zoom. Escape closes a panel. Roll shortcuts work outside panels and text fields. Phone: use the left joystick to move, swipe on the right to turn the camera and pinch outside the joystick area to zoom. Use the on-screen roll, jump and attack buttons.

About the Game

Your next roll could become your favourite aura. Aura Farm: RNG is a 3D collection game where random finds shape your adventure. Roll for auras and coins, equip a look you love, then improve your luck and roll speed. Discover a catalogue of 65 auras with fire, lightning, wings and cosmic effects. Four special auras come from bosses or crafting. Gather materials, craft potions and luck gloves, and work through daily quests and weekly challenges. Head into the Wilds for enemies, chests and resources. A growing collection opens deeper regions, while time of day and world events affect which auras you can find. Unlock auto-roll after 25 rolls and keep the search going. Will your next find fill a gap in the collection or become your new signature look?
