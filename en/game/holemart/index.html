About the Game

HoleMart is a fun and addictive casual game where you control a growing hole inside a busy market. Move around the area, swallow objects, and expand your size as you consume more items. Start small by absorbing tiny goods, shelves, and carts. As your hole grows bigger, you’ll be able to swallow larger objects and clear the market faster. The more you consume, the stronger and bigger your hole becomes. Simple controls, satisfying growth, and smooth gameplay make HoleMart perfect for quick play sessions and endless fun.
