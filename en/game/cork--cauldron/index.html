How to Play

Goal: fill each customer's order shown on the recipe card. Pour liquids between flasks, only onto the same color or into an empty flask. A flask filled with one color gets corked and becomes an ingredient. Send the ingredients the recipe asks for to the cauldron, then pour the brewed potion into a flask to complete the order. Controls: - Click (or tap) a flask to pick it up, then click where to pour. - Click a corked ingredient to send it to the cauldron. - When the potion is ready, click the cauldron, then a flask to pour it out. - Undo, Hint and Extra flask buttons help when you're stuck.

About the Game

Cork & Cauldron is a cozy potion brewing puzzle. Every level is an order: the card shows the potion a customer wants and the recipe for it. Pour glowing liquids between flasks to gather each ingredient into a flask of its own. A full flask gets corked: send it to the cauldron, brew the potion by the recipe and pour it back onto the board to fill the order. The recipes keep growing as you play: two-step brews where one potion becomes the ingredient for the next, rainbow essence that stands in for any ingredient, and double orders where you decide what to brew first. The shelves fight back too: murky layers that hide their color, flasks of different sizes, frozen flasks and wax seals. Every order you fill earns stars. Spend them to restore an old potion shop room by room, from the dusty trading hall to the stargazer's attic and the tower at the top. Each order also adds a new potion to your Potion Book, with its own name and a silly one-line story. Fill an order without boosters and you get its golden edition.
