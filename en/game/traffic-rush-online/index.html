How to Play

The goal of the race is to drive as far as possible along the highway and beat the record. Try not to crash into other cars. The farther you drive, the more money you will earn. The game also features an online mode — invite your friends and play together, or meet new players. Spend the money you earn on upgrading your car and buying new vehicles. PC Controls: WASD / Arrow Keys — Drive C — Change camera Spacebar — Handbrake Mobile Controls: On-screen buttons / on-screen steering wheel. You can switch between control modes in the game settings.

About the Game

TRAFFIC Rush Online is a dynamic 3D driving game set on the streets of a large city. Change lanes between moving cars, control your speed, and prove that you can make it through heavy traffic without mistakes. Choose from three game modes. Drive with other players online, explore the city without restrictions in Free Ride, or dodge traffic and earn more gold in Traffic mode. Level up, expand your garage, and customize your car with new colors, wheels, and exterior parts. Responsive driving physics, multiple camera views, and detailed urban roads make every run exciting. Take calculated risks, choose the right moment to move, and set a new record!
