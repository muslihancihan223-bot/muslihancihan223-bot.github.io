How to Play

Drag a familiar back from the slingshot, aim and release to launch. Defeat all guards to win. On mobile, tap during flight to activate your familiar’s ability. On desktop, click or press Space. Each shot has one ability use. Drag the background to rotate the camera. Use the + and − buttons to zoom. Switch between Overview and Flight to choose your camera mode. Use fewer shots to earn more stars. Stars unlock levels and are never spent. Coins buy crew costumes. If you lose, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one extra shot, once per attempt.

About the Game

Launch magical familiars, topple sky castles and master every shot. Take aim above the clouds in Sky Kingdoms: Castle Siege, a 3D physics puzzle adventure featuring 48 levels across 12 sky regions. Launch magical familiars from your slingshot and bring down enemy fortresses. Each of your 12 companions has a special ability: dash through supports, split into multiple projectiles, unleash chain lightning or dive into a courtyard. Time your moves and turn falling towers into spectacular chain reactions. Earn up to three stars per level to unlock new challenges. Collect coins from victories and chapter chests to unlock distinctive costumes for your entire crew. Replay battles, discover smarter shots and complete your three-star collection. Explore floating islands, face increasingly challenging defenses and choose your view: stay behind the slingshot or follow every projectile through the sky.
