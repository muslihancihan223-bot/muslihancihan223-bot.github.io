About the Game

Break The Walls is a bright, chunky 3D wall-smashing simulator. You are locked inside the biggest live show on the air, and the only way out is straight through the walls. Swing your tool, watch the wall burst into real tumbling blocks, vacuum them up and feed them to Chrum, a hungry pit in the floor that spits out golden coins, bars and cubes. Buy stronger tools, level them up, break through 11 zones and ride the elevator up to daylight. Then Rebirth and do it again, faster and stronger. SMASH, VACUUM, FEED Hit the wall until blocks come flying out. Switch to the vacuum, suck the stones into your backpack, then walk up to Chrum and drop them into the pit. Chrum burps, shakes the ground and pays you in a shiny pile of gold. The deeper the zone, the more each stone is worth. UPGRADE EVERYTHING Open the Upgrade Book and level up every tool: damage, speed, area and more, plus the vacuum, the backpack and how fast Chrum eats. Every upgrade is a visible step up, and each new tool breaks the next wall bolder than the last. EIGHT STORY TOOLS AND FIVE SUPER TOOLS Go from your fists to a hammer, crowbar, pickaxe, shovel, sledgehammer, drill and finally the roaring jackhammer. Watch an optional rewarded ad to borrow a super tool for one minute: the katana, claws, laser, quake maul or cannon, each with its own area of destruction and its own animation.
