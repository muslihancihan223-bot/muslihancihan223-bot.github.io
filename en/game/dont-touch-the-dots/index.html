How to Play

Goal: collect all the coins on the level, then reach the purple finish zone. Avoid the moving red dots. Desktop: move the square with the arrow keys or WASD. Press P or click the pause button to pause. Use the mouse for menus and buttons. Mobile: turn your phone sideways. Touch the left side of the screen and drag to move the square with the on-screen joystick. Tap the pause button to pause. Rules: the red dots move in fixed patterns. Touching one adds a death and sends you back to the last purple zone you reached, and the coins you collected after it return to the field. While the square is fully inside a purple zone, the dots cannot hurt it. The first level shows a short tip on how to move. Earn gems for clearing a level for the first time, for the daily gift and for daily tasks, and spend them on skins and trails in the shop.

About the Game

Don't Touch the Dots is a challenging arcade maze game about precise moves and good timing. Guide a little blue square through 30 levels full of moving red dots, collect every coin and reach the purple finish zone. Purple zones are safe spots and checkpoints: touch a dot and you go back to the last one you reached, and the coins you picked up after it return to the field. Watch the patterns, wait for a gap and slip through while a death counter tracks every death. Earn gems for clearing new levels, for daily gifts and daily tasks, and spend them on new skins and trails for your square.
