How to Play

🖱 On a computer: hold the left mouse button and drag in the direction you want the hero to run. You can also use the keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys. 📱 On a phone: touch the screen and drag your finger where you want to go. ⛓ The ball spins on its chain by itself. Pick your route, keep your distance, and bring the ball into the guards. 💥 Knock out several enemies in a row to build combos and land mega knockouts. 🚪 Collect coins, take down the guards, and run for the exit. A boss waits at the end of every prison. ⬆️ Upgrade your hero and switch balls between levels. 🏆 In the arena, join duels or the free-for-all, earn cups, and climb through the leagues.

About the Game

The guards are coming, and all you have is a heavy iron ball on a chain. Get it spinning and the way out is yours. 🔗 Guide the hero with one finger or a mouse — the ball spins by itself and knocks down everyone in its way. 💥 Take out guards in a row, build combos, and land mega knockouts. 🏃 Break out of very different prisons — from a city jail to a space station. Each has its own traps and a boss at the end. 🏆 Enter the arena: duels and a free-for-all, cups, leagues, and a leaderboard. 🎨 Collect new balls and outfits, and upgrade your hero. 🎁 Claim daily rewards and fill the piggy bank with gems.
