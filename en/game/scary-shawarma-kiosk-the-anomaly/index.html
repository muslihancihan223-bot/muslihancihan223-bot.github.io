How to Play

Goal: survive each night shift, serve real customers and refuse the anomalies. Read the shift rules (Tab), look closely at the customer, then serve or refuse. Mistakes add penalties; too many and the Overseer comes for you. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, E to interact, Q archive, R establishments, T daily rewards, Tab rules, P pause. Mobile: left joystick to move, swipe to look, on-screen buttons to act.

About the Game

A first-person horror night shift in a roadside shawarma kiosk. Take orders, roll shawarma, pour drinks and keep the kitchen clean, but study every customer at the window first: some of them only pretend to be human. Each shift brings new company rules, and every mistake brings the Overseer one step closer to climbing out of the floor. Earn money, unlock outfits and piece together the story of the company from its classified archive.
