About the Game

BBlaster — Shoot. Hit. Win! BBlaster is a fast-paced 3D arcade battler set on a neon court. You pilot a customizable mecha — the B-Bot — that fires marbles automatically. Victory comes down to smart positioning, quick reflexes, and well-timed specials. When your marbles clash with enemy fire, both shatter and charge your Drive gauge. Meanwhile, the chaos puck ricochets across the court — knock it past the enemy goal line for a PUCK GOAL and heavy damage. When your Drive is ready, unleash an anime-style Special with a full-screen cut-in that can turn the entire battle around. A boss guards every 5th stage. Defeat them to earn gold and rare parts, then upgrade and fuse equipment in the Workshop to build your strongest loadout. The stage ladder is endless — and your progress, gold, and loot are saved automatically.
