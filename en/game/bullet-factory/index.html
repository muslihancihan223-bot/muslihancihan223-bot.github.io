How to Play

- Day: the factory works and collects materials by itself. Click the upgrade cards to make it stronger. The night starts when the timer at the top runs out. - Night: - Click the screen to aim. Move the mouse to look around. - Left click: fire. - Right click: sniper scope on/off. - 1 / 2 / 3: sniper / rifle / rockets. - Q: air strike. E: slow the enemies. R: repair the gate. F: location power. - Esc: pause. - Don't let the stickmen break your gate. Survive as many nights as you can! How to play (mobile): - Day: tap the upgrade cards. - Night: drag your finger to look around. - Sniper: hold FIRE to zoom, slide the same finger to aim, release to shoot. - Rifle: hold FIRE. - Rockets: tap FIRE. - Tap the weapon icons to switch weapons.

About the Game

Bullet Factory is a 3D factory sim and shooter in one game. Day: 15 seconds to build up. Your factory runs by itself. The iron mine and the powder plant bring in materials, and three press lines turn them into sniper rounds, rifle bullets and rockets. Spend the coins you earned at night on upgrades: a faster mine and plant, a stronger press, new weapon lines, bigger ammo storage, a turret, a tougher gate, and exploding barrels and mines. Night: hold the gate. Climb the watchtower and fight in first person. Stickmen dash from tree to tree, sprint across the open ground and pile up at your wall. Pick the right weapon at the right moment: - Sniper: headshots on runners hiding behind trees. - Rifle: stops the crowd in the open. - Rockets: clear groups at the wall and break shields. Features: - 9 locations (forest valley, desert canyon, deep mine, Antarctic ice, volcano, ruined city, tropical island, misty swamp, ancient temple), each with its own special power. - A boss every 5 nights: Orc, Yeti, Demon, Mushroom King and more. - Abilities: air strike, slow-motion, gate repair, plus each location's power. - Shielded enemies, exploding barrels, mines and headshot combos. - Achievement
