How to Play

Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. After a clear, blocks fall toward the glowing wall; new full lines clear again as a chain. Gravity turns after every set of three pieces. Complete the level goals shown at the top (gems, ice, score or lines) to win. The run ends when none of your pieces fit. Controls: mouse or touch — drag and drop. Keyboard: 1-3 select a piece, arrow keys move, Space/Enter place, Z undo, R rotate, Esc/P pause.

About the Game

Grid Hole is a block puzzle where gravity turns every round. Drag pieces onto the 10×10 board and fill a row or column to clear it. Then the remaining blocks fall toward the glowing wall as solid shapes, and if their landing completes new lines, those clear too. One smart move can set off a ×2, ×3 or bigger chain. Gravity changes with every new set of pieces: down, left, up, right. Every block has a little face that watches your piece, cheers before a clear and gets nervous when space runs out. - 100 journey levels in 5 worlds, with a saga-style difficulty curve: easy wins while you learn, hard levels every 5th stage, a boss at the end of each world and a breather right after. - Level 1 is a short guided tutorial that teaches everything in one go. - Collect rubies, sapphires and emeralds, crack ice and earn up to 3 stars per level. - Endless Classic mode with saved runs, a Daily Challenge with streaks, daily quests and gifts. - Boosters: Undo, Rotate, Swap and Bomb. - 9 themes, a living background, 7 languages, and a low spec mode for older devices.
