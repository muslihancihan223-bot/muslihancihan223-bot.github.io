How to Play

Score more goals than the other team before the 6-minute timer runs out; a tie goes to overtime and the next goal wins. Keyboard: W A S D to drive, Space to jump (press twice to dodge), Shift to boost, C to toggle the ball cam, Esc to pause. Mobile: joystick to drive, JUMP and BOOST buttons, BALL toggles the ball cam. Gamepad supported.

About the Game

Car football with rocket boost! Drive, jump, fly and smash a giant ball into the opponent's goal. Play 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 against bots of three difficulty levels, drive on the arena walls, wreck rivals, and customize your car in the garage: paints, wheels, boost trails and goal explosions. Level up and climb the ranks from Bronze to Nitro.
