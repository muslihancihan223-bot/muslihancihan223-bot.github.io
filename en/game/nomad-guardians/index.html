Nomad Guardians is a free Action, Puzzle, Adventure game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Drag your finger from the animals to the ger. Animals close to your line follow it. Your ink is limited, so draw short, smooth lines and go around rocks and trees. Save enough animals before time runs out. Tap the eagle and the fox to scare them off. Want more coins? Watch a short video in the barn.

About the Game

Draw a path with your finger and lead a flock of sheep, goats, horses and camels to the ger before wolves, bears, snow leopards and eagles catch them. 60 levels across the steppe, taiga, Altai mountains and the Gobi desert, plus a daily challenge and a survival mode.
