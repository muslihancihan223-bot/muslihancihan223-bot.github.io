How to Play

Grab eggs at the altar and carry them home to hatch pets. Step on the gold pad to collect coins and on the red pad to lock your gate. Steal pets from open nests and run home before the owner catches you; touch a thief to take your pet back. Press the two-friends button to play online: quick match, a private room or join by code. Desktop: WASD or arrows to run, Space to jump, E to grab or steal, drag the mouse to look, wheel to zoom. Mobile: left thumb to run, swipe on the right to look, tap the hand button to grab.

About the Game

Steal-and-hatch pet tycoon on a floating sky island, online with friends or solo against bots. Grab eggs at the Sky Altar, run them to your nest and hatch pets that earn coins every second, even while you are offline. Online rooms for up to 6 players: quick match, a private room code or an invite link. Free nests are kept by 5 rival bots. Steal pets from open nests and race home before the owner catches you. Lock your gate in time and tag thieves to take your pet back. 24 pets, 6 rarities and 6 mutations, pet training up to level 10, nest upgrades and rebirths. Sky events every few minutes: Egg Rain, Thunderstorm, Golden Hour, Gale, Snow, Rainbow and Night. A sky dragon lands every 8 to 10 minutes: send your strongest pets into the arena and hit it yourself.
