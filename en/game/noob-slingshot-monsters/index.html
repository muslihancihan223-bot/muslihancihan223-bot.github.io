How to Play

Aim, shoot the slingshot, wreck the monsters' shelters and clear level after level. Every level is a new destruction puzzle: find the tower's weak spot and topple it with a single well-aimed shot. How many stars can you earn?

About the Game

Meet Noob, a clumsy hero you load straight into a slingshot. Pull back the band, follow the dotted aiming line, and launch him at the block towers where cartoon monsters hide. Every level is a small physics puzzle built from glass, wood, stone, and TNT. Glass shatters at a touch, wood needs a solid hit, and stone takes two hits before it falls. Each broken block scores points, so a clean shot and a big collapse both pay off, and your shots are limited. TNT crates start chain reactions that can fold half a tower in one hit, but blow them at the wrong moment and the last monster stays safe behind cover. Bonus cards hidden in the levels give extra shots when you need another try, turning each stage into a mix of arcade action and light puzzle planning. Clear levels to earn coins, then spend them back in the main menu to build and grow Noob's house, a steady goal between the tower-smashing stages.
