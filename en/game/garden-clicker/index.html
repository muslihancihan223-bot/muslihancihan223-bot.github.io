How to Play

Tap the garden to water it. Buy seeds and choose what to plant: better plants pay more, and three of the same in a row pay extra. Upgrade your watering can, sprinkler and beehive, and open new gardens with the gold of your run.

About the Game

Water your garden and watch it grow! Every tap is a splash from the bunny gardener's watering can: crops swell on every bush at once, and ripe ones pop into gold. Spend it on new seeds and upgrades, and grow a tiny backyard into a magical garden among the stars. Features - 10 gardens, from the Backyard and the Farm to the Sky Garden, the Candy Garden and the Star Garden - 50 plants to grow, each with its own crops and mastery stars - Weather that comes by itself and mutates your crops: Icy, Stormy, Rainbow, Golden and more - Tap fast to call a downpour that triples your harvest - Fill a row with one plant for a bonus, and grow giant crops - Fill customer orders for extra gold - Start a New Spring to earn fertility, unlock helpers and pets, and grow the Garden Tree - Collect every plant in the harvest catalog - Your garden keeps growing while you're away - Daily rewards, quests and achievements - Plays in 12 languages
