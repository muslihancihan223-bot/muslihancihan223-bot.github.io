How to Play

Build Your Loadout! Before each match, choose items from your collection. They’re what make every run uniquely powerful and engaging. Play the Puzzle! Three shapes appear at the bottom. Drag them onto the board to fill complete rows or columns. Clear more lines in a single move for bigger combos and better rewards. The match ends when no remaining piece can fit. Activate Item Powers! Items trigger automatically or can be activated manually. Enjoy everything from simple score boosts to powerful active abilities like bombs, lightning strikes, or even an undo move. Earn Rewards! Collect coins after every match (with a daily cap). After a game ends, watch a short ad to double your coins or continue your run. Spin the Gacha! Spend tickets in the gacha machine to pull new gear. The more you spin, the closer you get to rare and legendary trophies.

About the Game

A classic block-drop puzzle that transforms into a true collector’s experience! Drag pieces onto the grid, clear lines, and enjoy satisfying combos. But the real hook? 32 unique collectible items with awesome abilities. Before each match, you build your loadout: pick up to three items that work together. One boosts your score, another lets you blast blocks, and a third changes the board rules. Every combination delivers a fresh, highly engaging playstyle. All items are pulled from the gacha machine. Use a ticket, pull the lever, and feel the thrill as a capsule rolls out and pops open to reveal your newest gear.
