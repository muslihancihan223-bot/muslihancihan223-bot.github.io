How to Play

Move with WASD / arrow keys (joystick on mobile) — attacks are automatic. Space or Shift to dash through projectiles, 1–4 (or F) for active skills, M for the map, Esc to pause. Collect XP crystals and pick upgrades on level-up. Avoid red circles and lines on the ground. Defeat the lord before the timer ends and stay inside the shrinking zone. Between runs, open chests, equip better gear and upgrade skills with gold.

About the Game

Survive the endless Horde as Ratmir, the last archer of the Storm Guard. Fight hundreds of enemies, loot chests, level up skills and a huge Path of Exile–style skill tree, defeat six bosses with unique trial phases and grow stronger with every run. Story, Endless, Daily Run and Armageddon modes.
