How to Play

Choose a playground and build momentum by moving the arms and legs. On a computer, the arrow keys control the legs, A/D control the arms and Q/E twist the body. Press Space to let go of the bar. Tuck for a flip, then reach towards the bar to regrab it. In mouse mode, the left button tucks the body and the right button arches it. On mobile, use the on-screen controls and buttons. R restarts the attempt and Esc pauses. Adjust the game speed and control layout in Settings.

About the Game

A gymnastics and street workout simulator with body physics. Swing a stickman on the bar, let go, do a flip and catch it again. Arm and leg positions change your momentum and rotation, so each trick depends on speed and timing. Try giant swings, regrabs and jumps between bars. On the ground, practise flips and landings, then link moves into combinations. Adjust the game speed to work out a difficult move. Earn experience and coins for tricks, and unlock playgrounds and character skins. Save attempts as replays, watch them in slow motion and share your combinations. Customize the controls to suit how you play.
