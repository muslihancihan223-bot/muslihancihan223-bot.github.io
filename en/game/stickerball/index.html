How to Play

How to play: 1. Aiming: Move your cursor to aim-the arrow on the ball shows the strike direction. 2. Power Charging: Hold LMB to fill the arrow’s power gauge. Release to launch the ball! Striking too early cancels the shot. 3. Collecting Stickers: After striking, the ball bounces off walls, collecting stickers, coins, and crystals. The more you gather in one turn, the higher your combo and bonuses! 4. Triggers: Collect special icons (magnet, x2, freeze, etc.) to power up the ball or alter sticker behavior. For example, "Ghost" lets the ball pass through triggers, while "Double Move" grants a second strike without new stickers appearing. 5. Buying Packs: Before your first turn, choose a free Level 1 pack. Each pack contains 100 stickers, with 10 appearing per turn. 6. Strategy Tips: Use "Freeze" or "Bonus" to increase the number of stickers on the field. Combine triggers like "Magnet" + "x2" for double rewards. Prioritize Holo-stickers - they grant x2 coins!

About the Game

Stickerball is a fast-paced game where you collect stickers using a ball with unique abilities! Your goal is to collect 180 colorful stickers across 9 levels by completing challenges and activating triggers that change the game’s rules. Each turn, 10 stickers fly onto the field from a purchased pack, but any uncollected ones disappear—act quickly and precisely! The game shines with 14 dynamic triggers: freeze stickers, double rewards with "x2," turn the ball into a magnet or ghost, activate "disco" mode, or control a dual ball! The more stickers you collect in one turn, the higher your combo and the more coins you earn. Holo-stickers, with their unique designs, grant double the currency! Play across 12 vibrant, randomly changing fields. Buy sticker packs, upgrade your collection, and strategize by activating triggers at the perfect moment to maximize rewards.
