Blocky Aces is a free Action, Arcade, Skill game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Survive as many waves as you can and beat your best score. Desktop: click, then move the mouse to fly (the cursor is captured); hold the left button to fire the machine gun at whatever is under the sight; P pauses. Phone: drag a finger to fly, and the guns fire by themselves at a target in the sight. After each wave choose one of three upgrade cards. A hit costs a heart; when they run out the run ends, and the coins you earned buy permanent upgrades.

About the Game

Voxel arcade air shooter built around endless runs. Fly through islands, desert, snow and city, shoot down waves of fighters, bombers, ships and tanks, and break apart flying fortresses. After every wave pick one of three upgrade cards: homing missiles, chain lightning, wing turrets, air strikes and more. Between runs spend coins on weapons, engine and armour, unlock 14 aircraft, collect a daily bonus and climb the leaderboard.
