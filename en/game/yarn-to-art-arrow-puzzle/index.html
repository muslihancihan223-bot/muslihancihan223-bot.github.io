Yarn to Art: Arrow Puzzle is a free Puzzle, Mouse, Decoration game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Tap or click a yarn arrow whose path ahead is clear. Matching colors fill active spools; other threads go to the shared spool with limited space. Complete colored spools to stitch the picture and uncover more of the puzzle. Clear all yarn to finish the level. Use optional boosters to add storage, clear the reserve or remove a layer piece. Earn yarn and hold the build button to decorate rooms.

About the Game

Free the yarn arrows. Match the colors. Watch a picture come to life! Yarn to Art mixes arrow puzzles with colorful yarn art. Find arrows with a clear way out, send their threads to matching spools and complete pictures. Explore layered mazes, collect artwork, unlock outfits and decorate six cozy rooms with earned yarn. Includes 156 puzzles, mouse and touch controls, optional boosters and rewarded videos.
