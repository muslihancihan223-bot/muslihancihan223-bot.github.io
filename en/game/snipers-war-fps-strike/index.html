How to Play

Take out the enemy team before they get you. Use the scope for long shots and aim for the head. PC: • W A S D – move • Mouse – aim • Left mouse button – shoot • Right mouse button – scope in / out • Space – jump • R – reload • 1 / 2 / 3 or mouse wheel – switch weapon • F – inspect weapon • Tab (hold) – scoreboard • P or Esc – pause Phone and tablet (hold your device sideways): • Touch the left side – joystick to move • Drag on the right side – look around and aim • FIRE – shoot (Auto shot also fires when your sight is on an enemy) • SCOPE – scope in / out • JUMP, reload and weapon-switch buttons on the right • PAUSE and the scoreboard button at the top left

About the Game

Snipers War: FPS Strike is a 3D sniper shooter where every shot counts. Pick your rifle, find a good position and take down your enemies before they find you. Four game modes, all played against bots: • Team Deathmatch – team battles where you choose how many players join and how tough the enemies are • Overpass – a free-for-all deathmatch: every soldier for himself, fight your way to first place • Survival – hold out against waves of zombies for as long as you can • Parkour – jump and climb your way to the top of tricky levels New modes unlock as you level up, and you can practice your aim on the shooting range. • Many maps: rooftops above the clouds, a castle, desert, snowy mountain, container yards and more • Sniper rifles, pistols and knives, plus skins from cases • Battle Pass, daily rewards, missions and a lucky wheel • Ranks, a player profile, and headshot and jump-shot stats • Plays on PC, phones and tablets, with touch controls on mobile Aim for the head and become the best sniper on the battlefield!
