How to Play

Jump from platform to platform and press jump again while airborne to flap your wings. Landing restores your available flaps. Your jump height stays the same, so gain levels to make more jumps in the air! Stop above a training fan to earn Power. Walk onto a yellow trophy pad to collect your checkpoint reward and return to the hub. Spend trophies on better wings and new worlds. Desktop: WASD to move, Space or E to jump and flap, drag to rotate the camera, and R to return to the hub. Mobile: Touch and drag in the lower-left area to move. Swipe on the right to rotate the camera. Tap the wings button to jump and flap.

About the Game

Spread your wings and take on a colorful sky-high obstacle course in +1 Wings Escape! Jump between platforms, dodge moving obstacles, and use extra wing flaps to reach distant checkpoints. Collect trophies, unlock new wings, and grow stronger with every flight. Key Features - Level up your flight: Earn Power while airborne or train above powerful fans. Each level gives you an extra wing flap. - Upgrade your wings: Unlock colorful wings that increase your Power earnings. - Chase bigger rewards: Reach farther checkpoints to collect more trophies. - Explore new worlds: Unlock tougher courses with new environments and challenges. - Rebirth and progress faster: Reset your level for a permanent Power multiplier while keeping your wings, trophies, and trails. - Customize your character: Equip wings and colorful trails to stand out.
