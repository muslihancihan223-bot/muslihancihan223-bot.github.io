How to Play

Controls: mouse, touchscreenControls: mouse, touchscreen Click (or tap) buttons and cards — this opens cases, selects items, and switches sections via the bottom panel. Case: press the open button — the roulette spins on its own, then choose "Keep" or "Sell." Selling: set a price — drag the slider or just click the desired spot on the bar, then press "Put up for sale." Buyers write to you in "Chats": reply by choosing from ready-made lines — say hello, ask about the item, agree, or haggle. The "Work" section is a clicker: tap the computer to earn money and level up your income. At the top — balance, gifts, and leaderboards; at the bottom — section navigation. Collect all items and earn more money than other players to become a king of resale!
