How to Play

Drag your finger or the mouse along the top of the screen and the fruit follows. Let go to drop it into the cup. Two identical fruits that touch become the next one up: blueberry makes a cherry, cherry makes a kumquat, and so on to the watermelon. Merges chain, so one good drop can clear half the cup. Swipe down to fling the fruit instead. It drives into a tight pile and opens a way through. Watch the line near the top. If the fruit stays above it, the run is over. Put the big ones at the bottom and leave the small ones room up top, so they have time to find their pair. The bomb can be dropped like any fruit, or tapped where it lies to set it off.

About the Game

Eleven fruits, from blueberry to watermelon, and every one of them has a face. They blink, squint when something lands on them, beam when they merge, and start to doze off if you leave them alone. The physics is honest: a dropped fruit rolls, shoves its neighbours and can set off a chain of merges worth far more than you planned. Stacking well beats stacking fast. Now and then a bomb turns up in the queue. It scatters whatever has settled into a jam and gives a crowded cup a second chance. There is a leaderboard and a personal best, kept in your Yandex account so it survives a change of device. A run is short, losing costs you nothing, and you will want to start again at once.
