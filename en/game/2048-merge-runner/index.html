How to Play

1. Tap to start running. 2. Steer left and right into runners with YOUR number to merge and double it. 3. Choose the good gates, jump the ramps and dodge the obstacles: a hit halves your number. 4. At the finish, tap to shoot the boss. The damage you deal is kept for the next try. 5. Spend gems on POWER (more damage) and START (a bigger starting number), and on hats. Desktop: - Drag with the mouse, or A / D or the arrow keys: steer - Click, Space or Enter: start, and shoot the boss - P or Esc: pause Mobile: - Drag anywhere: steer - Tap: start, and shoot the boss

About the Game

## 2048 Merge Runner **Title:** 2048 Merge Runner **Languages:** English ### Description Run, merge and grow into the biggest number on the track! You are a little runner with a number on your head. Touch a runner with the same number and you merge: 2 becomes 4, 4 becomes 8, all the way up to 2048 and far beyond. Any other number just turns grey and tumbles off the road, so pick your lane carefully. Pass through x2 and x4 gates, avoid the halving ones, jump off ramps onto the upper road full of gems, and smash number walls that are smaller than you. Saws, swinging hammers, spinners, spikes, narrow bridges and forks in the road keep every level fresh. A new obstacle is introduced level by level, and then they all mix together. At the finish line a giant boss is waiting. Tap to fire everything you have collected. Bosses are tough: whatever damage you deal stays, so keep coming back stronger until it falls. Beat a boss to unlock a bonus level packed with gems and coins. - Simple, satisfying 2048-style merging in a runner - 8 kinds of obstacles: saws, ramps, forks, number walls, hammers, narrow bridges, spinners and spikes - Giant bosses whose damage carries over from run to run
