How to Play

Use your mouse to select a fruits and then drag and drop makeup headbands, select lipstick, eye colors and many many other components to create a beautiful Makeup Fruit!

About the Game

Have you ever imagined giving a watermelon a makeover? How about adding a flutter of eyelashes or a swipe of lipstick to a juicy melon? In Makeup Fruits, your mission is to help these delightful fruits dazzle with beauty. It's all in your hands to adorn them with the perfect makeup and accessories for their stunning transformation! Click to select the fruit you want to transform into a beauty icon, then proceed to choose and apply a variety of makeup options. Explore a diverse selection of brands, powder colors, eyelashes, lipsticks, stickers, and more! At the end, you'll be able to take a "selfie" of your fabulous creation to share with your friends. You'll also earn "gems" as a reward for your work. These gems can be used to unlock exciting new items and keep the fun going!
