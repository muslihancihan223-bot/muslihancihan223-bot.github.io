Grow the Baby is a free Arcade, Simulation, 3D game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Click or tap a job to send the founder over and complete it for cash. Upgrade equipment or buy another business, then hire crew and assign each employee to one owned job for automatic earnings. Match crew specialties and promote them for better output. Use Toys to build collections, Goals to claim earned rewards, and Home to expand when you meet the cash and level requirements. Boosts contains earned tokens, optional rewarded ads and platform-priced purchases where supported. Collect offline crew earnings when you return. Progress saves automatically. Sound and pause controls are at the top.

About the Game

A very small founder. An increasingly unreasonable toy empire. Start with an empty wallet and a working Complaint Desk, then choose which businesses to build in a colorful 3D idle room. Walk over to complete jobs, reinvest in equipment and hire Teddy, Goose and six specialist employees. Collect 12 ridiculous toys, claim 28 business goals and expand through eight properties to Planet Ego. Crew keep earning while you are away. Optional rewarded ads and platform purchases offer boosts; all jobs, crew and homes can be earned by playing.
