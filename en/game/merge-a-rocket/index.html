Play Merge a Rocket!

Merge a Rocket! is a free Action, 3D, Merge game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

Action3DMergeIdle

How to Play

Walk up to a rocket to pick it up. Carry it to another rocket of the same level to merge them into a higher-level rocket. Press Launch to fire your rocket at the enemy base, or Drop to put it down. Collect in-game coins and open Upgrades to get better rockets and carry more of them. Activate the Shield when the enemy attacks. Use Rebirth to reset your progress for an in-game bonus. You can also watch an optional ad to boost your rocket's damage or double your in-game coins. Controls: PC: WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to look around, Space to jump, Shift to sprint. Mobile: on-screen joystick to move, swipe the screen to look around, Jump button to jump. Use the on-screen buttons for Launch, Drop, Shield, Upgrades, Shop, Rebirth and the sound toggle.

About the Game

Merge rockets and defend your city! Pick up rockets, combine two of the same level to make a stronger one, then launch them at the enemy base before it destroys your town. Spend in-game coins on upgrades, raise your shield for protection, and use Rebirth to start over with bigger in-game bonuses.
