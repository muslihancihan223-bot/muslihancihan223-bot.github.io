Cozy idle village is a free Strategy, Simulation, Cozy game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Drag to move the camera, pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom. Tap the Build tab to construct a building, then place it on a free tile. Tap any building to assign villagers to it; tap Villagers to manage everyone at once. Complete daily quests, unlock the tech tree, and collect what the village produced while you were away. Everything is playable with one finger or the left mouse button.

About the Game

Cozy idle village is an idle sim where every villager is a person: they have names, families and stories - they work, fall in love, raise children, grow old, and their descendants visit the family graves. Build houses, mills, fishing huts and workshops, assign villagers to jobs in one tap, and expand your land toward the water and into new biomes. The world runs on its own clock: day turns to night, seasons repaint the landscape, rain and storms roll through, and dozens of wild animal species live around your village - track them all in the in-game codex. Unlock the tech tree, reach new epochs, complete daily quests, and open the traveler's chest when it appears for bonus supplies. Your village keeps working while you are away: come back and collect everything it produced offline. No timers pushing you, no pressure - a calm, warm world that grows with you. Playable in nine languages.
