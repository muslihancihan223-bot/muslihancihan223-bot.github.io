How to Play

Choose an available vehicle and environment, then explore, attempt jumps, and test different crashes. This is a sandbox: set your own challenges and repair your car to experiment again. Desktop controls: WASD or arrow keys - drive Space - handbrake Left Shift - boost R - repair the vehicle C - change camera Mouse - look around F - enter or exit a vehicle Mobile controls: Use the on-screen driving and action buttons. Drag on an empty part of the screen to rotate the camera. Pinch with two fingers to adjust the view.

About the Game

Turn every drive into a crash experiment in Realistic Car Destruction Sandbox. Explore steep downhill tracks, dirt roads, cliffside routes, and winter terrain, or test your vehicle against giant presses, swinging hammers, and grinders. Watch car bodies deform and parts break away as you experiment with jumps, impacts, and crushing machines. Break through fences and trees, repair your vehicle, and try a new approach in a 3D destruction playground. Explore at your own pace or join multiplayer sessions. Play with keyboard and mouse on desktop or use touch controls on mobile.
