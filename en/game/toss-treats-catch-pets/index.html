How to Play

A short guided tutorial shows each step. Walk into the ring under the pull-up bar to train: every rep gives XP and every level adds Throw Power. Walk onto the orange line and tap (or press Space) when the meter is high to throw. Animals you catch earn cash on your plot; spend it on better bars, food and plot slots. Controls: WASD / arrow keys or the touch stick to walk, drag to turn the camera, Space to jump, E to use the pad you stand on.

About the Game

Train on the pull-up bar until you're strong enough to hurl food all the way down the canyon. Whatever animal comes to eat it joins your plot and earns cash every second, even while you're away. Time the power meter for a PERFECT throw, upgrade your bar from Wooden to Cosmic, buy better food and fly past Maple Woods, Frosty Peaks, Lava Lands and all the way to Starfall Cosmos. Every catch is a surprise: 55 animals from Common to Legendary, Golden, Diamond, Galaxy and Rainbow mutations, and Huge and Colossal sizes worth many times more. Fill your Index to earn big rewards.
