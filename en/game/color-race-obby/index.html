How to Play

Run onto the color shown at the top of the screen before the timer runs out - every other tile drops. Survive all color rounds, get past the obstacles and reach the finish first. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, mouse to turn the camera, Esc to pause, M to mute. Mobile: left side joystick to move, JUMP button to jump, swipe on the right side to turn the camera.

About the Game

A colorful 3D obby race against other players. A color is announced, you have a few seconds to stand on the matching tiles before the rest fall away. Between color fields you jump gaps, dodge spinners, swinging hammers, pushers and crumbling paths across 5 levels that get harder each time. Two game modes (Classic with checkpoints, Elimination where one fall ends your run), 20 skins, 20 accessories, 20 pets, upgrades, daily rewards, gifts, quests and a wins leaderboard.
