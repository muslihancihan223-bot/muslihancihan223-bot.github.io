How to Play

Tap the LMB or on the smartphone screen to flip the knife, slice the obstacles on the way and reach the end. Have a good game!

About the Game

In this game, you need to slice the obstacles in front of you! Tap to flip the knife and carefully cut the various obstacles in your path into equal halves! This is the juiciest way, become a knife master! With your super sharp knife, no obstacle can stop you! Flip the knife over at the right moment and slice through various obstacles - pencils, pipes, anvils, and the list goes on! You will need to keep the knife in the air at all times. You can even bounce off obstacles with the handle of the knife to flip back over and get more chances to cut!
