How to Play

Move with the mouse, WASD / ZQSD or the arrow keys. On mobile, drag anywhere. Gobble anything smaller than you to grow. Press Space (or the spit button) to stun a rival. Be the biggest jelly when the timer ends!

About the Game

Squish, slurp, GOBBLE! You are a little jelly in a pastel town, and everything smaller than you is a snack. Start with candies and cones, then work your way up to bikes, cars, buses, houses and finally the giant ferris wheel. Every bite makes you bigger, and every size unlocks bigger food. Through your see-through belly you can watch it all get digested. Seven rival jellies want the same town. Outgrow them and gobble them whole, or spit a blob of goo to stun them and steal their lunch. Follow the colour craving for double points, catch the golden donut and go wild in the final Sugar Rush. Between rounds, spend your coins on upgrades and new flavours, level up to unlock Seaside, Snow Village and Robo City, complete a sticker album for every town, collect 14 hats and come back every day for new challenges, a login gift and a free chest. In Clean Sweep mode, no rivals: just you, 90 seconds and a whole town to swallow.
