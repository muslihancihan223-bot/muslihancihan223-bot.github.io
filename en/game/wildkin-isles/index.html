How to Play

Desktop: Move with WASD or arrow keys, jump with Space, and hit or interact with left-click or P. Press Escape to pause. Mobile: Use the touch joystick to move and the Jump and Hit/Use buttons. Hit trees and rocks to gather materials. Carry up to five items; heavier loads slow you down. Enter marked circles to deposit materials or craft equipment when requirements are met. Rescue specialists and escort them safely to camp to activate their roles. Monster hits remove your carried materials. Losing all hearts lets you return to camp with resource and weapon losses, or watch an available rewarded ad to revive with one heart and preserve your belongings. Rescue the entire crew, defeat the Cloudstep guardian, and enter the finish arch to complete your adventure.

About the Game

Explore, gather, and rescue in Wildkin Isles! Roam a colorful voxel world, collect resources, craft stronger axes, and bring stranded specialists safely back to camp. Each rescued helper brings a useful skill, from gathering materials to unlocking upgrades. Prepare for tougher monsters after dark, cross Cloudstep’s tricky platforms, and defeat the island guardian. Bring your whole crew home and reach the finish to earn a Cloudstep Win.
