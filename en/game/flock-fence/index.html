About the Game

Night is coming, and the wolves are hungry. Help the sheepdog guard the flock in Flock Fence, a cozy logic puzzle about drawing the perfect pen. Tap the grass to put down fences and close off a pen around your sheepdog. Every tile inside the pen scores a point, and every sheep you keep safe scores extra. But you only have so many fences, and the wolves need their share. On some nights you’ll have to leave a few sheep out in the forest on purpose. Who do you protect, and who do you let go? When the sun sets, the wolves come out of the forest. Leave even one gap and they’ll sneak into the pen!
