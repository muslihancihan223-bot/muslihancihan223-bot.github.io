How to Play

Your goal is to reach the Lost Crown on the far side of the world. Move with WASD or the arrow keys on desktop, or drag anywhere on a touch screen. Your hero chops trees, mines rocks and fights monsters automatically when you stand next to them. Stand on a glowing circle to pay for a new hex and unlock it. Turn raw materials into better ones at workshops, upgrade your tools, sword, armour and lantern at the Forge, hatch pets at the Nest and defeat the guardian on each bridge to enter a new island. At night you only see what your lantern lights up. Press E or tap the bubble to open buildings and traders.

About the Game

Hexlands is a relaxing 3D adventure RPG where everything happens by itself: walk up to a tree, a rock or a monster and your hero chops, mines and fights automatically. Your job is to explore. Start in a tiny village on a green meadow and buy new land one hex at a time. Further islands hide better ore, new monsters and workshops that turn raw materials into planks, bricks, bars and gems. Somewhere beyond the last bridge lies the Lost Crown.
