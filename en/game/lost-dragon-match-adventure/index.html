How to Play

Find and collect three identical tiles on the bottom panel — they disappear. Clear the entire field — victory. But be careful: if the panel fills up, you lose. Conditions change with each level, and the difficulty increases. 🚀 Boosters that will help: "UNDO" — removes the last tile from the panel "CRANE" — moves any three tiles to an empty spot on the field "MAGNET" — removes a set of tiles from the panel "SHELF" — shuffles the entire field "HAMMER" — removes an obstacle or moves a tile to an empty spot on the field "EXTRA SLOT" — expands the panel

About the Game

Lost Dragon: Match Adventure — is a captivating match-3-tile game with an engaging storyline and dozens of modifiers that make the experience more complex and interesting than a regular match-3-tile. Collect three identical tiles on the panel, clearing the field, and don't let it overflow — each level has its own limit. Cunning obstacles await you: some block tiles, others hide them, and still others grow if not removed in time. Go through hundreds of levels, uncovering the story and finding out who stole the dragon. And when you want a break from the main storyline, bonus themed levels with unique conditions and rewards await you. In a tough spot, boosters will help: "Undo," "Crane," "Magnet," "Shelf," and "Extra Slot."
