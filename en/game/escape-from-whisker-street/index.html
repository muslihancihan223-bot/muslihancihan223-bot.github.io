About the Game

Something is wrong on Whisker Street. The corner shop is dark, the mart shelves are crawling, and the stitched-up cats won’t stay down. You’re a night-shift cat with a mop, a spray bottle and no way home but through. Sneak past what you can and fight what you can’t. Shove, parry and bonk with the mop, or find the pistol, the shotgun and the yarn launcher and make some noise (but noise brings more of them). Hide in lockers when Mr. Whiskers comes looking, keep your nerve, and don’t look behind you. Between nights, rest in the Back Room: build the workbench, upgrade your guns, change character and outfits, decorate, and send your rescued friends on expeditions. Then go back out.
