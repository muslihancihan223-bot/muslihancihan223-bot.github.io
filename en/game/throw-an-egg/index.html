How to Play

Stand on the orange pad and LIFT (tap, hold or AUTO) to build Power. On the yellow launch pad press THROW and stop the meter in the green for a PERFECT throw. When the egg lands, run home toward the camera (W or stick up) before the tsunami hits. Walk up to a ready egg to hatch it. Controls: WASD or joystick to move, E / Space / click to lift, throw and hatch, drag to look, wheel to zoom.

About the Game

A Roblox-style egg simulator. Lift weights to build Power, time the throw meter and hurl your egg across seven themed zones from Sunny Meadow to the Cosmic Rift. Then grab it and sprint home before a giant tsunami catches you. Hatch 21 blocky pets across 9 rarities with Huge, Gold, Shiny and Rainbow variants. Your pets earn cash every second, even offline, so you can buy heavier weights, faster shoes and more pens, then rebirth for permanent multipliers.
