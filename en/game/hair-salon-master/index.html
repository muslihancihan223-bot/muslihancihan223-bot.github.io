How to Play

Walk up to a waiting customer and invite them to the chair, then start the haircut. Drag the clipper or scissors over the hair: strands highlighted in color still have to be cut. Pick the guard length the customer asked for, turn the head by dragging on empty space or with the arrow buttons, then brush off the clippings and press Done. Fill the bar to earn up to three stars. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to walk, E to interact, mouse to cut. On phones use the on-screen stick and button, and cut with your finger.

About the Game

Hair Salon Master is a 3D barbershop simulator. Invite customers to the chair, read their request and cut their hair with a clipper and scissors: every strand is cut exactly where the tool passes, and the clippings fall onto the cape. Brush the hair off, earn stars and cash, level up to unlock new tools and trickier requests, and dress up your barber, tools and salon in the shop.
