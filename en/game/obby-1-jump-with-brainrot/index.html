About the Game

Welcome to jump obby with brainrots! You run around the arena, and every jump earns agility: the higher your level, the higher you jump — from a meter to the skies. Ahead stand five tall walls like a staircase. Reach the top — a coin plate waits there: step on it, collect your coins and return to start for the next one. Spend coins wisely: buy 15 brainrots (from Tralalero Tralala to Bobrito Bandito), cool skins and pets that boost your coins. The cooler the brainrot, the faster you progress. Rebirth to raise your level cap and jump even higher — all the way to level 300. Open portals to the frost and fire worlds, climb ever taller walls and collect plates worth hundreds of thousands. Compete on the agility, coins and time leaderboards, come back every day for rewards and grab playtime gifts. Jumped — reach the top!
