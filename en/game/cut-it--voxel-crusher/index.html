How to Play

Drag/Swipe anywhere on the screen to move the robotic saw. Grind the saw against the voxel object to dismantle it. Keep an eye on the fuel bar! The saw consumes fuel as you move it. Collect coins from destroyed voxels and use them in the main menu to upgrade your Fuel Capacity and Arm Range.

About the Game

Step into the ultimate demolition workshop! In Voxel Crusher, you take control of a high-tech robotic arm equipped with a spinning chainsaw. Swipe to guide the saw and grind down structures built from hundreds of colorful voxels. Watch them crumble, explode, and disintegrate in a super-satisfying physical way! But watch your fuel tank! Moving the heavy saw consumes energy. Gather coins from crushed voxels and spend them in the shop to upgrade your fuel capacity and extend your arm's reach. Crush everything from simple shapes to complex figures, complete challenging levels, and unlock the ultimate satisfaction of total voxel destruction. Features: Satisfying Voxel Destruction: Grind and crush structures block-by-block with realistic crumbling physics. Interactive Robot Arm Controls: Simple drag-and-swipe controls to guide your robotic saw. Strategic Upgrades: Upgrade your robot's fuel tank size and arm range to crush larger models. ASMR Gameplay Experience: Relaxing sound effects and satisfying visual feedback. Progression & Variety: Grind through a wide array of creative voxel shapes and levels.
