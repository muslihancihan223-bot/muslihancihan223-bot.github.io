How to Play

Move with WASD or the on-screen stick, jump with Space or the arrow button. Walk to a garden bed to plant and harvest potatoes, carry them to the sink and the cutting board, then to the fryer, and hand the fries to the customer at the counter. Step on a purchase pad or tap the Buy button to unlock upgrades. At night, catch the runaway potato for a bonus. Q/E or right mouse drag turns the camera, the mouse wheel zooms.

About the Game

A blocky farm-to-fryer tycoon. Plant potatoes, dig them up, wash and cut them, fry golden fries and serve a queue of hungry customers. Spend coins on purchase pads: more garden beds, a bigger sack, a hotter fryer and helpers who work on their own. Every few minutes night falls and silly anomalies begin: a potato on legs runs away, a ghost orders fries without potatoes, and the fryer starts to sing.
