Pixel Color Quest is a free Drawing, Color game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Goal: completely color the selected picture. Find the number inside a cell, choose the color with the same number, and fill every matching cell. The picture is complete when every section has the correct color. Desktop: - Left mouse button — select a picture or color. - Left mouse button on a cell — fill it with the selected color. Phone and tablet: - Tap a picture or color to select it. - Tap the required cell to fill it.

About the Game

Choose a picture you like and turn a blank field into a bright pixel artwork. Pick the required colors and carefully fill the cells by following their numbers. Start with simple images or test your attention on large, detailed levels. Different themes and difficulty levels let you choose a quick picture for relaxation or a serious creative challenge. - Many pictures across different themes - Easy and challenging levels - Clear numbered color palette - Bright pixel images - Calm coloring without rushing
