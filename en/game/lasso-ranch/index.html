How to Play

Press and drag to draw a rope around the animals; when the rope meets its own trail it closes and pulls the animals in (on a computer you can also click once and move the mouse, click again to drop the rope). Catch enough animals before the clock runs out to pass the level, catch them all for the second star, and catch a big group in one loop for the third. Never loop a skunk. Spend the coins you earn on the ranch and in the shop between levels. Touch: drag a finger. Mouse: press and drag, or click, move and click again. Keyboard: P pauses, M mutes, Enter or Space presses the main button.

About the Game

The farm animals have got out, and you have a rope. Draw a loop around them with your finger or your mouse: when the rope crosses itself it closes, tightens and yanks everything inside into your pen. Big loops make big combos, but a bull that bolts into the rope snaps it, a pig slips out of a loose loop, and a skunk is best left alone. Rope your way through 64 levels in eight lands, from the Meadow and the Farm to the Woods, the Canyon, the River, the Snowy Peaks, the Jungle and the Moon. Every level brings something new: a goat that hops the rope, a rabbit that dives into a burrow, a wolf to chase off, a tractor, mud, wind, night. Earn up to three stars a level, then spend your coins on the ranch between rounds: build pens, buy a longer rope and tougher knots, fill the book of rare animals and dress your rancher in new hats and lasso looks. When the last level is done, The Big Roundup and Last Call Rush keep going for as long as you like.
