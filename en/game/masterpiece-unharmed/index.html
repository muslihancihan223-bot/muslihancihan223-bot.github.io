How to Play

Inspect the damaged area and select a brush. Change its size with the slider, select the color with a pipette or through the palette and fill in the gap area. The eraser removes unsuccessful strokes. The mouse wheel or side slider changes the scale, and dragging moves the canvas. Have time to click "Check" before the timer ends.

About the Game

Masterpiece Unharmed a game about the restoration of paintings. Match the colors, finish the damage and inspector to the end of the timer and save the museum! A night out, a closed museum, and a collection of famous paintings that someone managed to ruin right before the inspector arrived. In the game "Masterpiece Unharmed" you have to hide the traces of the most ridiculous incidents and convince the inspector that everything is fine with the art. Examine the damage, select the appropriate color with a pipette or palette, adjust the thickness of the brush and carefully restore the lost fragment. If you make a mistake, use an eraser or cancel the stroke. Scale and move the canvas to get to the small details, but keep an eye on the timer: the inspector is already on his way. Before each painting you will find a new funny story, and after work — a comparison with the original, highlighting errors and the caustic verdict of the inspector. With each level, the gaps become more difficult, there is less time, and the accident plots become more absurd.
