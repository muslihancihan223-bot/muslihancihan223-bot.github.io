CraftMart is a free food, minecraft, management game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Control Noob with the arrows on your keyboard (or by tapping on the screen of your mobile device). Build a building, pick up products and take them to the counter. When the customer has bought everything, help him pay at the cash register.

About the Game

Noob decided to go into business and open his own shop. And what do all the people of Minecraft want? That's right - to eat! In Noob's shop you can always buy beet soup and bread! And also a lot of other food. And now all the residents of CubeWorld rushed to the best store! Help Noob to serve all the customers! No one should go hungry! :) Build new fields for wheat and beets, get a cow and a chicken and expand the shop to the maximum assortment! Simulate a grocery shop in the world of Minecraft: - plant turnips and wheat, raise chickens and a cow - make turnip soup as well as warm bread - serve your customers - improve your means of production and your abilities - hire workers to help you Show your business skill!
