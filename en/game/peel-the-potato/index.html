About the Game

You were brought to a snowy potato camp, and there is only one way out of the bunker: the vault door. Its key is hidden inside one of the 10,000 potatoes of a giant mountain! Collect potatoes, peel them at the table, sell them on the conveyor for coins and buy better tools to clear the mountain faster. 🥔 A mountain of 10,000 potatoes with golden, diamond, rainbow and cosmic ones 🧤 Hand, shovel and a powerful vacuum that sucks up potatoes in bunches 🔪 4 peelers, from a knife-spoon to a triple peeler, plus crank and auto machines 🎒 A bucket, a sack and a cart to carry more potatoes at once 🚁 A helper drone that collects potatoes for you 🏅 6 classes with bonuses and permanent gem upgrades ⏱️ Escape timer and your fastest escape record Find the key, open the vault and escape faster than anyone!
