How to Play

Steal, hatch, upgrade. Steal eggs: approach NPC pets and other players, press the steal button. The higher your speed, the more stealthy your theft. Hatch pets: bring an egg to your base and activate the incubator. The pet will start earning coins automatically. Train on the treadmill: run on it to gain speed. Speed helps you steal faster and escape from thieves. Upgrade your base and treadmill: spend coins on upgrades — they speed up training and increase pet income. Controls: WASD — movement, mouse — camera control, E — steal egg, activate incubator. On mobile — joystick and on-screen buttons. Tips: Rare eggs glow or look unusual — they give the most valuable pets. Watch other players: if you see a slow one with an egg — take it. Invest in the treadmill first — speed pays off fastest.

About the Game

Is a competitive race for the coolest pet collection. Steal eggs from NPC creatures and other players, hatch rare pets — they earn coins passively. Use your earnings to upgrade your treadmill and base: the better the treadmill, the faster you train and gain speed. Speed is your ace: fast thieves steal eggs unnoticed, and you can easily escape from slow bosses. Hunt for rare eggs with mutations and unusual sizes — they give the most valuable pets and earn more money. Defend your collection from other players, improve your base and climb the leaderboards.
