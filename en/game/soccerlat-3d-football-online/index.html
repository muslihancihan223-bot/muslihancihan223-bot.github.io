About the Game

Soccer Online is a fast 3D football game you play in your browser against other people in real time. Pick your position, take the pitch and play a proper match: fouls and free kicks, penalties, corners, throw-ins, and a referee running the diagonal. Still level at full time? It goes to a penalty shootout. Play anything from 1v1 to 11v11. Short of players? Switch on the bot goalkeepers and fill the goals. The ball is properly simulated. Bend your shots by moving as you strike, loft them by aiming with the camera, dribble past defenders and slide in to win it back, but time it, because a late tackle is a foul and the referee will give it. Make the player yours: skin tone, kit designs and colours, shorts, boots, hats, your name and number on the back, and your team's name and three-letter tag. Two pitches to play on: a full indoor stadium and a caged street court. Switch to first person whenever you like. Play with mouse and keyboard, a gamepad, or touch controls on your phone.
