How to Play

The controls are simple and intuitive: Movement: Keys W, A, S, D Jump: Space bar (use it together with trampolines for super jumps!) The goal of the game is to explore the map, find and collect all the hidden capybaras. Look around! Capybaras can hide in the most unexpected places. Use trampolines and power-ups to reach the secret corners.

About the Game

Welcome to a huge and crazy world where your main goal is to find and collect all the hidden capybaras! Explore a giant, diverse map with dizzying trampolines that will take you to the highest platforms. But be careful — the territories are guarded by dangerous monsters! Don't let yourself get caught. To fight back, you have a whole arsenal of funny but effective "slaps": a giant hand, a roll of toilet paper, or even a venerable bream fish! Stun the enemy and run on. Use special power-ups: a magic carpet for flying, a speed spring for a lightning-fast dash and a jump spring to conquer inaccessible places. For the capybaras you find, you will receive three unique skins for your character. Become the most stylish seeker! Ready for a big adventure? Launch the game, explore every corner and become a legendary capybara hunter!
