How to Play

Step into the role of a pirate parrot shooter and blast colorful bubbles from your cannon to match 3+ of the same color—reds, blues, yellows, and specials—triggering chain reactions to pop clusters and clear encroaching bubble walls before they reach the bottom, collecting gems, coins, and power-ups to hit star and trophy goals in each tropical level! **Desktop Controls:** Mouse: Drag to aim (dotted line shows trajectory), release to shoot. **Mobile Controls:** Touch: Drag to aim, release/tap to shoot; tap barrels for power-ups.

About the Game

Traffic Puzzle Game Linky is a captivating logic puzzle game set in a lush forest meadow, where players connect pairs of numbered construction blocks with precisely the exact count of road segments—no more, no less—to link all cities and clear the board within time limits, earning stars and trophies in increasingly challenging levels tracked by a dynamic timer and handy undo feature.
