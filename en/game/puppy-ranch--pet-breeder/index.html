Puppy Ranch: Pet Breeder is a free Simulation, 3D, Animal game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag on the screen on mobile. Walk into stray dogs to catch them and bring them to your pet house to breed puppies. Take puppies to the sales counter to earn cash. Stand on price signs to buy new pet houses and upgrades. Open the puppy book with the book button (or C) to see your collection. Win dog races by tapping TAP, and train a champion for the puppy show (tap the trick buttons or keys 1-3).

About the Game

Run your own puppy ranch! Catch stray dogs, breed adorable puppies and sell them to happy customers. Wash and care for your dogs, hire helpers, and spend your cash on bigger pet houses, faster catching and better prices. Discover rare, shiny and golden puppies to fill your collection book, race your dogs to win cups, and train a champion for the puppy show. Grow a tiny yard into the best pet ranch in town!
