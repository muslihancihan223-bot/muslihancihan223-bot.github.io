How to Play

Drag two identical boats together to merge them into a higher-level boat. Place boats on available routes to earn coins automatically, then use those coins to purchase more boats and upgrades. Continue merging, expanding your fleet, completing tasks, and unlocking increasingly powerful boats. The main objective is to discover the highest-level boats and maximize your earnings. Controls: - Desktop: Click and drag with the mouse. - Mobile: Tap and drag with one finger. - No keyboard controls are required.

About the Game

Boat Merge is a relaxing idle merge game where players combine matching boats to create faster and more valuable vessels. Build your fleet, unlock dozens of unique boats, expand your waterways, earn coins automatically, and use upgrades and boosters to progress further.
