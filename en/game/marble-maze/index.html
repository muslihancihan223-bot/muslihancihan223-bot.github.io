How to Play

How to Play Roll your marble — Swipe up, down, left, or right. The marble keeps rolling until it hits a wall. Paint the maze — Dark tiles are unpainted. Roll over them to leave your marble’s color behind. Complete the maze — Visit and paint every open tile. There is no finish line; the maze is done when the whole board is painted. Use hints (optional) — Tap the lightbulb hint for 50 coins. It shows your next roll and updates after each move. Beat your rival — Finish more mazes than your opponent before the 90-second timer runs out.

About the Game

A head-to-head 3D maze puzzle. You swipe to roll your marble through a labyrinth and paint every open tile on the board. Each maze uses one color — your job is to cover the whole board, not reach a finish line.
