About the Game

Clock in for the ultimate graveyard shift in DeadMart: Outbreak Register! A biohazard zombie outbreak has turned the DeadMart supermarket into an action-packed 3D combat arena. Wield a razor-sharp katana, unleash bare-knuckle combos, and slice through infected walkers, rabid screamers, riot enforcers, biohazard butchers, and the hulking DeadMart Overlord! Key Features: Non-Stop Level-by-Level Progression: Clear zombie hordes across escalating levels (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3...) with automatic katana power-ups and health boosts after every completed level! 3D Slow-Motion Blade Mode & Zandatsu: Slice zombies and flying grocery shelves at any angle in slow-motion Blade Mode to restore full HP and ignite Ripper Mode! Animated 4-Directional Zombie Radar & Tactical Store Map: Track exact zombie counts in Front (⬆), Back (⬇), Left (⬅), and Right (➡) on your live sonar radar, or open the full zoomable 6-sector DeadMart Tactical Map [M]. Survival & Sandbox Modes: Battle for the highest level in Survival Mode or spawn custom waves and bosses in Sandbox Mode!
