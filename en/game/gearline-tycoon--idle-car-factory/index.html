How to Play

Build the biggest car factory! Upgrade every stage of your assembly line, grow your crew and hit each factory's sales goal to unlock the next car. Build all seven, from the Compact to the Hypercar. Workers are your biggest challenge: each one needs a parking space, a quick way through the gates and a break now and then. Desktop Controls: Left click: open any part of the factory and its upgrades. Click an upgrade button: buy one level. Hold it: keep buying. Click and drag: move around the factory. Mouse wheel: zoom in and out. Click spills, stuck parts and dirty solar panels: earn bonus cash. Mobile Controls: Tap: open any part of the factory and its upgrades. Tap an upgrade button: buy one level. Hold it: keep buying. Drag with one finger: move around the factory. Pinch: zoom in and out. Tap spills, stuck parts and dirty solar panels: earn bonus cash.

About the Game

Gearline Tycoon: Idle Car Factory is a free idle tycoon game you can play online in your browser, with no download. Build your own car factory and run its assembly line, from a bare steel sheet to a finished car. At every stage, a crane lifts the part off the conveyor, works it on its machine and sets it back on the belt: chassis, suspension, body, engine, tyres, interior, software, testing and rollout. Upgrade each stage, add workstations and grow your crew. Every worker needs a parking space and has to get through the gates and take breaks, so managing your workers is the real challenge. Hit your sales goal to open a new factory for a better car, from a small Compact to a Hypercar, while your old factories keep earning. A bright, busy car manufacturing game that’s satisfying to watch, and your factories keep running for 30 minutes after you leave.
