How to Play

Steer left and right; your robot shoots by itself. Drive into an upgrade pod to bolt that part on (pods marked STRONG HERE counter the monsters of the current land). Dodge enemy shots, obstacles and the red warning zones of boss attacks, collect gears and crystals, and defeat the boss at the end of each land to finish the chapter. Between runs, spend gears in the garage to get stronger. Controls: - Touch or mouse: drag left or right anywhere on the screen. - Keyboard: Left / Right arrow keys (or A / D). Esc pauses.

About the Game

Your little tracked robot rolls down a road full of monsters, and it fires on its own. You only steer. Drive into an upgrade pod and the part bolts right onto your robot: twin blasters, a gatling, homing rockets, a laser lance, a flamer, a tesla coil, side guns, drones, a saw ram, a shield dome and more. Every pick changes how the robot looks and how it fights, and each weapon is strongest against different monsters. Each chapter takes you through three lands, from the Overgrown Meadow and the Junkyard to the Sky Islands, the Candy Kingdom, the Zombie Marsh and the Robot Citadel: twelve lands in all. The road never stops loading; only a boss can stop it. Beat twelve giant bosses, earn three stars per chapter, spend your gears in the garage on upgrades that last forever, finish daily missions and fill your album with every monster you meet. Clear Chapter 6 to open the Endless Road.
