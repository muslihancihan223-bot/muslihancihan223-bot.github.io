How to Play

On PC: 1. WASD — fly. Space — up, Ctrl — down. Shift — dash. 2. Mouse — look around. Left mouse button — sting attack. 3. E — interact (hive, bee and field upgrades, customization shop). 4. Tab — settings (volume, camera sensitivity). 5. Pollen is collected automatically: just fly up to a flower and deliver it to the hive. On mobile: joystick on the left, buttons on the right (Attack, Dash, Action), and swipe the right part of the screen to turn the camera.

About the Game

A cozy 3D game about a little bee in an open world made of cubes. Collect pollen, turn it into honey, upgrade your hive and your bee, and challenge the bosses. Features: 1. Free flight: fly in any direction and dash. 2. Upgrades: improve speed, capacity, health, energy and sting damage (up to level 10), plus the hive that produces passive honey and the flower fields. Max out a field to open a new one far away. 3. Enemies and bosses: wasps, zombies and a dragon guard the fields, so collecting pollen won't be easy. 4. Customization: buy hats and glasses with honey and find secret items on the map. It's worth a try!
