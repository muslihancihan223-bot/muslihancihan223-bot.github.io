How to Play

How to Play Goal Score more points than your rival before time runs out. If your health hits zero, you’re eaten — the rival keeps scoring until the clock ends, and ties go to them. Controls You stay fixed in the center — you don’t move around the map. Your gun auto-fires at zombies inside its forward fire cone (~94° arc). Click/touch and drag anywhere on screen to swing the gun and aim manually. Release to return to auto-aim. Zombies behind you are outside your gun’s cone — they won’t get shot unless you turn toward them, but they can still bite you.

About the Game

A top-down survival duel. You’re pinned at the center of the arena while a horde of zombies pours in from the edges. Your rival survivor is doing the same on their side of the match — you’re racing to outscore them before the 90-second clock runs out. The twist is the merge mechanic: killing a zombie doesn’t always remove it from the field. Below max tier, it fuses into another zombie of the same type, upgrading it into something bigger, slower, tankier, and worth more points. Only the top-tier Colossus (Lv 5) dies for good. The better you play, the more dangerous the horde becomes — but also the more points you can farm.
