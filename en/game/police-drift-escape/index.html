How to Play

Control your car, avoid police, collect cash, and survive as long as possible. The longer you survive, the more intense the chase becomes. Police cars keep coming, the screen gets more dangerous, and every second matters.

About the Game

Police Drift Escape is a fast, simple, and addictive top-down drift survival game. You are the getaway driver. Police cars are coming from every direction. Drift, dodge, collect cash, and make the police crash into each other before they catch you. There is no road. No traffic. No complicated rules. Just pure arcade chasing on a clean open surface. Drift to Survive Your car keeps moving. Use smooth control to turn, slide, and escape at the last second. The police cars are faster, but they cannot turn as sharply as you. Use that advantage to make them miss. A perfect drift can save you. A wrong move can end the chase. Make Police Crash You do not need weapons. Your best weapon is your driving skill. Bait police cars into following you, turn at the right moment, and watch them crash into each other. Every police crash gives you more score and makes the escape more satisfying. Grab the Cash Cash bundles appear during the chase. Collect them for extra rewards, but be careful. Some cash is placed in risky spots, and chasing it too greedily can get you caught. Normal cash gives quick rewards. Golden cash gives bigger rewards. But survival always comes first.
