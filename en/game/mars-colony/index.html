How to Play

Use the joystick to move. If you have a keyboard, you can use: WASD or arrow keys to move. Move Rob to a resource and he will begin mining it. Move Rob to a production facility and he will begin processing the resource, and a production acceleration panel will appear. To speed up resource processing, quickly click the panel that appears. Move Rob to a construction zone and he will invest resources in the building. Move Rob to a warehouse and the resource movement panel will open. Move Rob to the transport assistant control zone and the assistant control panel will open.

About the Game

Rob the Robot, you have been given a crucial mission: to prepare Mars for human settlement! Mine resources. Build factories. Recycle resources. Prepare Mars for human settlement! "Mars Colony" is an idle game. The game is similar to "My Little Universe."
