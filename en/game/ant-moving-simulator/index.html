How to Play

Click or tap a front numbered box to move it into an empty slot. Your ants will automatically collect reachable blocks of the same color. Plan ahead and keep room for your next move! Linked boxes move together, so you need enough empty slots for the whole group. Clear every block to beat the level. Stuck? Try a power-up! Use the on-screen buttons to activate power-ups or pause the game.

About the Game

Tiny ants. Colorful puzzles. Big brain challenges! Lead your little helpers through 2,200 levels of colorful block-clearing fun. Pick the right numbered boxes, send ants to collect matching blocks, and watch each pixel picture disappear piece by piece. Space is limited, so plan your moves carefully—linked boxes can turn a simple choice into a tricky puzzle! Use handy power-ups, unlock new character skins, and see how far your ant team can go.
