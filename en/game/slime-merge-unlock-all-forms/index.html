How to Play

Drag and drop two identical slimes onto each other on the grid to merge them into the next form. Tap slimes on the board to earn extra coins and charge the Slime Frenzy meter, or press the Buy button at the bottom to spawn new slimes. Unlock up to 20 board cells, fulfill customer orders at the top for bonus gems, activate the 5-minute Merge Pairs and 10-minute x2 Income boosts, and reach Form 100 to Rebirth with permanent income bonuses.

About the Game

Slime Merge: Unlock All Forms! is a cozy idle merge and collection game with 100 unique slime forms across 10 themed worlds. Buy and drag matching slimes on an expandable 4x5 board to evolve them into higher-tier creatures, discover rare Shiny slimes with 5x coin income, and use Rainbow Joker slimes to upgrade any form. Complete customer orders for gems, spin the Lucky Wheel, trigger Slime Frenzy by tapping, and perform Rebirth at Form 100 for permanent multipliers!
