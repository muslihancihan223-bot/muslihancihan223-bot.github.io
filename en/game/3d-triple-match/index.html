How to Play

- Spot & Tap: Tap on three identical 3D items from the pile to collect them into the bottom tray. - Match to Clear: Once three identical items are placed in the tray, they merge and disappear automatically. - Complete the Objective: Clear all target items listed at the top of the screen before the countdown timer hits zero. - Watch the Tray Limit: Avoid filling up all slots in the bottom collector bar with mismatched items, or the game ends.
