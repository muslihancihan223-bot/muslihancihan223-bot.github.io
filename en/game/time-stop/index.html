How to Play

Goal: Survive as long as you can. Clear every wave in the arena, pick a perk, then go through the exit to the next arena. Kill enemies to earn points, build combos, and climb the leaderboard. Main idea: Time stop is your main weapon. While time is stopped, bullets freeze in the air. Grab them and throw them back, or take free shots at enemies that cannot react. Time stop lasts a few seconds and recharges, so use it wisely. Controls (PC): WASD — move Mouse — look around Left Mouse Button — shoot / throw F or Right Mouse Button — stop time E — pick up weapons and bullets Q — throw the held item Controls (mobile): use the on-screen joystick to move, drag the right side of the screen to look and use the on-screen buttons to shoot, stop time and grab. Tips: Riot-shield enemies block bullets from the front, so flank them. Kamikazes must be killed from a distance. Spend your points in the Upgrades shop for permanent bonuses.

About the Game

Stop time. Catch the bullets. Throw them back. TIME STOP is a fast first-person arena shooter in a manga comic style. You can stop time at any moment — enemy bullets freeze in mid-air, and you can grab them and throw them back, or line up the perfect shot while the world stands still. Fight through endless arenas packed with different enemies: kamikaze bombers, riot-shield soldiers, and a mysterious Chrono Boss who is not afraid of time itself. Every arena brings random modifiers like Darkness or Jammed Guns, and after each one you choose a new perk to shape your build. What makes it unique: a JoJo-inspired time-stop mechanic, a hand-drawn manga look with comic panels and sound-effect lettering, fully procedural music, permanent upgrades from a slot-machine shop, and a global leaderboard. Available in 5 languages (English, Russian, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese). Plays on PC and mobile.
