How to Play

In each chapter, destroy the necro spires and slay the horde, then defeat the chapter's boss. Desktop: move the mouse to steer and aim, hold the left mouse button to breathe, right mouse button to Strike, R to Burst, F to unleash your charged super, W or Shift to boost, S to brake, Q and E to roll out of attacks, P to pause, M to mute. Phones and tablets: drag on the left side to steer; your breath fires by itself at locked targets, and on-screen buttons handle Strike, Burst, Super, Roll and Boost. Start with First Flight for a short guided tutorial.

About the Game

Ride an elemental dragon and take back five skies from the undead. Pick one of four dragons, burn down necro spires and huge zombie hordes, and face five bosses across a full 3D campaign. Master breath, Strike, Burst and a charged super, earn new moves, relics and cosmetics, then test yourself in Skyfall survival, Contracts, Boss Rush and the Daily Flight. Dragons, monsters and scenery are 3D models made with AI generation tools; the game itself was built and tuned by hand.
