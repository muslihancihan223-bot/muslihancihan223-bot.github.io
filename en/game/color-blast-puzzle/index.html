How to Play

Tap matching blocks: Tap groups of two or more blocks with the same color to clear them from the board. Complete the objectives: Each level has specific goals, such as clearing a certain number of colored blocks. Plan your moves: You have a limited number of moves, so choose each tap carefully. Create bigger matches: Clear larger groups of matching blocks to earn higher scores and create powerful effects. Use power-ups: Use available boosters and special items to clear difficult areas and complete challenging levels. Win the level: Complete all required objectives before running out of moves. Controls: Tap or click on matching blocks to make your move.
