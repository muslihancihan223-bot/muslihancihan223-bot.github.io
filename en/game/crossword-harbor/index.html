How to Play

Read a clue in a clue cell; its arrow points to where the answer goes. Tap a clue to see it big. Your computer rival moves first, and you start with +30 points for going second. Drag a letter tile from your rack to a cell (or tap a tile, then a cell), then press Go. Right letters score points, star cells score double and finishing a word gives a bonus; wrong letters go back. Stuck? Hint lights up the right cells for your tiles and Swap deals new tiles. The higher score when the board is full wins. Mouse and touch both work.

About the Game

Crossword Harbor is a turn-based crossword duel. Each turn you get five letter tiles and place them on an arrowword grid where every clue sits right inside the board. Right letters score, finished words pay a bonus, and the best score wins the board. Sail a Journey of harbor ports against friendly computer rivals with boss duels and bonus rush levels, solve the Daily Crossword, climb seven leagues on the leaderboards, and duel friends live or with a turn timer, with chat and sticker packs.
