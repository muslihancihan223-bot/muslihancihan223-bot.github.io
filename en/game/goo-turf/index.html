How to Play

Lead your slime team in single-player 4v4 battles against bots. Cover the arena with your team's goo! The team with the most painted territory when time runs out wins. About the Game

Make a colorful mess in Goo Turf, a 3D action game starring tiny slimes with big blasters! Team up with bots in single-player 4v4 turf battles and cover more ground than the opposing team before time runs out. Dive into your team's goo to move faster and refill your ink, unlock 70 imaginative stages, and customize your slime with outfits and weapons. Every splash counts!
