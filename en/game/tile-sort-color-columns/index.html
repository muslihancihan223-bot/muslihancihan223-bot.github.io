How to Play

The tile at the bottom of the screen is the one you need to place. Choose a column: the tile is added to the top, and the bottom tile from that column becomes the tile for your next move. Move tiles between columns until each column contains tiles of just one color. Plan ahead: the tile you take from the bottom determines your next move. Some levels introduce hidden tiles and other mechanics. If you don't see a solution right away, try a different sequence of moves. There is no timer, and you can't lose.

About the Game

This is an engaging logic game with colorful tiles. Your goal is to sort them so that each column contains tiles of a single color. Choose a column, add a tile to the top, and take the bottom tile for your next move. To solve each puzzle, think carefully about where every tile will go. There's no need to rush: there is no timer and you can't lose, so you can take your time with every move. Explore levels of varying difficulty, from simple introductions to puzzles that require planning several moves ahead. Board layouts change, more colors appear, and hidden tiles and other mechanics bring new challenges. Play at your own pace, find clever solutions, and sort every color into its own column!
