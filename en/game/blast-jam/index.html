How to Play

- Tap (or click) a shooter at the bottom to move it into the dock above. - A docked shooter automatically fires at cubes of its own colour on the board. - The dock has a few slots only. If it fills up with shooters that cannot fire, the run ends - so think about which shooter you take next. - Three shooters of the same colour in the dock merge into one and keep all their ammo. - Clear every cube on the board to finish the level. - Stuck? Use the hammer (smash any cube), the magnet (pull one colour off the board) or an extra dock slot. Coins buy boosters, permanent upgrades and new characters. - Blockers: free the piggy banks, break crates by blasting cubes, hit each gem on a gem box with its own colour, and collect the key to open a locked shooter. Controls: tap / click only. No keyboard needed. Works on phone, tablet and desktop.

About the Game

Blast Jam is a colour-matching puzzle about clearing a picture made of jelly cubes. Tap a shooter to send it into the dock and it starts firing paint balls at every cube of its own colour. The dock has only a few slots, so the order you pick shooters in is the whole puzzle - and three shooters of one colour merge into a bigger one when they meet there. Over 100 hand-built levels hide pictures behind piggy banks, crates, locked shooters and gem boxes, with a fresh theme every few levels. Earn coins, open chests, keep a win streak going, and unlock eight characters - from a paint-bucket jelly cube to a ninja, an astronaut and a king - who stand on your podium and cheer you on. Every level is generated so that it can always be solved: plan the dock well and there is a way through.
