About the Game

🏔️ Ready to conquer the tallest slides? In this game, you can! 🎢 Climb higher and higher up the ladders, then rush down the thrilling slides! 💨 The farther you slide, the more coins you collect! 💰 And with cute pets by your side, you’ll earn even more — they’ll help you gather coins! 🐱🐢🐰 What awaits you: • Long ladders and exciting slides for speedy rides 🪜➡️🎢 • Funny pet companions 🐦 • Bright levels and new heights ⛰️ • Fun gameplay and colorful graphics 🎨 Climb up, slide down, and become the ultimate slide champion! 🎮✨ Controls: Desktop: WASD / Arrow keys - move around the map, Space - jump, click - confirm actions. Mobile: virtual joystick - move around the map, tap - confirm actions. Tips & Tricks: • Auto climb: allows you to release control of the character for a while. • Cup: Don't forget to separately go for the cup and collect it; without this action, climbing will only earn you coins for descending. Upgrades / Progression / Economy: • Pets: allow you to earn more coins, depending on the multiplier. • Boats: affect the speed of ascent and the number of coins earned during descent. Levels / Maps / Worlds: • 5 worlds • Each world features a slope, a boat shop, and three pet sales stations. The more expensive the unlock for a new world, the more challenging the ascent to the top will be, but the rewards will be greater (more coins, and at the top, you can earn not just 1 cup, but 5, 10, and so on).
