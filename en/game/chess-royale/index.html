How to Play

There are three types of magical abilities: - create new pawn; - create new obstacle; - change one piece to another. To use the ability, drag the card onto an empty cell (if it is a new pawn or a new obstacle) or onto a piece (if it is a change of piece).

About the Game

A non-standard chess game in which kings have magical powers. The player needs to cut down the enemy king, building a victorious tactics by combining the queue of moves and magical abilities.
