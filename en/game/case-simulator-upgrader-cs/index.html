Case Simulator: Upgrader CS is a free Management, Simulation game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Click or tap a case to preview its contents, then select Open to start the roll. Click or tap a skin in your inventory to view its details. In the upgrade and contract menus, select the skins you want to use and confirm your choice!

About the Game

Description Collect weapon skins in this case opening simulator. Preview each case’s contents and drop odds, then open it to see which skin you get. Keep your favorites, sell extras for in-game currency, or use them in four game modes. - Coin Flip - Pick a side and put up skins. If the coin lands on your side, you win the skins shown in the prize pool. - Rocket - Launch with skins and watch the multiplier climb. Claim your reward before the rocket crashes or lose the skins you put in. - Upgrade - Choose a skin you own and a more valuable target. Check the success chance or spend in-game currency to improve your odds before you roll. - Contracts - Trade several skins from your inventory for one new skin!
