How to Play

Goal: beat the enemies, free captured friends and reach the flag at the end of each level. Desktop: move with Left/Right arrows or A/D, jump with Up, W or Space, attack with F or the left mouse button (hold for a strong hit). Mobile: joystick to move and jump, red button to attack. Puzzles: swipe across a rope to cut it. Sandbox: pick an item at the bottom and tap the scene to place it, drag to throw, press the swords button to start the fight.

About the Game

The Cactus Anomaly is brewing "Rage-juice" and the garden's veggies are going wild. Grab a weapon and stop him! Veggie Battle is a funny physics brawler with floppy ragdoll vegetables: every hit, throw and explosion is pure physics, so no two fights are the same. Story: 40 levels in 5 acts with platforming, traps, rope-cutting puzzles and bosses. Arcade: endless runs that get harder every stage. Sandbox: spawn fighters, weapons, bombs and traps, pick teams, watch the chaos or take control of any fighter yourself. 9 vegetable fighters, 10 weapons from a stick to a bazooka, 6 locations, hero upgrades and stars to collect.
