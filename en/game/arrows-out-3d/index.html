How to Play

Left click or tap a block to slide it out in the direction its arrow points. A clear path lets it escape; a blocked path bumps it back and costs a heart. Click and drag, or swipe, to rotate the whole shape and see every side. Use the mouse wheel or pinch to zoom in and out. Tap Hint to see a block that can move, Pluck to remove any block you choose, or Wand to clear several blocks at once - each costs coins you earn by clearing levels cleanly. There is no time limit, so take a moment to read the shape before you tap.

About the Game

Arrows Out 3D is a relaxing 3D puzzle game about clearing a solid made of paper blocks, one arrow at a time. Every block has an arrow printed on it. Tap a block and it slides the way its arrow points - if the path is clear it escapes, if another block is in the way it bumps back and you lose a heart. Turn the shape around, read every side, and work out the right order to clear them all. Features 50 handcrafted levels across 5 chapters, each with its own art style, shapes that grow from simple cubes into big tricky structures, up to 3 stars per level for a clean clear, and coins you can spend on Hint, Pluck and Magic Wand helpers when you get stuck. No timer and no rush, so you can take your time and think.
