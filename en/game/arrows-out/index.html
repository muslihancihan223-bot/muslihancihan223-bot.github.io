About the Game

Arrow Escape is a puzzle game that delivers fast-paced, strategic challenges for players who enjoy logic-driven gameplay and intense escape scenarios. Each level is a tricky arrow maze puzzle where you must carefully study the arrow paths and decide which arrow to tap first in order to clear the board. One correct move can open the way forward, while a single mistake can trigger a chain reaction that costs you a heart. As you progress, the game evolves into a full arrow maze escape experience featuring twisting paths, looping routes, and increasingly complex layouts. Some stages feel like isolated arrow-filled arenas where every direction represents either danger or opportunity, turning each decision into a tense test of planning and precision. The core mechanic revolves around tapping arrows out in the correct order. When arrows collide, you lose a heart, and with only three hearts available, every move matters. Make three mistakes and the attempt ends immediately, pushing you to think carefully before each tap.
