About the Game

Run your own tiny movie theatre! Grab fresh popcorn from the machine, stock the snack counter and ring up happy moviegoers. Every guest pays twice: once for snacks and once for a ticket when they take their seat. Messy seats slow the queue, so tidy up between films. Stand on a pad to spend your earnings: more seats, a soda fountain, a second popcorn machine, a manager's office with upgrades, and helpers who clean, serve and restock for you. Open Hall 2 with a bigger screen and pricier tickets. Finish the cinema and open the Drive-In, a whole new venue. Cars roll in, park in front of the giant screen and order snacks, and you carry popcorn, sodas and hot dogs right to the car window. Hire carhops and an attendant, widen the lot, and turn it into the best drive-in in town.
