How to Play

Fly with WASD or arrow keys, hold the mouse where you want to go, or drag on touchscreens. Release to brake. Stop inside rescue circles to pick people up, then land on H to deliver them. Visit fuel pads to refuel and service pads to buy upgrades. Rescue as many people as possible before your shift ends!

About the Game

Take the controls in Harbor Dispatch, the colorful helicopter rescue game. Fly across coastal roads and island waters, pick up stranded people, and bring them safely to hospital. Upgrade your helicopter to carry more passengers and fly faster. How many people you can rescue in each five-minute shift?
