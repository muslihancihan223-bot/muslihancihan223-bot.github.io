About the Game

A satisfying physics puzzle shooter packed with 100 handcrafted stages. Take control of a powerful cannon, line up the perfect shot, and launch heavy cannonballs into towers, stacks, and tricky block formations. Your goal is simple: knock every block off the golden base plate before your shots run out. Each stage challenges you to think carefully about angle, force, timing, and momentum. As the stages progress, the layouts become more complex, with tougher structures, awkward angles, and fewer chances to make mistakes.
