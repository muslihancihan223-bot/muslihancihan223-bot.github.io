About the Game

By day you farm. By night you fight for it. Farm Defenders is a farming sim wrapped around a last-stand survival shooter, and the two halves need each other. Your crops pay for your firepower. Your firepower protects your crops. Every wave you clear is bigger than the one before, and every coin you spend decides whether you see the next sunrise. 🌱 GROW YOUR FARM Plant, water and harvest your fields, then stock the storage to turn produce into coins. Hire farmhands to work the land while you plan, upgrade your storage, and unlock new plots as your profits grow.
