Play Glow Up Diary: Blogger Dress Up

Glow Up Diary: Blogger Dress Up is a free Girls, Dress Up, Fashion game. No download or sign-up needed — play instantly in your browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How to Play

Tap a category, then tap an item to put it on; tap it again or press Take off to remove it. Items with a sparkle match your followers' theme. Change the backdrop or decorate your room, switch day or night light, then press Publish, add a filter and stickers, and post the photo. The better the look fits the theme, the more stars, likes and followers you get. Open gifts, finish story chapters and win style battles against Nina to unlock new outfits.

About the Game

Dress up Mia, a young fashion blogger! Followers ask for a new look every day: Beach Day, Party Night, Back to School, Red Carpet and more. Choose from 150+ hairstyles, faces, makeup, dresses, tops, skirts, socks, shoes, hats, bags and jewelry, pick a photo backdrop or decorate Mia's own room, add filters and stickers and publish the photo. The better the look fits the theme, the more likes and followers you get, and fans send new clothes as gifts. Follow a 10-chapter blogger story, beat the rival Nina in style battles where followers vote, complete daily tasks, collect login gifts, enter the weekly contest and climb the ranking of fashion bloggers. Holiday collections for Halloween, New Year and Valentine's Day.
