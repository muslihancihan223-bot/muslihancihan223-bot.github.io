About the Game

Trash Baron: Turn trash into treasure! Have you ever thought that trash is not waste, but a resource? In Trash Baron, you will create a waste recycling empire. Start with a small landfill and turn it into a high-tech enterprise! Gameplay: ♻️ Collect and sort: Tons of waste - plastic, glass, metal, and rubber. The future of the environment and business is in your hands!
