How to Play

**PC** - WASD / arrows (ZQSD works too) — move, steer, carve - Shift — run; on the board, tuck for speed - Space — jump; on the board, ollie (hold for a grab); in a vehicle, handbrake - Mouse — look around (click the game first) - E or Enter — talk, get in and out of vehicles, use shops - B — snowboard on / off - X — throw the ice axe (climb, zipline) - F — punch; C — call your snowmobile or car; H — horn; R — flip a stuck vehicle - M — map; Tab or T — phone; P — pause (shops, settings, language) **Mobile:** floating joystick to move, drag to look; on-screen buttons for jump, board, axe, punch and action; steering arrows with gas and brake in a vehicle.

About the Game

Kai Holm comes home to Frostport for the winter: a harbour town under a ski mountain, a frozen lake, a fjord full of ice and a ski village at the top of the lift. On his first run a blast echoes off the peaks and the slope breaks loose. NorrCo — Magnus Strand's mining company — is blasting the mountain, drilling under the lake and buying the town house by house, and the Wolfpack, a snowmobile crew on its payroll, scares off anyone who will not sell. Ride the Black Run ahead of a wall of snow. Skim a snowmobile across open water from floe to floe. Climb ice with an axe where no police car can follow and zipline down the lift cables. Race Varg for Olga's stolen snowmobile, brawl your way through NorrCo's office for their plans, rob NorrCo's payroll from the village bank with Finn, steal their armoured snowcat and vanish into a blizzard when the whole town is after you. Twenty-one story missions take you to the summit — and the whole mountain coming down.
