How to Play

PC controls: • Left mouse button — pick up an item and drag it to the board or the shop • R or right mouse button — rotate the item before placing it • The "Fight" button — launch the battle Phone and tablet controls: • Tap an item — pick it up • Tap a board cell — place the item there • The round arrow next to the item — rotate it • Tap the sell zone — get rid of what you don't need General rules: • Drag two matching items onto each other to merge them into a stronger version • Pick a hero from the list before each new run (some heroes unlock with gems) • Watch your gold at the top of the screen — it's what you spend in the shop • Once the battle starts, everything plays out automatically — your strategy was already locked in during the shop phase

About the Game

Heroes Shop Battlers stocked with weapons, armor, and potions — raw materials of a real legend. Pick a hero, each with their own personality, passive gift, and signature move. Buy gear, merge matching pieces into something stronger, and arrange everything on the board so no tile goes to waste. Once your build feels right, the battle begins — strategy decides the outcome, not reflexes. Win, climb the rating, and push further: five losses end your run, ten wins in a row make you champion.
