How to Play

The goal of the game is to start with a small gas station and gradually grow it into a profitable empire. Maintain cars, upgrade equipment, build a store, cafe, and restroom, hire employees, and earn more and more money to become the boss of all gas stations!

About the Game

You start with a small gas station in the middle of the highway, where everything depends on you. Service cars, fill up with gas, earn coins, and build even bigger! Over time, your gas station will grow into a true empire: with a store, restroom, cafe, and even a hot dog delivery service. The more customers, the more profit, upgrades, and fun! Oversee your business, upgrade your stations, and hire employees to keep everything running smoothly. Even when you're offline, your gas station continues to earn! This isn't just a simulator—it's your path to success and the fun of managing your own mini-empire.
