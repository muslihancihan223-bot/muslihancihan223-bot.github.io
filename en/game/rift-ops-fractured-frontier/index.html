How to Play

Move with A and D, fly with W or Space, aim with the mouse and left-click to fire. R reloads, G throws a grenade, Z picks up a weapon crate, P pauses. On touch screens the left stick moves and flies and the right stick aims and fires. Gamepads are supported. Multiplayer: open Multiplayer, tap Host a match and share the room code. Friends enter the code under Join a match, press Ready, and the host starts the match.

About the Game

Strap on a jetpack and pick your side in the fight over the Echo Core. RIFT OPS: Fractured Frontier is a 2D cartoon tactical shooter with a full 100-mission story campaign, where Vanguard Guard and Eclipse Legion each get their own dialogue, decisions and endings. Host a room and share its code for team deathmatch with up to 10 players, on phones and computers. Train in bot practice on six maps, earn Credits, and build your loadout with weapons, attachments, gadgets and cosmetics.
