How to Play

Use your mouse or a finger to play. A surprise machine is chosen for you. Follow its short gesture hint or press the main button, and pick up your surprise. All 13 machines appear in a shuffled order before any repeats. Drag to rotate an egg. Tap to strike its shell, or pull a wrapper from top to bottom when prompted. Open the capsule, then drag every toy part onto its matching dotted outline. The body and other pieces can be placed in any order. Add eyes, a mouth and stickers by dragging them onto the toy. Place stickers anywhere you like, then send your finished friend to its themed shelf. Tap a collected friend for a closer look and press Open Egg to begin again. Bonus ads are optional; the main collection can be played without them.

About the Game

Crack open a surprise egg and meet your next little friend! Little Monsters Toy 3D is a colorful toy-collecting game where every discovery becomes a creation of your own. Discover 13 randomly chosen egg machines, from a claw grabber and Plinko race to a toy train, fishing pond and spiral slide. Peel wrappers, break shells and open capsules, then drag the toy pieces onto their outlines in any order. Add expressive eyes, a smile and stickers wherever you like. Collect 49 original monsters across six themed shelves, including candy, crystal, forest and ice friends. Watch each silhouette become your decorated toy, and discover optional bonus creatures and a special dream collection. Your collection is saved automatically, so you can return for another surprise.
