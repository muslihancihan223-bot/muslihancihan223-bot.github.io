How to Play

Warp: Press the W key or the Up Arrow key to warp or move upward quickly. Move: Use the A and D keys or the Left and Right Arrow keys to control movement in different directions. Reset: Press the R key at any time to restart or reset the current level.

About the Game

Portal Hop is a platform puzzle game where you carry a portal above your head and use it to solve tricky challenges! Move through each level by warping yourself onto higher platforms, collecting plus and minus power-ups to adjust the height of your portal.
