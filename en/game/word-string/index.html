How to Play

🔡 How to Play? 🔡 ✔️ Start with the first word. ✔️ The next word must either: 🔹 Form a compound word with the previous word (e.g., “Sun” → “Flower” → “Pot”) 🔹 Complete a well-known phrase (e.g., Orange → Juice ("Orange Juice") ✔️ Keep linking words until you complete the full chain!
