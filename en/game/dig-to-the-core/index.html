How to Play

1. Tap the rock to place a charge. The circle shows how big the crater will be. 2. Place all your charges, then press BLAST – they go off in a chain. 3. Blast next to treasures to collect them. Every crater and every treasure earns cash. 4. Open the SHOP to upgrade: a stronger charge, more charges per blast, a deeper drill, a stronger explosive or more miners. 5. Buy nukes for huge craters. The Tsar Bomba is the biggest of all. 6. Keep going deeper. The rock gets harder every zone, so keep upgrading – and reach the center of the Earth!

About the Game

How deep can you go? Dig to the Core is an explosive digging game where every blast takes you closer to the center of the Earth – 6,371 km below your feet. Place your charges anywhere on the rock, press BLAST and watch the ground crumble. Every crater earns cash, and every treasure you blow free earns even more. Spend it on bigger explosives, more charges and stronger blasts – then grab a nuke and open a crater the size of a city. Digging deeper is travelling back in time. Pass rotary phones from the 1920s, knights' helmets, Roman coins, Stone Age axes and mammoth tusks, then T-Rex teeth, Jurassic ammonites and 500-million-year-old trilobites – all the way down to the rock the Earth was born with. You're not alone down there. Your mining crew slides down after every blast to clear the rubble and sell it, and the lamplighter hangs big floodlights to light up the dark shaft. Hire more miners to earn even faster. But the Earth fights back. Every zone the rock gets harder and your craters shrink. Break through granite slabs, set off methane pockets, old wartime bombs and coal-dust chain reactions, and push on through the glowing mantle into the molten core.
