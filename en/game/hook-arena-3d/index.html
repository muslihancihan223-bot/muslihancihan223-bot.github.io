About the Game

Hook Arena is an action-packed game where you battle the opponents on opposite sides of a river using unique hook weapons. Your objective is to hook enemies to eliminate them one by one, pull in beneficial rune bottles, or escape by hooking yourself to a physical support like a pole. Before the match, you can choose your team's color, upgrade your character, and change their skin and weapon. In the treasure menu, you can hack and open chests with both a chest and a key. Who knows what secrets they hold, but all items will be placed in your inventory. Fight in matches, earn trophies, and climb the leaderboard rankings. Good luck in the battle!
