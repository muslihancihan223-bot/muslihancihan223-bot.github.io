About the Game

Lead your clan through a tactical samurai tower defense campaign. Between battles, choose your path across the campaign map, earn Honor, unlock new provisions, strengthen your wall, and prepare for tougher encounters. Each run pushes your army deeper into hostile territory, with stronger enemies, boss fights, and permanent upgrades waiting along the way.
