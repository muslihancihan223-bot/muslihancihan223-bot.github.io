玩法说明

Tap or click the cube whose colour is different from the rest before the time runs out. A correct pick adds a little time and drops in the next, harder board; a wrong tap costs 3 seconds. Each level fills the ADD TIME bar, and a full bar gives +5 seconds. Found cubes shatter into voxels: on the home screen, hold BUILD to spend them on the album picture. Stuck? HINT removes some of the wrong cubes. Out of time? Take a second chance to keep going. Touch or mouse only, no keyboard needed.
