玩法说明

Drag a cat from the shop onto the grass next to the track (or tap a cat, then tap the grass). Press Play to send the next wave of jellies. Popping jellies earns coins: spend them on more cats and upgrades. Tap a placed cat to upgrade it, change its target, use its ability or sell it. Don't let jellies reach the end of the track, or you lose lives. Survive every round to win! Keyboard: Space = play / speed up, 1-9, 0, - and = pick a cat, Esc = cancel or pause.
