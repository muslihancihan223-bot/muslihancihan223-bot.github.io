玩法说明

Animal Hospital 3D is a sandbox game: there is no single goal to win. Create a world, place characters, vehicles and items, build, experiment with physics and explosions, and have fun. Unlock extra worlds and items by watching a short video. Controls (computer): - W A S D: move - Mouse: look around (click the game to start) - Left mouse button: use the selected item (place, shoot, grab, throw) - Right mouse button: aim with guns / second action - Space: jump (go up in the helicopter) - Shift: run (go down in the helicopter) - 1–6: choose an item in the bar - Q: open the Items menu - R: rotate an item before placing it - F: use doors and levers, get in and out of vehicles - Esc: settings and pause Controls (phone and tablet): move with the joystick on the left, swipe to look around, and use the on-screen buttons for actions, jumping and the Items menu.
