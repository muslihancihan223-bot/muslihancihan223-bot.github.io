玩法说明

The goal of Yukon Solitaire is to move all cards to the foundation piles, sorted by suit (hearts, spades, diamonds, and clubs) in order from Ace to King. In this Yukon Solitaire game, you can move a group of cards, even if they’re not in order. Just make sure the first card of the group follows the rules. Stack the cards in descending order (King to Ace) and alternate colors. For example, a red card like a Queen can only follow a black card like a King. Unlike classic Solitaire, all the cards in Yukon Solitaire are dealt face-up, so you can see them all from the start!
