玩法说明

Tidy up the candy shop! Pick up gummies from the heaps, find the bookcase with their category’s sign, and place each one on a shelf of its own kind. A shelf takes only one kind of gummy. A gummy in the wrong place shows it, and you can take it back off any time. Every gummy sorted right earns money to spend on upgrades: more pocket space, longer reach, stronger throws, sprint, jump and special abilities. Fill every shelf in all 20 categories to make the shop spotless and win. Then try to beat your best time! Desktop Controls: W A S D / Arrow keys: Move. Mouse: Look around. Left Click: Pick up a gummy, or take one back off a shelf (hold to keep picking up). Right Click: Place the gummy in your hand on a shelf, or throw it. Mouse Wheel: Switch which gummy is in your hand. Tab: Open the upgrades. Esc: Pause. Shift: Sprint (once bought). Space: Jump (once bought). C: Crouch (once bought). 1, 2, 3: Use abilities (once bought). Mobile Controls: Joystick (left side): Move. Push it all the way to sprint (once bought). Drag anywhere else: Look around. Hand Button: Grab, place or throw, depending on what you’re aiming at (hold to keep grabbing). Swap Button: Switch which gummy is in your hand. Upgrades Button: Open the upgrades. Jump, Crouch and Ability Buttons: These appear once you buy them. Gamepads are supported too.
