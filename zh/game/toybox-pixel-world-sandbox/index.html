玩法说明

Open the character-and-blocks button, choose a category and an item, then click or tap an empty spot in the world to place it. Build structures, add characters and equipment, and use the crossed-swords button to switch bot battles on or off. Open the hammer menu to craft equipment when you have the required resources. Desktop: use the mouse to place and drag objects. Drag empty space to move the camera; use the mouse wheel or + / - to zoom. Press the character-control button and select a character: A / D or Left / Right to move, W to jump, Space or right mouse button to attack, and G to drop the held item. Mobile: tap the menus and world to place items, drag to interact, and use the on-screen movement, jump and action controls when controlling a character. Use + / - to zoom.
