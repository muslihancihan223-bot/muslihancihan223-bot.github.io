玩法说明

Your goal is to dig the mine, sell the loot and get richer. PC controls: • WASD — move, Space — jump, mouse — look around • hold the left mouse button on a block — dig • E — inventory, T — blow up TNT, H — climb out of the mine • Ctrl — free the cursor to press buttons Mobile controls: • left stick — move, arrow button — jump • swipe the screen — look around, hold your finger on a block — dig How to progress: 1) Go down into the mine in the middle of the village and dig blocks — they go into your bag. 2) Bring the loot to the market: villagers buy it for emeralds and give profitable orders. 3) Buy better tools at the forge, a new mine and upgrades at the office. 4) Dig out the whole mine to get a reward and a fresh mine with new ores. 5) Hire golems and collect their income when you come back. 6) Rebirth for stars and unlock new worlds. Tip: keep digging without pauses — combos give you more loot!
