玩法说明

The game controls depend on the device you are playing on. On PC: Use the WASD keys to control the ball. W (up) — moving forward. A (left) — turn left. S (down) is a backward movement. D (right) — turn to the right. The space bar is a jump. To use the improvements, click on the improvement icon on the left side of the screen. To buy a skin, click on the store icon in the menu. On a smartphone/tablet: On touch devices, control is performed using a virtual joystick located in the lower left part of the screen. Move your finger across the screen to control the movement of the ball. To jump, click on the button in the lower right part of the screen.
