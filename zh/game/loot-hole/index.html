玩法说明

Hold the left mouse button (or touch and hold on a phone) to dig where you aim. Walk near a find to put it in your backpack. When the backpack is full, press H to go back to the surface and walk into the yard stall to sell everything. Buy better tools and bigger backpacks at the tool shed to dig through harder layers and reach the bottom. Keyboard: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump (or use the jetpack), H to surface, B or I for the backpack, C for the collection, X to throw an explosive, Q to switch its kind. On phones and tablets, use the on-screen stick and buttons. Press O (or open Play together from the menu) to host a co-op yard, join a room by code, find an open yard, or enter a Neighbours race. Up to four players can dig in one co-op yard. P pauses.
