Snowed Inは無料のアーケード, アドベンチャー, シミュレーションゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

アーケードアドベンチャーシミュレーション

遊び方

Dig the snowed-in village of Sunnyhollow free. Dig snow, carry it to the penguin's ice cream truck and sell it for coins. Buy better tools and upgrades in the shop, clear all 14 houses and the town square, and help the villagers along the way. Your progress saves automatically. Keyboard and mouse: WASD / Arrow keys - Move Mouse - Look around Left click (hold) / F - Dig Space - Jump, C - Crouch 1-8 / Mouse wheel - Switch tool E - Use (sell snow, shop, sled, ladder, pet the dog) R - Refuel Q (hold) - Village wishlist, M - Map, Tab - Progress V - First / third person I - Play with friends T / X (hold) - Quick chat / emotes P - Pause Touch: Left side - Move (joystick), right side - swipe to look DIG, JUMP, CROUCH and USE buttons on screen, plus tool, map, chat and emote buttons
