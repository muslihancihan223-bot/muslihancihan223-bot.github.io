ストラテジーシミュレーションタイクーン経営ロジック

遊び方

Goal: complete each mission's objectives (shown at the top left) by building a working village: roads, homes, food and production chains, then soldiers to hold off bandits. Mouse: left click to select and build. Right click to send the selected soldiers (ground: move, bandits: attack, watchtower: man it). Hold and drag to move the map, or WASD / arrow keys. Mouse wheel: zoom. Esc: cancel or menu. Space: pause. 1-3: game speed. R: road tool. X: demolish. Touch: tap to select and build, drag to move the map, pinch to zoom. Tap a company to select it, double tap the ground or a target to send it. New to the game? Start the Campaign: the first missions teach you step by step.
