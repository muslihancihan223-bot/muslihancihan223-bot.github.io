Kaboom Minerで遊ぶ

Kaboom Minerは無料のアクション, アドベンチャー, シミュレーションゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

アクションアドベンチャーシミュレーション3D物理

遊び方

1. Tap the rock to place a charge. The circle shows how big the crater will be. 2. Place all your charges, then press BLAST – they go off in a chain. 3. Blast next to treasures to collect them. Every crater and every treasure earns cash. 4. Open the SHOP to upgrade: a stronger charge, more charges per blast, a deeper drill, a stronger explosive or more miners. 5. Buy nukes for huge craters. The Tsar Bomba is the biggest of all. 6. Keep going deeper. The rock gets harder every zone, so keep upgrading – and reach the center of the Earth!
