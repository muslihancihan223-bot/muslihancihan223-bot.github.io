遊び方

Goal: Slide every arrow out of the board. 1= Tap a free arrow and it slides away in the direction it points. 2= Blocked arrows: if another arrow or object is in the way, the arrow bumps and you lose 1 heart. Clear its path first. You get 3 hearts per level. 3= Bent arrows: the whole arrow follows its head out of the board. 4= Rocks never move. Find another way around them. 5= Ice: tap a frozen arrow once to break the ice, then tap again to slide it out. 6= Locks and keys: free the arrow that holds the key to open the matching lock. 7= Win when all arrows are out. Fewer mistakes means more stars (up to 3). Tools: 💡 Hint shows a safe arrow to tap. ↩ Undo takes back your last mistake. Use coins or watch an ad to get more. Controls: Mouse click or touch tap. The pause button opens the menu (sound, restart, home).
