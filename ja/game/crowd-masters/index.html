遊び方

Reach the finish with a bigger army than your rival. Desktop: A/D or the arrow keys to steer, or hold the mouse button and drag. Mobile: drag a finger left and right. Go through gates that add or multiply knights and avoid the ones that take them away. Steer around traps and outnumber enemy squads. A gate the rival takes first closes for you. On castle levels your army breaks the gate first, then fights the lord. Buy upgrades in the menu between levels.
