Burrow Ballsで遊ぶ

Burrow Ballsは無料のアーケード, アドベンチャー, 物理ゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

アーケードアドベンチャー物理放置

遊び方

Drag left and right (or move the mouse) to aim the drill cannon. Marbles fire on their own while the fuel lasts. Break rock for gems, aim at gem veins for big payouts and crack the glowing relics for stickers. Between dives, buy upgrades in the Workshop, then dive again. Each new biome you reach becomes your new starting point. When the rock gets too tough, Rebirth for fossils that make you stronger for good. The goal: reach the Heart of the World, 2.4 km down. Controls: mouse or touch to aim. Esc or P to pause.
