遊び方

Tap Play and pick a toy. Drag the tool over the toy to clean it: touch anywhere to pick the tool up. Fill the bar to finish each of the 6 cleaning steps. Then pick an outfit, tap Done and tap the camera to take a photo for your album. Tap Next Toy to clean the next one. Clean a toy to unlock the next; earn up to 3 stars per toy. Controls: mouse or touch (drag). No timer, no losing.
