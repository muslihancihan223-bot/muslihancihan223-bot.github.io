遊び方

Pick a toy in the menu; everything works with touch or mouse and there is no losing. A hand shows how to start each toy, the Next button (top right) goes to the next one and the house button returns to the menu. The Toy of the Day comes first in the menu and earns double all day. Relax tasks under the zen panel tell you what to try next. Balloons: tap or swipe to pop them, then hold on the helium tank to inflate one and let go to add it to the arch. Fireworks: tap the night sky to launch, drag to draw with sparks. Tissues: pull them up out of the box, then tap the last bundle to fluff it into a flower. Sheep: shave the wool, spin it into yarn, knit a sweater. Water Beads: squish and stir to find the hidden charms. Tidy Up: tap or drag each thing to its spot, then swipe the dust away. Peel: grab a tab or an edge of the film and pull. Clicky Keys: type the glowing letters, or tap the line to type freely. Sand: cut along the dotted lines with the knife. Soap: drag the knife any way you like. Pop It: pop every bubble, then the other side. Spinner: flick it; the faster it spins, the more paint flies. Squishy: press, hold and smear until all five hearts fill. My Room (button at the bottom of the menu): drag things into the room, hold the cat to pet it, tap Relax for rain and fireplace sounds. Sound and Music switches are at the bottom of the menu.
