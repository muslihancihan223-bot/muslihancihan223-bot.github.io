Monster Cafe: Idle Tycoonで遊ぶ

Monster Cafe: Idle Tycoonは無料のシミュレーション, タイクーン, 経営ゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

シミュレーションタイクーン経営癒し放置

遊び方

Mouse or touch only. Tap a monster's order bubble (or the machine) to serve it. Tap coin bags on tables to collect tips and tap lucky bats for bonus coins. Use the tabs at the bottom to upgrade machines, buy tables and decor, invite monsters and hire waiters who serve for you. The moon button opens your places, the Moon Shop and trophies. Goal: open all 5 places, fill the 60-sticker album and make Full Moon Moves to grow even faster.
