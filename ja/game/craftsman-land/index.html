遊び方

In Craftsman Land, playing is simple and intuitive. On mobile devices you tap the screen, while on PC you use the mouse. Actions are performed automatically – all you need are the required items and resources. With just a click, you can trigger different activities such as building houses, crafting tools, or producing goods. Complete quests given by townsfolk to earn rewards and progress in the story. When you run out of quests, head to the crossroads where the bulletin board offers an endless supply of new tasks. Explore the map to visit different shops: Building materials store – get everything you need for construction. Tool shop – find useful equipment for your farm. Gardening store – buy seeds and expand your fields. Not everything has to be bought! Many materials can be produced by yourself. Chop wood, fire clay into bricks at the campfire, and create other essential resources to use in crafting and construction. Grow crops, raise animals, trade, and craft your way to success while helping the entire town thrive!
