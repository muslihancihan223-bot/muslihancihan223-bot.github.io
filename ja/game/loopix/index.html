Loopixで遊ぶ

Loopixは無料の.io, アクション, アーケードゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

遊び方

Draw a closed loop around creatures with your comet trail to capture everything inside. Grow bigger, beat the Guardian of each world and jump to the next world through the black hole. Never touch another comet's trail. PC: Mouse - steer your comet Hold left click or Space - boost (you lose a little size) Arrow keys / A-D - steer with the keyboard Mobile: Drag your finger - steer Double tap and hold - boost
