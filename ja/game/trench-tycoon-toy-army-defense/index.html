遊び方

Spend gold on the cards at the bottom: Economy buildings earn gold, Army and Defenses stop the waves. Tap the Gold Mine to speed it up. Each wave crosses no-man's land to your forward trench, the front-line trench and then the wall; clear waves to earn bonuses and unlock new units. Press Lead to take control of a soldier or gun. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys move, mouse aims, click or Space fires, V or F switches first person, Q/E turn, C charges, G follow me, H hold, Esc lets go. Mobile: drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, stick to move and FIRE to shoot.
