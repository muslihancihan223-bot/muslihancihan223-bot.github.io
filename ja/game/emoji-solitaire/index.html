Emoji Solitaireで遊ぶ

Emoji Solitaireは無料のパズルゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

パズル

遊び方

Identify the Categories: Look at the active category foundations sitting at the top of the screen. Match & Stack: Drag word cards onto foundations matching their theme, or stack matching cards together on the tableau to reveal face-down cards underneath. Cycle the Deck: When stuck, tap the deck to draw fresh cards into your waste pile. Use open tableau spaces strategically to park cards temporarily. Clear the Board: Group all keywords into their respective category piles using your limited pool of moves to win the level!
