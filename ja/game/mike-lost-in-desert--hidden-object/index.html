遊び方

You have about 8 minutes to make your escape through five desert locations. In each location, you must find 10 hidden objects before moving on to the next area. If the time runs out, the bandits will catch you. If you manage to find all the objects in all five locations before the time ends, you will escape the desert and survive. Search carefully, stay focused, and move quickly. Your life depends on it.
