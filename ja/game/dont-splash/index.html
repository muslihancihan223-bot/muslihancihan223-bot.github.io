Don't Splashは無料のホラー, 3D, オビーゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

ホラー3Dオビーサバイバル

遊び方

Reach the exit portal at the end of each level. Run and jump from platform to platform and avoid falling into the water. If you fall in, swim to the nearest edge and climb out before the blind monster reaches the splash. Desktop: WASD to move, Space to jump or climb out, move the mouse to look around (the cursor is captured during play; click the screen to capture it again), 1-3 to use tools, F to push, R to return to the checkpoint, P to pause. Mobile: left stick to move, swipe to look, buttons to jump and use tools.
