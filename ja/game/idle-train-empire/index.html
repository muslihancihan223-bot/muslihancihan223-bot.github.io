遊び方

Develop your railway station by purchasing and upgrading trains, as well as customizing routes! Acquire and furnish various spaces and waiting halls! Unlock access to all railway platforms and destinations! Increase visitor traffic and enhance profits with every ticket sold! The primary genre of the game is Idle Tycoon. Complete daily and regular tasks, receive valuable rewards, and expand your train collection! As your station progresses, new railway platforms, destinations, and trains will become accessible! Wisely configure routes and maximize profits from each visitor! Allocate technology points to various station and train improvements! All of this will assist you in developing a large and profitable business!
