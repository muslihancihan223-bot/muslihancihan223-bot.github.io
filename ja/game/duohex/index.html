遊び方

Win 10 trophies before you run out of lives. In the shop, drag spells onto your spellbook with the mouse or touch; buy another copy to merge it, reroll for 1 gold, lock offers and sell spells you no longer need. Adjacent spells on the same page link together. Duels then play out automatically. In Duo, your AI partner shops and fights alongside you.
