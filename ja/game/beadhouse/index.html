パズルアーケード癒しロジック

遊び方

1. Study the picture and find beads that do not match the color of their current area. 2. Tap the misplaced beads to move them into the bead tray. 3. Select a bead from the tray, then tap a matching empty space to place it correctly. 4. Plan each move carefully, as the tray has limited space. 5. Use Fix Area, Clear Tray, or Magnet when you need assistance. 6. Finish the picture before time runs out to earn stars and coins. 7. View completed artwork, save favorites, and replay the finishing animation in the Gallery.
