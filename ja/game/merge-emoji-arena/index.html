Merge Emoji Arenaは無料のクリッカー, マージゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

遊び方

Your main objective is to defend against endless waves of increasingly difficult enemy emojis and challenging bosses. Defeat enemies to earn gold, which you use to constantly expand and upgrade your army! Buy Units: Click the "Buy Unit" button to spawn a basic emoji onto your merge board. Merge & Evolve: Drag and drop two identical emojis onto each other to merge them into a higher-level, more powerful unit with greater damage. Deploy to Battle: Drag your strongest emojis from the merge board into one of the three battle lanes to start attacking the enemies. Controls: The game is played entirely with simple Left-Click (or tap/drag on mobile) controls. Use your mouse or finger to buy, drag, and merge units!
