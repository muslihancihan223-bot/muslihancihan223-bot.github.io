E-Rickshaw Simulatorで遊ぶ

E-Rickshaw Simulatorは無料のシミュレーション, 3D, 車ゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

シミュレーション3D車ドライブ

遊び方

Drive your e-rickshaw through the city and follow the map and distance indicator to reach each destination. Pick up passengers from the marked locations and safely drop them off at their destination. Avoid hitting pedestrians, vehicles, and other obstacles, as crashes can reduce your health or cause the mission to fail. Complete missions to earn money and unlock additional e-rickshaws. Controls: W / Up Arrow – Accelerate S / Down Arrow – Brake / Reverse A / Left Arrow – Steer left D / Right Arrow – Steer right Follow the mini-map and destination markers to complete each mission.
