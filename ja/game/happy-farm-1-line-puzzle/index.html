遊び方

Draw One Line to Save the Farm! Become a pipe-connecting master in Happy Farm 1 Line Puzzle! In this charming one-line puzzle game, your only goal is to draw a single continuous line that connects all the water pipes on the farm and makes the water flow. With clever spatial thinking and just one stroke, you’ll solve every level and bring life back to the vibrant cartoon farm filled with blooming flowers, cute animals, and relaxing scenery. Desktop Controls: Mouse: Click and drag to draw one continuous line. Left Mouse Button: Hold to draw, release to finish the line. Mobile Controls: Finger: Tap and drag across the screen to draw one continuous line. No extra buttons — just one smooth stroke to win!
