女の子着せ替え癒し

遊び方

Dress up four best friends for the spookiest Halloween party of the year! How to play: 1. Press Play and choose a bestie on the level select. 2. Open the wardrobe tabs – Dresses, Hair, Shoes, Accessories, Necklaces and Earrings – and tap an item to put it on. Tap the arrows to switch between categories. 3. Pick one item from every tab to fill the pumpkin meter. 4. Press Done to show off her look. Fill every wardrobe tab to earn 3 stars and unlock the next bestie. Need ideas? Tap "Surprise me" for a random outfit, or "Take everything off" to start again. Items with an AD badge unlock by watching a short video. Take a photo of your favourite look, and style all four besties to finish the game. Controls: Left mouse button – click to choose items and buttons. On mobile, tap with your finger.
