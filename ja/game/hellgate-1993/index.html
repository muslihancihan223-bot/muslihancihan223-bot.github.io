遊び方

How to Play - Move: WASD - Look: Mouse - Fire: Left Mouse Button - Use doors, switches, and terminals: E - Switch weapons: 1–5 or mouse wheel - Open automap: M - Pause: Esc - Fullscreen: F On mobile, use the left stick to move, drag the right side of the screen to look, and tap the on-screen controls to fire, interact, switch weapons, open the map, or pause. Look for keycards and terminals to unlock new areas. Explosive barrels can damage enemies but will also hurt you at close range. If your health or ammo becomes critical, you may use a Reward Ad once per attempt for an Emergency Supply. After death, one Reward Ad can restore you with a Revive.
