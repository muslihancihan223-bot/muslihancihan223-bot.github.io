Restore Labで遊ぶ

Restore Labは無料のシミュレーション, タイクーン, クリッカーゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

遊び方

1. Tap an item on a workbench to repair it and earn coins. Tap quickly to fill the rush meter for double income. 2. Press Upgrade to raise a bench's level and earnings. At levels 25, 50, 100, 200 and beyond the bench works twice as fast. 3. Hire a helper so the bench works on its own, including while you are offline. 4. Open new benches for more valuable items, and buy Tools for permanent multipliers. 5. Complete Goals for bonus coins. Use Buy x1 / x10 / MAX to upgrade in bulk. 6. Open the Map to move to a new district when you have earned enough: income is multiplied and you keep a permanent perk. 7. When a collector arrives, repair the item by hand: pick a tool and drag over the object to wash, scrape, glue and polish. Controls: mouse or touch. Progress saves automatically. Language: English or Russian in Settings.
