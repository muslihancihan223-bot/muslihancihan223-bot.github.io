Baker City: Family Bakeryは無料のシミュレーション, 女の子, 料理ゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

遊び方

1. Click the oven and other generators to get flour, dough, and ingredients. 2. Drag two matching items together to create a higher-level treat. 3. Collect the required items and complete a customer’s order to earn coins and fame. 4. Open the city map, earn neighborhood trust, take on timed deliveries, and beat the clock. 5. Build new branches, upgrade your vans, and expand your bag to keep your kitchen running faster.
