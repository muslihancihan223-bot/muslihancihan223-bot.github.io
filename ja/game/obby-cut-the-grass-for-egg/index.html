Obby Cut the Grass for Eggは無料のアドベンチャー, 3D, マルチプレイゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

遊び方

Move with WASD and look with the mouse. Click (or tap) to cut grass and gain Strength, follow the arrows to the eggs and bring them home. Press E to interact with shops and your farm, B to return to base. On phones use the joystick and the on-screen buttons. Booster pads near the spawn give x2 Speed or x2 Strength for a short video.
