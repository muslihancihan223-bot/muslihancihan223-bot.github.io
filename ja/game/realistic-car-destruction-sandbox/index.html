遊び方

Choose an available vehicle and environment, then explore, attempt jumps, and test different crashes. This is a sandbox: set your own challenges and repair your car to experiment again. Desktop controls: WASD or arrow keys - drive Space - handbrake Left Shift - boost R - repair the vehicle C - change camera Mouse - look around F - enter or exit a vehicle Mobile controls: Use the on-screen driving and action buttons. Drag on an empty part of the screen to rotate the camera. Pinch with two fingers to adjust the view.
