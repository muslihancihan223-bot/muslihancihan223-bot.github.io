遊び方

🎓 Complete the tutorial and launch your first Campaign mission. After your first run, choose your next mission on the Atlas. 🃏 Before a new run, open DECK to customize your starting cards. Chips unlock permanent Protocol choices or rent cards for one run. ⚔️ In battle, inspect enemy intents and click or tap enemies to read their dossiers. Select a card, then its target, or drag it onto an enemy or the battlefield. Cards cost energy; use attacks, Block and effects to survive. Press “End Turn” when ready. Energy refills each turn. 🗺️ Follow the map connections. Choose battles, events, shops and repairs. Take cards that fit your strategy and defeat the boss to clear the area or act. 🦾 Equip earned Modules between runs to strengthen your Operator in Campaign and Open Breach. Classic and Certified become available as you progress. 💾 Use “Save and quit” in the run menu, then “Continue” to resume. Weekly Breach saves expire at the weekly reset.
