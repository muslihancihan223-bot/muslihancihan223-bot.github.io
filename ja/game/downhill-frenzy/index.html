Downhill Frenzyで遊ぶ

Downhill Frenzyは無料のアクション, レース, アーケードゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

アクションレースアーケードスキル3D

遊び方

On a phone or tablet: - Drag left and right anywhere on the screen to steer. - In the air, hold one of the four trick bubbles. Let go before you land. - In the air, drag up or down to flip, sideways to spin. - On the ground, tap KICK when a rider is beside you. - Fly through the blue bottle above a big jump for nitro. On a keyboard: - Left and right arrows steer. - In the air: Z, X, C and V do tricks, up and down arrows flip, left and right arrows spin. - On the ground: Q and E kick left and right. - P or Escape pauses. Finish in the top three, beat the trick score and win the race to earn all three stars on each hill. Stars open the next hill; coins buy new bikes, tricks and colours.
