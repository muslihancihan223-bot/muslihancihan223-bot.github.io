遊び方

Survive until New Year: three months, each with waves, an elite and a boss. Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Left-click to swing the sword toward the cursor (J, K or Enter also work) and hold to keep swinging. Click again while the blade is in the golden zone at the end of the swing to parry: foes are knocked back and sparks fly back at them. Right-click or Space rolls toward the cursor, and nothing can hit you mid-roll. On phones use the joystick on the left, the sword button to attack and the arrow button to roll. Pick one of three cards on each level up. The Training Yard in the menu teaches every move. P or Esc pauses.
