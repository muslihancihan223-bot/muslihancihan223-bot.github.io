遊び方

How to Play Place dogs — Double-tap a tile to place a dog. Each row, column, and colored region needs exactly one. No touching — Dogs can never touch — not even diagonally. Illegal placements are rejected. Mark exclusions — Single-tap or swipe across empty tiles to exclude them with an X as you deduce. Use hints — Tap the lightbulb hint for 50 coins — it reveals one forced dog and updates each move. Win the duel — Solve more boards than your rival before the five-minute timer ends.
