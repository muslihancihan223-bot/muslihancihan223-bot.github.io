Speed Rush Simulator で遊ぶ

Speed Rush Simulator は無料のシミュレーション, 3D, オビーゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

シミュレーション3Dオビー

遊び方

PC: • WASD or arrow keys — move. • Space — jump. • E — interact with a training station or open an egg. • R — open three eggs by watching a rewarded ad. • Hold the mouse button and drag — rotate the camera. • Mouse wheel — zoom. • Shift — capture or release the mouse cursor. • Esc — release the cursor. During races, running is automatic. Use A/D or the left/right arrows to steer, and Space to jump. Mobile: • Play in landscape mode. • Use the left joystick to move. • Tap the right jump button while holding the joystick to run and jump together. • Swipe on the game world to rotate the camera. • Tap the on-screen buttons to interact and open menus. Use the fullscreen button to enter fullscreen mode.
