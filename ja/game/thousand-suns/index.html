Thousand Sunsで遊ぶ

Thousand Sunsは無料のストラテジー, 戦争ゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

ストラテジー戦争

遊び方

Left Click a planet or starbase you control = select it Left Click another planet or starbase while one is selected = deploy your fleet there — attack if it's enemy or neutral, reinforce if it's your own Left Click your selected planet again = deselect it Click and Drag from one of your planets to another = connect a ferry lane, so newly produced ships automatically flow to the destination Left Click an existing ferry lane = disconnect it Left Click the pulsing arrow above a leveled-up planet = choose your next upgrade (Industry / Agriculture / Research) TAB = quit to the main menu
