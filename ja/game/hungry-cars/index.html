Hungry Carsで遊ぶ

Hungry Carsは無料の.io, レース, 3Dゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

.ioレース3D車

遊び方

Go to cars with a lower power level to eat them and get stronger. Avoid stronger cars or get damaged. Desktop Controls: ↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward. ↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward. ← (Left Arrow): Move left. → (Right Arrow): Move right. Mobile Controls: On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
