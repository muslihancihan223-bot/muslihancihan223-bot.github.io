Shape Jamは無料のパズル, スキル, マッチゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

パズルスキルマッチ脳トレ

遊び方

Clear the board by collecting 3 matching shapes. When you make a set of 3 same shapes, they get cleared. Tap a shape to pick it. The picked shape goes into your pick/holding slots at the top (the small empty circles). Make a triple: If you collect 3 of the same shape in your slots, they clear automatically. Don’t fill the slots: Your pick area/slots are limited, so the order you pick shapes matters.
