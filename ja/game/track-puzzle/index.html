Track Puzzleで遊ぶ

Track Puzzleは無料のロジック, 脳トレ, アーケードゲームです。ダウンロードも登録も不要で、スマホ・タブレット・パソコンのブラウザですぐに遊べます。

ロジック脳トレアーケードパズル

遊び方

Read the numbers: each number shows how many track cells are in that row or column. A 0 means no track in that line. Mark cells: tap a cell once to mark it X (no track), tap again to mark it ? (track). Lay the track: drag across ? cells to connect them into one continuous track from Start to Goal. Drag along a track to erase it. Deliver gems: the train picks up a gem when it passes right next to it, and delivers it when it passes a house of the same color. The track must reach the gem before the house. Watch your wagons: each wagon holds one gem. When they are all full, the train can’t pick up more. Special tiles: tunnels connect their two ends, switches open gates of the same color, and one-way tiles must be passed in the arrow’s direction. Earn 3 stars: clear a level without using Hint or Undo for a perfect 3-star score. Controls: mouse or touch.
