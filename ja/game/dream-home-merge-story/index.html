遊び方

Merge two identical items on the board to create a higher-level item. Keep combining items to produce the objects needed for your current orders. 1. Find two identical items and merge them. 2. Create higher-level items. 3. Complete the orders shown at the top of the screen. 4. Earn stars for completed tasks. 5. Return to the room and spend stars on renovations and upgrades. 6. If you run out of energy, wait for it to recover or refill it using an available option. Try not to fill the entire board: free cells are needed for new items and future merges.
