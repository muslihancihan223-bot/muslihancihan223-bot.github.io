遊び方

Choose a slime hero, defeat enemy waves, collect experience, and pick upgrade cards to build powerful ability combinations. Open artifact chests and survive increasingly challenging battles, solo or in online co-op with up to 4 players. Desktop: Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space to dash and Esc to pause. Attacks use auto-aim by default. Press Shift to switch between auto-aim and manual aiming. You can also left-click to aim at the cursor and right-click to return to auto-aim. Use number keys 1–4 for manual abilities. Mobile: Play in landscape orientation. Use the left joystick to move, tap the dash button to dash, and touch and drag on the right side of the screen to aim manually. Use the on-screen buttons for manual abilities.
