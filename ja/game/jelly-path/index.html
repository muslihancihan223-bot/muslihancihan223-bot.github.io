パズルアーケード脳トレロジック

遊び方

Guide your squishy companion to its cushion! Your main objective is to plan your moves carefully across the grid to reach the goal while collecting stars to perfect your score. Every step you take leaves a solid trail behind, and navigating carefully is key because you cannot cross over your own path. Think ahead, find the most efficient route, and avoid trapping yourself to win! Desktop Controls: Keyboard: W / ↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward. S / ↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward. A / ← (Left Arrow): Move left. D / → (Right Arrow): Move right. Mouse: Left Click, Drag, Leave: Swipe up, down, left, or right anywhere on the screen to move the jelly in that direction. Mobile Controls: Touch: Swipe up, down, left, or right anywhere on the screen to move the jelly cell by cell in that direction.
