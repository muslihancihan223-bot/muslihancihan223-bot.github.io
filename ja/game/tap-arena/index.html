遊び方

Tap the yellow TAP button to gain strength and coins. Your fighter attacks automatically. Spend coins on Tap Power, Auto Train and pet eggs. Defeat each boss within 20 seconds to reach the next zone; a timed-out fight can be retried. Claim one free Daily Egg per day. Rebirth unlocks after reaching Zone 6 and resets your current run for permanent strength bonuses. Optional rewarded ads offer a free egg or a temporary strength boost. Continue restores your saved game; New Game asks before resetting it. Use the speaker button to mute sound and the globe to change language. Where supported, the trophy button opens Best Zone and Max Strength leaderboards.
