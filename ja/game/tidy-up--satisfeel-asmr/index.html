Bring Order to the Chaos! Step into a calming world of satisfying puzzles where your goal is to sort, organize, and place every item in its proper spot. Left Click: Pick up, move, and place objects. Scroll Wheel: Zoom or navigate if supported. Mobile Controls: Tap and Drag: Move items into the correct position. Tap: Select or place objects. Swipe: Navigate the scene if supported. Sort everything correctly to complete the level and unlock new organizing challenges.
