遊び方

Outrun the police for as long as you can. Drive through BLUE gates to gain speed and avoid RED ones - if your speed drops too low, the police catch you. Use nitro to break away, and spend your cash on upgrades between runs. Desktop: A / D or Left / Right arrows to change lane, Space for nitro, Esc to pause. In mouse mode: point at a lane to move there, click for nitro. Mobile: tap the left or right side of the screen to change lane, tap the bolt button for nitro.
