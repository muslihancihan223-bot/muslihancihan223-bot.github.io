Nasıl Oynanır?

Keep your bike balanced, control your speed, and stay stable on bumps and slopes. The physics are realistic, so smooth riding matters. Hit jumps, do stunts, and ride clean to earn more points. Use the points you gain from riding to upgrade things like stamina and strength (so you can ride longer and feel stronger).

Oyun Hakkında

Bicycle Mountain Xtreme is a relaxing but challenging mountain biking game where you ride on epic trails, perform cool tricks, earn points, and unlock new trails as you get better. The game feels smooth and fun because it uses realistic physics, has procedurally generated trails, and includes a day/night cycle plus a dynamic weather system that changes while you ride.
