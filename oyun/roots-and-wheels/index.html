Nasıl Oynanır?

Get behind the wheel of the powerful military Ural in this exciting simulation game! The driver of the military Ural challenges you not just to drive, but to deliver valuable cargo skillfully. Your task is to drive through complex tracks and bring to the finish line as many boxes as possible, using your agility to maneuver and wit to find the best solutions in difficult situations off-road. The controls are intuitive, whether you're playing on your phone or computer. On mobile devices you control the movement with touch pedals: right - forward, left - backward. On the computer you use the keys: D for forward and A for backward. Earn money. Invest it in improvements for your machines. Or save up to buy new cars - each with its own unique capabilities, ready for the most extreme tests. Expand your fleet, improve your skills and become the best off-road driver!

Oyun Hakkında

This exciting simulator presents you with an ambitious task: get behind the wheel of a powerful truck and transport as many crates as possible through dangerous and unpredictable locations to your destination. Success depends on your skill—your ability to feel the vehicle and react to road conditions—and your wits—your ability to find the best solutions in difficult situations, whether it's mud, steep climbs, or destroyed bridges. Every box delivered brings you closer to your reward. By earning money for completed trips, you get the opportunity to upgrade your truck – from the engine to the suspension to the wheels – and better equip it for the challenges of the terrain. You can also buy new vehicle models, expanding your options and gaining access to new types of missions and cargo. Show what a real driver can do! Translated with DeepL.com (free version)
