Nasıl Oynanır?

Goal: Slide every arrow out of the board. 1= Tap a free arrow and it slides away in the direction it points. 2= Blocked arrows: if another arrow or object is in the way, the arrow bumps and you lose 1 heart. Clear its path first. You get 3 hearts per level. 3= Bent arrows: the whole arrow follows its head out of the board. 4= Rocks never move. Find another way around them. 5= Ice: tap a frozen arrow once to break the ice, then tap again to slide it out. 6= Locks and keys: free the arrow that holds the key to open the matching lock. 7= Win when all arrows are out. Fewer mistakes means more stars (up to 3). Tools: 💡 Hint shows a safe arrow to tap. ↩ Undo takes back your last mistake. Use coins or watch an ad to get more. Controls: Mouse click or touch tap. The pause button opens the menu (sound, restart, home).

Oyun Hakkında

Arrow Out: Puzzle Escape is a colourful and relaxing brain puzzle game for kids and adults. Every level is a board full of cute arrows with funny faces. Your goal is simple: tap an arrow to slide it out of the board and set every arrow free. But be careful. Arrows block each other, so you need to think about the right order! Start with easy boards and learn the basics. Then try bent arrows that follow their head out, solid rocks that never move, frozen arrows that need two taps, and locked arrows that open only with a key. Each chapter brings fresh ideas and a new look, from sweet candy lands to the ocean, a meadow, a sunset sky and outer space. Stuck? Use a hint or undo your last move. Earn coins and stars, spin the lucky wheel, claim daily gifts and play the Daily Puzzle every day. Spend coins in the shop to unlock new board themes, arrow colour styles and funny arrow faces. Easy to learn, satisfying to master. Play free in your browser on mobile and desktop, in English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano, Türkçe and Bahasa Indonesia.
