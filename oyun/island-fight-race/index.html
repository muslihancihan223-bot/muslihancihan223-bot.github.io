Oyun Hakkında

Get ready for an exciting mix of high-speed racing and intense vehicle combat! Choose your favourite vehicle, compete against rivals, use powerful weapons and special abilities, and prove yourself on challenging tracks. 4 Exciting Game Modes: • Race: Complete the laps and reach the finish line first. • Fight: Attack your opponents and fight to survive. • Flag Chase: Chase the moving flag and be the first to catch it. • Meetup: Explore freely, meet other players, and enjoy unlimited free roaming. Weapons & Power-Ups: Use Rockets, Multi-Rockets, Bombs and Shields to attack or defend. Collect Fuel, Springs, Wrenches and Life items to recover and stay in the action. Activate Nitro, Shockwave and unique vehicle abilities to gain an advantage. More Features: • Multiple vehicles with unique special abilities. • Garage and Shop to unlock and customize your collection. • Collect coins, complete missions and earn rewards. • Leaderboards and player profiles. • Play with friends, create or join custom rooms, and chat. • Challenging tracks and exciting driving action. Drive fast. Fight hard. Chase the victory!
