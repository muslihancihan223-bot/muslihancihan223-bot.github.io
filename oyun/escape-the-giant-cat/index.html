Escape the Giant Cat Oyna

Escape the Giant Cat ücretsiz bir 3d, koşu, platform, obby oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

3DKoşuPlatformObby

Nasıl Oynanır?

Computer: move with WASD or the arrow keys, look around with the mouse, jump with SPACE (three jumps in a row make a super jump). In fight rooms, click or press SPACE to hit; hit fast for a three-hit combo. E uses a shop, the rebirth altar or a chest; P opens the shop, R rebirth, L achievements, O options, H goes back to the plaza and ALT frees the mouse. Phone and tablet: move with the stick on the left, drag to look and tap JUMP (it becomes HIT in fight rooms). Goal: run on the carpets to gain speed, open the barrier of each stage, claim trophies and reach the end of the Kitchen.

Oyun Hakkında

Escape the Giant Cat is a running obby inside a giant house. Every step on the soft carpets adds speed, and speed opens the barrier of the next stage. Your steps leave a glowing line on the carpet: follow your own line on the next run and every step is worth 50% more. Run through the Living Room, the Playroom and the Kitchen, jump over gaps and furniture, dodge paws, yarn balls and pencils, and survive the giant cat when it peeks over the walls and slams its paw in front of you. Some rooms lock their doors: punch the cats with three-hit combos to open the way. Claim trophies, spend your wins on trails, auras, pets and outfits, and rebirth for a bigger multiplier.
