Nasıl Oynanır?

1. Choose a picture from the collection. 2. Select a color from the palette. 3. Find the areas marked with the matching number. 4. Click or tap the corresponding areas to fill them with color. 5. Complete the picture to earn rewards. 6. Unlock new pictures and expand your collection. 7. Earn points and climb the leaderboard. Controls: Use your mouse to select colors and fill areas on a computer, or tap the screen on mobile devices.
