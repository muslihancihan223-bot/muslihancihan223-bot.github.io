Nasıl Oynanır?

Goal: score more points than your opponent by building words on the board. The game ends when the bag is empty and one player uses up their rack; the highest score wins. Mouse or touch - everything is drag and tap, no keyboard required: - Drag a tile from your rack onto the board to place it; drag it again to move it - Undo lifts the last tile, Undo-all takes the whole turn back - Play commits your word and scores it - Pass gives up the turn; Exchange swaps chosen tiles back into the bag - Shuffle re-orders your rack, Hint suggests a move Words must connect to what is already on the board, and every word you form is checked against the dictionary of the chosen language. Premium squares multiply a letter or the whole word, and using all your tiles in one turn earns a bonus.

Oyun Hakkında

LetterHold is a classic word board game: draw letter tiles, build words crossword-style on a 15x15 board, and take the premium squares before your opponent does. You see what a word is worth while you lay the tiles, so a turn is a decision and not a guess. Stretch a word through a triple, hook onto a word already on the board and score both, or play seven tiles at once for a 40-point bonus. Two hints and two wildcards per game get you out of a stuck rack. Play it however you like: - Solo against up to three bots: easy, medium or hard - Pass-and-play on one screen, two to four players - Online with a friend, by invite or room code, or let the game find you an opponent - Watch a live online game Three languages, English, Ukrainian and Polish, each with its own tiles and word list. Every word is checked on the spot. Finish a game and the next one is one tap away.
