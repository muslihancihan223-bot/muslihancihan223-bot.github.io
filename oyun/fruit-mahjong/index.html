Oyun Hakkında

Fruit Mahjong is mahjong solitaire with a twist you can see from every angle. Juicy fruit tiles are stacked into layered arrangements in front of an ancient jungle temple — and the match you're hunting for might be sitting on the far side. Drag to orbit the board, spot the pair, clear the stack. One six-minute clock runs across all 50 levels. Every board you clear buys you ten more seconds, so the deeper you go, the tighter it gets. Match fast to build a combo: each pair in a chain scores more than the last, and the match chime climbs in pitch as your streak grows — break the chain and the multiplier resets. Boxed in? Burn a hint to clear a pair for you, or shuffle the remaining tiles into a fresh layout. Run out and you can watch a short video to earn another. Your best score is saved, so every run is a chance to beat it. Tags: mahjong, match, puzzle, solitaire, 3D, fruit, casual, timed, brain Rules Clear the board by matching two identical fruit tiles. A tile can only be taken if it has a clear side. Tiles hemmed in on both left and right, or both front and back, are locked until you clear around them. Pick a tile, then pick its match.
