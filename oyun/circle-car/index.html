Become the Ultimate Traffic Master! Step into the high-stakes world of traffic control in Circle Traffic Car! Your main objective is to guide a queue of vehicles into a busy 3D circular intersection without causing a crash. Time your moves with precision to merge every car seamlessly into the moving traffic loop. Pay close attention to vehicle speeds, spacing, and tight timing gaps. Safely merge all queued cars onto the track to clear the level and advance to higher challenges! How to Win: - Analyze the Traffic Loop: Observe the speed and rhythm of vehicles already circling the roundabout. - Time Your Move: Launch the next vehicle at the perfect moment to merge safely into an open gap. - Avoid Crashes: A single collision will cause a traffic pile-up and fail the level! - Clear the Queue: Successfully send every car in your queue onto the track to win the level. Desktop Controls: - Left Mouse Click / Spacebar / Up Arrow (↑) / W Key: Launch the next car into the traffic loop / Start or retry level. - Mouse Drag (Click & Drag): Rotate and orbit the 3D camera around the intersection for the best angle. - Mouse Scroll Wheel: Zoom in and out of the track. Mobile Controls: - Tap Screen: Tap anywhere on the screen to launch the next car / Start or retry level. - One-Finger Drag / Swipe: Drag across the screen to rotate and orbit the 3D camera view. - Pinch (Two Fingers): Pinch in or out to adjust camera zoom.
