Nasıl Oynanır?

GOAL Enter at the green START in the middle of a base edge and work inward and upward to the golden SUMMIT at the centre. Reach it to clear the level, and you're carried straight into the next one. The pyramids never run out. DIFFICULTY Pick one at the start, or change it from the menu. Rookie: 10 beacons and nothing chasing you. Scout: 6 beacons and a slow chaser. Ranger: 3 beacons and a faster one. Ace: no beacons and the fastest chaser. MOVE Arrow keys or WASD, or the orange arrows at the bottom left on touch. Movement follows your view. CAMERA Drag to turn and tilt, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan. Q and E turn, + and - zoom, C snaps back to the level's framing. On a phone the pad at the bottom right turns and zooms. BEACONS AND THE CHASER Cyan rings along the route are checkpoints. A red ball hunts you through every corridor, always a little slower than you. If it catches you, you restart from your last beacon, or from the base if you haven't passed one. COINS Roll into a coin or tap it to collect it. Coins are worth more the higher they sit. Spend them on HINT, which lights the route to the summit for a few seconds, or FREEZE, which stops the chaser for 8 seconds. Hint can also be had for free by watching a short ad; the button says so before you press it. RACE ONLINE Press RACE ONLINE on the start card or in the menu, then QUICK RACE to be matched or PLAY WITH FRIENDS to share a room code. Everyone starts together and the first ball to the SUMMIT wins. Other players' balls pass straight through yours. One red chaser waits a third of the way up the route, sets off just after GO and hunts whoever is nearest: the ball it catches is sent 30 steps back down the route and left alone for 10 seconds, then it goes after someone else. There is no hint and no freeze, and a coin picked up by anyone reappears somewhere else. LEAVE puts you back on your level. OTHER CONTROLS The menu (☰) holds sound, hint, freeze, difficulty, the daily level, restart, save and load, themes and help. Progress saves automatically.

Oyun Hakkında

A 3D maze that IS a mountain. Roll a glowing ball up a stepped square pyramid, climbing terrace by terrace from the base edge to the golden summit — the maze wraps all four faces, so solving it and climbing it are the same act. A red chaser hunts you through the same open corridors you use, and catching you sends you back to your last beacon. Gold coins are scattered across all four sides, most of them off the safe route, so every detour is a gamble — spend them on a hint or on freezing the chaser. Race online against up to three other players on one pyramid: quick match, or a private room with a code for friends, with bots taking any empty seats. One red chaser hunts the whole field and knocks whoever it catches back down the route. First ball to the summit wins. Start small: the first three pyramids are quick warm-up climbs that grow with you, then 22 full-size hand-built pyramids, and after that the climb never ends — every level is a fresh pyramid, without limit. Four difficulties, from a relaxed Rookie climb with no chaser to Ace with no checkpoints at all, a daily challenge, best times for every level, and four themes from desert sandstone to midnight neon.
