Leap and Avoid 2 Oyna

Leap and Avoid 2 ücretsiz bir arcade, platform oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

ArcadePlatform

Nasıl Oynanır?

The new legendary adventure of the white ball! This time there are even more caves and even more laboratory levels. New mechanics, great sound and a nice visual design are all in the new "Leap and Avoid 2" game! The innovation is coins and secret passages. There are secret paths leading to coins all over the world. Collect coins to get nice bonuses in the form of an acceleration of the ball and shield for one touch with hazards! The more coins you collect, the higher your place in the leaderboard will be. Compete with your friends and find all the hidden coins! How to play: Your goal is to get through all hazards in your way to escape! To do this, move to the right, jumping on white platforms and dodging black platforms. Sometimes you will need to move to the left to jump to the exit or to the hidden paths. Desktop controls: ─ Arrows (←) (→) left and right or "A" and "D" ─ ball movement; ─ "R" button ─ restart; ─ "ESC" button ─ toggle pause; ─ "LEFT MOUSE BUTTON" ─ control buttons in the menu. Mobile controls: ─ Tap the right and left half of the screen to move the ball; ─ Tap the buttons in the menu to press them.
