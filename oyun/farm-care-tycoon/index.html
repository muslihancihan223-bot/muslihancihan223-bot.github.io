Nasıl Oynanır?

🎮 Move: drag on the screen (mobile) · WASD or arrow keys, or click and drag (desktop) 🌱 Farm: walk onto your fields to plant and harvest crops. 🧺 Carry: you pick up goods automatically. The counter at the top shows how much you're carrying. 🛒 Stock and sell: bring goods to the matching shelves. Customers buy them and pay at the checkout. 💵 Collect: walk over the cash piles to grab your money. 🔓 Expand: stand on an unlock spot to spend money on new fields, animals and factories. ⬆️ Upgrade: open the Upgrade menu to improve your Farms, Animals and Factories. ⭐ Level up: fill the star bar to level up and carry more. 🎁 Bonuses: grab Income ⚡ and Speed boosts, jump in the truck, and spin the free wheel every 10 minutes.

Oyun Hakkında

Welcome to Farm Care Tycoon, where your farm, your rules and your empire start here! 🚜 In Farm Care Tycoon you start with a few empty fields and build the most stacked farm in town. Grow crops, raise cute animals, fire up factories and keep your shop shelves full while customers line up to buy. Every coin unlocks new land, new animals and new machines, and the bigger you build, the faster the money rolls in. 🌽 10 crops to grow: wheat, corn, potatoes, tomatoes, broccoli, pineapples, onions, bell peppers, cucumbers and strawberries 🐔 Raise animals: chickens, cows, sheep and pigs 🏭 Run factories: bake bread, press juice, make jam and weave fabric 🛒 Run your shop: stock the shelves and serve a never-ending line of customers 🚚 Drive the truck: carry way more and move faster ⚡ Power boosts: double your income or your speed 🎡 Lucky wheel: free spins with cash and boost prizes 📈 Level up and upgrade: carry more and upgrade your farms, animals and factories Super satisfying, easy to pick up and impossible to put down.
