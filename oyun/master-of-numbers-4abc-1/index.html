Master of Numbers Oyna

Master of Numbers ücretsiz bir matematik, eğitici, zekâ, mantık oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

Nasıl Oynanır?

The goal: to reach the finish line by typing the maximum possible number. Absorb numbers smaller than yours highlighted in blue. If you encounter a red number larger than yours, you will lose your number and you will have to start over. Avoid electric saws, cross bridges and jump over ditches. At the finish line you will find a lot of walls to destroy

Oyun Hakkında

Experience the excitement of mathematics with Master of Numbers, a fast-paced online game. Race to collect beneficial numbers while dodging harmful ones, enhancing your math and strategy skills as you progress through increasingly challenging levels. Play on your phone or computer for an engaging mental workout.
