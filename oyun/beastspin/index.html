Beastspin Oyna

Beastspin ücretsiz bir aksiyon, arcade, beceri oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

Nasıl Oynanır?

Player move automatically but player can activate the skill Controls - Space or click/tap to time your launch. During a Clash, press Space or tap repeatedly. - Space uses your top’s main move during combat. 1–3 activate equipped skills. Q unleashes your ultimate when charged. - Esc or the on-screen pause button pauses combat. On touchscreens, tap the on-screen skill buttons.

Oyun Hakkında

Your top races around the arena on its own. You decide when to use its special moves. Time three taps for your launch, then watch for openings as tops collide, rebound off the rim, and rush back in. Pick your starter Choose Razorgale, Cindertusk, or Tidemanta, then unlock the rest of the five-top roster with coins earned in battle. Each top has its own moves and ultimate. Build between waves. Pick an upgrade after a wave clear to shape your run. Spend coins on permanent upgrades between attempts and change which skills can appear in your next run. Survive the boss clash. Fight five beast bosses across a 20-stage campaign. When a boss locks your top in a Clash, tap to push back before the timer runs out.
