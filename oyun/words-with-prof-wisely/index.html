Bulmaca

Nasıl Oynanır?

The goal of the game is to find the hidden words on the playing field and complete as many levels as possible to increase your rating. You need to make a word from the suggested letters, connecting them with a line. If the word is guessed correctly, it will fill the empty cells. As soon as the field is completely filled, the level is considered completed! If you find a word not specified in the tasks, it will be added to the bonuses, for which you will receive additional rubies! The game provides hints that can be used on difficult levels for rubies or by watching ads. Play and develop your mind every day! Good luck!
