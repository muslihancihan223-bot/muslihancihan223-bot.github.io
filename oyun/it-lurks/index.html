Each night runs from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. As the nights progress, the creatures become stronger and the game becomes more difficult. Check your windows regularly. Use your flashlight to see what is outside. Turn off the lights when something is nearby. Check under your bed for anything hiding there. Stay alert until 7:00 AM. Survive all 8 nights to complete the game.
