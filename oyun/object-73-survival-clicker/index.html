You control Object 73, a clone exploring the underground Jericho Complex. Your goal is to survive, gather resources, upgrade equipment and uncover the truth behind the experiment. *Combat Click on enemies to attack them and deal damage. Defeated enemies drop resources, ammo and crafting materials. *Resources Collect food, water, materials, caps and ammunition. These resources are used for survival, crafting and buying items in the shop. *Crafting and Shop Use collected materials to craft new weapons and armor or buy equipment in the shop. Oyun Hakkında

A post-apocalyptic survival clicker with idle and RPG elements. You awaken inside the underground Jericho Complex as Object 73 — a clone who keeps returning to life after every death. Each new cycle is a chance to uncover the truth behind the Jericho Project. Explore ruined laboratories, collect resources and fight dangerous enemies including mutants, rogue synthetics and combat drones. Defeated enemies drop materials that can be used to craft weapons, upgrade equipment and survive longer. Discover multiple locations across the complex such as laboratories, armories, archives and contaminated sectors. Find memory fragments, hidden logs and secret files that slowly reveal the story of Colonel Dmitry Yankovsky. Craft powerful weapons, upgrade armor and improve your character to defeat elite enemies and story bosses. Your decisions influence the humanity level of the protagonist and change how the story unfolds.
