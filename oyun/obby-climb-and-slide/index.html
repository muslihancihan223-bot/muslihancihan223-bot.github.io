Obby: Climb and Slide Oyna

Obby: Climb and Slide ücretsiz bir platform, obby, mobil oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

PlatformObbyMobil

Nasıl Oynanır?

Your goal is to reach the top of the tower. Earn coins as you climb up. Buy new floaties for your character to climb faster and new pets to earn even more. 🎮 On PC ● WASD – Move ● Mouse – Camera control ● Space – Jump 📱 On Mobile ● Use the on-screen controls ● Drag anywhere on the screen – Camera control

Oyun Hakkında

🏔️ Ready to conquer the tallest slides? In this game, you can! 🎢 Climb higher and higher up the ladders, then rush down the thrilling slides! 💨 The farther you slide, the more coins you collect! 💰 And with cute pets by your side, you’ll earn even more — they’ll help you gather coins! 🐱🐢🐰 What awaits you: • Long ladders and exciting slides for speedy rides 🪜➡️🎢 • Funny pet companions 🐦 • Bright levels and new heights ⛰️ • Fun gameplay and colorful graphics 🎨 Climb up, slide down, and become the ultimate slide champion! 🎮✨ Controls: Desktop: WASD / Arrow keys - move around the map, Space - jump, click - confirm actions. Mobile: virtual joystick - move around the map, tap - confirm actions. Tips & Tricks: • Auto climb: allows you to release control of the character for a while. • Cup: Don't forget to separately go for the cup and collect it; without this action, climbing will only earn you coins for descending. Upgrades / Progression / Economy: • Pets: allow you to earn more coins, depending on the multiplier. • Boats: affect the speed of ascent and the number of coins earned during descent. Levels / Maps / Worlds: • 5 worlds • Each world features a slope, a boat shop
