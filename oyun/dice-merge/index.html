Nasıl Oynanır?

Press Space or click the Roll button to create 2 dice and reset the timer. Merge dice numbers (like 1+5, 3+3, or 2+2+2) to create a 6. When you merge two sixes, you get the X dice, which can destroy any other dice. The higher the dice value destroyed and the more time left on the timer, the higher your score! You can click the "?" button to check every merge combination. The timer starts with 10 seconds, and then gets reduced to 5 seconds after 2 minutes.

Oyun Hakkında

Roll, merge, and race against time in this fast-paced dice puzzle game. Each roll resets a 10-second timer, and your score is multiplied by the time you have left. Drag and merge matching dice to build higher numbers, use special X dice to destroy others for extra points, and strategize before the clock runs out. Reach higher combos, manage your limited dice count, and aim for the best score before time hits zero.
