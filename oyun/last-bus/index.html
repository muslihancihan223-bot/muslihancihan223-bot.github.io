Last Bus Oyna

Last Bus ücretsiz bir aksiyon, 3d, nişancı, zombi oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

Aksiyon3DNişancıZombiHayatta Kalma

Nasıl Oynanır?

Reach the red smoke, rescue the survivors and get to the bus before it leaves. WASD: move, Shift: sprint, Space: roll or jump, C: crouch Mouse: aim, Left click: shoot, Right click: aim down sights 1 2 3 4: weapons, R: reload, V: knife, G: grenade (hold to aim) Q: switch to your partner, P: pause Mobile: left stick to move, drag to look, auto fire

Oyun Hakkında

Zombies have taken the city, and the last bus is leaving soon. Follow the red smoke, break the siege around trapped survivors and get everyone to the bus before it goes. Lead a squad of two: switch to your partner at any moment, swap weapons on the fly and pick a new power card every level. Rescue, loot, clear the streets, hold off the horde or raid a merc camp for a boss and its chest. Between runs, upgrade your people and 30+ guns in the garage, open supply boxes, collect your loot box and choose the next district on the map.
