Nasıl Oynanır?

Defend your base until the final zombie wave is defeated. Choose a defender card, then click or tap a valid position on the battlefield to deploy it. You can also drag and release with the mouse or your finger. Spend resources wisely, upgrade your defenders, and use abilities when the horde gets tough. Use the on-screen pause and settings buttons to pause the battle or adjust music and sound.

Oyun Hakkında

Survivors Vs Zombies is a colorful card-based tower defense game. Build your squad, place defenders across the battlefield, and hold back waves of quirky zombies. Combine different defender roles, manage your resources, and strengthen your collection as you progress through the campaign.
