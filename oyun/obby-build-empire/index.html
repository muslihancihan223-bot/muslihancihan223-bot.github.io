Obby Build Empire Oyna

Obby Build Empire ücretsiz bir çocuk, 3d, platform, obby oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

Çocuk3DPlatformObbyParkur

Nasıl Oynanır?

Goal: build all 8 houses. Step into the glowing supply yard to pick up cargo, run the obby course to the house and stand on the glowing pad to build the next stage. Falling into the water returns you to the last checkpoint. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, hold the right mouse button to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom, P to pause. On phones: on-screen stick to move, jump button, swipe to look around.

Oyun Hakkında

A 3D obby construction game. Grab building materials at the supply yard, carry them across parkour courses over the sea and drop them on your plot, where each house assembles stage by stage from the foundation to a luxury villa. Unlock 8 islands, upgrade speed and jump, collect rent from finished houses and dress your builder in helmets, vests, tools and trails.
