Oyun Hakkında

Obby: Mini-Games - play against other players and win Cups! Triumph in each mode and stand on the golden winner's pedestal! 🔴 MODES 🔴 🚀 PARKOUR 🚀 Complete colorful levels on the parkour map and reach the end! 🎯 COLORFUL FLOOR 🎯 In this mode, you need to step on tiles whose color is displayed at the top of the screen. 🎯 GLASS BRIDGE 🎯 And in this mode, you need to choose which glass tile to jump on, but one will break, and the other will remain intact. 🎯 FALLING TILES 🎯 In this mode, you have to run across tiles, but there's a condition: the tiles you've already run on will fall down and disappear after a second. So, be careful. ☀️ RAIN OF BLOCKS ☀️ This mode will unlock as you progress through the game!
