Pim Path Oyna

Pim Path ücretsiz bir arcade, beceri, mobil oyunudur. İndirme ve kayıt gerekmez; telefonda, tablette ve bilgisayarda tarayıcıdan hemen oynayabilirsin.

ArcadeBeceriMobil

Nasıl Oynanır?

Objective: Reach the trophy at the end of the level. Complete all the levels. PC Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys – Character Movement Space – Jump Mobile Controls: On-screen buttons.

Oyun Hakkında

Reach the trophy at the end of the level to win, but it's not as easy as it might seem. You can't fall off the platforms; spikes and blades will be everywhere, making the levels more challenging. One wrong step and it's game over. You'll need to stay calm, pay attention, be agile, have good reflexes, and most importantly, don't get angry.
