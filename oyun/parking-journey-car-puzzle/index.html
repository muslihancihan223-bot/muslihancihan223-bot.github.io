Nasıl Oynanır?

Your objective is to park every car in the parking bay that matches its color before time runs out. 🚗🅿️ 👆 Select a car by pressing or holding it. ✏️ Draw a route from the car to the place where you want it to move. 🚘 The car will follow the path you created. 🎨 Look at the car's color and find its matching parking bay. 🚧 If another vehicle blocks the way, move that vehicle somewhere else first. 🔄 Use empty spaces to temporarily reposition cars and create a clear path. 🅿️ Move each vehicle into the correct colored parking bay. ⏱️ Keep an eye on the timer — the level must be cleared before time runs out. ↩️ Use Undo if you want to reverse a bad move. 🚘 Use the Valet booster when you need help finding the next correct move. ⏰ Use Extra Time to add another 60 seconds when the clock is running low. ✅ Park all the cars correctly to complete the level and continue your journey. The key is to plan several moves ahead. Sometimes you need to move the wrong car first so you can free the vehicle you actually need. 🧠🚦 Think carefully, draw the route, clear the traffic, and become the ultimate parking puzzle master! 🚗🅿️🏆

Get ready to test your logic, planning, and parking skills in Parking Journey: Car Puzzle! 🅿️🚘 Your goal is simple: move every car to the parking bay that matches its color. But there’s a catch — the parking lot is crowded, cars block one another, and the correct path is rarely obvious. You’ll need to think ahead, move vehicles out of the way, and create a clear route before the timer runs out. 🧠⏱️ Unlike ordinary parking games, you are in control of the route. Hold a car and draw the path you want it to follow, and the vehicle will drive along that line. You can also tap an available position to move a car using a shorter route. 🚙➡️🅿️ Sometimes the car you need is trapped behind another one. Move blocking cars onto open spaces or even temporarily place them in a different parking bay to create room.
