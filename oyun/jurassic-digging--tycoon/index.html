Nasıl Oynanır?

Controls: On Mobile: Tap and swipe to interact. On Desktop: For movement use WASD or Left Mouse Click. Additional Features: - Explore various dig sites to find rare fossils. - Restore and polish bones to increase their value. - Design museum exhibits to attract more visitors. - Expand your museum and unlock new tools as you advance.

Oyun Hakkında

The goal of the game is to uncover ancient remains, clean them from dirt, and assemble complete skeletons. Progress through all levels and provide museums with an extensive collection of dinosaur skeletons.
