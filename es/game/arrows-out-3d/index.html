Jugar Arrows Out 3D

Arrows Out 3D es un juego gratis de Puzles, 3D, Combinar. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Cómo Jugar

Left click or tap a block to slide it out in the direction its arrow points. A clear path lets it escape; a blocked path bumps it back and costs a heart. Click and drag, or swipe, to rotate the whole shape and see every side. Use the mouse wheel or pinch to zoom in and out. Tap Hint to see a block that can move, Pluck to remove any block you choose, or Wand to clear several blocks at once - each costs coins you earn by clearing levels cleanly. There is no time limit, so take a moment to read the shape before you tap.
