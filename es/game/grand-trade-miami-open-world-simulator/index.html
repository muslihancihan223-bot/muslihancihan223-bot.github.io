Cómo Jugar

Goal: get rich by flipping things - buy low, sell high and save up for a penthouse. Your seller reputation opens new places in town. Desktop: WASD walk, Shift run, mouse look, E action, Space jump, Tab phone, B bag, M map, hold G weapons, P pause. Phones and tablets: the left side of the screen moves, the right side looks around, action buttons are on screen. The phone has listings, chats with buyers, the map, tasks, the garage and plates.
