Jugar Obby Build Empire

Obby Build Empire es un juego gratis de Niños, 3D, Plataformas. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Niños3DPlataformasObbyParkour

Cómo Jugar

Goal: build all 8 houses. Step into the glowing supply yard to pick up cargo, run the obby course to the house and stand on the glowing pad to build the next stage. Falling into the water returns you to the last checkpoint. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, hold the right mouse button to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom, P to pause. On phones: on-screen stick to move, jump button, swipe to look around.
