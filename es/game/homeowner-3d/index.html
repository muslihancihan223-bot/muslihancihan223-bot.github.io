Cómo Jugar

🏠 Welcome to Homeowner 3D! Take on jobs, turn run-down houses into cozy homes and build your own property empire! 🎮 Controls 🖥️ FOR PC 🚶 WASD — walk, Shift — run, mouse — look around 🧹 Hold LMB — clean up trash and take apart old furniture 🚪 E — doors, laptop, van 🧰 F — tools, Tab — tablet with the catalog 🛋️ Furniture: Q/E or mouse wheel — rotate, LMB — place 📱 FOR PHONE 🕹️ Joystick at the bottom left — walk 👆 Swipe the screen — look around ✋ Action button at the bottom right (hold) — clean, take apart, open doors 🛒 Cart at the top left — furniture catalog 🧰 Tools — mop, roller, wall finish, flooring 🛋️ Furniture: arrows — rotate, tick — place ✨ How to play 💻 Pick a job on the laptop and drive there in your van 🗑️ Clear the trash, mop the puddles, mow the lawn 🎨 Paint walls, hang wallpaper, lay tiles and floors 👻 Arrange furniture the way the owner wants it 📸 Take photos of your work and get rated
