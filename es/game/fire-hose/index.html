Jugar Fire Hose

Fire Hose es un juego gratis de Disparos, Firefighter. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Disparos

Cómo Jugar

On desktop use the mouse to aim and click to release the water. Press Q to toggle mouse lock On mobile user the virtual joystick and the button to trigger the water from the hose. You win by extinguishing as many fires fires on the screen without running out of water
