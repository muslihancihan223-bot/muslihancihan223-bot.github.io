Cómo Jugar

HOW TO PLAY JIGSOLTAIRE OBJECTIVE: Rebuild the scrambled image by swapping tiles until all pieces are in their correct positions. BASIC CONTROLS: - Drag a tile and drop it onto another tile to swap their positions - Continue swapping until the complete image is revealed - The puzzle is solved when all tiles are in the correct order GAME MODES: 1. Play Level - Progress through levels in your current collection - Each level increases in difficulty - Earn coins and points for completion 2. Daily Puzzle - New puzzle available every day - Complete for bonus coins (+50) - Track your daily streak DIFFICULTY LEVELS: - Easy: 3x3 grid (9 pieces) - Medium: 4x4 grid (16 pieces) - Hard: 5x5 grid (25 pieces) - Expert: 6x6 grid (36 pieces) - Master: 7x7 grid (49 pieces) - Insane: 8x8 grid (64 pieces) - Legendary: 9x9 grid (81 pieces) TIPS FOR SUCCESS: 1. Start with corner and edge pieces 2. Look for distinctive colors or patterns 3. Use the Hint button if you get stuck 4. Take your time - there's no time pressure 5. Complete easier puzzles first to earn hints FEATURES: - Hints: Tap the hint button to highlight a correct swap - Gallery: View all your completed puzzles - Quests: Complete challenges for bonus rewards - Collections: Unlock new puzzle packs - Leaderboard: Compare scores with other players Enjoy the game!
