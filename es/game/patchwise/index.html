Jugar Patchwise

Patchwise es un juego gratis de Puzles, Decoration, Relajantes. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

PuzlesDecorationRelajantesLógica

Cómo Jugar

Fill the whole cloth with the patches shown in the tray below the board. Use each patch exactly once. Patches may be turned and flipped. • Drag across the cells to sew a patch. • Drag on from the end of a patch to make it longer. • Tap a patch to remove it. • UNDO takes back your last move. RESET clears the board. • Stuck? HINT sews one correct patch for you. Pinned patches came from a hint and stay put. Each cloth gives up to 2 stars: one for finishing it, one for finishing without hints. Collect half the stars in a world to open the next one. You don't have to finish every cloth.
