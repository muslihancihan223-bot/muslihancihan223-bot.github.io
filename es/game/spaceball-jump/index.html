Cómo Jugar

Features Orbital Physics: Use the spin of the planets to slingshot yourself across space. Hazards & Collectibles: Dodge spinning spike walls and collect 3 stars in every level to open the escape portal! Controls Spaceball Jump is designed to be played with a simple, responsive one-button control scheme: Desktop / PC: * Press the Spacebar or Up Arrow key to jump. Alternatively, Left-Click anywhere on the game screen. Mobile / Tablet: * Tap anywhere on your screen to jump. Pro Tip: Pay close attention to the rotation speed and direction of your current planet—your jump trajectory will be heavily affected by it!
