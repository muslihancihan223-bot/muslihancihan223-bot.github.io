Jugar Merge Mania

Merge Mania es un juego gratis de Puzles, Number, Math. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

PuzlesNumberMathFusionarLógica

Cómo Jugar

1. A numbered tile appears at the bottom of the grid. 2. Tap a column to place the tile upward. 3. When two identical numbers meet, they merge into double the value. 4. Keep merging while preventing the grid from overflowing.
