Jugar Fill and Swim

Fill and Swim es un juego gratis de Simulación, 3D, Correr. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Simulación3DCorrerObbyRelajantes

Cómo Jugar

Desktop: WASD or arrows to move, drag the mouse to look, Space to jump. The hands work by themselves: your bucket fills at the well and pours out at the pool. On the slide: A/D steer, W faster, S slower, Space jump. In the pool: Space up, C down. Mobile: joystick to move, drag to look, tap the arrow to jump. 1. Walk to the well to fill up. 2. Walk to the pool to pour. 3. Lift weights to carry more. 4. Fill the pool, slide in and swim. 5. Open stronger gyms and dig deeper pools.
