Jugar Arrows: Make It Cozy!

Arrows: Make It Cozy! es un juego gratis de Puzles, Arcade, Decoration. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Cómo Jugar

Tap an arrow — it will slide straight in the direction it points. If the path is clear, the arrow escapes. If another arrow is in the way — they collide and you lose a life. You have 3 lives ❤️ per level and limited time ⏱️. Free all the arrows before you run out! On PC: click on an arrow with the mouse. On mobile: tap the arrow with your finger. Boosters: 1. 💡 Hint — highlights the path of one arrow that can safely leave the field. 2. 🗺️ Compass — reveals all arrow paths on the level until it's completed. Chain combos! 🔥 Send arrows off in a row without collisions and within 10 seconds between moves — combo points go to the leaderboard.
