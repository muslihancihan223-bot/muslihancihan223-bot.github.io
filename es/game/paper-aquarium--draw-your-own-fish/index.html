Jugar Paper Aquarium: Draw Your Own Fish!

Paper Aquarium: Draw Your Own Fish! es un juego gratis de Animales, Drawing, Decoration. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

AnimalesDrawingDecorationSimulaciónNiños

Cómo Jugar

Select Draw a fish, use the brush and colors, then bring your drawing to life. Give the fish a name and release it into the aquarium. To feed your fish, hold the food jar above the water and shake it with your mouse or finger. Earn coins as your fish live through aquarium days and use them to buy decorations.
