Cómo Jugar

Eat smaller creatures to gain experience and grow. Choose upgrades as you evolve, combine your skills and relics, and survive the predators of the abyss. Collect Essence and Crystals to strengthen future dives. Desktop: Use WASD to swim, Space to activate your innate skill, and J / K / L for acquired skills. Click buttons to choose upgrades, open menus and pause. Mobile: Drag on the gameplay area to swim and tap the on-screen skill buttons. Tap upgrade cards and menu buttons to select them. The Guided Dive teaches the basics and can be replayed from Settings. Optional reward videos offer bonuses only after a completed viewing.
