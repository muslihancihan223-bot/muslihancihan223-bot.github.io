Cómo Jugar

Choose a Puzzle: Start by selecting a puzzle from the collection. Anime puzzles offers a variety of both free and premium anime-themed puzzles. You can also create your own puzzles using your family photos for a personal touch. Arrange the Pieces: Once you've chosen a puzzle, you'll be presented with scattered puzzle pieces on the screen. Your goal is to arrange these pieces to form the complete image. Drag and Drop: To move a puzzle piece, simply tap/click and hold it, then drag it to the desired location on the puzzle board. Release your finger/mouse button to drop the piece in place.
