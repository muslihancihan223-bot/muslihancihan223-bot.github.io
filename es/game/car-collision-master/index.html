Cómo Jugar

Master the Arena of Chaos! Rev up your customized jeep and dive into intense head-to-head car battles against fierce rivals in wild looping arenas framed by jagged canyons. Ram opponents with perfect timing, flip through loops, survive deadly hazards, and smash their vehicles to knock out drivers—dominate rounds, climb leaderboards, and prove you're the ultimate collision champion in this high-octane thrill ride! Main Objective and Winning Method: The primary goal is to defeat your opponent by colliding your vehicle into theirs to dislodge the driver's helmet. Each successful knockout scores you a point (winning the round). The first player to score 5 points wins the match. Use arena physics, loops, and daring maneuvers to line up devastating hits while avoiding getting smashed yourself! Desktop Controls (via emulator): Spacebar: Hold to accelerate/gas forward (release to slow down; physics handle direction and flips). Mobile Controls: On-Screen Gas Pedal (bottom right): Hold to accelerate forward and power your vehicle (the car drives based on its orientation and physics—master timing to flip, bounce, and attack!).
