Mini Chess 3D es un juego gratis de Puzles, Estrategia, 3D. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Cómo Jugar

Objective: Solve each tactical puzzle by capturing target pieces or delivering checkmate within the allowed number of moves. Controls: Click / Tap on a chess piece to select it and reveal its legal moves. Click / Tap on a highlighted target square or enemy piece to move and capture. Use the Hint and Undo buttons if you need assistance solving tricky puzzles!
