Jugar Nuts Bolts Sort

Nuts Bolts Sort es un juego gratis de Combinar, Mente, Mobile. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

CombinarMenteMobile

Cómo Jugar

-Click on any bolt to move the nut to another bolt. -The rule is that only nuts of the same color can be stacked and sorted. -There must be enough space on the bolt to allow movement. -Try not to get stuck, don't worry, you can always restart the level. -Free props to help you pass levels.
