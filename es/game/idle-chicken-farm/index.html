Cómo Jugar

Build Your Ultimate Egg Farm! Start with a single chicken and grow it into a thriving egg production empire. Collect and sell eggs, merge chickens to unlock stronger breeds, upgrade your coop and production line, and expand your farm with new facilities. Take a risk by incubating eggs for a chance to hatch new chickens and earn valuable rewards! Desktop Controls Left Mouse Click: Interact with all game elements. Click & Hold + Drag: Collect and sell multiple eggs by dragging the cursor across them. Mobile Controls Tap: Interact with all game elements. Tap & Drag: Collect and sell multiple eggs by dragging your finger across the eggs.
