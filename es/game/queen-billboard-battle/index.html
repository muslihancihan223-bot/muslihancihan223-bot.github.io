Jugar Queen Billboard Battle

Queen Billboard Battle es un juego gratis de Chicas, 3D, Correr. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Chicas3DCorrerFashionRunner

Cómo Jugar

Drag left or right on the screen (or use A/D or the arrow keys) to steer the standee. Drive through blue gates to gain beauty points, avoid red gates and paint buckets, and collect pink gems on the way. Finish with more points than your rival so the crowd votes for your billboard. Spend crystals in the Wardrobe on new frames, sparkle trails, auras, runways and victory fireworks.
