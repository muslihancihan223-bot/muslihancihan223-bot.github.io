Cómo Jugar

Goal: collect a large brainrot animal collection, grow your zoo, unlock rare pets, and increase your income. Controls: on computer, use WASD to walk, Space to jump, LMB to act, and RMB to rotate the camera. On mobile, use touch and swipe. Buy new creatures, place them on special spots, earn money, and gradually expand your base. The more rare pets you collect, the faster your income grows and the richer your zoo becomes.
