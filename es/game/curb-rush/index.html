Jugar Curb Rush

Curb Rush es un juego gratis de Carreras, Arcade, Aventura. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Cómo Jugar

Goal: skate the city, do tricks, win races against bots, deliver parcels and collect golden tokens to earn coins and unlock gear. Desktop: W / Up arrow push, A/D or Left/Right arrows steer, S / Down brake, Space jump (press again in the air for a flip, arrows pick the flip), Shift or E boost, M big map, P pause, drag the mouse to look around. Mobile: drag left/right to steer, tap to jump, tap again in the air to flip, boost button bottom right, map button above the minimap.
