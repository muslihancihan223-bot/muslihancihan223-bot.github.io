Cómo Jugar

Controls Left click / Tap -> Attack a tile, activate a mask, press a button That's it — the entire game is played with a single click/tap. No keyboard required. Gameplay 1. Click tiles to deal damage equal to your Power stat. Destroyed tiles drop Gold and Energy. 2. Enemies (red tiles) fight back — they reflect damage to you. If your HP is too low to survive a hit, the enemy becomes locked until you heal. 3. Boss appears on stage 5 as a large 3x3 tile. Clear the tiles covering it first, then defeat it to complete the location. 4. Return to the Village (safe zone) to spend gold on upgrades: Max HP, HP Regen, and Power.
