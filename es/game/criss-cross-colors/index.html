Cómo Jugar

Master the Grid! Become a color-matching genius in Criss Cross Colors! In this fun and addictive color puzzle game, your goal is to drag colorful blocks from the bottom palette and fill the entire grid so that every row and every column has matching colors. With simple drag-and-drop moves and clever logic, you’ll clear every square and win each level in the most satisfying way! Desktop Controls: Mouse: Click and drag any colored block from the bottom palette directly onto the grid. Left Mouse Button: Hold to drag, release to place. Mobile Controls: Finger: Tap and drag any colored block from the bottom palette onto the empty squares in the grid. No extra buttons — just one smooth drag to match colors and win!
