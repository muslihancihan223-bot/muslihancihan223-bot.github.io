Jugar Color the Room

Color the Room es un juego gratis de 3D, Decoration, Relajantes. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

3DDecorationRelajantesDrawingColor

Cómo Jugar

Pick a room and bring it back to color. Desktop: left mouse paints, right mouse rotates the camera, Shift + right mouse pans, the wheel zooms, and the middle mouse button opens a round color wheel. Mobile: one finger paints, two fingers zoom and rotate. Open the Palette to choose brush, eraser, fill (solid or brick, wood, tiles, grass, metal, fabric textures) or the eyedropper. Every newly colored object gives points, a fully colored room gives a bonus. Save your favorite looks in slots.
