Jugar Far Island: Tactical Warfare

Far Island: Tactical Warfare es un juego gratis de Acción, Disparos, Gun. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

AcciónDisparosGunArmyTower DefenseFirst Person ShooterGuerra

Cómo Jugar

Start by selecting equipment in the store. Choose weapons to suit your style. After landing on the island, study the map – it shows enemy bases to capture. To capture a base, eliminate all enemies within its perimeter. After capture, establish defense: the enemy will try to retake positions. Use turrets, fortifications, and tactical terrain advantages. Controls: WASD – movement Shift – run/swim down Space – jump/swim up 1,2,3,4 – weapon switch Left mouse button – fire Right mouse button – aim R – reload T – deploy turret G – throw grenade V – switch grenade type E – interact Q – roll Tab – camera switch H – holster/draw weapon I – inventory/settings P – pause M – map Earn resources for successful captures and base defense. Spend them in the store on new weapons, upgrades, and equipment. The more difficult the mission – the more valuable the reward. Tips: study the map, change tactics, use elevation, upgrade equipment, and combine weapon types.
