Jugar Bus n Ball Jam

Bus n Ball Jam es un juego gratis de Puzles, 3D, Combinar. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Cómo Jugar

🚌 Arrange the buses: Tap and move the buses at the bottom to clear the path. Each bus has its own direction, so choose carefully! 🔴 Drop the balls: As buses leave the track, colored balls drop onto the conveyor belt. Watch each drop and plan your next move. 🟡 Move along the belt: Balls roll along the moving conveyor belt. Don't let the belt get overcrowded! 🟢 Match colors: Fill all the slots to solve the color puzzle. 🔵 Avoid overload: If too many balls pile up on the belt without a matching color slot, you lose. Think ahead, arrange smartly, and manage every drop!
