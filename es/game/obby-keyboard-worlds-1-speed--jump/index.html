Cómo Jugar

Desktop • WASD or arrow keys: move. • Space: jump. Press again or hold to use your available midair jumps. • Mouse: look around. When the cursor is visible, hold the right mouse button and drag to rotate the camera. • Mouse wheel: zoom in or out. • Tab: show or hide the cursor. • Hold E near sneakers: buy or equip them. • Use the on-screen controls to adjust your speed and camera zoom. Mobile Use the joystick on the left to move and swipe on the right to look around. Tap the jump button to jump, or hold it to use your available midair jumps. Use the on-screen buttons and sliders to interact, adjust speed, and zoom.
