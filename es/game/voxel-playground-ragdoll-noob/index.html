Jugar Voxel Playground: Ragdoll Noob

Voxel Playground: Ragdoll Noob es un juego gratis de Divertidos, Ragdoll. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

DivertidosRagdoll

Cómo Jugar

Hold down the left mouse button from your computer or your finger on your phone to drag the character and objects. Throw the character into obstacles, explode until it is destroyed into pieces to complete the level.
