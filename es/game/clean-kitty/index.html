Jugar Clean Kitty

Clean Kitty es un juego gratis de Puzles, Relajantes, Cats. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Cómo Jugar

Look at where the tap is and where the tub is. Hold and drag to draw a line. Lines become solid objects that the water flows along. When you let go, the tap turns on. Movable objects (rubber ducks, soap bars, blue pipes and the tub) also start to fall. Plan your line with that in mind. You can keep drawing while the water flows, as long as you have ink left. How to win Fill the tub until the gauge beside it is full, and the level is complete. The more ink you have left, the more stars you get (up to 3). You lose if you run out of ink before drawing a line, or if not enough water reaches the tub. Watch out for Glowing red plates: water that touches them turns to steam. Levels with two taps: catch water from both. Controls Mouse: Hold the left mouse button and drag to draw. Release to finish the line. Touch: Put your finger down and drag to draw. Lift your finger to finish. Use the Hint button to see a suggested line when you're stuck.
