Jugar Lane Clash

Lane Clash es un juego gratis de Estrategia, Tower Defense. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

EstrategiaTower Defense

Cómo Jugar

Defend your fortress across five lanes. Enemies run toward your wall — tap a lane shield to spawn warriors (when cooldown is ready). When units clash, their power cancels out: the weaker is destroyed, the stronger keeps going with the leftover power. If an enemy reaches the wall, your base loses HP equal to that enemy’s power. How to win Clear each wave by defeating or blocking all enemies before your base HP drops to zero. Survive the wave to earn rewards, upgrade your lanes, and advance to the next wave. Upgrades & items Lane gears: upgrade tier and power with coins, cooldown with gems Wall HP bar: base upgrades and repair Inventory: Shield (arm a lane), Bomb (clear nearby enemies), Freeze (slow a lane) Tap coins on the field to collect them Ad Rewards: watch ads for gems and coins Items shop (tap an empty inventory slot): buy items with gems; in-apps when payments are available Controls Mouse / touch: tap a lane shield to spawn Inventory buttons: use Shield, Bomb, or Freeze during a wave Continue: after a win Soft Retry (watch an ad for an easier wave) or Back: after a defeat
