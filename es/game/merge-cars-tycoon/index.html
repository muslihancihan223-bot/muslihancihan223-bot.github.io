Jugar Merge Cars Tycoon

Merge Cars Tycoon es un juego gratis de Fusionar. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

Fusionar

Cómo Jugar

🖱️ Controls: mouse (drag & drop) on desktop, touch on mobile. Merge: drag a car onto another car of the same level to merge them into a better one. Race: drag a car onto the race track to start earning coins every lap. Buy & open: tap 📦 boxes to get free cars, or buy new ones in the Shop with coins. Boost: use Speed Up to double your earnings, and spin the Wheel of Luck for rewards. Grow: complete Quests and buy Upgrades to increase your income. Tidy up: don’t need a car? Drag it onto the Remove bin.
