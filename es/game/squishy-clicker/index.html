Cómo Jugar

Tap or click the squishy to earn coins. On touch screens you can squeeze with several fingers at once, and sliding across it earns a little too. Swipe quickly over the squishy or next to it to make it spin. On the Squishies tab, buy new squishies that earn coins every second; owning 25, 50 or 100 of one kind doubles its income. On the Upgrades tab, boost your taps and your squishies. Tap fast to fill the bar and trigger a mega squeeze. Catch the golden squishy when it floats by. When progress slows down, rebirth on the Stars tab: each star adds +10% income forever. Mouse on desktop, touch on mobile.
