Jugar Bus Jam

Bus Jam es un juego gratis de Puzles, Arcade, Combinar. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

PuzlesArcadeCombinarMenteLógica

Cómo Jugar

- Tap (or click) a vehicle in the car park to drive it out along its arrow. - It can only leave if nothing is parked between it and the way out; otherwise it bumps back into place and you lose nothing but the move. - A vehicle that gets out pulls into a free bay and boards passengers of its own colour from the front of the queue. - A full bay drives away and frees itself up again. If every bay is full and the passenger at the front has nowhere to go, the level is over. - Clear the whole queue to win the level, and earn coins and stars. - Boosters: VIP lifts any vehicle straight out, Arrange sorts the queue by colour, Jumble reshuffles it. Coins buy more, or watch a short ad. - Locked bays can be opened with coins or a rewarded ad at any time. Controls: tap / click only. No keyboard needed. Works on phone, tablet and desktop.
