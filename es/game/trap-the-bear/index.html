Jugar Trap the Bear

Trap the Bear es un juego gratis de Puzles, Estrategia, Animales. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

PuzlesEstrategiaAnimalesMenteLógica

Cómo Jugar

Click or tap a penguin, then click or tap a highlighted mound next to it to move it one step. After each of your moves, the bear moves too. It can step one mound, or jump over a penguin next to it into an empty space and catch that penguin. To win, block every path so the bear can't move. You lose if the bear catches too many penguins or your penguins can't move. Cracked ice breaks after something steps off it, whale holes can't be crossed, and the bear can't jump the seal. Use Undo to take back a move, and Hint if you get stuck. To earn three stars, trap the bear without losing a penguin, in the fewest moves possible.
