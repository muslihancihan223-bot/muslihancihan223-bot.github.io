Cómo Jugar

Move with WASD or the on-screen stick. Mine ore and gather materials, then spend them on buildings and upgrades. Repair and upgrade fences before waves break through. Hire shooters, place turrets, and keep production running so you always have ammo. Survive each wave, beat bosses, and expand the base between attacks.
