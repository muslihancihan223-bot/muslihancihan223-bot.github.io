Jugar Sweet Merge Cafe

Sweet Merge Cafe es un juego gratis de Story, Fusionar, Relajantes. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

StoryFusionarRelajantes

Cómo Jugar

Drag two identical items together to merge them into a better one. Tap generators to get new items (uses energy). Complete customer orders and press Serve to earn coins. Spend coins in the Cafe to fix furniture and decor and unlock new locations.
