Jugar Circuit Master 2

Circuit Master 2 es un juego gratis de Puzles, Mobile. Sin descargas ni registro: juega al instante en el navegador de tu móvil, tablet u ordenador.

PuzlesMobile

Cómo Jugar

Place all components at the right places on grid, each component has unique properties and it is needed to be placed where it is supposed to be. Use hint for additional help Desktop: Use mouse to drag and drop the components on to the grid and then click on red power button to power up the circuit. Mobile: Use screen touch to drag and drop the components on to the grid and then tap on red power button to power up the circuit.
