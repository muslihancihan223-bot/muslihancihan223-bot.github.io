Cómo Jugar

Use the arrows ⬆️⬇️ or "WS" to move and ⬅️➡️ or "AD" to spin. Sometimes you’ll need to slow down, and other times you’ll need to speed up to win. Try different tactics. If your bike gets stuck, press ⬅️➡️ or "AD" to hop!
