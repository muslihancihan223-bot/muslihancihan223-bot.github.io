Come Giocare

- Keyboard: Use WASD or the arrow keys to move, Space to jump, E to interact, B to open upgrades, H to highlight remaining leaves, 1–4 to select a tool, Q to cycle tools, Tab to pause, and Escape to close an open menu or resume. - Mouse: Move or drag to look around, hold the left mouse button to use the current tool, and use the mouse wheel to cycle tools. - Touch: Use the left joystick to move, swipe the right side to look, and use the on-screen buttons to collect leaves or interact, highlight remaining leaves, jump, switch tools, open upgrades, and pause.
