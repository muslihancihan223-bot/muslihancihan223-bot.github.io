Come Giocare

Train on your base's treadmill to increase your speed, then enter the arena. Hold E near a case to steal it and wake its boss. Escape to the lobby before you get caught! Place the case on your base's floor, wait for the timer, and open it. Put the weapon on a pedestal to earn money, then buy upgrades and take on tougher zones. Merge three weapons of the same type and star level in the Fuse Machine to get a higher-level case. PC: - WASD or arrow keys — move. - Space — jump. - Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse — rotate the camera. - Hold E — pick up a case. - Mouse wheel — zoom. - Left click — place cases, open ready cases, and select pedestals. Mobile: - Use the on-screen joystick to move. - Swipe on the right side to rotate the camera; in portrait mode, swipe on the upper half. - Hold the on-screen pickup button near a case to take it. - Tap to place cases, open ready cases, and select pedestals. - Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
