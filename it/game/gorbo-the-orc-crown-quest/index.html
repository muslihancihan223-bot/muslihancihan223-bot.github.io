Come Giocare

Reach the door at the end of each level. The last level of each world is a boss: beat it to open the door. Find the 3 hidden gems in every level, collect coins and spend them at the merchant. Lose all your hearts and you get back up at the last banner. Keyboard: arrows or A D to run, Space, W or up to jump (hold to jump higher), X or J to swing the cleaver (tap fast for a 3-hit combo), C or L to throw the axe, E to drink a potion, Esc or P to pause. Touch: arrow buttons to run, the jump, sword and axe buttons on the right, tap the potion to drink.
