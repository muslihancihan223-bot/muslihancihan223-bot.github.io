Come Giocare

Knock your rival out to win the round; most fights are best of three. Climb the Arcade ladder of four rivals and a boss, beat the new Daily Challenge every day, or last as long as you can in Survival. Keyboard: A / D or the arrow keys move, W, Up or Space jump (again in the air for a double jump), J or Z punch, K or X kick, L or S block, Shift dash (or double-tap a direction), I special when your power bar is full. P pauses, M mutes. Touch or mouse: drag the stick to move, flick it up to jump and twice sideways to dash. Tap Attack to build combos, hold Block to guard, tap Super when your power bar is full. On a computer, click the fight once to show these buttons; any key hides them again. Combos: Punch Punch Punch = sky upper, Punch Punch Kick = spin finish, Punch Kick = rising knee, Kick Kick = double kick, Kick Punch = lunge. Two players on one keyboard: P1 uses A D W S F G H and Left Shift, P2 uses the arrows, K L ; and Right Shift.
