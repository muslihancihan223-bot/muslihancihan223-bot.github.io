Come Giocare

There is no single goal: build whatever you like, set up scenes with characters and destroy them with physics and explosions. WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to look around, Shift to run, Space to jump. Left mouse button to place, use or shoot. Right mouse button to aim or remove. Mouse wheel or 1–6 to switch hotbar items. Q or Tab opens the item menu. R rotates the item, F to use, enter or exit a vehicle. In the helicopter, Space and Shift to go up and down. Esc pauses the game. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and buttons.
