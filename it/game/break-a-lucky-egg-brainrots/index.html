Come Giocare

Start breaking eggs to collect Brainrot characters. Carry the Brainrots to your base before their timer expires. Avoid skeleton enemies while carrying Brainrots. Earn money from your base and upgrade your tools. Expand your base floors to increase capacity. Collect tickets and use them for cosmetics and special actions. Steal Brainrots from other players if you have enough tickets. Use rebirth to increase your earning multipliers.
