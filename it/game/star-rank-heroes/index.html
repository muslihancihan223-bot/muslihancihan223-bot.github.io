Come Giocare

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Click enemies to attack, or turn on auto-click. Space to jump, Q to dash, M to ride your mount. Drag to rotate the camera and scroll to zoom. At a World Star, press E to summon companions or R to auto-summon. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and tap to attack. Defeat enemies to earn Yen, summon and equip stronger companions, and upgrade your power. Unlock new worlds with tougher enemies, complete quests, and beat the Gamemode waves and bosses. Reach the top rank and fill your Index!
