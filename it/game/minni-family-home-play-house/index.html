Come Giocare

Make your own story in Minni Town! Create your family, pick a place on the map and step inside. Pick up anyone and anything, drag them around the room and see what happens. Cook a snack, brush teeth at the dentist, fill a shopping basket, try a new haircut or feed the birds in the tree house. There is no score, no timer and no way to lose. The goal is to explore, find every surprise and play out the stories you imagine. • Create and dress up characters: change their hairstyles, outfits, hats, eyes and expressions. • Open the map and tap a building to visit it. Four places are open from the start. One short optional video opens the other five for your play session. • Collect free tickets every day and use them at the Prize Pop machine to win a random new outfit. Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button: Select characters, objects, buttons and buildings. Click and Drag: Pick up characters and objects and move them anywhere. Drag the Background: Scroll around the room. Mouse Wheel: Scroll through the outfit and shop lists. No keyboard needed. Mobile Controls: Tap: Select characters, objects, buttons and buildings. Touch and Drag: Pick up characters and objects and move them anywhere. Swipe the Background: Scroll around the room. Swipe a List: Scroll through outfits and shop items.
