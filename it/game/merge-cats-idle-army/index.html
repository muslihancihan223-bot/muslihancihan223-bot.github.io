Come Giocare

🖱️ PC: click and drag📱 Mobile: tap and drag Recruit cats: tap the spawn button to buy a new cat with Soul Fragments. New recruits also drop in for free over time. Merge cats: drag a cat onto an identical cat (same level) to merge them into a stronger one. 🐱 + 🐱 = 😼 Battle: drag cats onto the battle tiles to send them into the fight. They attack automatically. Collect souls: enemies drop Soul Fragments with every hit. Use them to recruit and upgrade cats. Beat bosses: defeat a boss to advance to stronger enemies and bigger rewards. Boost up: activate x2 Damage, Auto-Merge or Time Jump, and grab the free boosts that fly across the screen. 🎁 Upgrade and Ascend: upgrade spawn level and speed, and Ascend when progress slows to come back permanently stronger. Come back daily: claim offline loot, daily rewards and quest prizes. 📆
