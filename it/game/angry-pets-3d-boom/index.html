Come Giocare

🎯 Demolish the building before you run out of charges. Watch its health bar at the top. 🖱️ On a computer, hold the left mouse button and drag sideways to rotate the camera. Left-click a spot on the structure to launch a pet. 📱 On a phone, swipe to rotate and tap your target to shoot. 💥 Pets attach where they hit. Place your charges, then press the round lightning button on the left to detonate them. Select the barrel in the bottom panel to use TNT. Your remaining shots are shown at the bottom. 🐾 Between levels, open the pets menu and spend coins on upgrades. Try different angles and set up your best collapse!
