Gioca a Sunny Farm: Animal Simulator

Sunny Farm: Animal Simulator è un gioco gratuito di tipo Simulazione, Animali, Gestionale. Senza download né registrazione: gioca subito nel browser su telefono, tablet o computer.

SimulazioneAnimaliGestionaleFarm

Come Giocare

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, click on animals, pens, fields and buildings to interact, E or Space for the action button, mouse wheel to zoom. Mobile: drag anywhere to walk with the joystick, tap to interact, pinch to zoom. Feed and water your animals, tap the product bubbles to collect eggs, milk and wool, sell them at the market or deliver orders for bonus coins and XP. Level up to unlock new animals, fields and workshops. Breed two adults in the Love nest to get babies with better stars and rare colors.
