Come Giocare

In Brainrot Puzzle, your goal is to merge absurd animal ingredients into fully fused brainrot creatures. The game starts with a grid filled with random ingredient tiles like bananas, monkeys, cars, teapots, and more. by touch and drag or click and drag the image tiles and sort them in the right order to solve the puzzle.
