Come Giocare

- Day: the factory works and collects materials by itself. Click the upgrade cards to make it stronger. The night starts when the timer at the top runs out. - Night: - Click the screen to aim. Move the mouse to look around. - Left click: fire. - Right click: sniper scope on/off. - 1 / 2 / 3: sniper / rifle / rockets. - Q: air strike. E: slow the enemies. R: repair the gate. F: location power. - Esc: pause. - Don't let the stickmen break your gate. Survive as many nights as you can! How to play (mobile): - Day: tap the upgrade cards. - Night: drag your finger to look around. - Sniper: hold FIRE to zoom, slide the same finger to aim, release to shoot. - Rifle: hold FIRE. - Rockets: tap FIRE. - Tap the weapon icons to switch weapons.
