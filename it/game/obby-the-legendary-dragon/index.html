Come Giocare

An exciting and addictive pet catching simulator for pokemon lovers. Travel through different roblox worlds and a lot of leveling heroes in one place! Collect health, upgrade your hero to explore the most amazing worlds of this game. Catch pets in battles, hatch them from cases and eggs, buy and exchange them to get the strongest helpers and friends. Earn money and upgrade your Obby character with the help of ranks and titles. PC control: AWSD - running; Space - jump; Tab - hide/show the cursor; Mouse - turn. On smartphones: Joystick movement - running; The up arrow is a jump.
