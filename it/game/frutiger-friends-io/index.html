Come Giocare

Explore the hub and walk through a portal to join a game. SKYSCRAPER: Red chasers catch blue runners by touching them. Caught runners become chasers too! Runners win if someone stays free until time runs out. Chasers win by catching everyone. Run up walls and collect power-ups to help your side. DOLPHINDITCH: Ride a dolphin, pass the ball, and throw it through the opposing team's hoops. The team with the most points wins. DESKTOP CONTROLS: WASD - Move Mouse - Look around Space - Jump C - Let go of a wall R - Use a collected power-up E - Interact Tab - Show the mouse cursor Esc - Open the menu In Dolphinditch: Left mouse button or F - Throw the ball or lunge to steal Q - Pass B - Toggle ball camera MOBILE CONTROLS: Drag to move and use the on-screen buttons to jump, use power-ups, and interact. In Dolphinditch, use the throw, pass, and camera buttons.
