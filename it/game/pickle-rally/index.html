Come Giocare

Win each rally by landing the ball where your rival can’t return it. A game is played to 7, 11 or 15 points and you must win by 2. Clear the World Tour to unlock Elite, Endless and the Champion Tour. Your player runs to the ball automatically. You choose the shot and its direction. Switch Movement to FREE in Settings if you want to move yourself. Keyboard: J = Dink (soft shot). K = Drive (power shot), press K on a high ball to SMASH. L = Lob. Hold A/D or Left/Right while hitting to aim. P = Pause. FREE movement: WASD or arrow keys. Touch: Tap = Dink (tap left or right of centre to aim). Swipe up = Drive, or Smash on a high ball (swipe diagonally to aim). Swipe down = Lob. FREE movement: the left half of the screen is a joystick, the right half hits.
