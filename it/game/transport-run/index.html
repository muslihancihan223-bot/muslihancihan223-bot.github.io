Come Giocare

Your main goal is to drive forward as far as possible without crashing into other vehicles, survive the traffic to rack up distance-based scores, and use careful stops to upgrade your vehicle for better performance—reach high scores by mastering timing and avoiding collisions! PC Controls: Mouse / Arrows: Swipe or hold left/right to steer and change lanes. Hold Mouse Button / Space: Accelerate forward. Release: Stop or brake. Mobile Controls: Touchscreen: Tap and hold screen to drive forward; release to stop. Swipe left/right: Steer and switch lanes.
