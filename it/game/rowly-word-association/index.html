Gioca a Rowly: Word Association

Rowly: Word Association è un gioco gratuito di tipo Puzzle, Words, Rilassanti. Senza download né registrazione: gioca subito nel browser su telefono, tablet o computer.

Come Giocare

Goal: sort all tiles into rows so that every row is one theme. Drag a tile onto another tile, or click one tile and then another, to swap them. When a row holds only tiles of one theme it locks and shows the theme name. Lock every row to finish the level. Stuck? Use a Hint: it shows a swap that brings a row closer to done. Controls: mouse on desktop (drag and drop or two clicks), touch on phones and tablets.
