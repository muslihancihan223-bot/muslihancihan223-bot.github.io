Come Giocare

Move with WASD and look with the mouse. Space swims up and Shift swims down. Press E to steal an egg, use shops and place eggs on your farm, Esc for the menu. On phones use the left thumb to move, the right thumb to look and the ACTION button. Video stands at the spawn give Swim and Luck boosts.
