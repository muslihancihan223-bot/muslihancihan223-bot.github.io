Come Giocare

Fight your way down through the dungeon: clear every room of monsters, pick up the weapons they drop and choose one of three relics after each floor. Reach floor 15 and beat the final boss. If you die, the coins you collected stay with you — spend them on permanent upgrades and new heroes, then dive in again. A new daily challenge with the same conditions for everyone is available every day. PC: WASD or arrows — move Mouse — aim, left button — shoot Space — hero skill Shift — dash R — reload E — pick up / interact Q — switch weapon Esc — pause Mobile: Left stick — move Right side — aim and fire (auto-aim available) On-screen buttons — skill and dash
