Come Giocare

1. Open a box in the “Cases” tab — the roulette shows what's inside. 2. Put your find up for sale: “Storage” → item → price → “List”. A price near the market sells faster, above the market pays more, but buyers will haggle. 3. Answer buyers in “Chats” with the buttons below: accept, name your price or decline. Sometimes saying no makes a buyer raise the offer! 4. Never follow links from “buyers” — they are scammers. Block them and get a vigilance bonus. 5. Invest your revenue in the “Upgrades” tab and complete collections for price bonuses. Short on money? Tap the laptop on the home screen. Controls: mouse or touch.
