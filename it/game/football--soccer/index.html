Come Giocare

On PC: WASD - controlling the movement of a football player on the playing field. The arrows on the keyboard control the player's actions. If the ball belongs to a player, then: Left arrow - pass to another player; Up arrow - kick towards the opponent's goal; The right arrow is the acceleration of the football player. If the ball belongs to the opponent, then: Left arrow - choose another player; Up arrow - selection of the ball from the opponent; Right arrow - following the player with the ball and further struggle. On mobile: The player's movement is controlled by a joystick. The buttons control the actions of the football player. There are corresponding text hints on the buttons.
