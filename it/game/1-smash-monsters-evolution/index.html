Come Giocare

Click or tap to gain Power, then walk through the gate into a stage: your hero fights the monsters on its own. Clear the stage, step on its reward pad for trophies and beat the bosses to move on. Spend trophies on swatters, pets, auras and upgrades, and rebirth for a bigger multiplier. Desktop: WASD to move, Space to jump, hold the right mouse button to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom, E to buy, open or use what the prompt shows, E or F for the special attack. Phone: touch the left side of the screen for the joystick, drag on the right side to turn the camera, tap to gain Power, tap the prompt and the Special Attack button.
