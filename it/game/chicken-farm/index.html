Come Giocare

🎮 Controls: WASD — movement, mouse — camera control. 🐔 How to play: Buy chickens and place them on your farm. 🐣 🥚 Chickens lay eggs automatically — collect them and exchange them for money. 💰 Use your earnings to buy new chickens and expand your farm. 🔀 Merge identical chickens to get rarer, more profitable breeds. 🏡 Upgrade your farm and unlock new features as you progress. 🌙 Even when you're offline, your chickens keep laying eggs.
