Come Giocare

DESKTOP Move - W A S D (read by physical key position, so it also works on AZERTY) Cast - hold SPACE at the water's edge, release to throw. Hold longer to cast further. Strike, reel and shoot - F or the left mouse button Aim the bow - drag the mouse while SPACE is held Interact with shops, cars and jetskis - E Fishdex - Q Switch tool (rod, sword, bow) - 1 2 3 Pause - P MOBILE / TABLET (Portrait & Landscape) Move - the on-screen joystick at the bottom left Cast, reel, strike - the big round button at the bottom right Switch tool - the three buttons beside it Interact - tap the prompt that appears on screen Menus - the icons in the top corners To land a fish, cast into the water, wait for the bite, then hold the action button to keep the line tight and release when the tension meter fills. Bigger fish pull harder and pay more coins. Coins buy better rods, lures, swords and bows, plus cars, jetskis, accessories and skins from the shops on the beach.
