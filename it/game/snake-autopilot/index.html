Come Giocare

Control your snake with the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on your phone. 🍎 Apple: +10 points ⭐ Star: +30 points Press "P" to pause or "Esc" to open the menu. Activate "Autopilot" by watching an ad or spending 30 coins. The AI controls your snake for 15 apples in regular mode or one round per duel. In "Duel" mode, win two rounds to claim victory! If you crash, your opponent wins the round. If time runs out, the player who collected more food wins. Power-ups activate instantly: positive effects help you, while negative ones affect your opponent. You can turn music and sound effects on or off separately in the menu.
