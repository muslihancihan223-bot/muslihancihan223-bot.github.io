Come Giocare

Your goal is to be the first team to reach the frag goal. When you go down, you respawn instantly. On desktop: • WASD — move • Mouse — look around • Left mouse button — shoot • Right mouse button — aim • R — reload • Space — jump • Shift or C — slide • 1, 2, 3, 4 — rifle, pistol, melee, grenade On mobile: • Left joystick — move • Swipe on the right side of the screen — look around • On-screen buttons — shoot, reload, jump, slide and switch weapons Win a round to get a case. Open cases to get gems, skins and new weapons. Spend gems on weapons and cases in the menu. Before the battle, choose the weapons you take into the round.
