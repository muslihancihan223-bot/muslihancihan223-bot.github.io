Come Giocare

Drag anywhere on the screen to aim: up and down raises the barrel, left and right turns the turret. Release to fire. Hold the arrow buttons to drive, but your fuel is limited each turn. Pick a special shell from the bottom row before you shoot, and tap the screen while a Split Shell flies to split it. Your last shot stays drawn as a line, so you can correct your aim. Destroy the rival's tank before it destroys yours. Hit its commander for a critical hit, or blow up nearby barrels for bonus damage. Win duels to rank up, earn rewards and take on new islands. Works with a mouse or touch.
