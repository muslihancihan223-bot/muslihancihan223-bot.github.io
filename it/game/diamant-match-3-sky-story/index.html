Come Giocare

Line up three or more colorful gems to achieve your goals and solve challenging puzzles! Prepare to face a variety of obstacles - from solid stone blockades and pesky bees to adorable spiders, sticky webs, rushing rivers, and more! Earn valuable artifacts as you conquer levels and complete diverse quests to upgrade your town and decorate it with unique, eye-catching items. Call upon helpful Golem companions on your journey and become a true hero in this magical Match-3 adventure!
