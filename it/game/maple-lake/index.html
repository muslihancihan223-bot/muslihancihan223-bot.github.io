Come Giocare

🏗️ Tap BUILD to create a new building. 🔄 Drag two identical buildings together to merge them into a higher-level building. 🔁 Drop a building onto a different one to swap their positions. 🗑️ Drag unwanted buildings to the bin to remove them. ⬆️ Use UPGRADE to increase the level of buildings created by BUILD. 🗺️ Use EXPAND to unlock more land for your resort. 💰 Buildings from level 5 onward generate passive income automatically. 🌲 Place decorations like trees, fountains, lanterns, and wells to increase your income. 📋 Complete contracts to earn cash and unlock free land. 🎡 Spin the Lucky Wheel for extra rewards. 🏆 Keep merging and expanding until you build the ultimate Maple Lake resort. Merge, build, expand, and turn Maple Lake into the resort of your dreams! 🏡🌲✨
