Come Giocare

Goal: grow life from the first molecule to a star-faring civilization, then seed new worlds. Desktop: click the planet (or press Space) to create energy; buy life forms in the Life tab; spend DNA on traits in the Evolution tab and press "Make the leap" to enter a new era. Keys 1–8 cast Eon powers, M toggles sound, Esc or P pauses. Mobile: tap the planet to create energy; switch tabs at the bottom of the screen; Eon powers are the round buttons on the scene. Keep the temperature near the white mark for a harmony bonus. Progress is saved automatically.
