Come Giocare

Goal: survive each night shift, serve real customers and refuse the anomalies. Read the shift rules (Tab), look closely at the customer, then serve or refuse. Mistakes add penalties; too many and the Overseer comes for you. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, E to interact, Q archive, R establishments, T daily rewards, Tab rules, P pause. Mobile: left joystick to move, swipe to look, on-screen buttons to act.
