Come Giocare

Flying in "Sky Rush" is pure fun and super easy to start! Here is how to play: — Controls: Click and hold your mouse or touch the screen anywhere. Your plane will follow your finger or cursor instantly. The controls are very responsive, so you can easily dodge any obstacle. — Collect Coins: Fly around the screen to grab golden stars. They are the key to getting stronger and upgrading your flight! — Maneuvers: Avoid enemies and incoming missiles. As the tempo rises, feel the rhythm and find the safest paths through the sky. — Upgrades: If you feel the speed is getting too high, visit the Shop. Spend your earned coins on powerful buffs to make your journey smoother, or grab a new skin to fly in style! — Your Record: Just enjoy the flight and see how far you can go this time. Every new start is a chance to beat your own record and climb the leaderboard! Have a great flight and enjoy the ride!
