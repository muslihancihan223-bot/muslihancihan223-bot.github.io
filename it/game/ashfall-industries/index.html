Come Giocare

How to Play 1. Buy an operation and click its Run button to begin producing Scrap. 2. Spend Scrap to level up operations, unlock more businesses, and improve your income. 3. Hire Crew Chiefs to automate production cycles. 4. Purchase Retrofits and collect Lucky Finds to accelerate your growth. 5. Use Reclamation when you are ready to earn Reactor Cores and rebuild faster. Mouse - Left-click buttons to run operations, collect rewards, make purchases, and navigate menus. Touchscreen - Tap to use the same controls.
