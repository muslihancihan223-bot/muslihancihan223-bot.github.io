Come Giocare

Controls on computer: mouse — building and camera (right button — rotate, wheel — zoom), WASD/arrows — move camera, R — rotate item, Delete — sell, Esc — exit walk mode. Controls on phone: tap — build and select, two fingers — rotate and zoom camera, joystick — walking in walk mode.
