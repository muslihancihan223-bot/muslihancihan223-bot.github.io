Come Giocare

Score as many points as you can before the two minutes run out. Mouse or touch: press anywhere on the screen and drag. The circle is the highest point the ball will reach, and the dotted line shows how it gets there. Let go to shoot. If you let go without dragging, the throw is cancelled. Keyboard: the arrow keys move the circle, Space shoots. Longer shots score more. Clean swishes, bank shots and makes in a row raise your multiplier, and a miss resets the streak. P or Esc pauses the game. On a phone, play in landscape.
