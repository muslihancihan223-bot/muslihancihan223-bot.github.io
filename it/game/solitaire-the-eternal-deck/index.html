Come Giocare

Objective of the Game Sort the entire deck into four foundation piles, arranging each suit in ascending order—from ace to king. How to Play Cards can be moved by dragging or with a single tap. Cards are placed in each foundation pile strictly in ascending order within a single suit. Only a king may be placed on an empty space on the playing field. Cards on the playing field are arranged in descending order, with colors alternating (for example, a red card one rank lower is placed on a black card). You can move either a single card or entire sequences, provided they are arranged according to the rules. Click on the deck to draw a new card. Once the deck is empty, you can refresh it using the refresh button. Additional Information Cards from the “homes” may be returned to the playing field. Use the undo feature to adjust your strategy. Bonus points are awarded if you first place a card on the playing field and then send it to a “home.”
