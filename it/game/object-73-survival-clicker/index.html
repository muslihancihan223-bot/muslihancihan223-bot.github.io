Come Giocare

You control Object 73, a clone exploring the underground Jericho Complex. Your goal is to survive, gather resources, upgrade equipment and uncover the truth behind the experiment. *Combat Click on enemies to attack them and deal damage. Defeated enemies drop resources, ammo and crafting materials. *Resources Collect food, water, materials, caps and ammunition. These resources are used for survival, crafting and buying items in the shop. *Crafting and Shop Use collected materials to craft new weapons and armor or buy equipment in the shop. Stronger gear allows you to defeat tougher enemies. *Story and Choices Throughout the game you will discover memory fragments and meet different characters. Your choices influence the humanity level of the hero and affect how the story develops. Upgrade your gear, defeat powerful enemies and uncover the dark secret of Project Jericho.
