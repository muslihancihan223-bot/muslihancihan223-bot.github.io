Come Giocare

* Select an item from the menu, then left-click the battlefield to place it. * Press Esc to cancel placement. * Click and drag objects to move them. * Drag empty space or hold the middle mouse button to move the camera. Scroll to zoom. * Press P to switch between Build and Play modes. In Play mode, click a soldier to take control. * Use A/D to move, W to jump, and the mouse to aim. Left-click or press J to shoot or attack. * Press Space to pause or resume. Press Delete to remove the selected object. * On mobile, tap to select and place items, pinch to zoom, and use the on-screen joystick and action buttons to control your soldier.
