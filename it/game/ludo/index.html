Come Giocare

Roll the dice, move your tokens, and race your opponents to the finish! Bring all four of your tokens out of the starting area, navigate around the board, and safely reach your home zone. Land on your opponents' tokens to send them back to the starting area, and use strategic moves to stay ahead. The first player to get all four tokens home wins! Desktop Controls: - Mouse Click: Roll the dice. - Mouse Click: Select and move a token. - Mouse Click: Navigate menus and game options. Mobile Controls: - Tap: Roll the dice. - Tap: Select and move a token. - Tap: Navigate menus and game options.
