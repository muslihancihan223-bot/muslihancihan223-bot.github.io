Gioca a Escape the Giant Cat

Escape the Giant Cat è un gioco gratuito di tipo 3D, Corsa, Piattaforme. Senza download né registrazione: gioca subito nel browser su telefono, tablet o computer.

Come Giocare

Computer: move with WASD or the arrow keys, look around with the mouse, jump with SPACE (three jumps in a row make a super jump). In fight rooms, click or press SPACE to hit; hit fast for a three-hit combo. E uses a shop, the rebirth altar or a chest; P opens the shop, R rebirth, L achievements, O options, H goes back to the plaza and ALT frees the mouse. Phone and tablet: move with the stick on the left, drag to look and tap JUMP (it becomes HIT in fight rooms). Goal: run on the carpets to gain speed, open the barrier of each stage, claim trophies and reach the end of the Kitchen.
