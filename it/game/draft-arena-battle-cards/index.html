Come Giocare

Win matches by building the stronger army. Each round, click or tap one of three cards to add fighters to your army, then watch the armies fight on their own. Tap the bomb or lightning button, then tap the arena to use it at the right moment. Lose all hearts and the match is lost; win to move along the Victory Road and unlock new fighters. Controls: mouse or touch.
