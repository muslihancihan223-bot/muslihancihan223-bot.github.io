Come Giocare

Your goal is to grow from a Newbie into a Legendary artist and build the coziest studio in town. A client writes to you. Open the chat, choose your replies and read the order: what to draw, the wishes, the pay and the deadline. Accept the order, try to negotiate a higher price, or decline it. Tap Draw. Trace the light sketch lines with the Pen, then pick a color and tap the areas with Fill. Use the Eyedropper on the reference to copy the exact colors, and remember the client's wishes. Tap Finish before the time runs out. You get 1 to 5 stars and earn coins, tips, experience and reputation. Spend coins in the Shop on tools, palettes and furniture, decorate the room in Studio, and learn Skills with the points you get for new levels and ranks. Between orders, complete daily tasks, pet the cat, or open the Sketchbook from the Gallery and draw for fun. Controls on a computer: Draw with the left mouse button. Zoom with the mouse wheel. Hold Space and drag, or use the Move tool, to move the canvas. Tools: B Pen, M Marker, F Fill, E Eraser, I Eyedropper, S Stamps, H Move. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, [ and ] brush size, G show or hide the sketch, P pause. Controls on a phone or tablet: Draw with a finger or a stylus. Use two fingers to zoom and move the canvas. Tap with two fingers to undo, with three fingers to redo. On a phone held upright, the chat button at the bottom opens your chats, and you can swipe the room left and right.
