Come Giocare

Goal: finish each mission by collecting the target resource, defeating enough enemies or taking down the boss, then reach your ship before the bugs get you. Controls - Desktop: move with W A S D or the arrow keys. - Phone and tablet: drag on the screen to steer with the floating joystick (a mouse works too). - You attack and mine automatically. Stay close to enemies and rocks and your gun and pickaxe do the rest. Tips 1. Dig into glowing rocks to collect gold and crystals. 2. Pick up blue elixir to fill the XP bar. Every level-up lets you choose a new skill: speed, firepower, faster mining, healing, a drone or a shield. 3. Watch the progress bar. At 100% your ship arrives. Get to it to finish the mission. 4. If you fall, you can watch an optional video ad to revive once per mission. Video ads can also unlock all offered skills at once or reroll them. 5. Between missions, spend your crystals on permanent upgrades to health, damage and defence.
