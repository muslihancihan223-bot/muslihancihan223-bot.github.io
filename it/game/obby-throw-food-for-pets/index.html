Gioca a OBBY: Throw Food for Pets!

OBBY: Throw Food for Pets! è un gioco gratuito di tipo Arcade, Simulazione, Bambini. Senza download né registrazione: gioca subito nel browser su telefono, tablet o computer.

Come Giocare

Walk into the THROW circle and tap when the needle is in the green: a PERFECT throw flies farther. Tap again when the ring appears to bounce the food even farther. The pet that comes to eat it is yours: carry it to your pen to earn money every second. Train at your pull-up bar to gain power, upgrade the bar and buy better food in the shop to reach new worlds and rarer pets. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, drag the mouse to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom, Space to jump, click or Space to throw. Mobile: joystick on the left to move, drag on the right to look, on-screen buttons to jump and throw.
