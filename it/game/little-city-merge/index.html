Come Giocare

Goal: grow your city and build a Public building of the level the city asks for, then travel to the next city. - Tap or click an empty lot to build. Building costs energy, which refills over time. - Drag a building onto another one of the same type and level to merge them into the next level. - Drag a building onto an empty lot to move it, or onto another building to swap them. Drop it on the trash bin to remove it. - Shops earn coins. Homes bring residents. - Public buildings open new land and raise the max level of homes and shops. Merging them needs enough residents. - Spend coins in Upgrades to earn more, build faster and get more energy. - When the city goal is done, open World and move to a new city. Controls: mouse or touch. Tap / click to build, drag and drop to merge, move or remove.
