نحوه بازی

Find the needle to complete each haystack; clearing every straw is optional. Click Upgrades below Collection to open Barn, Tools and Engineering remotely and switch shop tabs. Currency Exchange opens after the first needle. Sell cargo at the green Hay Market. Mobile: tap hay to collect or hold to keep gathering. Drag to look, move with the joystick and tap Jump. Tap the backpack or Hay Market marker to look toward the market, then walk there and tap the highlighted stall to sell. Hold the bomb button to aim and release your touches to throw; aiming off the haystack cancels. Desktop: WASD to move, click the world for mouse look, hold LMB to collect, Tab to open Upgrades, E or click to interact, Space to jump, hold/release Q to aim/throw a bomb, F for the drone, R for the scanner and P to close a menu or pause.
