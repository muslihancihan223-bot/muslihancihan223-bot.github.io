بازی Miner Builder

Miner Builder یک بازی شبیه‌سازی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

شبیه‌سازی

نحوه بازی

⁃ Move using the on-screen stick/WASD/Arrows; - To board a bulldozer, approach it. Wait for the gauge to fill; - To exit the bulldozer, press the button; - Board the bulldozer and collect cubes; - To board a crane, approach it. Wait for the gauge to fill; - To exit the crane, press the button; - Board the crane, build a house from cubes, and earn coins; - To upgrade construction equipment, go to the upgrade menu and press the button; - Upgrade your character and unlock new types of cubes; - To unlock a new conveyor belt, accumulate coins and purchase a conveyor belt; - To hire assistants, accumulate coins and purchase an assistant.
