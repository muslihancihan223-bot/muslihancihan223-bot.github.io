نحوه بازی

Build, then watch. Each round you get scrap: tap a part card to buy it, buy the same part again to upgrade it, reroll the shop for new offers, then press READY. Your robot fights on its own. First to 5 round wins takes the match, and the loser of a round gets bonus scrap to catch up. Parts of the same brand give set bonuses. RANKED: online matchmaking and a season leaderboard. ONLINE: private rooms with a code and tournaments. LOCAL: VS CPU or 2 players on one device. Mouse and touch work everywhere. Keyboard in local 2-player: Player 1 uses W/A/S/D to move, Space to buy, R to reroll, E for ready; Player 2 uses the arrow keys, Enter to buy, P to reroll, O for ready.
