بازی Ants: Color Puzzle

Ants: Color Puzzle یک بازی منطقی, جورچین, پازل رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

Click or tap a colored tile at the front of a queue to send out a team of ants. The ants automatically collect accessible blocks of their own color and carry them to the hole. The number on each tile shows how many blocks that team can collect. Choose colors available along the edges of the picture and keep an eye on your open team spaces. When a team finishes its job, its space becomes available again. Clear the whole picture to complete the level. Use boosters if you get stuck!
