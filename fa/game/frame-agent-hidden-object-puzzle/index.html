نحوه بازی

Goal: Find the hidden agent in each painting before the timer runs out. The faster you find him — and the less you zoom — the more gold you earn. Controls: Tap or click the agent to catch him. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom. Tap the UV flashlight to reveal the agent with a moving violet light (zoom locks while it's on). Tap the case file to see a close-up of your target. Extras: Watch a rewarded ad or spend gold to refill boosters. Complete the daily art quiz for bonus gold, and swipe through the Tip Line to judge whether an agent is really hiding in each painting. Cleared paintings unlock in your museum gallery.
