نحوه بازی

Are you ready for a hidden object game that's as cute as it is challenging? Jump into "100 Hidden Capybaras" – the ultimate hidden object adventure where your mission is to find and collect 100 adorable capybaras, each hiding in beautifully designed scenes! 🌿 Explore stunning environments filled with lush forests, charming villages, secret dungeons, and unknown planets. 🏘🐉🪐 Discover all hiding spots for our fluffy friends as you put your observation skills to the test. 🔍 In the black and white picture provided, find the capybara that is displayed in the upper right corner of the screen. Click on its location, then look for the next one. Enjoy vibrant graphics, and a delightful soundtrack that will keep you engaged throughout your journey.
