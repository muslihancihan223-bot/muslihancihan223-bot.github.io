نحوه بازی

Get across every room without touching the water and reach the ESCAPE pad at the end. 1. In the lobby, stand on a door's pad, pick a difficulty and press CREATE. 2. Jump from tile to tile. If you touch the water, the creature hears you and comes for you. 3. Throw a pebble into the water far away. The creature goes after the splash, and that is your chance to cross. 4. Pick up pebbles on the tiles, or get more with the FREE PEBBLES button. 5. On Normal and Hard, turn both valves to open the exit door. The valves are loud, so move away fast. 6. Step on the ESCAPE pad. You get coins, then go straight on to the next level. If the creature catches you, you go back to the last checkpoint.
