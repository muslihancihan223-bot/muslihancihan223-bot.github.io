نحوه بازی

Move with WASD and look with the mouse. Walk over the ice cream field to grow your roll, then run north onto the Sweet Road. When a buyer catches you, press E to sell, or watch a short video to decline and run deeper for a better price. Press E at shops, Tab frees the mouse. On phones use the joystick and the on-screen buttons. Booster pads near the spawn give x2 Speed or x2 Ice Cream for a short video.
