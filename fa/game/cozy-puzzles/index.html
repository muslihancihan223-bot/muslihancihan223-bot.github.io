Cozy Puzzles یک بازی پازل, آرامش‌بخش, منطقی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

پازلآرامش‌بخشمنطقی

نحوه بازی

The goal is to complete the selected picture from all pieces. 1. Open a collection and choose an image. 2. Select a difficulty from 16 to 1024 pieces. 3. Drag pieces onto the puzzle table. 4. Matching pieces snap together near the correct position. 5. Connected pieces form a group and move together. 6. Zoom and pan the board with the mouse wheel or a pinch gesture on mobile. 7. Use a hint or auto-solve if you need help. 8. Complete puzzles to earn coins and stars, unlock collections, and finish tasks.
