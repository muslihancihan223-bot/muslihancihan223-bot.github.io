نحوه بازی

PC controls: Move: WASD or arrows Camera: mouse Jump: Space Interact: hold E (stands, portals) Camera zoom: mouse wheel Phone controls: Move: left joystick Camera: swipe on the right side Jump: jump button Interact: hold the action button Inventory, shop and rewards: on-screen buttons How to play: 1. Jump around the arena — every jump gives agility and new levels. 2. Reach the top of a wall, step on the coin plate and collect coins. 3. Spend them on your first brainrot at a stand, a skin at the shop or a pet from an egg. 4. Repeat: higher level — taller walls and richer plates. 5. Rebirth for a new level cap and unlock the frost, then the fire world.
