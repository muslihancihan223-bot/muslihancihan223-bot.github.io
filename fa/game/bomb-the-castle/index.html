نحوه بازی

Destroy the opposing crew or castle to win. Mouse or touch: drag back on the battlefield and release to fire; return to the starting point to cancel. Tap a crew card to choose a weapon. Hold the left and right arrows to drive. Tap while a Rainmaker shell is flying to split it. Keyboard: A/D or arrow left/right to drive, W/S or arrow up/down to aim, Q/E to adjust power, Space or Enter to fire, number keys to select crew, Z to zoom, and P to pause. Between battles, open chests, train your crew and expand your castle. The shop has offers, currency packs and a daily crew market. Claim a free daily gift; optional videos grant bonus coins, double base victory resources or shorten chest timers. Optional purchases provide gems, a one-time starter bundle or permanent removal of automatic battle-break ads. All battles remain free to play.
