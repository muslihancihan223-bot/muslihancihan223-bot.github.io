نحوه بازی

Goal: be the last player alive. Open the 3 chests on your island, gear up, then build bridges to the middle island and to enemy islands. Players who fall into the void are out. PC: WASD move, mouse look, Space jump, Shift sneak (sneak at an edge to bridge safely), Ctrl or double W sprint, left click attack or mine, right click place block, open chest, shoot bow or eat, 1-9 or mouse wheel select item, E inventory, F5 camera, Tab scoreboard, Esc pause. Phone (landscape): left stick to move, drag the right side to look, buttons for attack, place/use, jump, sneak, inventory and camera. Holding a block near an edge auto-sneaks so you do not fall while bridging.
