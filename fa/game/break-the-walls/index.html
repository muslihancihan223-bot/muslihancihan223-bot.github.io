بازی Break The Walls

Break The Walls یک بازی آرکید, شبیه‌سازی, سه‌بعدی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

GOAL: break through 11 zones of walls, earn gold, upgrade your tools and reach the elevator to escape the show. Then Rebirth for a faster, stronger run. LOOP: hit the wall -> vacuum the flying stones into your backpack -> drop them into Chrum, the hungry pit -> collect the gold he spits out -> spend it in the Upgrade Book and on new tools. DESKTOP • Mouse: look around (click the game to capture the mouse) • W A S D: walk · Space: jump • Left mouse button (hold): hit, drill, vacuum • E: drop stones into Chrum, use stands, call the elevator • 1–6: switch tools · B: Upgrade Book · U: shop · R: rewards · I: inventory • Shift: switch between a free cursor and mouse capture • P or the pause button: pause MOBILE • Left stick: walk · drag on the right side: look • HIT: smash (drag the same finger to aim) · JUMP: jump • On-screen buttons open the Upgrade Book, shop, rewards and inventory
