نحوه بازی

Get ready for some lighthearted fun! Explore whimsical black-and-white scenes filled with charming details and see how many cleverly hidden pups you can find. Each dog is playfully tucked away in different settings — from cozy parks to bustling streets — just waiting to be spotted. There's no rush, just relax and enjoy the search! Controls: 🖱 Mouse: Move your cursor to explore the image. 🖱 Left Click: Click on a dog to find it. 🔍 Scroll Wheel: Zoom in and out to get a closer look. 🖱 Hold Left Click + Drag: Pan around the image. 💡 Hint Button (bottom left): Tap it to reveal the location of a hidden dog when you're stuck. Have fun, and see if you can find them all!
