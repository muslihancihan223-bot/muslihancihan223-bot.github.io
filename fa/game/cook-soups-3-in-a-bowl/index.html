نحوه بازی

🥄 How to play: 🍲 Different ingredients float in the pot. Your goal is to collect them all! 👆 Tap ingredients to take them out of the pot. Collect 3 of a kind — and they'll go onto a plate as a finished portion. 🍽️ If there's no plate for an ingredient yet, it goes into an extra slot at the bottom. It waits there until you find a match for it. ⚠️ Careful! There are only a few extra slots. If they all fill up and there's no room for a new ingredient — the soup is ruined and the level is lost. 🏆 Collect all the ingredients from the pot — and the soup is ready! The level is complete, and a new dish is added to your recipe book. Think one step ahead, free up slots in time, and become a master of soups! 🥣
