🎯 Game Objective Throw knives at ragdoll enemies and hit the target accurately! To pass a level, defeat all enemies using skill and precision. The more accurate your throw, the faster you win! 💻 On PC: Aim with the cursor and click the left mouse button to throw a knife in the chosen direction. 📱 On Mobile Devices: Tap the screen at the desired spot—the knife will fly where you point your finger. 🔥 Tip Watch the enemies' movements and choose the right moment to throw. Use different types of knives and become the sharpest ninja in the arena!
