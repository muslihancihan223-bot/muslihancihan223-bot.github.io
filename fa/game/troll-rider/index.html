بازی Troll Rider

Troll Rider یک بازی دویدنی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

دویدنی

نحوه بازی

Tap / Left Click / Space to jump. Hold while airborne to rotate backward and perform backflips. Land the X-bike smoothly to keep your speed and build your score. Collect Golden Gears to fill your Repair Meter and recover lost hearts. Watch the environment carefully—trees, ramps, bridges, signs, rocks, and even rewards may troll you. You have 3 Hearts. Crashing costs one heart. After a troll defeats you, you quickly retry the same challenge. Beat it to earn REVENGE. Survive longer, discover new trolls, master their tricks, and ride as far downhill as possible.
