بازی Hunting Master

Hunting Master یک بازی تیراندازی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

تیراندازی

نحوه بازی

Your main goal is to aim and shoot your bow at the animals on different floating islands. Pull back to adjust power and angle, then release to fire accurate arrows. Defeat as many animals as possible to earn points and stars. Use powerful abilities like Meteorite and Thunder to clear multiple targets at once! Mobile Controls: Touchscreen: Tap and drag on the screen to aim and pull the bowstring, then release to shoot.
