نحوه بازی

In the settings menu, you can enable the auto-shoot feature, which is perfect for mobile gaming. You can also adjust mouse sensitivity for PC and swipe sensitivity for mobile directly from the main menu. Use the radar to easily find your way around the level! Input Layout: For PC: Mouse – Rotate camera Tab or Esc – Pause WASD – Move Left Mouse Button (LMB) – Shoot F or E – Interact For Mobile: Swipe on screen – Rotate camera Joystick – Move Tap on screen – Shoot
