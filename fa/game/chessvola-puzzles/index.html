نحوه بازی

Solve each chess puzzle by finding the best move or sequence of moves from the given position. Click or tap a piece, then click or tap the destination square to make your move. If your move is correct, the puzzle continues automatically; if it is wrong, try again or use a hint. Complete the 5 Daily Puzzles to build your streak and earn rewards, or play from the full puzzle library to improve your training rating and leaderboard position. Controls: Mouse: click a piece, then click the target square Touchscreen: tap a piece, then tap the target square Hint button: shows the next correct move Reset button: restarts the current puzzle Skip button: moves past the current puzzle when available
