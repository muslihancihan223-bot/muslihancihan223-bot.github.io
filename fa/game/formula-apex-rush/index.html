بازی Formula Apex Rush

Formula Apex Rush یک بازی مسابقه‌ای, آرکید, رانندگی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

مسابقه‌ایآرکیدرانندگی

نحوه بازی

Use W / Up Arrow to accelerate and S / Down Arrow to brake. Steer with A / D or the Left / Right Arrow keys. Use ERS/Nitro on straight sections or when overtaking rivals for a temporary speed boost. Avoid leaving the track because grass and off-road areas will slow your car down significantly. Complete every lap as quickly as possible, overtake the AI racers, and cross the finish line in the best position you can. Earn credits from races and use them in the Garage to improve your car for tougher events. On mobile, use the on-screen steering, accelerator, brake, and boost controls.
