بازی TCG Shop: Dino Adventure

TCG Shop: Dino Adventure یک بازی سرگرم‌کننده رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

The goal Battle league rivals, earn coins, open packs, and build your prehistoric card collection. Climb the league ladder, grow a stronger battle deck, and fill your binder with rare finds. Core loop Battle a league opponent in a 3v3 fight using your cursor or your finger Win to earn coins and progress toward promotion. Buy packs in the Shop and tap to open them. Swipe each pulled card: left to sell for coins, right to sleeve into your binder. Repeat — climb the league and complete your collection.
