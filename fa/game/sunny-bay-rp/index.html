بازی Sunny Bay RP

Sunny Bay RP یک بازی شبیه‌سازی, سه‌بعدی, اوبی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

Move with WASD or the on-screen stick, look around by dragging the mouse or swiping, jump with Space and sprint with Shift. Press E or the action button to sit, open doors, get into vehicles, start a job or talk to townsfolk through the interaction wheel. Open the phone for jobs, the map, houses, the garage, the shop and daily gifts. Finish job shifts to earn money, level up your jobs and spend your cash on outfits, pets and cars.
