بازی Avocado Yard: Ragdoll Sandbox

Avocado Yard: Ragdoll Sandbox یک بازی پازل, آرکید, شبیه‌سازی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

Build a scene, play with physics and complete challenges to earn stars. - Place items: pick a toy on the shelf (Avocado, Watermelon, Banana, Orange, Crate, Rock, Plank, Bomb, Barrel, Anvil, Balloon, Trampoline, Spikes, Fan, Glass, Sled, Snowman, Present and more), then tap or click the field. - Grab: press on an object, drag and release to throw it. A quick tap on an avocado opens its moves. - Sled: place a sled next to an avocado and it sits down; on the Snowy hill it rides down the slope. - Moves: pick Moves and tap an avocado to make it run, dance, backflip, jump, flex, dab, floss or kick, or tap "Everyone dance". - Wand: pick a spell in the row above the shelf (Grow, Shrink, Ghost, Float, Bubble, Revive, Disco, Snow, Fireworks, Gift), then tap an avocado, a toy or the field. Tap a bubble to pop it. - Blast: tap the field to set off an explosion. Pistol: press, drag toward the target and release. Zap: tap to call down lightning. - Freeze: tap an object to lock it in place, tap again to release it. Delete: tap an object to remove it. - Nail and Rope: tap an object, then another object or the background. - Challenges and Daily: tap the star panel at the top left for goals, the daily gift, quests, the Winter calendar and skins. The gift button at the top right opens today's calendar door. - Modes: pick a map, play missions (limited moves, up to three stars each) or Rampage (60 seconds of damage for the leaderboard). - Premium toys unlock with stars, pits or a short optional ad. Exclusive toys unlock forever after one optional ad. - Top bar: pause, slow motion, gravity, clear, reset, Modes, sound, language, how to play. - Keyboard: Space pause, S slow motion, G gravity, C clear, R reset, T Rampage, D delete, M avocado.
