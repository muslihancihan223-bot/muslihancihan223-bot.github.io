نحوه بازی

Sorting these magic blocks is easy to learn but hard to master! Here is how you play: 🎯 Tap to Move: Tap any tube to lift the top block, then tap another tube to drop the block into it. 📏 Sorting Rule: You can only place a block on top of another block if they have the same color/symbol, or if the destination tube is completely empty. 📦 Fill the Tube: Keep sorting until each tube contains only one matching type of magic block. 🧠 Use Special Tools: Stuck on a hard level? Use the Undo button to take back your last move, or Restart the level to try a fresh strategy! ⏳ No Time Pressure: Play at your own pace! There are no timers, so you can relax and find your calm through sorting.
