بازی Bun Boss

Bun Boss یک بازی آرکید, شبیه‌سازی, تایکون رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

Touch and drag anywhere to move (WASD / arrow keys or mouse on desktop). Everything else is automatic: walk through the wheat field to harvest, stand at the oven or mill to load it, pick up fresh bakes, stand next to a shelf to stock it, and stand at the counter to serve customers. Collect the coins they leave and step on glowing pads to buy new machines, upgrades and helpers. A yellow arrow always points to the next thing to do. Watch for Rush Hour (higher prices) and VIP guests with big orders.
