بازی 99 Night Survivor

99 Night Survivor یک بازی ماجراجویی, ترسناک, سه‌بعدی رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

ماجراجوییترسناکسه‌بعدیبقاآرامش‌بخش

نحوه بازی

Goal: survive 99 nights and rescue the four lost kids. Keep the campfire burning, upgrade it to clear the fog, and defend the camp at night. PC: WASD - move, mouse - look, left click - attack / use item, Space - jump, Shift - sprint, E - interact, F - upgrade the fire (near it), 1-6 - hotbar, Tab - inventory, Q - drop, R - reload, M - map, J - quests, N - notes, K - shop, Esc - pause. Keys can be rebound in Settings. Mobile: joystick on the left to move, swipe on the right to look, on-screen buttons to attack, jump, sprint and interact. Gamepads are supported.
