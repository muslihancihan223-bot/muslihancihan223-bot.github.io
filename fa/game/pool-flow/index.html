بازی Pool Flow

Pool Flow یک بازی پازل, فیزیک, فکری رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

Fill the bucket below the pool before the timer runs out. Cut off safe sections of the pool and let their water fall into the bucket. Reach 100% on the bucket's progress indicator to win. Keep the sea creatures safe, avoid cutting through them, and time your cuts around moving hazards. Earn stars and coins, unlock later levels, and use optional boosters when you need help. Desktop: hold the left mouse button, drag a straight line across the pool, and release to cut. Click the on-screen buttons to select boosters, open settings, or pause. Escape opens or closes the pause menu during play. Mobile: touch, drag across the pool, and lift your finger to cut. Tap the booster and pause buttons. Both portrait and landscape are supported.
