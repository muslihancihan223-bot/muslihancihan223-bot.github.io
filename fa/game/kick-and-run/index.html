نحوه بازی

Become the ultimate runner! 🥊 Race through action-packed levels, dodge traps, outmaneuver enemies, and kick your way to the finish line. Keep moving, choose a safe path, and knock opponents aside before they stop your run. Desktop Controls: Arrow Keys or WASD: Move your character. Mouse and Arrow key "Right": Click and drag to attack. Mobile Controls: Swipe on the screen: Move your character. Touch on the screen: Attack Goal: Reach the finish line and complete each level.
