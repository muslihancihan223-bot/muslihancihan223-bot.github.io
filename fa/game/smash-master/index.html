نحوه بازی

Aim the cannon at the stacked objects. Find the weakest or most effective target point. Tap or release to fire the cannonball. Use physics and chain reactions to knock down multiple objects. Complete the required objective before running out of shots. Clear the level and move on to the next challenging structure.
