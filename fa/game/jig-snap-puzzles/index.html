نحوه بازی

Getting Started: Drag and drop puzzle pieces to their correct positions on the board. When two correct pieces are next to each other, they'll automatically merge together! Controls: Drag: Move individual pieces or groups Swap: Drop a piece on another to swap positions Hint Button: Use hints to automatically place pieces (costs coins or limited per level) Progression: Complete puzzles to earn ⭐ stars and 💰 coins Collect enough stars to unlock new themed packs Difficulty increases as you progress (3×3 → 4×4 → 5×5) First completion of each level earns stars - replay anytime for more coins! Tips: Start by finding corner and edge pieces Group similar colors or patterns together Use hints wisely for tricky puzzles Complete earlier packs to unlock harder challenges Ready to become a Puzzle Master?
