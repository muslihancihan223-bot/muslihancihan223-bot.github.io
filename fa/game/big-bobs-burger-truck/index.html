بازی Big Bob's Burger Truck

Big Bob's Burger Truck یک بازی آرکید, شبیه‌سازی, تایکون رایگان است. بدون دانلود و ثبت‌نام — فوراً در مرورگر گوشی، تبلت یا کامپیوتر بازی کن.

نحوه بازی

Goal: earn stars by serving burgers people love. Earn enough stars in a town to open the road to the next one, then win over all three towns. Menu: tap ingredients to stack a burger (every burger needs a patty), set a price and save it to the menu board. Lunch rush: tap a patty bin to grill a patty and tap it when its ring turns green. Add the toppings in the order on the ticket, then the top bun, and tap the customer to serve. Listen: whatever customers say goes in your Notebook. Spend your money in the Shop on new ingredients and upgrades. Plays with a mouse or touch. Press P to pause.
