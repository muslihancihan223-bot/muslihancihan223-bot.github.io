Cara Bermain

On the computer: To make the character move to the right, left, up or down, press the D, A, W, S keys or the arrows to the right, left, up or down, respectively. On a smartphone or tablet: To make the character move to the right, left, up or down, press the arrow buttons to the right, left, up or down, respectively.
