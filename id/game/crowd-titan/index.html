Main Crowd Titan

Crowd Titan adalah game Aksi, Arcade, 3D gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

AksiArcade3DLariRunner

Cara Bermain

Reach the finish with as many people as you can. Desktop: drag with the mouse or use A/D or the arrow keys to steer, Space or the Titan button to become the giant when the bar is full, P to pause. Mobile: drag a finger left and right to steer, tap the Titan button. Go through gates that add or multiply people and avoid those that take them away. Traps knock people out, so steer around them. Outnumber enemy squads to get past. On boss levels your crowd throws people at the boss on its own: keep out of the red circle where it lands and use the Titan to hit back.
