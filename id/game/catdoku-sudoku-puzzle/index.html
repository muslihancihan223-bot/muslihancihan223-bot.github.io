Cara Bermain

Rule 1 Place one cat in each colored region Every territory needs exactly one cat. Empty regions are incomplete, and two cats in one region break the puzzle. Rule 2 Avoid same rows and columns A placed cat controls its entire row and column, so no other cat can share either line. Rule 3 Do not let cats touch Cats also cannot touch diagonally. Corner contact is a conflict. Rule 4 Protect your hearts Wrong taps cost hearts, so every placement should come from logic instead of guessing.
