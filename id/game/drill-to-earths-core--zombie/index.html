Cara Bermain

Explore the world and find the perfect place to start drilling. Use your drilling machine to break through layers of the Earth. Collect valuable ores, gold, diamonds, and other resources. Sell your resources to earn coins. Upgrade your drill's power, speed, and efficiency. Unlock better equipment to dig even deeper. Watch out for zombies and avoid their attacks. Discover new underground layers with rare treasures. Keep upgrading your equipment and make your way toward the Earth's core! Your goal is to drill as deep as possible, collect valuable resources, survive zombie attacks, and reach the center of the Earth!
