Main Cozy idle village

Cozy idle village adalah game Strategi, Simulasi, Santai gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

StrategiSimulasiSantaiIdle

Cara Bermain

Drag to move the camera, pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom. Tap the Build tab to construct a building, then place it on a free tile. Tap any building to assign villagers to it; tap Villagers to manage everyone at once. Complete daily quests, unlock the tech tree, and collect what the village produced while you were away. Everything is playable with one finger or the left mouse button.
