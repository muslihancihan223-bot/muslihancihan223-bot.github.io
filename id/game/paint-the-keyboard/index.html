Main Paint the Keyboard

Paint the Keyboard adalah game Clicker, Obby, Drawing gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

ClickerObbyDrawing

Cara Bermain

A game about painting keys: press buttons, earn money, hire workers and expand the keyboard. On the computer: WASD — move. Hold down the right mouse button and move the mouse — rotate the camera. Mouse wheel — zoom in and out of the camera. On a mobile device: The joystick is moving. Swipe on the screen — rotate the camera. Move two fingers together or apart to indicate the camera's proximity and distance. To fill the roller with paint, go to the paint tank.
