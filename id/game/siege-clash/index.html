Cara Bermain

Enemies march toward your castle wall. Stop them before it falls. Press on an enemy, drag to lift it, then flick and let go to throw it. Harder throws hit harder when they land. Quickly tap an enemy to swat it, even heavy ones. Flick a body sideways to bowl over a whole line, or lift one high and flick down for a ground slam. Swipe fast across empty ground to sweep enemies off their feet. Tap a weapon button, then tap the field to fire. Tap traps marked "TAP!" and grab dropped power-ups. Throw enemies into the moat, lava or quicksand for bonus gold. Between days, spend gold in the Keep on upgrades, defenders and abilities. Works with mouse or touch.
