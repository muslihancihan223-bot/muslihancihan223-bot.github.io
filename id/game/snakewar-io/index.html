Cara Bermain

Grow the longest snake in the arena by eating fruits scattered across the map. Defeat other players by forcing them to crash into your body. When enemies die, they drop huge amounts of fruits. Collect them quickly and keep growing. Movement & Boost PC: Move your mouse to control the snake. Need speed? PC: Hold Left Click Boosting makes you faster but reduces your snake’s length. Use it carefully to escape danger or trap enemies. Growth & Kills Eat fruits for steady growth. Eliminate rival snakes for massive rewards. If another snake’s head touches your body, they are eliminated instantly and explode into fruits. Collect the dropped fruits before other players steal them. Arena Strategy Stay near the center for more fruits. Trap smaller snakes by circling them. Avoid giant snakes until you grow bigger. Use boost wisely — too much boosting makes you smaller and vulnerable. Watch the leaderboard and target distracted players. Key Features Multiplayer Arena Combat: Real-time battles. Compete against global players in nonstop sessions. Customizable Skins: Pumpkin heads. Pirate themes. Pig designs. Tailor your snake's appearance before each match. Speed Boost Mechanic: Burn length for temporary acceleration. High-risk, high-reward positioning tool. Nostalgic Snake Gameplay: Classic snake logic meets modern IO-style competition. Top-down 2D perspective. Flat vector graphics. Runs lag-free on any device. Leaderboard Progression: Infinite climb. Every kill and orb pushes you higher. No download required. How do I eliminate other players? Force enemy snakes to crash into your body. Does boosting reduce size? Yes. Boosting consumes part of your snake’s length. Can I customize my snake? Yes. Different skins are available before matches. Is SnakeWarz.IO multiplayer? Yes. All matches are real-time online multiplayer battles.
