Main Break and Steal Eggs

Break and Steal Eggs adalah game Arcade, 3D, Obby gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Arcade3DObby

Cara Bermain

Break an egg in a zone, grab the pet that jumps out and carry it back to your base. Place pets in your pen to earn money, then buy stronger pickaxes and more speed to reach farther zones. 💻 PC Controls: WASD – Move Hold Right Mouse Button – Rotate camera Space – Jump Left Mouse Button / F – Swing the pickaxe or the bat 1 / 2 – Pickaxe / bat Hold E – Grab a pet R – Drop the pet 📱 Mobile Controls: Joystick – Move Swipe on the right side of the screen – Rotate camera On-screen buttons – Swing, jump, grab and other actions
