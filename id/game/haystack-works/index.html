Main Haystack Works

Haystack Works adalah game Arcade, Simulasi, 3D gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

ArcadeSimulasi3DTycoonClickerManajemenIdle

Cara Bermain

Click the game, then hold the left mouse button on the hay to gather it; carry it to the sale dock on the left and press E to sell. Walk with WASD. T opens the research tree, I the tools, B the build catalog; R rotates, PageUp/PageDown change build height, conveyors take two clicks, Q cancels, F loads hay into a machine. Tab opens the menu and pauses (press Tab again to return); Esc also works as a fallback. Automate production, fill orders and find all six hidden needles to win.
