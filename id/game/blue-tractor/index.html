Main Blue Tractor

Blue Tractor adalah game Simulasi, Anak, 3D gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Cara Bermain

Goal: follow the arrow to each character, complete their delivery and collect-quests, and earn coins to upgrade your tractor. Desktop: W/A/S/D or arrow keys — drive and steer; Space — brake. Pause button in the corner. Mobile: drag the on-screen joystick to drive and steer. Tips: keep an eye on the fuel bar — buy fuel in the shop (if the tank runs empty, the farmers refill it for you after a short stop). Repair broken wheels in the shop, or wait and the farm mechanic will fix them. Avoid deep water. The pause menu shows your longest ride and longest time on the farm — try to beat the leaderboard!
