Cara Bermain

Your first race starts automatically. After that, choose a mode and course in the menu and press PLAY. Race to the finish or survive the round by avoiding moving obstacles and staying on the platforms. Earn rewards and unlock characters as you play. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move; Space to jump; Left Shift to dive; F or Left Ctrl for your action; Q/E or right-mouse drag to rotate the camera; P to pause; Tab to spectate. Mobile: use the on-screen joystick and action buttons; drag the camera area to look around.
