Main Tint Tap

Tint Tap adalah game Anak, Santai, Drawing gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

AnakSantaiDrawingColor

Cara Bermain

Drag your finger or mouse over the glowing part to paint it with the current colour. When a colour is done, the next one lights up; finish every colour to complete the picture. On some pictures you first draw the outline: drag along the dotted line from the dot to the X. Stuck on a tiny spot? Tap Hint to see where paint is still missing. Open the Album to choose any picture, use the pen button to switch between the marker, pencil and spray can, and Pause for music and sound.
