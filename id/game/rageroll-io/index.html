Cara Bermain

Battle other rollers in fast-paced arena combat. In DeathMatch, score the most kills before time runs out. In Elimination, survive longer than every other opponent to claim victory. Keyboard and Mouse Controls Use WASD to move your roller. Use Q to shield. Use E to use. Hold F to use the attractor. Tap Shift to dash. Hold Shift to brake. Use Space to float up using anti-gravity. Look with the mouse. Use Left Click to fire bullets at enemies. Use Right Click to launch missiles.
