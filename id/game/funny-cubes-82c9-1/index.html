Cara Bermain

The goal of the game is to collect as large a cube as possible. Throw a cube on the rest to collect the big ones in the genre of 2048. I.e. if you throw a cube with a face value of 2 on a cube with a face value of 2, then 2 of them will get a cube of 4. I.e. the face value doubles. Nothing happens with cubes with different denominations, so they begin to accumulate and overflow the container, which complicates the game and can lead to a loss. There are also boosters that allow you to sort out a bunch of unassembled cubes, but you need to earn them by trying to collect several cubes at a time.
