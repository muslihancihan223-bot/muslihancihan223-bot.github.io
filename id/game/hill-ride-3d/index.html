Cara Bermain

Drive carefully and keep your balance while climbing hills, landing jumps, and crossing dangerous track sections. W / S — accelerate / brake A / D — tilt forward / backward N — nitro P — pause Use tilt controls to land more smoothly after jumps, avoid flipping over on steep terrain, and stay stable on rough sections. Nitro helps you gain speed quickly, but using it at the right moment is key. The better your control, the farther you can drive and the more coins you can collect.
