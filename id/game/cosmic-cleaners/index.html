Cara Bermain

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, or hold the mouse button and point where to go. Mobile: hold and drag anywhere on the screen. Your tool cleans automatically as you move and the dust goes into your tank. When the tank is full, bring it to a depot to sell it for coins. In the shop, buy wider and stronger tools and vehicles, a bigger tank, faster moon boots, a dust compressor and cleaning robots. Thick dust in distant sectors needs a stronger tool. When you have enough coins, step on a permit pad to open the next sector. Every sector hides a lost item — collect them all in the Lost & Found.
