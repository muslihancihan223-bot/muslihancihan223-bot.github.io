Cara Bermain

Build the biggest car factory! Upgrade every stage of your assembly line, grow your crew and hit each factory's sales goal to unlock the next car. Build all seven, from the Compact to the Hypercar. Workers are your biggest challenge: each one needs a parking space, a quick way through the gates and a break now and then. Desktop Controls: Left click: open any part of the factory and its upgrades. Click an upgrade button: buy one level. Hold it: keep buying. Click and drag: move around the factory. Mouse wheel: zoom in and out. Click spills, stuck parts and dirty solar panels: earn bonus cash. Mobile Controls: Tap: open any part of the factory and its upgrades. Tap an upgrade button: buy one level. Hold it: keep buying. Drag with one finger: move around the factory. Pinch: zoom in and out. Tap spills, stuck parts and dirty solar panels: earn bonus cash.
