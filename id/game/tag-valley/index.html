Cara Bermain

Runners cross the field from one safe line to the other before the timer runs out; whoever the chaser catches joins the chasers. Teammates can revive a caught runner by holding E next to them. Three clean escapes in a row win the match for the runners, catching everyone wins it for the chasers. On PC: WASD - move Space - dash (jump when standing), dive as a chaser LMB - attack (chaser) RMB - look around Q - ability F - item E - interact (shop, crates, revive a teammate) G - emotes, 1-6 - quick emotes On mobile: Joystick - move Swipe - look around Buttons in the bottom right - dash, attack, ability and item
