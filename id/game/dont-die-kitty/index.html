Cara Bermain

Computer WASD / Arrow keys: move J or Left click: attack Space, Shift or Right click: roll (dodge) Q: Big Meow (when charged) 1 / 2: use toys E: use / interact I: gear P: pause M: sound on / off Phone & tablet Left side of the screen: drag to move The cat attacks on its own (the AUTO switch turns it off) Roll button: dodge Meow button: Big Meow when charged Toy buttons: use toys
