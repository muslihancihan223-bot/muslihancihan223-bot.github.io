Main Restaurant Day

Restaurant Day adalah game Makanan, Manajemen, Simulasi gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

MakananManajemenSimulasiMemasak

Cara Bermain

Choose a dish from the menu and check which ingredients are needed. If something is missing, buy it in the shop. Then start cooking and complete mini-games: drag food, slice ingredients, mix, hold the button, tap at the right moment and follow the hints on the screen. The more accurately you complete each action, the better your result. Earn coins and stars, unlock new dishes, upgrade appliances and expand the restaurant menu.
