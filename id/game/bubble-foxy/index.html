Cara Bermain

• Aim by dragging your finger across the screen. • Release to shoot the bubble. • Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them. • Rescue cute animals and clear every level. • Bounce shots off the walls to reach difficult spots. • Create big combos for higher scores. • Complete level objectives to unlock new magical worlds. Plan every shot carefully, use boosters wisely, and become the ultimate Bubble Shooter master!
