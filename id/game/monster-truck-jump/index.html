Cara Bermain

One thumb, one skill: catch the sweeping needle in the green. Tap to launch. The top of the green is a HOLESHOT and lifts your top speed for the whole run. Too early and you bog down. Too late and you spin the wheels. Tap again to change up. Six gears, same green window each time. Over-rev a shift and you lose the speed you just built. Hit the ramp as fast as you can. Hold anywhere in the air to tuck. It pushes you further but drops the nose. Let go in time to level out. Landing nose-first is a wreck. Use nitro whenever you want to force it. Land on your wheels to bank the run. Land on your roof and you still keep every car you flattened.
