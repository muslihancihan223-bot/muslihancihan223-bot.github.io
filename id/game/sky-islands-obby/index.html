Main Sky Islands Obby

Sky Islands Obby adalah game Petualangan, Keterampilan, 3D gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

PetualanganKeterampilan3DPlatformObbyParkour

Cara Bermain

Reach the flag at the end of each stage: it saves your progress as a checkpoint. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to run, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, drag the mouse to look around, mouse wheel to zoom, E to open an egg, Esc for the pause menu. Mobile: drag on the left side to run (push the stick to the edge to sprint), swipe on the right side to look around, the blue button to jump. Collect coins, open eggs at each island's shop and take up to three pets along — each one gives a boost. A short tutorial at the start shows everything.
