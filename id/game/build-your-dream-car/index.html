Cara Bermain

Use the arrow keys and mouse on PC to control your car, shift at the right moment, and win drag races. On mobile, simply hold your finger on the screen and slide left or right to control the car. The controls are very simple and intuitive, so you can focus on building, upgrading, and racing your dream car.
