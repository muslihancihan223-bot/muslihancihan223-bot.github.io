Main Kitten Never Dies

Kitten Never Dies adalah game Puzzle, Petualangan, Anak gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Cara Bermain

Embark on an interdimensional journey to aid the kitten in finding its way back home! Each dimension presents a unique challenge that you must overcome. Think it's going to be a piece of cake? Oh, meow! Let's see if you've got what it takes! Just tap a key, click with the mouse, tap the screen, or use your gamepad — the kitty will know what to do! If you want to play together on the same computer: Player 1 selects a button While Player 2 chooses a different one.
