Main Tile Sort: Color Columns

Tile Sort: Color Columns adalah game Puzzle, Mencocokkan, Logika gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

PuzzleMencocokkanLogikaColor

Cara Bermain

The tile at the bottom of the screen is the one you need to place. Choose a column: the tile is added to the top, and the bottom tile from that column becomes the tile for your next move. Move tiles between columns until each column contains tiles of just one color. Plan ahead: the tile you take from the bottom determines your next move. Some levels introduce hidden tiles and other mechanics. If you don't see a solution right away, try a different sequence of moves. There is no timer, and you can't lose.
