Cara Bermain

Touch: drag on the left side to walk. PUNCH and SMASH attack. JUMP and GRAB work as before, and tapping GRAB again throws. The orange button fires the special, and the round arrows turn the camera. Combos: PUNCH PUNCH PUNCH (One-Two Hook), PUNCH PUNCH SMASH (Uppercut), SMASH SMASH (Hammer Fist). In the air, SMASH gives a Ground Pound and PUNCH a Flying Punch. Holding something, SMASH gives an Overhead Slam. Keyboard and mouse: WASD or the arrow keys walk. J or left-click punches, K or right-click smashes, Space jumps, L or F grabs, I fires the special, and Q and E turn the camera. The combos work the same way. Goal: level the city, finish the three goals for a bonus, and earn up to five stars. HOW TO PLAY on the title screen replays the tutorial.
