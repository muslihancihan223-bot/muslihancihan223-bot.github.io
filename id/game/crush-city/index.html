Cara Bermain

Drag to rotate the city, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a building to fire the selected weapon. Weapons have limited shots per city. Destroy 50/70/90% for 1-3 stars. Coins unlock new weapons and upgrade their power and shots. Keyboard: arrows/WASD rotate, Space fires, 1-9 pick a weapon.
