Main Squish Jar

Squish Jar adalah game Puzzle, Arcade, Gabung gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

PuzzleArcadeGabungSantaiFisika

Cara Bermain

Move the mouse (or drag your finger) to aim, then click (or release) to drop a squish into the jar. When two identical squishes touch, they merge into a bigger one and give you points. Chain merges quickly for combos. Don't let the squishes stack above the dashed line at the top of the jar, or the game is over. Stuck? Use the Shake, Pop and Upgrade boosts.
