Cara Bermain

Drop & Tumble: Release marbles from the top trays and let real physics take over! Roll With It: Marbles fall onto the endlessly spinning conveyor belt. Time your moves wisely! Sort & Fill: Steer marbles into bottom boxes, 3 slots per matching color. Space Is Limited: Let the belt overflow with no matching box in sight, and it's game over! Stay sharp and drop smart.
