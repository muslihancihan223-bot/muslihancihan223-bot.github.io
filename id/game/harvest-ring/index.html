Main Harvest Ring

Harvest Ring adalah game Arcade, Clicker, Manajemen gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Cara Bermain

Use the mouse on desktop or touch controls on mobile. Select a glowing green sector to open a plot. Tap or click a thirsty plot to water it, then wait for the vegetables to ripen. Drag the donut saw from its dock across ripe vegetables to harvest them and build combos. Spend money to buy plots and upgrade crops. Use tickets to unlock the auto-saw, automatic watering and other improvements. Complete quests for rewards, and use gems to buy skins and themes. When a new season becomes available in the Prestige tab, start it to gain prestige and permanent bonuses.
