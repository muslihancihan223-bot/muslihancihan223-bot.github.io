Main Pet Run Bridge Of Heart

Pet Run Bridge Of Heart adalah game Arcade, 3D, Hewan gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Cara Bermain

- Move: Swipe left/right to steer your pet. - Collect & Match: Gather bricks that match your current color. Watch out for color-changing zones on the track! - Boost & Dodge: Pick up Magnets to attract bricks, hit green plus gates (+), and avoid red obstacles or minus gates (-). - Build: Automatically lay your stacked bricks to bridge gaps and reach your partner at the finish line.
