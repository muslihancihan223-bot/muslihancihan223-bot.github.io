Cara Bermain

Overcome obstacles and reach the finish line as fast as possible. Level 1 features a tutorial, and you will discover some gameplay mechanics as you progress.  Maximum speed is achieved when moving straight forward.  🖥️ PC Controls: • A / 🡸 — Move left / Wall-run (hold against the wall) • D / 🡺 — Move right / Wall-run (hold against the wall) • Space — Jump / Push off the wall • Shift — Slide (you can still steer left/right while sliding) • R — Restart  📱 Mobile Controls: Tap anywhere on the screen to bring up the virtual joystick. Move and hold your finger within the joystick area to perform actions: • Center — Idle (no action) • Top area — Jump • Left area — Move left / Wall-run (hold against the wall) • Right area — Move right / Wall-run (hold against the wall) • Bottom center — Slide • Bottom left — Slide + Move left • Bottom right — Slide + Move right  You can adjust the joystick size in the Options menu.
