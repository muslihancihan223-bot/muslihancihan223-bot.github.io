Cara Bermain

Objective: Hit the falling notes exactly as they align with the piano keys at the bottom of the screen to score points and build your combo. Controls: • Mobile/Tablet: Tap the virtual piano keys on your screen. • Desktop: Press the corresponding letter keys on your computer keyboard. • Pro Mode: Connect a real MIDI keyboard via USB to play with actual piano keys!
