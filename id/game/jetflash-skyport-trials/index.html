Main JETFLASH: Skyport Trials

JETFLASH: Skyport Trials adalah game Balap, Keterampilan, 3D gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Cara Bermain

Reach the finish gate while staying on the course. On desktop use W/S to accelerate or pitch in flight, A/D to steer or bank, Space for jet thrust and Shift to brake or reverse. On phones use the left joystick, JET and the separate BRAKE button. Use Pause to retry from a checkpoint.
