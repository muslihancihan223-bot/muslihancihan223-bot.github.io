Cara Bermain

1. Choose your starting hero, then click or tap around them to earn aura. 2. Spend aura on Click Power, Auto Click, Aura Multiplier, and Crit Chance. Hold a purchase button to buy several levels. Auto Click generates aura while the game is open. 3. Keep earning to advance through Noob, Chad, Sigma, Aura Lord, Legend, and GOAT. Rank progress uses the aura earned during your current run, so buying upgrades doesn't lower your rank. 4. Open the shop tab to summon pets with aura. Each summon gives you a pet you don't already own in your current region. All owned pets provide permanent bonuses; choose one in your collection to accompany your hero. 5. Tap treasure chests when they appear, claim daily rewards, and complete achievements for extra rewards. 6. Your first rebirth unlocks at 300 million aura earned in a run. Rebirth gives prestige and resets your aura, rank, and upgrades, while keeping your pets, unlocked heroes, and prestige. Later rebirths need more aura; check the requirement on the rebirth button. Prestige gives permanent bonuses and unlocks new regions on the map. Travel there to collect more pets and keep growing your aura.
