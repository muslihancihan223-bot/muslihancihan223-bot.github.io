Cara Bermain

Get behind the wheel of the powerful military Ural in this exciting simulation game! The driver of the military Ural challenges you not just to drive, but to deliver valuable cargo skillfully. Your task is to drive through complex tracks and bring to the finish line as many boxes as possible, using your agility to maneuver and wit to find the best solutions in difficult situations off-road. The controls are intuitive, whether you're playing on your phone or computer. On mobile devices you control the movement with touch pedals: right - forward, left - backward. On the computer you use the keys: D for forward and A for backward. Earn money. Invest it in improvements for your machines. Or save up to buy new cars - each with its own unique capabilities, ready for the most extreme tests. Expand your fleet, improve your skills and become the best off-road driver!
