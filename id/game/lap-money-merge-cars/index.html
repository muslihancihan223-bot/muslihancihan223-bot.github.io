Main Lap Money: Merge Cars

Lap Money: Merge Cars adalah game Arcade, 3D, Mobil gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Arcade3DMobilTycoonGabungIdle

Cara Bermain

1. Tap the car at the bottom of the screen to buy it with coins. 2. Drag a car onto a matching one to merge the pair into the next model. 3. Drag a car onto the track if you have an available pilot: it drives itself and earns coins with every lap. 4. Tap boxes on the parking lot to get cars. 5. Drag an unwanted car onto the bin to sell it. The magnet button is Mega merge: it merges every pair at once when you have at least three. You get three charges, plus one every 5 minutes. The Turbo button speeds up your cars for watching an ad. The wheel of fortune unlocks at player level 3. The “Next car” plate shows which car you unlock next. The clipboard button opens your daily tasks. From car 16 you can move to a new garage: your cars and coins reset, your income doubles forever, and free cars and wheel charges come faster. Each next move opens six cars further on. Claim a daily reward every day: miss a day and the streak starts over.
