Main Tavern Cardsharp

Tavern Cardsharp adalah game Arcade, Cards, Strategi gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

ArcadeCardsStrategiRetroRpg

Cara Bermain

Drag a creature from your hand to an empty slot on your side of the table. Tap your creature, then tap a target to attack: an enemy creature or the rival's portrait when the way is clear. Tap the seal to end your turn. Your bet is your life: every hit on a portrait takes coins from that pile. Empty the rival's pile to win the duel and his deck. Neighbours matter: some cards grow stronger in formation, others fight best alone. Buy packs from the innkeeper, pick a table that matches your deck and climb the ladder to the champions upstairs.
