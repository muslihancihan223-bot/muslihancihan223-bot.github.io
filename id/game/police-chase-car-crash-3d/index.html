Cara Bermain

Escape as far as you can: the run ends if your car is wrecked or if you stay stopped next to a police car until you are busted. Pass cars closely to fill nitro, ram police cars to take them down, use ramps for air time, avoid roadblocks, spike strips and road works. Coins buy new cars and upgrades in the garage. PC: W or Up to accelerate, S or Down to brake (hold it when stopped to reverse), A/D or Left/Right to steer, Space for a handbrake drift, Shift or N for nitro, Esc or P to pause. Mobile: left and right buttons to steer, GAS pedal and BRAKE on the right, nitro button above the gas pedal.
