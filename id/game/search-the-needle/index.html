Main Search The Needle

Search The Needle adalah game Simulasi, 3D, Lucu gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Simulasi3DLucuFarm

Cara Bermain

Collect straw until your bag is full, then deliver it to Clara for cash. Upgrade your tools and bag to search faster. On desktop, use WASD to move, the mouse to look, and hold the left mouse button to collect. On mobile, use the floating joystick, drag to look around, and aim at straw to collect automatically.
