Main World's Deepest Hole

World's Deepest Hole adalah game Arcade, 3D, Manajemen gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Arcade3DManajemen

Cara Bermain

Goal: dig as deep as you can and turn your hole into the biggest roadside attraction on Route 66. Hold the left mouse button to dig (DIG on mobile). When your bucket is full press E (WINCH UP) to ride up and sell. Spend cash in the shop (B) on tools and upgrades; harder rock needs stronger tools. Move with WASD, look with the mouse (click the game to grab the mouse; if your browser doesn't allow that, drag to look and use the arrow keys to turn), jump with Space. Q or right click throws dynamite, M opens the museum, J the daily orders. Buttons marked AD play an optional video for a reward. Watch out for red TNT in the rock.
