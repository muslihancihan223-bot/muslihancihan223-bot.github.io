Main Wash Master

Wash Master adalah game Puzzle, Arcade, Simulasi gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

PuzzleArcadeSimulasiKeterampilan3DPopularSantai

Cara Bermain

Clean every dirty surface to complete each level. Desktop: move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, hold left click to spray. Press V to get a bonus tool (rewarded video). Press B to open the shop. Mobile: use the left side of the screen to move, drag to aim, and hold the spray button to clean. Earn coins, buy upgrades and unlock new tools to clean faster!
