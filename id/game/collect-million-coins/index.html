Main Collect Million Coins

Collect Million Coins adalah game Simulasi, 3D, Clicker gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

Simulasi3DClickerFisikaIdle

Cara Bermain

Move with WASD. Look around with the mouse, or hold the right mouse button if pointer lock is unavailable. Hold E or the left mouse button to collect coins. Press E at the receiver, upgrade table or minecart to interact. Select tools with 1, 2 and 3. Press P for the Prime collection and Esc or Tab to pause. Empty each vault and deposit every coin to enter the next.
