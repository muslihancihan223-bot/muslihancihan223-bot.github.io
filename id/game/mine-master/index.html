Main Mine Master

Mine Master adalah game Puzzle, Gabung gratis. Tanpa unduh dan tanpa daftar — langsung main di browser HP, tablet, atau komputer.

PuzzleGabung

Cara Bermain

Tap two matching shovels to merge them into a higher-level shovel. Select a shovel and tap “Down” to dig through the blocks below. Collect coins, complete the required objectives, and keep upgrading your tools to advance through each level.
