Cara Bermain

Sink Enemy Pirate Ships! Command the Pirate Cannon A Mega Battle, the thrilling bubble shooter puzzle where you launch numbered cannonballs from your mega cannon at the bottom to match and burst clusters of hex-shaped enemy pirate boats with identical numbers floating above the water. Clear all ships before your balls run out to claim victory and dominate epic sea levels! Desktop Controls: Mouse: Drag to aim the cannon, release to fire the numbered cannonball. Mobile Controls: Touch: Drag to aim the cannon, release to fire the numbered cannonball.
