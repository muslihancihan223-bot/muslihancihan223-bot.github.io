Cara Bermain

Press Roll to receive a random aura and coins. Open Auras, choose My auras and equip a find. Spend coins on luck and roll-speed upgrades. Gather materials in the Wilds and use Crafting to make items. Auto-roll unlocks after 25 rolls. Desktop: R or Enter to roll; WASD or arrow keys to move; Space to jump; F or a short click on the world to attack the nearest enemy in range. Drag the mouse or use Q/E to turn the camera. Scroll to zoom. Escape closes a panel. Roll shortcuts work outside panels and text fields. Phone: use the left joystick to move, swipe on the right to turn the camera and pinch outside the joystick area to zoom. Use the on-screen roll, jump and attack buttons.
