Cara Bermain

How to Play Click to Launch: Manually trigger production cycles to generate your initial capital and kickstart the economy. Invest & Upgrade: Spend earnings on Business Upgrades and Skill Improvements to increase the efficiency of every action. Expand Ventures: Purchase and unlock new Business Branches to diversify your portfolio and raise your profit ceiling. Automate Growth: Hire Managers to automate cycles, transforming manual clicks into a high-speed passive income stream. Prestige Reset: Perform a Reset to sacrifice current progress in exchange for permanent Prestige Multipliers, allowing you to reach significantly higher milestones in the next run.
