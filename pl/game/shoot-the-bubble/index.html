Jak Grać

The goal is simple: pop the bubbles by matching three or more bubbles of the same color. Aim carefully, use bank shots off the side walls, and clear the board. On Desktop * Aim: move the mouse to adjust the aiming line, which starts from the launcher at the bottom and shows you the bubble’s trajectory. * Shoot: left-click to launch the bubble in the indicated direction. * Bank shots: the bubble bounces off the side walls. Use the aiming line to hit groups of bubbles that are difficult to reach with a direct shot. On Mobile * Aim: place your finger on the screen and drag it to adjust the direction. The aiming line is always visible and helps you aim precisely. * Shoot: lift your finger (or tap the screen) to launch the bubble toward the target. * Bank shots: just like on desktop, use the side walls to angle your shots. In Challenge Mode: the wall of bubbles continuously moves downward. Create combos to push it back, and don’t let the bubbles cross the danger line.
