Jak Grać

Your goal is simple: Jump high, grab coins, and soar to new stages. Miss a grab, and it's Game Over—stay sharp and quickly make your next move. Keep aiming for the highest score. Use the arrow keys to move left or right to jump on the platforms. Mega Jump Game Tips: Gold Coins: Snag 100 for a boost. x2 & x5 Coins: Blue and red-centered coins are worth double and quintuple—cha-ching. Trickster Coins: Watch out; these sneaky coins run away when you get close. Moving Coins: Slide side-to-side to catch these shifty treasures. Boost Stars Shiny stars launch you higher. Yellow, green, blue, and red—each more potent than the last. Collect as many as possible to gain massive boosts and fly to new heights. Save Stars Falling? Tap your Save Star to rocket back up. You start with 5, so use them wisely and don't waste them. Power-ups to the Rescue Umbrella: Float down gently and magnetize coins. Jump Boots: Bigger, faster, higher jumps. Magnet: Pull in coins like a boss. Lucky Blast: Transform everything into coins and stars. Avoid These Dangers Anvil: Stops you cold. Dodge it. Ball & Chain: Slows you down—ugh. Shrinkify Potion: Tiny and tricky. Bounce on blocks, outsmart enemies, and blast past obstacles. Ready to jump into the fun? Let's go.
