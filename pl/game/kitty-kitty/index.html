Jak Grać

Become a Kitty Guardian! Step into the shoes of a loving cat caretaker and nurture adorable kawaii cats in a festive snowy winter wonderland. In this charming casual idle simulation game, you’ll collect cat food as the core idle resource (with passive production like +50/s) to level up your felines, raise their Sweetness stat, and unlock new features via daily check-ins and challenges. Manage cozy habitats, complete tasks, and show them who’s the best cat parent by building the ultimate heartwarming feline paradise! Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button: Collect cat food and interact with cats or resource icons. Click on CheckIn icon: Claim daily rewards. Click on Challenges icon: Complete tasks and unlock features. Click on bottom icons: Access Toy Room, food bowl, Kitty Wiki, or Recycle. Mobile Controls: On-Screen Tap: Collect cat food and interact with all elements by tapping the screen. Tap Button: Tap CheckIn or Challenges for daily rewards and tasks. Tap Bottom Icons: Tap to open Toy Room, feed cats, view Kitty Wiki, or use Recycle.
