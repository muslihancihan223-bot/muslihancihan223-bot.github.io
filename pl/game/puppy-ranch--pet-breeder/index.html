Graj w Puppy Ranch: Pet Breeder

Puppy Ranch: Pet Breeder to darmowa gra z kategorii Symulacje, 3D, Zwierzęta. Bez pobierania i rejestracji — graj od razu w przeglądarce na telefonie, tablecie lub komputerze.

Jak Grać

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag on the screen on mobile. Walk into stray dogs to catch them and bring them to your pet house to breed puppies. Take puppies to the sales counter to earn cash. Stand on price signs to buy new pet houses and upgrades. Open the puppy book with the book button (or C) to see your collection. Win dog races by tapping TAP, and train a champion for the puppy show (tap the trick buttons or keys 1-3).
