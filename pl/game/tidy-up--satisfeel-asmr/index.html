Jak Grać

Bring Order to the Chaos! Step into a calming world of satisfying puzzles where your goal is to sort, organize, and place every item in its proper spot. In Satisgame, each level gives you a messy space filled with scattered objects, and it’s your job to turn the clutter into perfect order. Arrange items by color, size, type, or category, and enjoy the relaxing feeling of making everything neat and tidy. As you progress, the puzzles become more creative and challenging, rewarding careful thinking and attention to detail. Desktop Controls: Mouse: Click and drag items. Left Click: Pick up, move, and place objects. Scroll Wheel: Zoom or navigate if supported. Mobile Controls: Tap and Drag: Move items into the correct position. Tap: Select or place objects. Swipe: Navigate the scene if supported. Sort everything correctly to complete the level and unlock new organizing challenges.
