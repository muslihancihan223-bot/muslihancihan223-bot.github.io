Jak Grać

Drive each route from start to finish before the creature catches you. Keep your speed up, stay on the road and steer around abandoned cars, barriers, crates and puddles. Checkpoints save your place: if you are caught, a revive puts you back at the last one, and you have three revives per run. Beat the target times for bronze, silver and gold medals. Every finish pays cash for upgrades and new cars. Controls W / Up Arrow: Accelerate S / Down Arrow: Brake A / D or Left / Right Arrow: Steer Space: Handbrake Q / E: Look left and right C: Look back P: Pause M: Sound on and off Touch: Drag on the left half of the screen to steer, hold GAS and BRAKE on the right, HAND is the handbrake
