Jak Grać

Grow a small clothes shop into a giant mall! - Walk with the joystick (drag anywhere on the screen) or with WASD / arrow keys. - Take boxes from the stock room and put them on the shelves. - Customers buy and pay at the checkout. Walk over the cash to collect it. - Stand on a buy tile to buy it: cashiers, shelves, stockers, a manager and more. - Walk into the manager's office to upgrade your staff. - Open new stores, a parking lot, a bus stop and side entrances to bring more customers. - Every 2 minutes it's Rush Hour: keep the shelves full so nobody leaves without buying! There is no end: build all 8 stores and the biggest mall you can.
