Jak Grać

🎯 Clear the island Destroy every block to complete a location and move on. Your ammo is limited, so make each shot count! 🛒 Upgrade and try again Out of ammo? Destroyed blocks stay gone. Visit the shop, upgrade your weapons, and finish the job. 🖱️ Mouse & keyboard - Hold left mouse button: shoot. - A / D: rotate the camera. - W / S: tilt the camera. - + / −: zoom. - F: reset the camera. - 1–9, Q / E, or mouse wheel: switch weapons. - Esc: pause. 📱 Touch controls Hold a finger on blocks to shoot. Use two fingers to rotate and zoom, or use the camera buttons at the bottom right.
