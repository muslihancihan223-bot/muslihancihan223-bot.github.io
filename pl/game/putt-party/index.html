Jak Grać

Drag back from the ball and release to putt — the farther you pull, the harder the hit. Reach the hole in as few strokes as you can to earn up to three stars, and mind the walls, water and sand along the way. Two tools help when you're stuck: Ball Back rewinds your last shot, and Aim Line shows a longer trajectory preview. Controls: drag & release (mouse or touch).
