Jak Grać

Take Every Cat Home! A whole shelter of kittens is waiting to be picked, and you're the one they're waiting for. Match three of a kind and they're yours to keep — clear every goal before your moves run out, breaking through crates, ice and stone to reach the ones hiding behind them. Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button (click): Select a cat, then click the one next to it to swap them. Left Mouse Button (drag): Or drag a cat onto its neighbour to swap in one motion. Click a special cat: Set off a rocket, bomb or Paw where it stands. Boosters (before the level): Click a booster on the briefing screen to take it into the board with you. Pause button: Open the menu, restart the level, or turn the music down. Mobile Controls: Tap: Tap a cat, then tap its neighbour to swap them. Swipe: Or swipe a cat towards the neighbour you want it to trade places with. Tap a special cat: Fire it off on the spot. Boosters: Tap them on the briefing screen before you start. Match 4 for a rocket, a bent 5 for a bomb, 5 in a row for a Paw — and 6 in a row for a glowing Paw that clears more and scores double.
