Jak Grać

Race to the finish in this frantic multiplayer platformer! Navigate through obstacle-filled stages alongside up to 9 other players in real time. Dodge hazards, ride moving platforms, and unleash your dash to surge ahead of the pack! Desktop Controls: ← / → (Arrow Keys or A/D): Move left and right. ↑ (Up Arrow) / W / Space: Jump — double jump supported! Shift: Dash — burst forward for a speed boost. Mobile Controls: On-Screen Joystick: Drag to move left and right. J Button: Tap to jump. D Button: Tap to dash forward.
