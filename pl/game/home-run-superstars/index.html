Jak Grać

Tap READY to call the pitch, then tap SWING as the ball reaches the plate. Better timing means a longer hit. Three strikes is an out, and three outs end the run. Each level-up lets you pick one of three powers. FOCUS slows the pitch and FLOW supercharges your next swing. Open MENU / UPGRADES to buy training and bats with the coins and lumber you earn. Keyboard: Space to swing, Q for Focus, E for Flow, 1–3 to pick a power, P for the menu.
