Jak Grać

Take control of your chef and prepare customer orders. Serve dishes quickly to maintain satisfaction levels. Complete level goals to unlock new recipes and improvements. Earn coins to upgrade your kitchen and expand operations. Each stage increases the pace, requiring sharper time management. Desktop Controls: ↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward. ↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward. ← (Left Arrow): Move left. → (Right Arrow): Move right. On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
