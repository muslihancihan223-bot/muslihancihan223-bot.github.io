Jak Grać

Knock every rival off the island while keeping your own vehicle on the snow. Win the round to advance to the next arena. Desktop: Hold the left mouse button and drag to steer. Keep moving to grow your snowball. Release the button to launch it in the direction your vehicle faces. Mobile: Touch and drag to steer and build your snowball. Lift your finger to fire. Land larger snowball hits to push opponents farther. Collect power-ups for a temporary advantage, avoid icy traps and open water, and watch the island edges. Use the pause and audio controls whenever needed. The opponents are computer-controlled; no external account is required.
