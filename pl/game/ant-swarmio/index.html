Jak Grać

Goal: Grow the biggest ant colony on the map. Classic: 2 minutes. The biggest colony when time runs out wins. If your swarm gets eaten, you respawn. Battle: No respawns. Eat the other colonies and be the last one standing. Solo: No rivals. Eat as much of the map as you can before time runs out. Clear 40%, 65% or 85% to earn 1, 2 or 3 stars. Rules: You can eat anything smaller than your swarm. If an item shows a lock with a number, that’s how many ants you need to eat it. Big items take a moment to eat: your ants surround them and carry them away. Touch a smaller colony to steal its ants. Run away from bigger ones! Controls: Mobile: touch and drag anywhere on the screen to move your swarm. PC: move the mouse to steer, or use WASD / arrow keys. Esc or P: pause.
