Jak Grać

Watch the ring. Colored bubbles sit in a circle and slowly spin around you. Check your current ball. The ball in the center is the color you will shoot next. Aim + tap at the right time. When the arrow lines up with a group of the same color, tap to shoot Clear the circle. Keep matching and popping until no colored bubbles are left to finish the level. Use power-ups wisely. The template includes power-ups like Bomb and Colorful Ball (and some builds mention Rocket or Freeze). Use them to clear tough clusters.
