Jak Grać

Sort every parcel into the bin that matches its sticker colour before it rolls off the end of the belt. Get through the shift to pass it; high accuracy and the target score earn up to three stars. After the scanner, a tag above each parcel shows what is inside. A red tag with "!" means contraband (weapons and explosives, later batteries and toxic or radioactive goods) — send it to bin 0. Fix parcels on the way: tape torn boxes, flip the ones with arrows pointing down, cut bundles apart. Golden express parcels score ×3 if you sort them before their timer ends; blue cryo cases must be sorted before they thaw. "?" check: the belt stops — sweep the lens over the box and tap the forbidden item, or press "All clear". Weight check: boxes over 10 kg need the yellow sticker. Computer: 1–4 — bins, 0 — contraband bin (for the front parcel in the frame) Mouse — drag any parcel to a bin, or click a bin Space or click a parcel — tape, flip or cut it B — emergency brake (after buying the upgrade) P — pause "?" check: arrows pick an item, Enter seizes it, C — all clear Weight check: 1 — keep the sticker, 2 — relabel Phone or tablet: tap the bins and drag parcels with your finger. Play in landscape. A dropped parcel, missed contraband or thawed cryo cargo costs a heart; lose them all and the shift is over. When the timer runs out, clear the parcels left on the belt.
