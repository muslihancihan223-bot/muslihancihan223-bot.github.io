Jak Grać

Win a card duel! Turn a standard deck into a weapon: every card you play strikes, heals or burns your rival's mana. Reduce your opponent's life to zero to win. Desktop Controls: Left Click on a card: Select it for your combo — a single card, a pair, a flush or a run. Bigger combos hit harder. Play button: Commit the combo and end your turn. The preview shows damage, heal and mana first. Blade / Shield / Swap: Spend mana to sharpen a strike, soak one, or trade up to three cards. ULT button: Fire your hero's ultimate once its gauge fills. Adventure map: Click the die, then a highlighted cell to move. Mobile Controls: Tap a card: Select it for your combo. Play button: Tap to commit and end your turn. Blade / Shield / Swap: Tap to spend mana. ULT button: Tap once the gauge fills. Adventure map: Tap the die, then a highlighted cell. No keyboard needed.
