Jak Grać

1. Goal: reach 400 match points before your opponents. 2. Place a tile so equal numbers meet on shared edges. 3. No move: draw from the boneyard (−5) or pass (−10). 4. Score the sum of the tile’s numbers plus bonuses for opening, bridges, hexes, and emptying your hand. 5. A round ends when someone plays their last tile or the game is blocked. 6. Use the menu for the tutorial, skin shop, quests, and the coin wallet.
