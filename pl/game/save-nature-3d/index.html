Jak Grać

Heal every dry patch of the level to win. Spray water on the ground to grow grass, put out fires and push back the robots; refill your tank at the water machine. Rescue animals and collect coins to upgrade your tools and hire helpers. PC: move with WASD or the arrow keys, spray / attack with Space or by holding the left mouse button, drag the globe to spin it and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Mobile: drag on the left side of the screen to move, hold the SPRAY button to spray, drag and pinch the globe. The first level has a tutorial that shows everything.
