Jak Grać

Fly to an island, approach an egg and hold the interaction button to pick it up. Escape the guardian and carry the egg back to your farm. Place it in a nest, wait for it to hatch, then interact with the ready egg to reveal your pet. Use pet income to buy better wings, upgrade speed and stamina, and expand your farm. Land on safe platforms to restore stamina. Desktop: - WASD or arrow keys: move. - Hold Space: fly upward. Release it to stop climbing and save stamina. - Hold E near an egg: pick it up or open it when ready. - Press E near your farm: place the carried egg. Use E near shops to open them. - Drag the mouse to rotate the camera; use the wheel to zoom. - R: return to the hub. Warning: you lose any egg you are carrying. Mobile: - Use the left joystick to move. - Hold the wings button to fly. - Tap or hold the interaction prompt beside an egg, nest or shop. - Drag the screen to rotate the camera.
