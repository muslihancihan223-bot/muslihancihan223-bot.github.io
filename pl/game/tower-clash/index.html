Jak Grać

Capture every building on the battlefield to win, including neutral towers. Keep at least one blue building under your control to stay in the battle. On desktop, hold the left mouse button and drag from a blue tower or factory to another building to send units. On mobile, touch and drag with one finger. Swipe across one of your own routes to remove it. Drag an artillery targeting ring to aim, and use an airstrike when available. Pause with the on-screen pause button or Escape; adjust music and sound effects in the pause menu. Use the speaker button to mute or enable sound.
