Graj w Obby Way

Obby Way to darmowa gra z kategorii Przygodowe, 3D, Śmieszne. Bez pobierania i rejestracji — graj od razu w przeglądarce na telefonie, tablecie lub komputerze.

Jak Grać

Your goal is to survive each evil obby level and reach the real finish door. Watch the environment carefully, time your jumps, and remember where the hidden traps activate. Collect coins to unlock new levels and hint eggs that warn you about traps. If a trap catches you, try again, learn its trick, and find the safe route! Desktop Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys: Move. Hold Right-Click Mouse: Rotate the camera. Spacebar: Jump. E: Interact. Left Mouse Button: Use the selected item. Q: Drop the selected item. 1–0: Select an inventory item. Esc: Pause the game. Mobile Controls: Left Joystick: Move. Right Joystick: Rotate the camera. Jump Button: Jump. Interact Button: Interact with objects. Use Button: Use the selected item. Drop Button: Drop the selected item. Inventory Button: Open the inventory. Pause Button: Pause the game.
