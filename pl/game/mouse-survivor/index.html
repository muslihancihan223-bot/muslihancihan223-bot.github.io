How to play Mouse Survivor? Keyboard Controls W / Arrow Up / Spacebar: Jump A / Arrow Left: Left D / Arrow Right: Right For Mobile:Scroll 🐭 Run, Jump, and Explore Infinitely Divided Worlds with the Brave Mouse!🧀 Embark on an epic journey with our fearless mouse hero in this exciting 2D endless running game! 🏃 Endless Running Adventure: Guide your mouse through a dynamic world full of ever-changing tiles. Watch out for those with lower alpha; One touch can be dangerous! Dodge, jump and slide your way through endless challenges. 🐍 Encounter Sneaky Snakes: Watch out for crawling snakes! Snakes will try to hinder your progress. Swipe right, left or up to perform daring moves and escape their cunning clutches.
