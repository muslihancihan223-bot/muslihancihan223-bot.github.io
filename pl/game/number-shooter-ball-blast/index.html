Graj w Number Shooter: Ball Blast

Number Shooter: Ball Blast to darmowa gra z kategorii Akcji, Zręcznościowe, Bubble Shooter. Bez pobierania i rejestracji — graj od razu w przeglądarce na telefonie, tablecie lub komputerze.

Jak Grać

Goal: survive as long as you can and score as high as you can. There is no finish line — waves keep coming and the numbers keep growing. Your run ends the moment a ball touches your cannon. Controls - Hold the mouse button (or keep your finger on the screen) to play. Release to pause. - Move the cannon: drag left and right, or use the Arrow keys (← →) or A / D. - The cannon fires by itself, so aiming is all about where you stand. Tips - Score = the damage you deal. Chipping a big number down earns you every point of it, so you do not have to land the final hit to be rewarded. - Catch the drops that fall from broken balls: ⚡ raises fire rate, 🔥 raises damage, and $ is coins. - Special items come from the first ball of a wave — multi shot, shield, slow and magnet. - Every wave showers bonus coins, and every 5th wave pays out a lot more. - A shield blocks one hit. Without it, one touch ends the run. - Spend coins in the Shop on permanent upgrades (fire rate, attack, item duration) — those stay forever.
