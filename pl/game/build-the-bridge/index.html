Jak Grać

Objective: Guide the explorer safely across each gap by adjusting both sides of the bridge. Build a stable path and reach the finish flag to complete the level. Controls (Keyboard): A – Extend the left side of the bridge. S – Retract the left side of the bridge. K – Retract the right side of the bridge. L – Extend the right side of the bridge. M - Mute, unmute Enter – Start the game / Continue. Space – Continue after completing a level. Goal: Create the perfect bridge, avoid mistakes, and complete every level with precise timing and careful planning.
