Graj w Pace Note Rally

Pace Note Rally to darmowa gra z kategorii Wyścigi, 3D, Samochody. Bez pobierania i rejestracji — graj od razu w przeglądarce na telefonie, tablecie lub komputerze.

Jak Grać

Reach the finish as fast as you can and win medals. Watch the pace note cards: the number shows how sharp the corner is (6 nearly flat, 1 very tight), the colour shows the danger. Keyboard: W/S or arrows to accelerate and brake, A/D or arrows to steer, Space handbrake, E wipers, C camera, R reset to the road, Esc pause. Gamepad supported. On phones use the on-screen buttons.
