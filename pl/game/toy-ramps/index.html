Jak Grać

Desktop: - Steer: A / D or the left / right arrow keys, or hold the left mouse button and drag left / right. The car drives forward by itself. - In the air: W / S or the up / down arrow keys flip the car (or drag up / down), A / D or left / right roll it; let go and the car levels out for the landing. - Nitro: Space, or click the NITRO button. - Pause: P, or the pause button in the corner. - Menus and buttons: left mouse button. Track Builder: drag to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom. Mobile: - Drag left and right to steer; in the air drag up / down to flip and sideways to roll; tap NITRO for a boost; tap the buttons for the menus. Track Builder: drag to turn, pinch to zoom.
