Jak Grać

1. Click "Create Game" and choose a genre 2. Customize the settings: speed, gravity, number of enemies, and more 3. Go to Drawing — create your own unique sprites or pick from ready-made ones 4. Choose a music track in the Sounds tab 5. Click Test — play your game! 6. When everything is ready — click Publish and earn coins and XP 7. Share your games with friends using a code — they'll be able to play your creation!
