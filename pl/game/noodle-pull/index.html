Jak Grać

Serve each customer enough noodles to earn stars. Collect at least 10 stars during a level to unlock the next one. Hold the left mouse button anywhere in the play area, or hold Space, to pull the dough. Release the mouse button or Space when the tension marker reaches the green zone. A successful pull doubles your strands. Choose Pull Again to risk your current strands and pursue a larger reward. Choose Serve once you have enough strands to complete the customer’s order. Use the menu buttons to select levels, buy kitchen upgrades, improve recipes, change your chef style, and adjust audio. A mistimed release can snap the dough and fail the order, so balance precision against risk.
