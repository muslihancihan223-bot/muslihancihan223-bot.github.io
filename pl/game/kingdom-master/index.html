Jak Grać

Goal: keep your castle standing for as many nights as you can. Day — build up. Ride to a sawmill, quarry or mine and stand beside it to collect resources; sell them at the Market for gold. The timer at the top shows how long until the night attack. Spend gold. Stand on a glowing hex to unlock it, or walk up to the castle and towers and tap Upgrade. Choose your towers. Opening a tower hex lets you pick Ballista (fast), Cannon (heavy) or Frost (slows enemies). Night — defend. Enemies march from the portals to your castle. Towers fire automatically — tap RELOAD (or press Space) when they run dry, and ride in with your sword. If the walls fall, the run ends. Grow between runs. Nights survived earn Crowns — buy permanent upgrades in the crown menu, finish daily quests, open chests and claim your daily gift. Controls — Mobile: drag anywhere to move, tap to interact. Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, mouse to tap, Space to reload.
