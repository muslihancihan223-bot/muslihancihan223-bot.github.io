Welcome to the chaotic world of slaps 🖐️ — a fast-paced multiplayer slap battle inspired by Slap Battles! Your main goal is to collect as many slaps as possible. Slaps are the main currency in the game — the more players you hit, the more slaps you earn. Use your slaps to unlock stronger gloves and become more powerful in battle. Game modes: ⚔️ Arena — free-for-all chaos 🥊 Ring — stay on the platform and don’t fall 🏃 67 — reach the end while avoiding dangerous heads Every match is full of action, chaos, and competition 😂 Can you become the player with the most slaps?
