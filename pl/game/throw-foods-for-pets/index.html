Jak Grać

Objective: Throw food to attract pets, place them on your plot to earn money, and use that money to throw farther and find rarer pets. There’s no single ending: aim to reach the final biome, The Last Garden, and complete the Pet Index. How to play: Click THROW, then click when the marker reaches the glowing end of the power bar. Better timing means a longer throw. Wait to see which pet comes to the food, then press COLLECT. Pets found farther away earn more. Pick a pet from the hotbar, then click your plot to place it. Placed pets earn money every second, even while you’re away. Walk into a glowing circle to use a station: Train builds Throw Power, the Food Shop sells food that attracts rarer pets, and Upgrades sells permanent boosts and plot space. When you’ve earned enough, Rebirth to restart your run with permanent bonuses. Controls: Mouse or touch: tap or click buttons, the ground to walk and stations to use them; drag to rotate the view Mouse wheel: zoom W, A, S, D or arrow keys: walk Space: start a throw, stop the power bar, or skip the pet reveal 1–9: pick a pet from the hotbar Enter: collect a pet
