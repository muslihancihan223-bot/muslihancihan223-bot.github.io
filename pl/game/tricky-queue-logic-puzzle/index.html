Jak Grać

🔎 Read the clue cards beside the queue. They tell you where an animal belongs or how two animals should stand relative to one another. 👆 Drag one animal onto another to swap their places. You can also tap or click two animals in turn. When the order follows every clue, press Check Order. 💡 Use Undo to reverse your last move, Restart to try again, or Hint when you need help.
