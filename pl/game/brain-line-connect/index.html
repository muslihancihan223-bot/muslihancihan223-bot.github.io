Jak Grać

Start a level and look at all the dots you must connect. Place your finger on a dot to begin drawing. Drag your finger to draw one continuous line that connects every dot. Do not lift your finger (keep it as “one touch / one stroke”). Avoid obstacle lines if your drawn line touches a blocked/obstacle line, the try is considered wrong. When all dots are connected correctly, the level completes and you move to the next puzzle.
