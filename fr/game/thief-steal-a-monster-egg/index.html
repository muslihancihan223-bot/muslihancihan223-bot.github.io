Comment Jouer

Cross the red Safe Zone line into a monster zone, break an egg's shield with your pickaxe (Auto Swing can do it for you) and carry the egg back to a nest at your base. If a guardian spots you, run for it: dash or stun it with a power chord. Eggs hatch into monsters that earn coins every second. Collect them on the pad, buy better pickaxes and conveyor eggs, turn on the base shield and rebirth for more income. Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space to jump, left click or F to swing, E to interact, Q to drop, 1 and 2 to switch tools, Shift to dash, R for the power chord, T for Auto Swing, hold the right mouse button to look around. Mobile: joystick on the left, swipe on the right to look, on-screen buttons for actions.
