Comment Jouer

Tap the nest and the berry bush to drop eggs and plants (1 energy per tap). Drag an item onto two matching ones to merge three into a better one; merge five to get two. Tap an egg twice to hatch a dino. Put two different dinos together to make a hybrid, merge next to a dig spot to dig up fossils, and merge beside lava to cool it into treasure. Tap Orders to deliver what your dino friends ask for, then spend stars and cash in Restore to rebuild five islands. Controls: mouse or touch, tap and drag; drag empty ground to move, wheel or pinch to zoom.
