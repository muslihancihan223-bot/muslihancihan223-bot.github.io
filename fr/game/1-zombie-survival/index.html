Comment Jouer

Survive every night: zombies come from all streets, and each night ends when the horde is gone. Your power grows by +1 every second and makes every shot stronger. PC: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to shoot, right click to aim, Space to jump, Shift to sprint, R to reload, 1-0 or wheel or click the weapon bar to switch weapons, B or the hammer button for the blueprint, E to open or close a door or get in and out of a car (W/S gas and brake, A/D steer), F to repair, U to upgrade, G to start the night, Esc to pause. Mobile: left stick to move, drag on the right to look, fire button to shoot, tap a weapon in the bar, hammer for the blueprint, DRIVE / GET OUT next to a car, buttons under the sight to open, repair and upgrade.
