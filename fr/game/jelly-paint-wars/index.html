Jouer à Jelly Paint Wars

Jelly Paint Wars est un jeu gratuit de type Action, Tir, Color. Sans téléchargement ni inscription : jouez directement dans le navigateur sur mobile, tablette ou ordinateur.

ActionTirColor

Comment Jouer

Win the round: paint more of the map in Turf Clash, hold the zone in Zone Control and Moving Hill, deliver gems in Gem Collect, survive the shrinking arena in Rush Paint, finish first of 8 in Free-for-All, hold 10 zombie waves and beat the giant zombie, or deal as much damage as you can to the Daily Boss in 2 minutes. Computer: WASD to move, mouse to aim, left click to shoot, right click (held) to zoom with the sniper and the bow, Space to melt into your paint (swim and refill ink), Shift to dash, Q to throw a bomb, E for your special, T to emote, Y for quick chat online, P to pause. Phone (landscape): left stick to move, right stick to aim and shoot, and buttons for dash, melt, bomb and special.
