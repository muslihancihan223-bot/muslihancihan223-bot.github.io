Super Realistic Ragdoll est un jeu gratuit de type Ragdoll, Physique, Simulation. Sans téléchargement ni inscription : jouez directement dans le navigateur sur mobile, tablette ou ordinateur.

Comment Jouer

Goal: experiment on the living mannequins with tools and the test-ground machines, earn coins for damage and unlock new tools and polygons. PC: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to use the tool, right click for the second action, Space to jump, Shift to run, 1-9 or the wheel to pick a tool, G spawns a mannequin, R restores everyone, X toggles X-ray, Q opens the arsenal, M the polygons, P pauses, Tab frees the cursor. Mobile: left stick to move, swipe to look, on-screen buttons for actions.
