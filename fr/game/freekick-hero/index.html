Comment Jouer

Become a Freekick Hero! Step onto the pitch and score spectacular free kicks past defensive walls and goalkeepers. Choose between two modes: Career, where you play seasons and climb the leagues, and the Free Kick Challenge, where you chase your highest score before running out of lives. Adjust your aim for the wind, choose your strike point to control height and curve, and upgrade your power and curve and unlock special balls along the way. Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button + Drag: Pull the ball backward to aim and set shot power, then release to choose your strike point. Left Click: Select a point on the close-up ball to shoot. Ball Icon: Open the ball selection menu. Info Button on the Strike Screen: Show or hide the strike-point guide. Mobile Controls: Touch + Drag: Pull the ball backward with your finger to aim and set shot power, then release to choose your strike point. Tap: Select a point on the close-up ball to shoot. Ball Icon: Tap to open the ball selection menu. Info Button on the Strike Screen: Tap to show or hide the strike-point guide.
