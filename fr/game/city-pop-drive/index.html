Comment Jouer

Tap to start, then drag left or right to steer (mouse drag, arrow keys or A and D on desktop; P pauses). The car accelerates by itself. Pass close to traffic for near misses: they add time and fill the Breeze meter, and a full meter starts a Boost that knocks cars aside. Reach each checkpoint before the timer ends and pick a side at every fork. Reach a goal to collect its cover. Spend Miles on upgrades, cars and new maps.
