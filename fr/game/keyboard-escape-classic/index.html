Comment Jouer

Move with WASD or the arrow keys and jump with Space; on mobile use the left stick and the jump button, swipe on the right side to turn the camera. Every key you step on adds speed, and treadmills add it while you stand on them. Reach the next Safe Zone, step on the yellow Wins pad to bank Wins, and use them for trails, auras and cosmetics. Avoid falling into the chocolate, rolling marshmallows, the chocolate wave and the arena guards. Press E or tap the button near a stand to open it. Rebirth when you reach the level to get a permanent multiplier.
