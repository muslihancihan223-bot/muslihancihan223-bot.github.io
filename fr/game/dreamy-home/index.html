Comment Jouer

The game has simple controls. If you are playing on a computer: - Click on an item with the mouse; - Hold down the mouse key and drag the item to the desired location. If you are playing on a smartphone: - Tap the item with your finger; - Hold your finger on the touchscreen and drag the item to the desired location. There are also 3 boosters available to you: - Hint (light bulb icon). Sets the item to its correct location; - Freeze. Freezes the timer for 60 seconds; - Compass. Will show the location of any 4 items.
