Comment Jouer

Desktop: W or Up to ride, A/D or Left/Right to steer, S to brake, Shift for a wheelie, H for the horn, C to switch the camera. Mobile: hold the screen to ride and drag left or right to steer; use the on-screen buttons to brake, wheelie and honk.
