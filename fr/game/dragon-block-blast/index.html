Comment Jouer

How to Play — Dragon Egg Quest Enter the quest — Tap Dragon Egg Quest from the home screen. Pick your level on the forest map (new levels unlock as you clear the one before). Place blocks — Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 grid. You always have 3 pieces; when they’re all used, a fresh set appears. Clear lines for points — Fill a full row or column to blast it away and earn bonus points. Chain multiple clears for combo multipliers. Hit the score goal — Each level has a score target that climbs as you advance. Reach it to clear the level — there’s no clock in solo mode, but the grid can fill up. Watch your dragon grow — Every 10 levels triggers an egg milestone: your dragon evolves on the campaign map, with new backdrops and story beats as you progress. Earn rewards — Clearing levels earns coins for hints and cosmetics. Stuck with no valid moves? Watch a short ad to get fresh blocks and keep going. Unlock the full game — Reach Level 25 to unlock Head to Head rival duels. Push to Level 100 to complete the quest and see your dragon’s final form.
