Comment Jouer

Click or tap a resource to harvest it before the timer runs out. Between phases, spend your resources on the upgrade tree to get stronger and unlock new biomes. Keep hunger and thirst up: hungry = weak hits, thirsty = half the materials. Desktop: mouse, or Arrows/WASD + Space to move and harvest; P or Esc to pause. Mobile: tap resources and upgrades; II button to pause.
