Jouer à Charm Studio: Jewelry Design DIY

Charm Studio: Jewelry Design DIY est un jeu gratuit de type Simulation, Filles, 3D. Sans téléchargement ni inscription : jouez directement dans le navigateur sur mobile, tablette ou ordinateur.

Comment Jouer

Listen to the client's order and the themes she asks for. Tap the + on each piece in the jewelry box to make it. Pick a chain and its material, then tap a charm to hang it on the next free link, or drag it onto the link you want. Tap a hung charm to restyle its color and metal. Press Ready to put the piece in the box. Once the bracelet, necklace and ring are in, decorate the box and press Showdown! Rate each rival's box from 1 to 5 stars, then see where yours lands. Finish on the podium to sell the box and earn coins. Desktop Controls: Left Click: Pick a chain, charm or button, and hang a charm. Drag: Move a charm to another link, or turn the view. Mouse Wheel: Scroll the galleries, or zoom in and out. Mobile Controls: Tap: Pick a chain, charm or button, and hang a charm. Drag: Move a charm to another link, or turn the view. Pinch: Zoom in and out.
