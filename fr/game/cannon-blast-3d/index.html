Comment Jouer

Aim the cannon at the best target and fire. Use physics to knock objects down, break supports and trigger chain reactions. Explosives can help destroy several targets at once. Your shots are limited, so examine each structure carefully and choose the most effective target. Destroy all required objects to complete the level and move on to the next challenge.
