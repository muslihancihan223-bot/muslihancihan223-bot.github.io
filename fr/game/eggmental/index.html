Comment Jouer

Hold the Prism Core until the end of each day. Mouse: move the pointer over the field to strike the Blight and crack eggs; it hits whatever it touches. Click buttons, shop offers and Lattice nodes; drag or use the arrows to pan the Lattice. Space: show the stats of the creatures under the pointer. P or Esc: pause. Q / E: switch tabs on the upgrade screen. Hold R: give up the current try. Touch: drag your finger over the field (the pointer sits just above it), or tap AUTO and the pointer sweeps by itself. On upgrades, tap once to read, tap again to buy.
