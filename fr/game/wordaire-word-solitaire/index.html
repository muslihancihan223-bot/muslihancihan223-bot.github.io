Jouer à Wordaire: Word Solitaire

Wordaire: Word Solitaire est un jeu gratuit de type Puzzle, Cards, Words. Sans téléchargement ni inscription : jouez directement dans le navigateur sur mobile, tablette ou ordinateur.

PuzzleCardsWordsSolitaireRelaxantsLogique

Comment Jouer

Goal: put every card on a foundation. Drag a category card onto an empty foundation slot, then drag the words of that category onto it (a counter shows how many are left). Tap the deck to draw new cards. Words of one category can be stacked on each other in the columns to free hidden cards. Double-click (double-tap) a card to send it to its category. You have a limited number of moves per level; Hint, Undo and the Star (wild card) boosters help on tricky levels. Controls: mouse on desktop (drag and drop, click), touch on phones and tablets.
