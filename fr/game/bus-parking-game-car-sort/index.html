Comment Jouer

🎯 Board every passenger before the parking slots run out. 👆 Tap a bus to drive it to a free parking slot. 🚧 Road blocked? The bus bumps back, so clear the way first. 🎨 Passengers board the bus that matches their color. 🚌 A full bus drives away and earns coins. 🔀 Stuck? Shuffle swaps bus colors, Sort reorders the queue, VIP slot teleports a bus. 😵 Slots full and no match? Restart and try a new plan. 💡 Tip: park the bus that matches the front of the queue first.
