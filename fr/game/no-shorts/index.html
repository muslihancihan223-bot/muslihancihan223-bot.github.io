Comment Jouer

🎯 Your task is to cause as much damage to stickmen and the environment as possible by shooting from a cannon. You can quickly switch between levels, like and create your own ragdoll stories. 🕹️ Controls - Click in the right place to shoot. - Quickly switch between levels by swiping up and down or using a virtual button. 💡Tip: You can create your own ragdoll stories in the level designer and like others.
