Jouer à Cube Rivals

Cube Rivals est un jeu gratuit de type 3D, Tir, First Person Shooter. Sans téléchargement ni inscription : jouez directement dans le navigateur sur mobile, tablette ou ordinateur.

3DTirFirst Person Shooter

Comment Jouer

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to shoot, right click to aim, Space to jump, Shift to dash, Ctrl or C while running to slide, 1 / 2 / 3 to switch weapons, Q for the last weapon, G for your gadget, R to reload, Tab for the scoreboard, Esc to pause. Mobile: the left side of the screen is a floating joystick, drag on the right side to look, tap the big button to shoot (or enable auto-fire in the settings), plus buttons for jump, dash, crouch, aim, reload, weapon swap and gadget. Eliminate rivals to score. Health regenerates after a few seconds without damage. Aim for the head, use cover, dash to dodge and throw your gadget at groups.
