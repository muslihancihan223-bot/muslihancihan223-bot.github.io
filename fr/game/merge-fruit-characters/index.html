Comment Jouer

Throw fruits into the glass and combine the same ones to create new fruits and unlock unique characters. For example, two Baby Blue Blueberries will turn into Princess Stella "Little Beak" Strawberry-Belle, and two sisters will turn into Champion Cherry "Bouncy Bunny" Cherryton. Clicking allows you to make a quick throw, while holding and moving activates a laser sight for more accurate hits. Fill the glass, but be careful, if even one fruit falls out of the glass, the game will end. Use the space wisely, and develop your own system! Use bonuses to progress faster in the game. Spin the wheel of fortune to receive random rewards, and don't forget to check out the daily quests - completing them will earn you useful bonuses. Upgrade your abilities in the store and progress through the levels, unlocking new features and characters. Become a leader in points and levels, chat with others, and share your achievements!
