Comment Jouer

A number next to a row or column is the length of a run of filled cells, and its colour is the colour of that run. Several numbers mean several runs in that order; runs of the same colour have at least one empty cell between them. • Tap a cell to fill it with the selected colour; drag to fill a line. • Pick a colour with the buttons below; "×" marks cells that are surely empty. • Undo takes a move back, any number of times. • The "+1 cell for 3 seeds" hint reveals a correct cell. • "Zoom" or a pinch zooms in: one finger paints, two fingers pan. A big grid opens zoomed in; "Whole field" shows all of it. • On a computer: arrows move the cursor, Space or Enter fills, X crosses, 1–5 pick a colour, Z undoes, +, − and 0 zoom, Esc opens settings; the wheel pans a big grid. A finished line greys out its numbers. Start with lines whose numbers nearly fill them. With mistake checking on, a mistake costs a leaf; out of three, restart, take three more for an ad or play on without checking.
