Jouer à Cleaning Simulator: Spotless

Cleaning Simulator: Spotless est un jeu gratuit de type Simulation, Relaxants. Sans téléchargement ni inscription : jouez directement dans le navigateur sur mobile, tablette ou ordinateur.

SimulationRelaxants

Comment Jouer

Goal: make each thing spotless. Choose a tool at the bottom of the screen and move your finger or the mouse over the dirt while holding it down. The hint on the picture tells you what to do next, and the tool you need glows with a green outline. A level ends by itself when everything is clean; then press Next level. On a computer, keys 1-9 also pick tools. The pause button is in the top corner.
