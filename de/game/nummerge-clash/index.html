So wird gespielt

Become a Master of Number Combinations! Your main goal is to achieve the highest score and survive as long as possible. Connect adjacent cards with matching values, increase their worth, and clear space for new cards. The game ends when there are no available moves left and you lack energy for tactical actions. Plan your strategy, watch the upcoming card queue, and use tactical moves to avoid deadlocks! Controls: Desktop Controls: Left Click on Card: Merge the card with all neighbors of the same value. Long Press on Card: View card description and properties. Swap Button: Activate mode to swap two adjacent cards. Destroy Button: Remove selected card using energy. “+” Button near Card Queue: Open shop and buy cards with coins. Mobile Controls: Tap on Card: Merge the card with all neighbors of the same value. Long Press on Card: View card description and properties. Energy Panel: Select swap or destroy mode. Destroy Button: Remove selected card using energy. “+” Button near Card Queue: Open shop and buy cards with coins.
