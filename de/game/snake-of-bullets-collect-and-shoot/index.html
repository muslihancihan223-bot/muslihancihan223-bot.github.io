So wird gespielt

Swipe your finger or mouse cursor across the screen so that the snake from the cartridge moves along the most promising route. Start with one simple bullet, and collect as many improved bullets as possible (harder than you think). Try to avoid traps and saws on the road. If you hit an obstacle, you lose a few bullets. Load ammo into the best guns (tanks, planes, submachine guns, pistols, etc.), and shoot to destroy the walls standing in your way.
