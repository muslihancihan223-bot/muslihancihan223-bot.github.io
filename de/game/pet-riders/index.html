So wird gespielt

Desktop controls: WASD or arrows — move; mouse — camera; mouse wheel — zoom; E — interact; Space — jump and rise in flight; Shift — descend; B — pets; M — map; R — dismount; C — camera behind; H — home; Esc — pause. Phones and tablets: stick — move; swipe the screen — camera; pinch — zoom; big button — interact; arrow buttons — jump, rise and descend. One-tap flight and mirrored controls can be turned on in the settings.
