So wird gespielt

Help Smudge the raccoon prank his grumpy neighbour, Mr. Crabtree! In every level, get the prank (a water balloon, a pie, a paint bucket and more) to hit Mr. Crabtree. Draw one line, like a ramp, a bridge or a lever, then let go and physics does the rest. The less ink you use, the more stars you earn (up to 3 per level). Desktop Controls: Mouse: Click and drag to draw your line, release to start the prank. R: Retry the level. P: Pause. Space or Enter: Continue (home screen). ? button: Get a hint. Skip button: Skip a level you're stuck on (after 3 tries). Mobile Controls: Swipe: Draw your line, lift your finger to start the prank. Arrow button: Retry the level. ? button: Get a hint. Pause button: Pause. Skip button: Skip a level you're stuck on (after 3 tries).
