So wird gespielt

Computer: WASD to walk, mouse to look, E to take a window or leave it, right click for the scope, mouse wheel to change zoom, click to shoot, SPACE to hold your breath, F for the drone, H for a hint, T for the pulse (Chroma rifle), R to reload, G to open a gift, ALT to free the mouse, ESC to pause. Phone and tablet (portrait or landscape): move with the stick on the left, drag to look, tap TAKE POSITION at a window, the scope button to zoom, the bullet button to shoot, the DRONE and HINT buttons, and BACK to return from the drone. Goal: find every hidden stickman in each room before you run out of bullets, earn stars and open the five floors.
