So wird gespielt

Race to the finish gate ahead of your rivals, or pick Free smash and wreck everything around you. Desktop: W/S or up/down arrows to accelerate and brake, A/D or left/right arrows to steer, Shift for nitro, Space for the handbrake, P or Tab to pause. Mobile: steering buttons on the left, gas, brake and nitro on the right. When the car is wrecked, press Back on the road and repair to return to the road. Damage, stunts and your place earn points that unlock new tracks.
