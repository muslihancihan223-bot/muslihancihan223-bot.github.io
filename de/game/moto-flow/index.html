So wird gespielt

Hold the gas (Right Arrow, or the right half of the screen) to ride the hills. In the air, Left Arrow (left half of the screen) leans back for a backflip, and holding both stretches you into a Superman. Land on both wheels for a perfect landing and a turbo. Ride as far as you can, collect coins and spend them on bikes, stages and tuning. Space or Enter rides again, M mutes.
