So wird gespielt

Dodge the police cars and survive as long as you can to achieve the highest score. Collect coins to fill the garage with cars. Mobile Controls: Hold Left Side of Screen: Steer left. Hold Right Side of Screen: Steer right. Hold Both Sides: Reverse. Release: Straighten up — the car keeps accelerating on its own. Button Mode: Switch to on-screen arrows with GO and REVERSE pedals at any time; your choice is saved. Camera Button: Tap to drop the view in behind the car. Reset Button: Tap to free your car if it gets stuck. Desktop Controls: ← (Left Arrow) or A: Steer left. → (Right Arrow) or D: Steer right. ↓ (Down Arrow) or S: Reverse. ↑ (Up Arrow), W or Spacebar: Accelerate (in button mode — by default the car accelerates on its own). Mouse: Hold the left button on the left or right side of the screen to steer that way. R: Reset your car if it gets stuck, without ending the run. Esc or the on-screen pause button: Pause.
