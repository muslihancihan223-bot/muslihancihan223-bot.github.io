So wird gespielt

Inspect each customer's car correctly to earn money, then spend it on new tools, lifts, staff and businesses. Follow the goal card at the top to always know your next step. Computer: WASD or arrow keys to walk and drive, mouse to look and click to interact, E to interact, Space for the handbrake, C for the hood camera, M for the map, Esc to pause. Phone and tablet: left joystick to move, on-screen buttons to interact, gas and brake pedals while driving.
