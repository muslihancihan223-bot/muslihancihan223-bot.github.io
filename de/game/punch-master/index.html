So wird gespielt

Clear each level by knocking out every target robot. Tap or click a robot to punch it: the glove flies out on the stretching arm. Hold the mouse button or your finger to charge the glove; a full charge pierces up to three robots in a line. Shield robots block plain punches, so hit the head or the side, or use a charged punch. Bomb robots explode and knock their neighbours over. Do not hit spectator robots. Hits in a row build a combo for extra coins.
