So wird gespielt

Goal: sort the sand so that each vessel holds only one colour. Click or tap a vessel to lift the top layer of sand, then click or tap another vessel to pour it in. Sand can only be poured onto the same colour or into an empty vessel. The level is solved when every colour is gathered in its own vessel. Stuck? Use a helper: undo your last move, shuffle the board, or add an extra empty vessel. Helpers cost coins, which you earn by solving levels. If no moves are left, restart the level. Restarting or giving up costs one life; lives refill over time. Controls: mouse or touch only, no keyboard needed.
