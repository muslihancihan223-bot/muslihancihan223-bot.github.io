So wird gespielt

Cover more floor than the rivals before time runs out. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold left mouse button to spray, hold Shift to melt into your goo (faster, refills your tank), Space to jump, E or right mouse button to throw a balloon, Q or F for the Geyser when charged, P to pause. Mobile: drag on the left to move, drag on the right to aim, and use the on-screen buttons. Win a match to unlock the next one.
