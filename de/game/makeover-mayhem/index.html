So wird gespielt

How to play Scrub the grime — Drag across the dirty item to wipe away grime and reveal the cleaner layer underneath. Use the right tool — Each stage gives you a new tool in order: scraper → foam spray → scrub brush → squeegee. Complete each stage — Clear enough grime (about 90% of the dirty area) and the stage finishes on its own; the next tool unlocks. Clean all five items — Work through every stage on all five items in a match: rug, sneaker, trophy, teapot, and watch. Beat your rival — Clean all five items before your rival does to win. If time runs out, whoever finished more items (or made more overall progress) wins.
