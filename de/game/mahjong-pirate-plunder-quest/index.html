Embark on a Pirate Treasure Quest! Set sail as a fearless pirate captain and plunder hidden riches across the Caribbean seas in this addictive Mahjong solitaire adventure. Match pairs of identical free tiles featuring vibrant pirate icons—like treasure chests, gold coins, parrots, cannons, ships, anchors, and skulls—to clear the board and claim your bounty! Your main goal is to remove all tiles from the board by finding and matching open pairs, earning up to 3 stars per level: 1 for completion, a second for finishing without hints, and a third for beating the time limit. Progress through over 4,000 levels, unlock new backgrounds, and tackle daily challenges to become the ultimate mahjong pirate!
