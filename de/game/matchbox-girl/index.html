So wird gespielt

Survive until New Year. Keep the thermometer out of the blue zone and the energy bar above zero. WASD to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, Ctrl or C to lie down on cardboard. Hold the left mouse button to pick up and carry boxes and food; food in your hands gets eaten. F takes out a match, click to strike it and set a box on fire to warm up. Q hugs yourself. H shows all controls. On phones use the joystick, swipe up to strike a match, and tap the buttons on screen.
