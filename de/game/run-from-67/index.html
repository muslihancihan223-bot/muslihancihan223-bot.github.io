So wird gespielt

Goal: find 6 backpacks hidden around the floor. Collect them all and the seventh door opens, and that is the exit. Sixseven hunts by ear. Sprinting is heard from 20 metres, walking from 10, crouching from 4, and standing perfectly still makes no sound at all. The circle on the minimap is how far you can be heard right now. Jumping is quiet; the landing is what gives you away. The flashlight is a trade. Without it you see nothing, but shine it at Sixseven and he charges from across the floor even if you were silent. The light cone is drawn on the minimap as a second danger zone. The battery lasts about a minute; hold E at a wall panel to refill it. Coin: press Q to throw one. It clinks where it lands and he goes to investigate. It has its own hearing radius, shown on the map, so you can see exactly where you sent him. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to sprint, C to crouch, Space to jump, E to open doors, climb into lockers and hold at charging panels, Q to throw a coin, F for the flashlight, P to pause. Mobile: left stick to move, pushed fully forward it sprints on its own. Drag on the right side of the screen to look, double tap to jump. The hand button opens doors and lockers, tapping the battery toggles the flashlight, crouch is in the bottom left corner. Sprinting drains the blue stamina bar, so do not run all the time. A burger permanently raises it, cola gives unlimited sprint for 10 seconds. When he is close, hide in a locker: press E to climb in, Space to get out. Inside he cannot see you even if he walks right past.
