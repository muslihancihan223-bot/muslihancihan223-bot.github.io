So wird gespielt

On desktop, move with WASD. Zoom the camera with the mouse wheel or the + and − buttons. Walk to the water, press SPACE, aim at an available spot and confirm the cast. While reeling, click when the moving bar enters the highlighted zone. Land several accurate hits to place the fish in your keepnet. On mobile, move the fisher with the round joystick, tap the fishing button and choose the casting point with your finger. Use + and − to adjust the camera. Carry the catch into the bright circle by the receiving station. Fish are automatically placed on an available conveyor, and coins are paid after processing. Use the shop to buy rods, tackle, clothing, workshops, new fishing areas, lanterns and other upgrades. Follow the tutorial to learn the full route from casting to your first rod upgrade. Ads are optional and only provide extra bonuses.
