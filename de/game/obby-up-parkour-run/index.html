So wird gespielt

Reach the top of the obstacle course by crossing platforms and avoiding traps. Activate checkpoints to save your progress, and use the size slider to shrink or grow when the route demands it. Collect coins along the way and explore side challenges. If you fall, return to a checkpoint and try again. Optional rewarded videos can unlock extra boosters, pets, and outfits. Desktop controls - WASD or arrow keys: move. - Space: jump; press again in the air when double jump is available. - Click and drag the camera area: look around. - Drag the size slider: shrink or grow. - E or the on-screen hammer button: strike when the hammer is equipped. - Escape: pause or resume. - Use the on-screen buttons to activate boosters and open settings. Mobile controls - Drag the left joystick to move. - Swipe the camera area to look around. - Tap the jump or hammer button for the current action. - Drag the size slider to shrink or grow. - Use the settings button to control music and sound effects. Play in landscape orientation. Purchases are optional and are offered only when the platform supports them.
