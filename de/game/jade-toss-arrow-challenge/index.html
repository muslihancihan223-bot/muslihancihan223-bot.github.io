So wird gespielt

Hit the Jade Pot! Step into a quiet temple courtyard and play pitch-pot, the ancient East Asian game of tossing arrows into a jar. Each hit scores points and keeps your lives; each miss costs one of your 5 lives. Chain 3 hits in a row to enter the zone and double your points, land in the centre of the mouth for a Perfect bonus, and keep going as the pot moves farther away and the wind picks up. Beat your best score, clear the challenges and take on the daily challenge! Desktop Controls: Left Mouse Button (hold on the arrow): Grab the arrow. Drag Down: Set the throw power. A longer pull throws farther. Drag Left / Right: Aim to the side to fight the wind. Release Left Mouse Button: Throw the arrow. Click: Start the next throw. Speaker Icon (top bar): Turn sound on or off. Menu Icon (top bar): Pause, challenges and settings. Mobile Controls: Touch the Arrow: Grab the arrow. Drag Down: Set the throw power. A longer pull throws farther. Drag Left / Right: Aim to the side to fight the wind. Lift Your Finger: Throw the arrow. Tap: Start the next throw.
