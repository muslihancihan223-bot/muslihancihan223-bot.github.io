So wird gespielt

Move with WASD, arrow keys or the on-screen joystick (mouse or touch). Space or the Dash button makes a quick dash, E or the Decoy button sends a ghost decoy. As a hider, stay out of the red vision cone until the timer ends, hide in bushes and stand next to brainrots to grab them. Locked laser gates block the way: stand at a gate for a moment to hack it, or step on the lock button inside a base to lock it. As the seeker, catch every hider before time runs out. In My Base, put brainrots on podiums and collect the coins they earn.
