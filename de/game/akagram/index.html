So wird gespielt

Place bulbs to illuminate every empty cell. Light travels horizontally and vertically until it hits a wall, and bulbs must not shine on each other. Match all clues: wall numbers count adjacent bulbs (no diagonals), and edge numbers count bulbs in each row or column. Click or tap to place or remove bulbs. Use the × tool to mark cells where no bulb belongs, and the on-screen buttons for undo, redo, restart, and hints. Zoom with the mouse wheel on desktop or pinch on mobile. Drag or swipe to move around the board.
