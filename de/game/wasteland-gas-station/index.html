GOAL Survive 30 days and nights as the gas station's guard to earn the $20,000 contract. DAY • Fill up customers' cars at the pumps and serve customers at the register. • Take boxes from the storeroom and restock the shelves. • Bring the jerrycan to the generator. Without fuel, the lights go out at night. • Inspect suspicious customers and decide who to help. • Build fence panels behind and beside the shop. NIGHT • At 20:00 buy ammo, medkits, fuel and traps with your earnings. • Zombies attack from 2 a.m. Survive until 5 a.m. and complete the night's mission. • Go for headshots: zombies hit in the body can get back up. Shoot their legs to make them crawl. • Lay traps in the zombies' path and use your flashlight in the dark. PC CONTROLS WASD – move · Shift – sprint · Mouse – look E – use / interact (hold) · Q – inspect · X – refuse · G – drop Left click – shoot · Right click – aim · R – reload · F – shove 1 / 2 / 3 – weapons · 4–7 or T – traps · H – medkit · V – flashlight Space – break free when grabbed · M – mute MOBILE CONTROLS • Left side: virtual stick to move (push to the edge to sprint) • Right side: drag to look around • Fire button – shoot (auto-fire is on by default), aim button – aim, reload button – reload • Hold the round icons on objects to use them (pumps, register, shelves, generator) • Bottom bar: weapons, traps, medkit and flashlight • Mash the fire button to break free when a zombie grabs you • Play in landscape mode
