So wird gespielt

Goal: survive as long as you can and defeat the bosses. Desktop: move with WASD or the arrow keys, or hold the mouse button to steer toward the cursor; Esc or P pauses. Mobile: drag anywhere on the left side of the screen to move. Collect essence gems to level up and pick one of three mutations that change your body.
