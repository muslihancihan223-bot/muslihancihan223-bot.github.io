Simulation

So wird gespielt

Find the legendary needle hidden in the haystack. Aim at the hay and grab it to fill your backpack, then walk into the Hay Press to sell it for coins. Buy the Seeker Compass, tools and bigger backpacks in the workshop and follow the compass arrow; dig down where the needle glints. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, left mouse button to grab, E to buy or open, Q to throw a firecracker, Space to jump. Mobile: stick on the left, swipe on the right to look, Grab button to collect hay.
