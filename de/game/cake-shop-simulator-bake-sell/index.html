So wird gespielt

Open the Chats tab and tap a customer to read the order. Tap "Take the order!" and build the cake step by step: shape and tiers, glaze and drips, cream, a drawing on top and decorations. The order card at the top shows what the customer asked for: matching items turn green and raise the price. On the Decor step pick a decoration and tap the cake to place it. Drag to spin the cake, use the mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom. Tap "Send" and haggle: pick a friendly, fair or pricey ask, then accept the counteroffer, meet halfway or hold your price. Each hold costs one heart of the buyer's patience; when the hearts run out, the buyer leaves. Close all orders of the day and tap "New day". Post sold cakes on Cakegram for likes and followers, and spend coins on kitchen upgrades, cake stands and interiors. Controls: mouse on desktop, touch on mobile.
