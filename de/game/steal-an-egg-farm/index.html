So wird gespielt

Approach a basket and take an egg. Run back across the safe line before the guard catches you. If you are caught, the stolen egg returns to its basket. Bring a secured egg to the incubator and wait for it to hatch. Pick up the animal, carry it to your pasture and place it inside. Coins are earned automatically. Step onto your treadmill with empty hands to train. Improve your speed, expand your pasture and grow feed for your pets. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, E to interact, Q to put down an egg or animal. Hold the right mouse button to rotate the camera; use the mouse wheel to zoom. Mobile: left joystick to move, swipe on the right to rotate the camera. Purple button to jump, green button to interact, gold button to put down cargo or place an animal in the pasture.
