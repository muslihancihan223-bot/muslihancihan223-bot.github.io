Press or tap ROLL to throw two dice and move around the board. Land on coin, crown, mystery, upgrade, and mini-game tiles to earn rewards. On mobile, use touch controls and follow the on-screen prompts. Complete every building and objective in Sunny Keep, Pirate Cove, and Ember Kingdom, then finish the Royal Festival to complete the campaign.
