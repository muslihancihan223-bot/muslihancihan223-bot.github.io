Welcome to our thrilling adventure game! Your journey consists of two main parts: merging items and exploring dungeons. Merging Items In the lower part of the screen, you will find the merging grid. This is where you combine different items to gain various effects: - Weapons: Merge different weapon parts to create powerful weapons that deal damage to enemies. - Potions: Combine potions to restore health and boost your survivability. - Keys: Merge keys to unlock chests scattered throughout the dungeon, which contain valuable loot. - Coin Pouches: Collect and merge coin pouches to purchase new items from the shop and expand your inventory by buying travel bags from the merchant. Dungeon Exploration In the upper part of the screen, your character moves through the dungeon level by level. Use the items you've merged to overcome challenges and defeat enemies as you delve deeper into the dungeon. Embark on this epic journey, combine powerful items, and conquer the depths of the dungeon!
