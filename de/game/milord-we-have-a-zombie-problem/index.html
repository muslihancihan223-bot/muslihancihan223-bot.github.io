So wird gespielt

Goal: keep the dead from reaching your keep until the whole horde is gone. Before each battle, pick a tower in the bottom bar (or press 1-7) and click on the walls or raised ground beside the road to build it, or drag it from the bar onto the map. Then click or drag to set its aim; towers sweep around that point. Drag your hero to any spot on the road. Press Space (or tap Start Battle) to begin. During the battle, click the horde to hurl a Royal Fireball (hold to keep casting) and use your other spells with keys 1-4. Right mouse button drag or the arrow keys pan the map, the wheel zooms, Space pauses. After the battle, spend Souls, Royal Seals and Holy Relics in the War Council to unlock and upgrade towers, spells and heroes. On phones: tap to build, drag to aim, pinch to zoom.
