So wird gespielt

Finish ahead of five rivals. Races and tournaments pay prize money: spend it in the shop and the workshop on parts, tyres and setup. Broken parts slow you down until you repair them. W / ↑ — throttle S / ↓ — brake and reverse A / D or ← / → — steering. By default it works like a real transmitter: when the car drives toward you, left and right are reversed. Switch to Simple in Settings if you prefer. Esc — pause and back, Enter — main action, R — restart F — full screen, M — sound, mouse wheel — camera zoom Gamepads and USB RC transmitters are supported (set up in Settings).
