So wird gespielt

Tap or click an empty patch beside the path to plant. Tap a plant to upgrade one of its three branches or to sell it. Tap the coins that pests drop before they vanish. Start each wave with Next wave (or wait for it), stop the pests before they reach the garden: you have 20 hearts and one continue. Mouse wheel or pinch zooms, drag pans. Keys: 1-3 pick a branch, S sells, Space calls the next wave, F speed, R Rich Soil, P pause.
