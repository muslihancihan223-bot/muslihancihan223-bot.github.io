Dronner 3D - Immerse yourself in dynamic racing in a unique car that can turn into a quadcopter and overcome exciting tracks. Collect points and battery charges, avoid collisions that are especially dangerous in quadcopter mode, and monitor damage status and battery charge levels. As you progress through levels, upgrade your car by increasing battery capacity and repairing damage. At the end of each level you will find a table with statistics where you can evaluate your achievements. Desktop Controls: 1. Movement on the ground: ↑ (Up Arrow) or W : Move forward. ↓ (Down Arrow) or W : Move back. ← (Left Arrow) or A : Move left. → (Right Arrow) or D : Move right. 2. Switching modes: Spacebar: Transform the machine into a quadcopter and back. 3. Air control: ↑ (Up Arrow) or W : Move forward. ↓ (Down Arrow) or W : Move back. ← (Left Arrow) or A : Move left. → (Right Arrow) or D : Move right. Q: Move upward. E: Move downward.
