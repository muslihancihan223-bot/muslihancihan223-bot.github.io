So wird gespielt

Your goal in Save Nemo is to help the fish escape dangerous situations and save the Fish Princess. On desktop, use the left mouse button. On a phone or tablet, tap and drag on the screen. - Choice puzzles: click or tap one of the available options to choose an action. - Pin puzzles: click or tap the right pin, or hold and drag it to move it. Think about the order before releasing water or opening a path. - Pipe puzzles: click or tap pipe pieces to turn them and connect a continuous route. Avoid traps and the shark, solve each puzzle to continue the adventure, and use decorations to make your island your own.
