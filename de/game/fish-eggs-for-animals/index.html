So wird gespielt

Objective: Catch fish, display them in your aquarium, and earn enough to cast farther and farther. There’s no game over. Your goals are to reach the final zone, The Last Deep, and complete the Fish Index by catching every species. How to play: Cast: Press CAST, then stop the moving meter as close to the glowing end as you can. Better timing means a longer cast. Catch: Where your bait lands decides which fish you can catch. Farther zones have more valuable fish and better luck. Display: Pick your new fish from the hotbar and place it in your aquarium. Visitors pay to see it, earning you money every second, even while you’re away. Train: Do practice casts at the TRAIN station to level up your Cast Power and reach new zones. Upgrade: Spend your money on better bait for rarer fish, new rods for faster training, and a bigger aquarium. Rebirth: Once you’ve earned enough, reset your run for permanent stars that boost your income, XP and luck. Controls: Space: cast and stop the meter W, A, S, D or arrow keys: walk Mouse click or tap: walk, use stations and place fish 1–9: pick a fish from the hotbar Enter: collect a catch Mouse drag: rotate the camera Mouse wheel: zoom On mobile, tap to play and drag to rotate the camera
